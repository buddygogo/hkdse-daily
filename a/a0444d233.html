English

Hong Kong can act as a natural bridge between mainland China’s fast-moving artificial intelligence sector and the rest of the world, DBS chief executive Tan Su Shan said ahead of the bank’s inaugural Asia Future Forward Conference. Speaking before a dialogue session with Singapore Prime Minister Lawrence Wong and TikTok chief executive Shou Chew, Tan argued that the city is well placed to absorb breakthroughsto take in and make practical use of new discoveries or technologies吸納新技術突破 in small language models and enterprise AI developed across the border, then help mainland firms use Hong Kong’s financial infrastructure to reach international markets.

Tan framed the city’s role less as a technology developer in its own right and more as a connective huba central point that links different places, markets or systems together聯繫樞紐 where mainland innovation meets global capital. She pointed to DBS’s own experience, saying its use of AI and machine learning has already generated roughly HK$6.1 billion in economic value for the bank, and that Hong Kong remains one of its core markets for wealth managementprofessional services that help clients grow and protect their financial assets財富管理, capital markets and digital-asset business. The bank also confirmed plans to hire 600 frontline advisers and platform engineers in the region by 2028, a sign of continued headcount expansionan increase in the number of staff a company employs人手擴張 despite widespread automation elsewhere in banking.

Tan’s remarks come as banks across the region compete to define how generative AI will reshape financial services without displacing frontline jobsremoving human workers from roles because machines or software now do the work instead取代前線職位 entirely. She argued that trust, data safety and human oversight must remain central as institutions move from being merely AI-enableda system or organisation that uses artificial intelligence tools to support its existing processes具備人工智能功能 toward becoming fully AI-native, telling the audience that "AI should save humans, not just jobs." Economists say Hong Kong’s bid to position itself this way will depend heavily on whether local talent and regulation can keep pace with the mainland’s rapid AI development.

中文翻譯

星展銀行行政總裁高博德(Tan Su Shan)在該行首屆「亞洲未來前瞻會議」舉行前表示,香港有能力成為中國內地快速發展的人工智能產業與世界其他地方之間的天然橋樑。她在與新加坡總理黃循財及TikTok行政總裁周受資對話前發言,指香港有條件吸納新技術突破,涵蓋內地研發的小型語言模型及企業人工智能應用,再協助內地企業借助香港的金融基建接通國際市場。

高博德將香港的角色定位為並非自行研發科技,而是作為連接內地創新與全球資金的聯繫樞紐。她以星展銀行自身經驗為例,指該行運用人工智能及機器學習至今已為銀行創造約61億港元的經濟價值,而香港仍是該行在財富管理、資本市場及數碼資產業務上的核心市場之一。該行亦確認計劃在2028年前於區內增聘600名前線顧問及平台工程師,顯示即使銀行業各處自動化浪潮不斷,仍錄得人手擴張。

高博德的言論正值區內各大銀行爭相定義生成式人工智能將如何重塑金融服務之際,同時力求避免完全取代前線職位。她認為,隨著機構由僅僅具備人工智能功能邁向成為完全「AI原生」,信任、數據安全及人為監督仍須保持核心地位,並向在場人士表示「人工智能應該拯救人類,而不只是拯救職位」。經濟學者指出,香港能否成功定位為這座橋樑,將高度取決於本地人才及監管能否追上內地人工智能的飛速發展。
