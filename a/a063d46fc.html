English

Hong Kong schools handled an average of 496 bullying casesformally reported incidents in which a student is targeted with repeated, intentional harm by others學生持續遭受他人故意傷害而被正式舉報的事件 a year between the 2022/23 and 2024/25 academic years, a 32 per cent increase on the roughly 420 cases recorded annually in the three years before that, according to new Education Bureau figures. The rise comes despite the bureau's long-standing “zero tolerance” stance on bullying in schools and has reignited concern about student wellbeing across the sector.

A local youth concern group said part of the increase likely reflects greater willingness among students, parents and teachers to report incidentsto formally tell a teacher, school or authority about something that has happened向老師、學校或有關當局正式講述已發生的事情 rather than stay silent, a shift that officials and educators have spent years trying to encourage. At the same time, the group warned that many younger students still struggle to distinguishto recognise or identify the difference between two similar things辨識或識別兩件相似事物之間的分別 good-natured joking from genuine bullying, meaning some disputes escalate before teachers intervene, while others may be mislabelled as bullying when they are not.

Counsellors say the figures likely understate the true scale of the problem, since much peer conflictdisagreement or fighting between people of similar age or social status年齡或社會地位相近人士之間的爭執或衝突 among teenagers now plays out partly online, beyond what a school-based reporting mechanisma formal system within a school through which students or staff can report problems學校內部讓學生或職員舉報問題的正式制度 can easily capture. They are calling for clearer anti-bullying guidelinesofficial rules or advice designed to prevent and respond to bullying旨在預防及處理欺凌行為的官方規則或指引 tailored to social media, alongside more training for teachers on spotting early warning signsearly indications that a problem may be developing before it becomes serious問題在惡化前的早期徵兆, and have urged the government to review whether current support structures can keep pace with a form of conflict that increasingly follows students home.

中文翻譯

根據教育局最新數字，香港學校在2022/23至2024/25三個學年間，平均每年處理496宗欺凌個案，較之前三年平均每年約420宗大增32%。儘管教育局一直對校園欺凌採取「零容忍」政策，數字上升仍再次引起社會對學生身心健康的關注。

一個本地青年關注組織指出，部分升幅可能反映學生、家長及教師更願意舉報事件，而非選擇沉默，而官員及教育界多年來一直致力推動這種轉變。該組織同時警告，不少年幼學生仍難以分辨善意玩笑與真正的欺凌行為，意味部分爭執會在教師介入前已經升級，而另一些情況則可能被誤標籤為欺凌。

輔導人員表示，實際數字可能較官方統計更高，因為不少青少年之間的同儕衝突如今部分在網上發生，難以完全透過校內舉報機制掌握。他們呼籲制訂更清晰、針對社交媒體情況的反欺凌指引，並為教師提供更多培訓，協助識別早期警號，同時促請政府檢討現行支援系統能否應對日漸延伸至學生放學後生活的衝突。
