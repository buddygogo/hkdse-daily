English

Hong Kong's cycling calendar reached a milestone on Sunday when the Sun Hung Kai Properties Hong Kong Cyclothon drew more than 6,000 riders for a mass-participation sporting eventa sports event designed for large numbers of ordinary participants, not just elite athletes大型群眾參與體育活動 that, for the first time, sent cyclists along a stretch of the Central Kowloon Bypass. All four rides began and ended at the West Kowloon Cultural District, with the signature 56-kilometre challenge setting off at 5.15am over what organisers called a "five-tunnel, three-bridge" course.

The bypass section, opened to motor traffic only in recent years, required extensive road closuresthe temporary shutting of roads to normal traffic for an event封路 and coordination between the organiser and the Transport Department. Riders who completed the stretch described it as the most demanding part of the route, citing its narrow carriageway, gentle inclinesslight upward slopes that still tire riders over distance緩坡 and confined airflow inside the tunnels, which trapped heat and left little room to overtake.

For the government and event sponsors, folding a strategic road project into a cycling festival carries value well beyond a single Sunday. It lets planners showcase infrastructureto present a public facility to the public in a favourable light展示基建設施 built at public expense and lean on the Cyclothon's established following to build cycling tourismtravel motivated specifically by the chance to cycle in a destination單車旅遊 around Hong Kong. Officials have in recent years cast such mass-participation races as a way to extend the legacy valuethe lasting benefit a piece of infrastructure or event continues to provide after its original purpose延續價值 of transport projects, pairing an everyday commuter route with a once-a-year spectacle that draws both local riders and overseas entrants eager to test a course unlike any other in the region.

中文翻譯

「新鴻基地產香港單車節」於星期日吸引超過六千名車手參與,是本港單車活動的一大里程碑,成為一項大型群眾參與體育活動,首次讓車手駛經中九龍幹線的一段路段。四項賽事均以西九文化區為起點及終點,其中最矚目的56公里挑戰賽於早上5時15分出發,途經主辦單位稱為「五隧三橋」的賽道。

中九龍幹線近年才開放予車輛使用,這次活動需要大範圍封路,並須與運輸署緊密協調。完成該路段的車手形容這是全程最艱辛的部分,指路面狹窄、緩坡不斷,加上隧道內空氣不流通,熱氣難以散去,亦難以超車。

對政府及活動贊助商而言,將一項策略性道路工程融入單車節,其價值遠超一個星期日的意義。這讓規劃者得以向公眾展示以公帑興建的基建,並借助單車節既有的號召力推動單車旅遊發展。近年官員一直將此類大型群眾參與賽事視為延伸交通基建延續價值的方法,將日常通勤路線與一年一度的盛事結合,既吸引本地車手,亦吸引渴望挑戰區內獨有賽道的海外參賽者。
