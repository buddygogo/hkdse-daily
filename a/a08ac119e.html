English

Hong Kong's government has unveiled a two-year plan to create 30,000 job placements for young people, including 10,000 subsidisedpartly paid for by the government to reduce the cost to employers獲資助 full-time entry-level posts, as officials scramble to respond to a cooling graduate job marketa job market for new graduates that is weakening or offering fewer opportunities降溫的畢業生就業市場 increasingly reshaped by automation. The scheme, due to launch in the first quarter of 2027, comes as the city's unemployment rate has ticked up for several consecutive months, with fresh graduates among those hit hardest.

Under the proposed terms, each employer taking part will receive a wage subsidy of up to HK$6,000 a month, roughly a third of a typical entry-level salary, for the first six months of employment. Crucially, officials insist the posts must be genuinely additional toextra or supplementary, on top of what already exists在現有基礎之上額外增加的 a company's existing headcount rather than simply replacing vacancies it would have filled anyway, with a target of five thousand such new posts created in each of 2027 and 2028.

Commentators remain divided on whether the scheme will work. One widely discussed opinion piece argues the government must judge success by whether participants go on to secure permanent employmentobtain a long-term, stable job獲得長期穩定的工作, not merely by how many placements are filled on paper, warning that without close monitoring, some employers could treat subsidised interns as disposable cheap labourlow-cost workers treated as easily replaceable可隨時被取代的廉價勞動力 rather than investing in their long-term development. Others counter that even a temporary, subsidised foothold is far better than the alternative facing many graduates: prolonged joblessnessa long period without paid work長期失業 at the very start of their careers.

中文翻譯

香港政府公布一項為期兩年的計劃，將為青年提供三萬個就業名額，當中包括一萬個獲資助的全職入門職位，此舉正值官員急於應對一個日益受自動化重塑、降溫的畢業生就業市場。該計劃預計於二零二七年第一季推出，當時本港失業率已連續數月回升，新畢業生更是受影響最嚴重的一群。

根據建議條款，每間參與企業將可獲發每月最多6000港元的薪酬補貼，大約相當於一般入門職位薪酬的三分之一，補貼期為僱用首六個月。官員強調，有關職位必須是真正在現有基礎之上額外增加的，而非單純取代企業原本就會填補的空缺，目標是在二零二七年及二零二八年各創造五千個此類新增職位。

評論員對計劃能否成功意見不一。一篇廣受討論的評論文章認為，政府必須以參與者日後能否獲得長期穩定的工作作為成功指標，而非單純計算紙面上填補了多少個名額，並警告若缺乏嚴格監察，部分僱主或會將受資助的實習生，視為可隨時被取代的廉價勞動力，而非真正投資於他們的長遠發展。另一些人則反駁，即使只是暫時性、有補貼的立足點，也遠勝於許多畢業生在事業起步階段便要面對的長期失業。
