English

Hong Kong used the Applied Artificial Intelligence Conference 2026 in Vienna this week to set out its ambitions in artificial intelligence, meeting Austrian officials and industry representatives to discuss deeper collaboration in research and innovation. Secretary for Innovation, Technology and Industry Sun Dong told delegates that the city has elevated AI into a core industrymade artificial intelligence a central, priority sector of the economy將人工智能提升為核心產業, pursuing a dual strategy of developing "industries for AI" alongside "AI for industries".

It is widely argued that such international outreach carries real strategic weight for a city seeking to remain competitive in a crowded global AI raceintense worldwide competition among many countries developing AI競爭激烈的全球人工智能競賽. Discussions in Vienna reportedly touched on industrial digitalisationthe process of applying digital technology to transform traditional industries產業數碼化, start-up collaboration and joint research partnerships, with Hong Kong positioning itself as a bridge between Mainland Chinese and European innovation ecosystems. Nevertheless, sceptics caution that high-level diplomatic engagementofficial cooperation conducted at a senior government level高層外交參與 does not automatically translate into concrete technology transferthe passing of technical knowledge or equipment from one party to another技術轉移 or investment on the ground.

By the same token, some local commentators question whether the benefits of such global partnerships will be felt by ordinary students and workers, rather than remaining confined to policy circles and corporate boardrooms. It is often observed that Hong Kong's own AI ambitions can only be realised if they are underpinned bysupported at a fundamental level by以……為基礎 sustained investment in local talent, computing infrastructure and school-level digital literacy. Whether this latest round of overseas engagement will eventually filter down to the classroom and the workplace remains, for now, an open questiona matter not yet resolved or answered懸而未決的問題.

中文翻譯

香港藉著本週在維也納舉行的2026年應用人工智能會議,向外闡述其人工智能發展願景,並與奧地利官員及業界代表會面,商討在研究與創新方面加強合作。創新科技及工業局局長孫東向與會者表示,香港已將人工智能提升為核心產業,同步推行「為人工智能發展產業」與「以人工智能賦能產業」的雙軌策略。

輿論普遍認為,對於一個力求在競爭激烈的全球人工智能競賽中保持競爭力的城市而言,這種國際交流具有重要的戰略意義。據報,維也納的討論涉及產業數碼化、初創企業合作及聯合研究項目,香港藉此將自己定位為內地與歐洲創新生態系統之間的橋樑。然而,懷疑論者提醒,高層外交參與並不會自動轉化為實質的技術轉移或在地投資。

同樣地,部分本地評論員質疑,這類全球夥伴合作的好處,最終會否惠及普通學生及打工仔,而非只局限於政策圈子及企業董事會。有人指出,香港的人工智能發展願景,只有基於持續投資本地人才、運算基建及學校數碼教育,才有可能實現。至於這次海外交流最終能否逐漸惠及課堂與職場,目前仍是一個懸而未決的問題。
