English

Hong Kong's environment chief has defended the city's target of cutting average fine suspended particulatestiny airborne pollutant particles, commonly measured as PM2.5, that are small enough to be inhaled deep into the lungs微細懸浮粒子 to below 14 micrograms per cubic metre by 2030, describing the goal as realistic but challenging. Speaking to the government's Advisory Council on the Environment, Secretary for Environment and Ecology Tse Chin-wan said the target, set out in Hong Kong's first-ever Five-Year Development Plan, would require sustained effort given that vehicle emissionsexhaust and pollutants released into the air by cars, buses and trucks車輛排放 remain the single largest source of local air pollution.

Tse acknowledged that air quality has already improved markedly as electric vehicle adoptionthe growing use of cars and buses powered by electricity instead of petrol or diesel電動車普及 accelerates across the city, but said further gains will depend on tackling sources beyond the road. The government plans to introduce legislation next year to tighten emission standardsmake the legal limits on pollutant output stricter收緊排放標準 for marine vessels, while also exploring hydrogen-powered machineryequipment that runs on hydrogen fuel instead of diesel, producing water rather than pollutants氫能驅動機械 for use at remote construction sites where connecting to the electricity grid is difficult. Officials are separately re-examining their Scheme of Control Agreementsthe regulatory contracts that set the permitted profits and obligations of Hong Kong's power companies《管制計劃協議》 with the city's two power companies, CLP Power and HK Electric, as part of a wider push toward carbon neutrality by 2050.

The Five-Year Plan also commits Hong Kong to cutting its carbon intensitythe amount of carbon emissions produced per unit of economic output碳排放強度 by 32.5 percent by 2030, measured against 2024 levels. Green groups have broadly welcomed the direction but questioned whether voluntary industry cooperationbusinesses choosing to follow environmental guidelines without being legally forced to業界合作 will be enough without firmer enforcement, leaving officials to walk a line between ambition and what remains achievable in a city still heavily reliant on fossil fuels for power generation and shipping.

中文翻譯

香港環保局局長為本港目標進行辯解——該目標是要在二零三零年前，將平均微細懸浮粒子（即PM2.5）濃度降至每立方米十四微克以下，並形容此目標「現實但具挑戰性」。環境及生態局局長謝展寰向政府環境諮詢委員會發言時表示，此目標訂於香港首份五年發展規劃之中，鑑於車輛排放仍是本地空氣污染的最大單一來源，達標將需要持續努力。

謝展寰承認，隨著電動車普及加快，空氣質素已明顯改善，但他表示，要取得進一步成效，仍須處理道路以外的污染源。政府計劃明年立法收緊排放標準，規管船舶排放，同時探討在難以接駁電網的偏遠建築工地，使用氫能驅動機械。當局亦正另行檢討與兩家電力公司——中電及港燈——簽訂的《管制計劃協議》，作為邁向二零五零年碳中和目標的一部分舉措。

《五年規劃》亦承諾香港在二零三零年前，將碳排放強度——即每單位經濟產出所產生的排放量——按二零二四年水平計算削減百分之三十二點五。環保團體普遍歡迎這個方向，但質疑單靠自願性的業界合作，在缺乏更嚴格執法的情況下，是否足以達標，令官員須在雄心與一個仍高度依賴化石燃料發電及航運的城市所能實現的範圍之間，取得平衡。
