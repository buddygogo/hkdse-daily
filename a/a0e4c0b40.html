English

Hong Kong Green Week 2026, held from 7 to 11 September under the theme “Future-proofing Sustainable Growth”, brought together financial regulators, investors and sustainability experts for a series of events including the HKMA-organised Green and Sustainable Finance conferences and the AIIB-HKMA Joint Conference on mobilising private capitalto encourage and organise privately owned funds to be invested towards a goal調動私人資本 for climate resilience. It is widely argued that the week-long programme, now an established fixture on the city's calendar, underscores Hong Kong's ambition to position itself as Asia's leading hub for green and sustainable financefinancial products and investment specifically directed at environmentally friendly projects綠色和可持續金融.

Proponents point out that Hong Kong has made tangible progressreal, measurable improvement rather than something abstract or symbolic實質進展 on environmental fronts in recent years, with the annual average concentration of major air pollutants having fallen sharply since 2004 and the number of registered electric vehicles rising roughly six-fold since 2021. Critics, however, contend that financial conferences and roadmaps alone cannot substitute forto serve as a replacement for something取代 concrete emissions-reduction policy, noting that the government has yet to publish a detailed timetable for achieving its 2050 carbon neutrality target.

Nevertheless, organisers maintain that channelling private investment intodirecting financial resources from private, non-government sources towards a specific purpose將私人投資引導至 climate adaptation projects is precisely what will allow the city to close the funding gap that public spending alone cannot cover. By the same token, environmental groups argue that Hong Kong must pair its financial ambitions with binding, measurable commitmentspromises that are legally or formally required to be fulfilled and can be objectively assessed具約束力、可量度的承諾 if the city's sustainability rhetoric is to be matched by real-world outcomes.

中文翻譯

2026年香港綠色周於9月7日至11日舉行，主題為「為可持續發展未來定位」，匯聚金融監管機構、投資者及可持續發展專家，參與一系列活動，包括由金管局主辦的綠色和可持續金融會議，以及亞投行與金管局聯合舉辦、探討如何調動私人資本以提升氣候韌性的會議。論者普遍認為，這項為期一週的活動已成為本港年度盛事，彰顯香港致力將自己定位為亞洲領先的綠色和可持續金融樞紐。

支持者指出，香港近年在環保方面已取得實質進展，主要空氣污染物的年均濃度自2004年以來大幅下降，而登記電動車數目自2021年以來亦增長約六倍。然而，批評者認為，單靠金融會議和路線圖並不能取代具體的減排政策，並指出政府至今仍未公布實現2050年碳中和目標的詳細時間表。

儘管如此，主辦方堅稱，將私人投資引導至氣候適應項目，正正是讓本港能夠彌補單靠公共開支無法填補的資金缺口的關鍵。同樣地，環保團體認為，香港必須將其金融方面的雄心與具約束力、可量度的承諾相配合，才能令可持續發展的言辭與現實成果相符。
