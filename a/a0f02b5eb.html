English

The Development Bureau has come under renewed scrutiny after confirming a last-minute U-turna reversal of an earlier decision or policy政策上的大轉變 on plans for the new Pak Shek Kok railway station, with public housing quietly dropped from the site in favour of private flats. Permanent Secretary for Development Doris Ho Pui-ling told reporters on Friday that the station, part of the East Rail Line extension works and earmarkedset aside for a particular purpose指定用途的；預留的 to open as early as 2033, would instead be financed by allowing the project's developer to build and sell private housing on the surrounding land.

It is widely argued that such reversals undermine public trust in long-term planning, yet officials insisted the decision was pragmatic rather than political. Ho explained that the area around the proposed station currently lacks the requisiterequired; necessary for a particular purpose必需的；所需的 community facilities — schools, clinics and transport links — to support a sizeable public housing estate, and that building purely private flats would better align withto be consistent or in agreement with與……一致；配合 existing development in the neighbourhood. Nevertheless, lawmakers have pressed the bureau to explain why the original mixed-use blueprint, which had promised a share of subsidised flats, was not honoured.

By the same token, the government was keen to allayto reduce fear, worry or doubt減輕；緩解（憂慮） public anxiety over the city's overall housing supply. Officials stressed that the revised proposal would not jeopardiseto put something at risk of being harmed or destroyed危害；使陷入危險 the administration's ten-year target of delivering 420,000 flats, including 294,000 public units, by 2036, since sufficient land has reportedly already been securedobtained or guaranteed, especially through effort獲得；確保 elsewhere to meet that quota. Critics, however, contend that ad hocdone or arranged only when necessary, not planned in advance臨時的；特別安排的 adjustments like this one risk eroding confidence in the Northern Metropolis strategy as a whole, and have called for greater transparencyopenness and honesty in communicating decisions透明度；公開程度 in how such housing trade-offs are decided.

中文翻譯

發展局確認就白石角新鐵路站計劃作出臨門大轉變後，再次受到社會審視——公屋悄悄被剔出該地段，改由私人樓宇取代。發展局常任秘書長何珮玲星期五向記者表示，該車站屬東鐵綫延線工程的一部分，預留最早於二零三三年通車，將改以允許項目發展商在周邊土地興建及出售私人住宅的方式融資。

普遍認為，此類政策逆轉會削弱公眾對長遠規劃的信心，但官員堅稱這項決定純屬務實考量，並非政治決定。何珮玲解釋，建議車站周邊地區目前缺乏必需的社區設施——學校、診所及交通連接——未能支援規模可觀的公共房屋項目，而純粹興建私人樓宇則更能與該區現有發展配合。然而，議員已追問發展局，為何未有兌現原來承諾提供部分資助房屋的混合用途規劃藍圖。

同樣道理，政府亦急於緩解公眾對全港房屋供應的憂慮。官員強調，經修訂的建議不會危害政府在二零三六年前提供四十二萬個單位（包括二十九萬四千個公共單位）的十年目標，因為據報已在其他地點確保足夠土地以達標。然而，評論者認為，像這次的臨時調整有可能削弱公眾對北部都會區整體策略的信心，並要求當局在決定此類房屋取捨時提高透明度。
