English

Hong Kong's border infrastructure took a historic step forward at 6.30am on Monday, when the revamped Huanggang Port opened its Joint Inspection Building, replacing the ageing facility that had served cross-boundary travellers for nearly three decades. Shenzhen officials expect an initial passenger throughputthe number of people a facility can process within a given time客流量 of 50,000 trips a day, climbing towards 100,000 to 200,000 as travellers adjust, before eventually exceeding 300,000 once a planned rail spur line is completed.

The port's signature feature is a joint clearance arrangementa system in which officials from two jurisdictions check travellers together at a single checkpoint instead of two separate ones聯合查驗安排 letting Hong Kong and mainland officers check documents side by side in one building, rather than at separate checkpoints. Authorities say this should cut clearance times from roughly 30 minutes to about five, a change resting on smooth coordination between two systems that have rarely shared a roof. More than 160,000 residents pre-registered through the Immigration Department's e-Channel app, and shuttle buses linking the old port to Lok Ma Chau for nearly thirty years made their final runs on Sunday night.

Security officials, wary a surge of curious first-time users could overwhelm the facility, ran large-scale stress teststrial runs that push a system to near its limits to check it can cope before real use壓力測試 involving tens of thousands of participants beforehand and activated a command centre to monitor opening-day congestionthe state of being overcrowded, especially with people or traffic擁塞. Officials have urged agencies marketing early tours to hold back, framing the port as a step in regional integrationthe process of bringing separate places closer together, especially economically and administratively區域融合 rather than a one-off attraction, as planners phase outto gradually withdraw something from use over a period of time逐步淘汰 older crossings for a single, higher-capacity gateway.

中文翻譯

香港邊境基建踏入歷史性一步,全新皇崗口岸的聯合驗放大樓於星期一早上6時30分正式啟用,取代服務跨境旅客近三十年的舊有口岸設施。深圳當局預計首日客流量約為五萬人次,並預計隨著旅客逐漸適應新口岸,數字將攀升至十萬至二十萬人次之間,待日後鐵路支線通車後,長遠更有望突破三十萬人次。

新口岸的標誌性設計是聯合查驗安排,讓兩地關員在同一座建築內並排查核旅客證件,而非像以往般分設於邊界兩側。當局表示,新安排可將一般通關時間由約三十分鐘縮短至約五分鐘,但成效取決於兩套甚少共用同一屋頂的出入境系統能否順暢協調。啟用前數日,逾十六萬名市民已透過入境處的e-道手機應用程式預先登記使用新服務;連接舊皇崗口岸與落馬洲近三十年的穿梭巴士,亦已在星期日晚上完成最後一班車,正式退役。

保安當局憂慮大批市民因好奇而首日湧往新設施,或令系統不勝負荷,因此在啟用前數星期進行大型壓力測試,動用數以萬計人員參與,並啟動應急指揮中心監察啟用首日的擁塞情況。官員亦呼籲早前推銷「搶閒」口岸團的旅行社暫緩行程,強調新口岸是推動香港與內地區域融合的長遠一步,而非一時的新鮮景點,並會隨規劃者逐步淘汰舊有口岸,改以單一、更高產能的通道取而代之。
