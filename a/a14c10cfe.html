English

A dispute over a multimillion-dollar lottery prize has landed a Hong Kong man in police custody, after he allegedly refused to share a Mark Six jackpot worth approximately HK$30 million with fourteen of his colleagues. According to police, the group, made up of staff members from the Food and Environmental Hygiene Department, had reportedly pooled their moneycombined funds from several people for a shared purpose集合各人的金錢作共同用途 to purchase the winning ticket together, with the 36-year-old man responsible for buying the ticket and holding the bank account into which the prize was ultimately paid.

Nevertheless, once the ticket proved to be a winner, the man allegedly refused to distribute the winnings evenly among the group, despite a meeting having been convened in an attempt to resolve the disputea disagreement or conflict between parties爭議或衝突 amicably. A 53-year-old colleague subsequently reported the matter to police, prompting an investigation on suspicion of theft. Officers have since frozento officially prevent money in an account from being used or withdrawn正式凍結戶口內的資金,禁止動用 the HK$30 million held in the man's bank account while the case is investigated further.

It is widely argued that this case serves as a cautionary tale about the importance of establishing clear, written agreements before entering into any form of shared financial venture, however informal it may seem. By the same token, the incident highlights a recurring legal grey area in Hong Kong: verbal arrangements between friends or colleagues, though seemingly built on mutual trust, often carry little weight without documented proof, leaving aggrieved parties with limited recourse besides pursuing costly and time-consuming legal action. Critics contend that greater public awareness of such risks could help prevent similar disputes from arising in the future.

中文翻譯

一名香港男子因一宗涉及過千萬元彩票獎金的糾紛被警方拘捕,他涉嫌拒絕與十四名同事分享一注約值三千萬港元的六合彩頭獎。據警方透露,這班同事來自食物環境衞生署,據報曾一同集資購買該注中獎彩票,由一名三十六歲男子負責買票,並以其銀行帳戶接收獎金。

然而,當彩票中獎後,該名男子卻涉嫌拒絕將獎金平均分配給各人,儘管各方曾召開會議,試圖友好解決這場爭議。其後一名五十三歲同事向警方報案,促使警方以涉嫌盜竊展開調查。警方已凍結該男子銀行帳戶內的三千萬港元,案件仍在進一步調查。

有人認為,這宗案件是一個警世教訓,說明在進行任何形式的集資活動前,即使看似非正式,訂立清晰的書面協議仍屬必要。與此同時,事件亦揭示香港一個常見的法律灰色地帶:朋友或同事之間的口頭承諾,儘管建基於互信,但在缺乏書面證明的情況下往往難以生效,令受屈一方在追討時只能依靠昂貴且耗時的法律程序。批評者認為,提高公眾對這類風險的警覺,有助防止同類糾紛再次發生。
