English

Researchers at the Hong Kong Jockey Club Centre for Suicide Research and Prevention at the University of Hong Kong have revealed that the suicide rate among children under 15 tripled last year, even as the city's overall suicide rate edged downto decrease slightly微跌 from 13.8 to 13 per 100,000 residents. The centre found that most of the young students who died by suicide had been struggling with academic pressurestress caused by schoolwork, exams or the pursuit of good grades學業壓力 or difficulties with peers, and that the great majority of suicide notes and expressions of suicidal ideation were left in digital form.

It is widely argued that this trend lays bareto reveal something clearly, especially something troubling揭露 how early emotional distress can take root among Hong Kong's youngest students, well before the pressures typically associated with public examinations. Researchers have urged schools to strengthento make something more robust or effective加強 mental health awareness from an earlier age, and have called on media outlets to report on such cases more responsiblyin a careful and considerate manner that avoids causing harm負責任地, in line with established guidelines that discourage sensationalised coverage of suicide.

Critics contend that schools remain ill-equippedlacking the necessary skills, resources or training裝備不足 to identify early warning signs, particularly when they are expressed only through private messages or social media rather than face-to-face. Nevertheless, education officials maintain that counselling resources have expanded significantly in recent years. By the same token, mental health advocates argue that resourcing alone is not enough unless teachers and parents are also trained to recogniseto identify or notice something, especially something significant識別 digital signs of distress. The findings have reignited debate over how families, schools and social media platforms should share responsibility for safeguarding young people's mental wellbeing.

中文翻譯

香港大學香港賽馬會防止自殺研究中心的研究人員披露,去年15歲以下兒童的自殺率較前一年上升三倍,而全港整體自殺率則由每十萬人13.8人微跌至13人。研究中心發現,大部分死於自殺的年輕學生,生前均受學業壓力或與同儕關係困擾所苦,而絕大部分遺書及自殺意念的表達,均以數碼形式留下。

普遍認為,這一趨勢揭露了情緒困擾可以早於公開考試所帶來的壓力之前,已在香港最年幼的學生當中紮根。研究人員促請學校從更年幼的階段開始加強精神健康意識,亦呼籲傳媒機構按照不鼓勵渲染自殺報導的既定指引,更負責任地報導同類事件。

批評者認為,學校仍然裝備不足,難以識別早期警號,尤其是當這些訊號只透過私訊或社交媒體表達,而非面對面交流時。然而,教育官員堅稱,近年輔導資源已大幅增加。同樣地,精神健康倡議者認為,除非教師及家長同樣接受培訓,學會識別數碼形式的求助訊號,否則單靠資源投放並不足夠。這項研究結果,重新引發社會對家庭、學校及社交媒體平台應如何共同承擔保障青少年精神健康責任的爭論。
