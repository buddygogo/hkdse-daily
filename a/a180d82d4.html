English

Government officials, business leaders and youth representatives from Hong Kong, the mainland, ASEAN countries and beyond gathered at the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre for the flagship Hong Kong Youth Development Summit 2026, an event underwrittenfinancially supported or guaranteed資助；擔保 by the Hong Kong Jockey Club Charities Trust. Organised by the Home and Youth Affairs Bureau, the summit was pitched as a platform for exchangea forum where different groups can share ideas交流平台 rather than a one-off ceremony, bringing young delegates into direct contact with policymakers and employers.

In a video address, Chief Executive John Lee said that under the city’s first Five-Year Plan, covering 2026 to 2030, the government would step upincrease the level or intensity of an effort加強；提升 opportunities for young people’s development, framing youth policy as part of a longer-term strategic blueprint rather than a series of standaloneexisting or done separately from other things獨立；單一 initiatives. The Jockey Club Charities Trust has committed HK$10 million to this year’s related youthfest@HK programme, continuing as the festival’s principal funderthe main organisation providing financial backing主要資助機構 since 2023.

Supporters say bringing overseas and mainland youth groups into the same room as local students helps widen horizons and build the kind of regional networks that can later translate into career pathwaysroutes or sequences of jobs leading toward a career goal職業發展路徑. Sceptics, however, question whether a single annual summit can meaningfully address the deeper, day-to-day pressures — from housing costs to job insecurity — that weigh oncause worry or burden to someone困擾；造成壓力 many young Hongkongers, warning that glossy flagship events risk becoming substitutes for sustained, harder-to-measure policy follow-through.

中文翻譯

來自香港、內地、東盟國家及其他地區的政府官員、商界領袖及青年代表,齊集香港會議展覽中心,出席由香港賽馬會慈善信託基金資助的旗艦活動——香港青年發展峰會2026。這次由民政及青年事務局主辦的峰會,被定位為一個交流平台,而非一次性的慶典儀式,讓青年代表直接與政策制定者及僱主接觸。

行政長官李家超在視像致辭中表示,在涵蓋2026至2030年的香港首個五年規劃下,政府將加強青年發展機會,將青年政策定位為長遠策略藍圖的一部分,而非一系列單一的計劃。賽馬會慈善信託基金已向今年相關的「青年節@香港」計劃捐出1000萬港元,自2023年起一直擔任該活動的主要資助機構。

支持者認為,讓海外及內地青年團體與本地學生共處一室,有助拓闊視野,建立日後可轉化為職業發展路徑的地區網絡。然而,質疑者則提出,單靠一年一度的峰會,能否真正解決那些困擾許多香港青年的深層日常壓力——例如房屋開支及就業不穩定,並警告亮麗的旗艦活動,可能淪為難以量度卻持續不斷的政策跟進工作的替代品。
