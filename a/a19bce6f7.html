English

French luxury house Hermès has turned the Freespace black-box theatrea simple, flexible performance space with plain walls, used for experimental staging黑盒劇場（簡約多用途表演場地） at the West Kowloon Cultural District into a free venue for heritage storytellingusing history and tradition as the basis of a narrative to inform or persuade an audience以傳統歷史為題材的敘事, staging a short play about its own 140-year history alongside a standalone exhibition of archival objects. Running from 24 October to 8 November, the production, “Hermèstories”, was written by Pauline Bayle and directed by Joe Hufton under the house's corporate foundation, and forms part of a wider pattern of corporate patronagesupport, especially financial, given by a company to the arts or another cause企業贊助文化活動 of public cultural venues in Hong Kong.

Suitable for audiences aged seven and above, the roughly 50-minute performance traces the maison's origins as a saddlery and its craftsmanship heritagea legacy of skilled, traditional methods of making things by hand工藝傳承 in leatherwork, while the accompanying exhibition can be visited independently during the day. Admission is free, though organisers recommend advance bookingthe act of reserving a place or ticket before the day of the event預先訂座 given limited seating, with walk-in visitors admitted only when places remain. The project is not staged on 26 October or 2 November, when Freespace reverts to its usual public programming.

The collaboration illustrates the growing use of cultural placemakingthe use of arts and cultural activity to shape the character and identity of a public space以文化活動塑造地方特色 by international brands seeking to deepen their presence in the flagship cultural districtthe principal, most prominent cultural zone representing a city's cultural ambitions旗艦文化區, raising a familiar question for commentators: whether such sponsored programming genuinely enriches the public's access to the arts or risks blurring the distinction between commerce and culturemaking the difference between business promotion and genuine cultural activity less clear模糊商業與文化的分界. For many Hong Kong families, however, free admission lowers a financial barrier that commercial galleries and ticketed exhibitions elsewhere in the district do not.

中文翻譯

法國奢侈品牌愛馬仕將西九文化區的自由空間黑盒劇場變成免費場地，透過以傳統歷史為題材的敘事方式，上演一個關於品牌一百四十年歷史的短劇，並同場設有獨立的典藏品展覽。製作名為《愛馬仕故事》（Hermèstories），由十月二十四日至十一月八日舉行，劇本由寶琳·貝爾（Pauline Bayle）撰寫，祖·赫夫頓（Joe Hufton）執導，由品牌企業基金會主辦，屬企業贊助文化活動的最新例子，反映此類合作在香港日益普遍。

這套約五十分鐘的演出適合七歲或以上觀眾，追溯品牌由馬具工場起家、以及皮革製作的工藝傳承；附設展覽則可於日間獨立參觀，無須觀看演出。活動免費入場，但主辦方建議觀眾預先訂座，因座位有限，現場候補名額視乎剩餘座位而定。十月二十六日及十一月二日休演，期間自由空間將回復一般公眾節目。

這次合作反映國際品牌日益透過以文化活動塑造地方特色手法，加深其在這個旗艦文化區的存在感，亦引來論者慣常提出的疑問：這類由品牌贊助的節目，究竟是真正拓寬大眾接觸藝術的機會，還是有模糊商業與文化的分界之虞。然而對不少香港家庭而言，免費入場畢竟消除了區內其他商業畫廊及收費展覽所設下的經濟門檻。
