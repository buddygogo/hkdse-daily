English

Hong Kong recorded just 29,700 births in the year to mid-2026, a 15.6 per cent year-on-year plungea sudden and severe fall or decrease急挫 that marks the lowest tally since records began in 1962. In the first eight months of this year alone, only 19,255 babies were born, a 10 per cent drop on the same period last year, intensifying pressure on a government that has staked considerable political capital on reversing the trend.

Officials have responded by extending a baby bonus scheme for three more years, continuing to award HK$20,000 for a first child while raising the payout for a second or subsequent child to HK$30,000. Yet with more than 40 per cent of the original scheme's funds left unclaimednot taken up or collected, despite being available未被領取, commentators question whether cash alone can shift deep-seated attitudeschange beliefs or habits that are firmly established and hard to alter改變根深蒂固的觀念 towards parenthood. A survey by the Federation of Youth Groups found only 36.5 per cent of respondents aged 13 to 29 were even interested in having children, with 30.6 per cent ruling it out altogethercompletely, with no exception完全.

An editorial response argues the government is right to treat handouts as only one strand of a wider strategy, since the real deterrentsthings that discourage someone from doing something阻嚇因素 to having children in Hong Kong lie elsewhere: cramped housing, long working hours and a shortage of affordable childcare. Unless these structural barriersdeep, systemic obstacles built into how a society or economy works, not just surface problems結構性障礙 are addressed in tandem with financial incentives, the city risks pouring billions into a scheme that treats the symptom while leaving the underlying cause untouchednot dealing with the root problem that actually produces an effect未觸及根本原因.

中文翻譯

香港在截至2026年中的一年間僅有29,700名新生嬰兒誕生，按年急挫15.6%，創自1962年有紀錄以來最低。僅今年首八個月就有19,255名嬰兒誕生，較上年同期下跌10%，令一直把逆轉人口趨勢當為重要治理議程的政府承受更大壓力。

官方改為將新生嬰兒獎勵金計劃延長三年，繼續向首生子女發放2萬元，第二胎或以後子女則提高至3萬元。但原有計劃中超過四成款額未被領取，評論者質疑僅靠金錢是否能真正改變根深蒂固的觀念。香港青年協會一項調查發現，13至29歲受訪者中，僅36.5%表示有興趣生育，而30.6%則完全排除這個可能性。

一篇評論文章指出，政府正確地將獎金視為整體策略的其中一環，因為真正令香港人生育的阻嚇因素其實在其他地方：狹居、工時長和可負擔託兒服務不足。若不將這些結構性障礙與金錢激勵同步解決，本港即使投入巨資推行計劃，亦只會治標而未觸及根本原因。
