English

Hong Kong's Executive Councilthe body of senior officials that advises Hong Kong's Chief Executive and approves major government decisions, including regulated fare changes行政會議 approved a new round of fare increases for the Star Ferry on Wednesday, meaning passengers on the operator's two franchised routesferry or bus routes operated under an exclusive government licence, which also regulates the fares charged專營航線 will pay up to HK$1 more per journey on weekdays and up to HK$1.80 more on weekends and public holidays from 25 January next year. Under the approved schedule, weekday single-trip fares will rise by 60 cents to HK$1, capping the adult upper-deck fare at HK$6, while weekend and holiday fares will increase by HK$1 to HK$1.80, bringing the top fare to HK$8.30. The price of a monthly ticket will remain unchanged at HK$190.

The approved rise is noticeably smaller than what the ferry operator had originally sought in its fare increase applicationa formal request submitted by a transport operator asking the government to approve higher fares加價申請, which proposed increases of between 80 cents and HK$2 per trip, equivalent to an average rise of about 30 percent on both the Central–Tsim Sha Tsui and Wan Chai–Tsim Sha Tsui crossings. Officials said the government had moderated the increasereduced a proposed change to a smaller, less severe version before approving it調低加幅 after weighing the ferry's financial difficulties against concerns, raised repeatedly by lawmakers, that ordinary commuters should not bear the full cost of keeping a century-old service afloat. The revised fare schedule must still pass through negative vettinga legislative procedure in which a regulation automatically takes effect unless lawmakers vote to block it within a set period先訂立後審議程序 at the Legislative Council next Wednesday before it can take effect.

The Transport and Logistics Bureau said the adjustment was necessary because the Star Ferry recorded operating lossesthe amount by which a company's running costs exceed its income from normal business activities經營虧損 exceeding HK$100 million between 2018 and 2023, and has since relied on debt financingraising money by borrowing, such as through loans, rather than through revenue or investment債務融資 simply to maintain its franchised service. The last fare increase, approved in January 2023, raised prices by more than 50 percent, yet the operator argues that rising costs and falling patronage since the pandemic have continued to erode its finances. Commuter groups have urged the government to explore alternative subsidies rather than repeatedly passing costs onto fare-paying passengers.

中文翻譯

香港行政會議周三批准天星小輪新一輪加價,意味渡輪兩條專營航線的乘客,由明年1月25日起,平日每程最多加價1元,假日及周末每程最多加價1.8元。根據獲批的新收費表,平日單程票價將上調6角至1元,成人上層票價上限為6元;周末及公眾假期票價則上調1元至1.8元,最高票價為8.3元。月票價格維持不變,仍為190元。

獲批的加幅,明顯低於渡輪公司原先在加價申請中提出的幅度——該申請要求兩條航線(中環至尖沙咀,以及灣仔至尖沙咀)每程加價8角至2元,平均加幅約30%。官員表示,政府在衡量渡輪公司的財政困難,與議員多次提出、不應由普通乘客全數承擔維持這項逾百年歷史服務所需開支的訴求後,調低加幅。修訂後的收費表仍須於下周三提交立法會,經先訂立後審議程序,方可正式生效。

運輸及物流局表示,有必要調整收費,因天星小輪在2018年至2023年間錄得超過1億元的經營虧損,其後僅靠債務融資維持其專營服務運作。上一次加價於2023年1月批准,加幅超過五成,但渡輪公司指,疫情後成本上升及客流下跌,持續侵蝕其財政狀況。多個關注團體促請政府研究其他資助方案,而非一再將成本轉嫁予付費乘客。
