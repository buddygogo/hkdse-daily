Hong Kong is positioning itselfto establish oneself in a favourable role or status將自己定位為 as a gateway connecting European technology talent with opportunities across the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area, following a hackathon co-organised by Hong Kong Talent Engage and the EuroTech Federation. According to organisers, more than 90 per centnine-tenths, used here to indicate an overwhelming majority百分之九十 of the European finalists reported a stronger interest in relocating to Hong Kong or the wider GBA region after taking part in the programme, which gave winning teams a first-hand look at the city's innovation ecosystem.

It is widely argued that such initiatives play a pivotal role ina role that is central and highly influential to an outcome關鍵作用 addressing Hong Kong's long-standing shortage of specialised technology talent, particularly in fields such as artificial intelligence, robotics and advanced manufacturing. Critics, however, caution that one-off exposure programmesshort, isolated initiatives rather than sustained, long-term ones一次性的體驗計劃 are unlikely to translate into lasting relocation decisions unless they are backed by competitive salaries, housing support and a smoother visa process.

Nevertheless, the initiative comes at a time when Hong Kong has been doubling down onto increase commitment or effort towards something加倍投入 its ambitions to become a regional artificial intelligence hub, with a growing number of Mainland AI firms choosing to list on the Hong Kong stock exchange this year. By the same token, officials argue that talent attraction and capital markets development must go hand in hand if the city is to maintain its competitive edgeto preserve an advantage over rivals保持競爭優勢 as a global centre for innovation and technology in the years ahead.

中文翻譯

香港正透過由香港人才服務辦公室與歐洲科技聯盟（EuroTech Federation）合辦的一場黑客松活動，將自己定位為連繫歐洲科技人才與粵港澳大灣區機遇的橋樑。據主辦方表示，超過百分之九十的歐洲決賽隊伍參加該計劃後，表示對移居香港或整個大灣區的興趣有所增加，該計劃讓獲勝隊伍能親身了解本港的創新生態系統。

有人廣泛認為，這類計劃在應對香港長期短缺專業科技人才的問題上，擔當關鍵作用，尤其是在人工智能、機械人技術及先進製造業等領域。支持者指出，該計劃不但讓海外人才接觸本地機遇，亦有助培養跨境合作，連繫歐洲初創企業與大灣區內的內地企業。然而，批評者則提醒，除非配合具競爭力的薪酬、住屋支援及更順暢的簽證程序，否則一次性的體驗計劃未必能轉化為長遠的移居決定。

然而，這項計劃正值香港加倍投入力求成為區域人工智能樞紐之際，今年已有愈來愈多內地人工智能企業選擇在香港交易所上市。同樣地，官員認為，若香港要在未來數年保持競爭優勢，成為全球創新科技中心，吸引人才與發展資本市場必須雙軌並行。
