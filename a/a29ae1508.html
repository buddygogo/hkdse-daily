English

The government has confirmed that only two residential sites will be released by public tendera process in which developers submit competing bids to buy or develop government land公開招標 in the final quarter of the year, maintaining a cautious land-supply approacha deliberately careful policy of releasing only limited amounts of land for development審慎的土地供應策略 even as the property market shows signs of recovery. Both parcels lie in Sha Tin: one is earmarked for conventional housing, while the other has been set aside specifically for a student hostel projecta development built to provide affordable, purpose-built accommodation for university students學生宿舍項目, reflecting long-standing complaints that tertiary students struggle to find nearby housing.

Officials say the modest offering is consistent with a calibrated supply strategyan approach that carefully adjusts the amount of land released according to market conditions經調節的供應策略 set out in the latest Long Term Housing Strategy report, which projects that roughly 419,300 flats will be needed over the coming decade against a supply target of 420,000. Property analysts note that a sudden release of extra land could risk an oversupply glutan excess of available units far beyond what buyers currently need供應過剩, depressing prices just as developer sentimentthe overall confidence or pessimism that property companies feel about the market發展商信心 begins to recover.

Critics counter that such caution ignores the scale of Hong Kong's housing backlogan accumulated shortage of homes that has built up over previous years房屋短缺累積, arguing that families on the public housing waiting list cannot afford to wait for the market to find its footing. Others welcome the restraint, warning that releasing land too freely could trigger a repeat of past boom-and-bust cyclesrepeated patterns of rapid price growth followed by sharp collapse大起大落週期 that left thousands of flats unsold for years.

中文翻譯

政府證實，今年第四季只會以公開招標方式推出兩幅住宅用地，即使樓市出現復甦迹象，仍維持審慎的土地供應策略。兩幅地皮均位於沙田：一幅用於一般住宅發展，另一幅則專門預留作學生宿舍項目，回應大專學生長期難以在校園附近覓得居所的訴求。

官員表示，今次供應量雖少，但與最新《長遠房屋策略》報告所訂立的經調節的供應策略一致，報告預測未來十年香港需要約四十一萬九千三百個單位，而供應目標則為四十二萬個。地產分析員指出，若突然大增土地供應，可能令市場出現供應過剩，正值發展商信心開始回升之際，反而拖低樓價。

批評者則反駁，這種審慎態度忽視了本港累積已久的房屋短缺累積，指出正在等候公屋的家庭，根本無法等待市場自行復甦。另一批人則歡迎這種克制，警告若過於放鬆供應，可能重演過去大起大落週期所造成的後果，令數以千計的單位滯銷多年。
