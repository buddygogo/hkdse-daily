English

Cantopop veteran Alan Tam returned to the Hong Kong Coliseum on 11 September for the first time in eleven years, launching his "Unforgettable" World Tour – Grand Finale before a packed arena. The opening run, billed as the "Chapter of Gratitude," continues until 15 September, with a second block, the "Chapter of Unchanged Love," scheduled to follow from 18 to 22 September. Across the ten-show residencya series of performances by the same artist at one venue over a period of time駐場演出, the singer has revisited beloved hits from Cantopop's golden era of the 1980s, weaving personal anecdotes and expressions of gratitude to long-time fans between musical numbers.

It is widely argued that the enduring appeal of veteran performers such as Tam speaks to a deep well of nostalgia among Hong Kong audiences, many of whom grew up with his music during a formative period for the local entertainment industry. Cultural commentators contend that such concerts do more than simply entertain; they serve as a form of collective memoryshared memories held by a community about its cultural past集體回憶, reconnecting audiences with a shared cultural heritagetraditions, values and achievements passed down within a society文化遺產 at a time when K-pop and Mandopop dominate much of the regional music scene. By the same token, Tam's ability to sell out an eleven-year-return residency underscores the extent to which Cantopop, once thought to be in decline, continues to command genuine commercial and emotional loyaltycontinued support driven by both profit and feeling商業與情感上的忠誠度.

Nevertheless, some critics contend that the local music industry's continued reliance on veteran acts risks overshadowing emerging homegrown talentnew artists developing within the local scene冒起的本地新秀, who often struggle to secure comparable stage time and media attention. Sceptics further point out that concerts of this nature tend to draw an ageing fanbasea group of supporters that is growing older over time日漸老化的粉絲群, raising questions about whether Cantopop can cultivate a new generation of listeners rather than simply relying on nostalgia. Supporters counter that veteran performers like Tam, by continuing to headline major venues, help sustain the broader live music ecosystemthe interconnected network supporting live performances現場音樂生態系統 that newer artists ultimately benefit from. Whether nostalgia alone can secure Cantopop's long-term future remains an open question, but Tam's triumphant returna highly successful comeback after an absence凱旋歸來 to the Coliseum suggests its emotional pull is far from fading.

中文翻譯

粵語流行曲殿堂級歌手譚詠麟於9月11日重返香港體育館,相隔十一年再度登上這個舞台,展開「譽滿一生」世界巡迴演唱會的終章,全場爆滿。首輪演出名為「感激篇」,由即日起舉行至9月15日,第二輪「不變的愛篇」則定於9月18日至22日接續舉行。在這一連十場的駐場演出中,歌手重溫多首來自八十年代粵語流行曲黃金時代的經典金曲,並在歌曲之間穿插個人往事及向長期支持他的歌迷表達謝意。

有人認為,像譚詠麟這類資深歌手歷久不衰的魅力,反映香港觀眾心底裡深厚的懷舊情懷,當中不少人正是在本地娛樂業的黃金時期伴隨他的音樂成長。文化評論員指出,這類演唱會的意義不止於娛樂,它們更是一種集體回憶的載體,讓觀眾在K-pop及國語流行曲主導區內樂壇的今天,重新連繫一份共有的文化傳承。與此同時,譚詠麟相隔十一年復出仍能一票難求,正好說明一度被視為式微的粵語流行曲,依然能夠凝聚真正的商業與情感忠誠度。

然而,部分批評者認為,本地樂壇持續依賴資深歌手,可能令冒起的本地新人相形見絀,這些新人往往難以獲得相若的演出機會及媒體關注。質疑者亦指出,這類演唱會往往吸引日漸老化的歌迷群,令人不禁質疑粵語流行曲能否培養新一代聽眾,而非單靠懷舊情懷維持。支持者則反駁,指譚詠麟等資深歌手持續擔正大型場館,有助維持新晉歌手最終亦得以受惠的整個現場音樂生態。懷舊情懷能否單獨撐起粵語流行曲的長遠未來,仍是一個未有定論的問題,但譚詠麟凱旋重返香港體育館的一刻,足證其情感感召力絲毫未減。
