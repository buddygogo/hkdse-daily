English

It has often been observed that Hong Kong possesses no shortage of environmental targets, yet critics contend that the city still lacks a unifying climate narrativea clear, shared story or vision that connects separate policies into one coherent goal缺乏統一的氣候論述 capable of rallying the public behind its green ambitions. Under the newly unveiled first five-year plan, the government has set out twenty-two major indicators, five of which are binding targetsgoals that must legally or officially be met具約束力的指標 concentrated largely on environmental protection, including reductions in carbon emissions per unit of GDP and an increased share of zero-carbon electricity generation.

Nevertheless, observers argue that isolated statistics, however ambitious, do little to galvaniseto shock or excite someone into action激勵 public engagement unless they are woven into a coherent story that ordinary citizens can understand and support. The administration has reaffirmed its long-standing pledge to cut total carbon emissions by 50 per cent before 2035, relative to 2005 levels, en route to achieving carbon neutralitya state in which the amount of carbon released is balanced by an equivalent amount removed碳中和 before 2050, chiefly through energy conservation, the promotion of green transport, and reduction of waste. By the same token, such commitments will only translate into tangible progress if implementation is accompanied by sustained public communication.

It is nonetheless argued that Hong Kong’s climate policy need not start from scratch; rather, what appears to be missing is a compelling framework capable of connecting disparate initiativesseparate and different efforts that do not obviously belong together各自為政的措施 , from waste levies to green financing, into a single, comprehensible vision. Should such a narrative fail to materialise, critics warn, ambitious targets risk remaining abstract figures on a policy document rather than a shared civic mission.

中文翻譯

人們常常留意到，香港的環保目標從不缺乏，但評論者認為，本港仍然缺乏一套能夠凝聚公眾支持其綠色雄心的統一氣候論述。在剛公布的首份五年規劃下，政府訂立了22項主要指標，其中五項為具約束力的指標，主要集中於環境保護，包括減少每單位本地生產總值的碳排放及提高零碳電力發電的比例。

然而，觀察人士認為，無論數字多麼進取，若未能編織成一個普通市民能夠理解及支持的連貫故事，單靠個別統計數字難以激勵公眾參與。政府重申其長期承諾，將於2035年前把碳排放總量相對2005年水平削減50%，並於2050年前邁向碳中和，主要透過節約能源、推廣綠色運輸及減少廢物來實現。同樣道理，這些承諾唯有在落實過程中配合持續的公眾溝通，才能轉化為實質進展。

然而亦有人認為，香港的氣候政策不必從零開始；相反，似乎缺少的是一個能夠將各自為政的措施——從垃圾徵費到綠色融資——連繫成單一而易於理解的願景的具說服力框架。評論者警告，倘若這種論述未能成形，再進取的目標也只會淪為政策文件上的抽象數字，而非一項共同的公民使命。
