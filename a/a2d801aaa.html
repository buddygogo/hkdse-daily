English

Hong Kong's Environmental Protection Department recorded a “serious” rating — the top tier of its Air Quality Health Indexthe government's official scale for communicating how harmful the air is to breathe at a given time and place空氣質素健康指數 — at several monitoring stations from Tuesday afternoon, and unhealthy pollution persisted into Wednesday across large parts of the city. Causeway Bay's Air Quality Index reached 154 on Wednesday morning, a level classified as unhealthy for the general public, while most other districts registered levels rated unhealthy for sensitive groupspeople more likely to suffer harm from a given condition, such as children, the elderly or those with existing heart or lung disease敏感人群 rather than the population as a whole.

Forecasters attributed the episode to a now-familiar combination: weak windsair movement of unusually low speed, which allows pollutants to accumulate instead of dispersing偏弱的風力 settling over the city alongside unusually strong sunshine, conditions that together favour the formation of photochemical smoga type of air pollution created when sunlight reacts chemically with existing pollutants to generate ozone and fine particles光化煙霧. Intense sunlight accelerates the reactions that turn vehicle exhaust and industrial emissions into ground-level ozoneozone gas formed near the earth's surface, a key component of smog and harmful to breathe, unlike the protective ozone layer high in the atmosphere地面臭氧 and fine particulate matterextremely small airborne particles, often written as PM2.5, that are small enough to penetrate deep into the lungs微細懸浮粒子, both of which build up fastest across the wider Pearl River Delta when air stagnates in this way.

The Hong Kong Observatory has forecast some relief, saying a fresh easterly airstreama current of air moving in from the east, which can help disperse accumulated pollution偏東氣流 reaching the Guangdong coast later in the week should gradually help clear the haze. Until then, authorities have repeated their now-routine advice: residents with existing respiratory or heart conditions should limit time outdoors, while otherwise healthy Hongkongers are being reminded that repeated episodes like this one are becoming an unwelcome fixture of the city's autumn weather pattern, rather than a rare exception.

中文翻譯

香港環境保護署自週二下午起,在多個監測站錄得空氣質素健康指數「嚴重」級別的讀數,而不健康的污染水平一直持續至週三,影響本港大部分地區。銅鑼灣的空氣質素指數週三早上錄得154,屬對整體市民「不健康」的級別,而其他大部分地區的污染水平則被評為對敏感人群「不健康」,未及影響全體市民的程度。

預報人員將此次事件歸咎於一個現已頗為熟悉的組合:偏弱的風力籠罩全市,同時陽光異常猛烈,兩者結合正正有利於光化煙霧的形成。強烈日照會加速化學反應,將車輛廢氣及工業排放物轉化為地面臭氧及微細懸浮粒子,而當空氣像現時般停滯不動時,兩者在整個珠江三角洲地區的累積速度都會特別快。

香港天文台預測情況將有所緩和,指一股清新的偏東氣流將於本週稍後抵達廣東沿岸,有助逐步驅散煙霞。在此之前,當局重複了近乎例行的建議:已患有呼吸系統或心臟疾病的居民應減少戶外活動時間,而身體健康的市民則被提醒,類似事件一再出現,已逐漸成為本港秋季天氣中一個令人不快的常見現象,而非罕見例外。
