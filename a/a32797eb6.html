English

It is widely reported that Hong Kong is facing a deeply troubling paradoxa situation that seems contradictory but may be true看似矛盾卻可能屬實的情況 in youth mental health. According to new research from the University of Hong Kong's Centre for Suicide Research and Prevention, the city's overall suicide rate declined across nearly all age groups last year. Nevertheless, the rate among children under the age of fifteen climbed to a decade-highthe highest level in ten years十年來最高水平 of 2.9 per 100,000, the worst figure recorded since 2016. This finding runs directly counter to the broader downward trend, and it has prompted researchers to sound the alarm over what appears to be an emerging crisisa serious problem that is only just becoming apparent剛開始浮現的嚴重問題 among the city's youngest residents.

Critics contend that heavy social media use is a significant contributing factorsomething that helps cause a result促成某結果的因素 behind this troubling shift. The centre's researchers found that social media activity was strongly predictive of suicide cases among young people, suggesting that online exposure to harmful content, cyberbullying and unrealistic comparisons may exacerbateto make a problem or situation worse使問題或情況惡化 existing vulnerabilities. By the same token, the timing of a recent spate of suicide attempts, clustered around the start of the new academic year, points to the added weight of examination pressure and the notoriously competitive nature of Hong Kong's schooling system, which is often blamed for placing undue strainan unreasonable or excessive amount of pressure不合理或過度的壓力 on students.

In light of these findings, the centre's founding director has urged schools to give pupils more time and space to build rapport with teachers and classmates, arguing that strong relationships can act as a protective factorsomething that reduces risk or vulnerability能減低風險的因素 against psychological distress. It is widely argued that a purely academic response is insufficient; instead, a holistic approachone that considers the whole person, not just one aspect顧及整體而非單一方面的方法 involving families, educators and platform regulators will be necessary if Hong Kong is to reverse this alarming trajectory and safeguard the wellbeing of its next generation.

中文翻譯

據報道,香港在青少年精神健康方面正面對一個令人深感不安的矛盾現象。根據香港大學防止自殺研究中心的最新研究,去年全港整體自殺率在幾乎所有年齡組別均見下降。然而,15歲以下兒童的自殺率卻攀升至每十萬人2.9人的十年新高,是自2016年以來最差的數字。這項發現與整體下降趨勢背道而馳,促使研究人員就本港最年幼居民似乎正面臨的新興危機發出警號。

批評者認為,過度使用社交媒體是造成這一令人憂慮轉變的重要促成因素。研究中心的研究人員發現,社交媒體使用情況能強烈預測青少年的自殺個案,反映接觸網上有害內容、網絡欺凌及不切實際的比較,可能使現有的脆弱性惡化。同樣地,近期一連串自殺企圖集中在新學年開始之際發生,反映考試壓力及香港學制競爭激烈的本質,而後者常被指對學生造成不合理的壓力。

鑑於這些研究結果,研究中心的創辦總監促請學校給予學生更多時間及空間,與老師及同學建立融洽關係,認為緊密的人際關係可作為對抗心理困擾的保護因素。普遍認為,單靠學術層面的回應並不足夠;相反,若香港要扭轉這令人憂慮的趨勢,並保障下一代的福祉,必須採取涉及家庭、教育工作者及平台監管機構的全面方法。
