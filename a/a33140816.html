English

Beijing has lashed outreacted with sudden anger or strong criticism猛烈抨擊 at a new United States ruling that bars Chinese laboratories from certifying electronic devices for the American market, warning that the move threatens to disrupt supply chains stretching from mainland factories through Hong Kong’s ports and trading houses. The US Federal Communications Commission voted to prohibit test labs based in China, and in any country lacking a mutual recognition agreement with Washington, from carrying out the safety and compliance checks that electronics makers need before their products can legally enter the US. China’s Commerce Ministry called the decision “unilateraltaken by one side alone, without agreement from others單方面的” and said it had “seriously disrupted the stability of global industrial and supply chains.”

The ruling matters far beyond Washington and Beijing. The FCC itself estimates that roughly 82 per cent of devices currently certified for the US market are tested in Chinese-recognised laboratories, meaning manufacturers across the mainland — many of whom ship finished goods through Hong Kong — now face the prospect of longer testing queues, reroutedsent along a different path or through a different channel改道 logistics and higher costs once the rule takes effect in 2028. Hong Kong’s long-standing role as an entrepota trading hub where goods are received, stored and re-exported轉口港 for electronics bound for Western markets leaves local trading firms and freight forwarders particularly exposed to any slowdown.

Beijing’s ministry urged Washington to “immediately revoke” the measure and said China had earlier proposed negotiating a bilateral recognition deal, only to find the American side’s response lukewarmshowing little enthusiasm or interest反應冷淡 at best. It stopped short ofdid not go as far as to do something未有進一步 detailing retaliation, saying only that countermeasures would follow “if the US side persists in acting unilaterally.” Trade analysts in Hong Kong caution that the dispute, arriving weeks before a planned Trump-Xi meeting at the Shenzhen APEC summit, could cast a shadow overcreate a troubling, uncertain atmosphere around something為…蒙上陰影 efforts to extend the fragile tariff truce between the two economies, with knock-on effects for a city that depends heavily on the free flow of goods through its borders.

中文翻譯

北京猛烈抨擊美國一項新規定,該規定禁止中國實驗室為美國市場認證電子產品,並警告此舉可能擾亂從內地工廠經香港港口及貿易行流通的供應鏈。美國聯邦通信委員會投票通過,禁止設於中國,以及任何與美國未簽訂互認協議的國家的測試實驗室,為電子產品進行安全及合規檢測——而這些檢測正是產品合法進入美國市場前的必要程序。中國商務部形容這項決定屬於「單方面」行動,並表示此舉「嚴重擾亂全球產業鏈供應鏈穩定」。

這項裁決的影響遠超華盛頓與北京兩地。聯邦通信委員會本身估計,目前約八成二獲認證進入美國市場的裝置,均在中國認可的實驗室完成測試,意味著眾多內地製造商——當中不少透過香港轉運成品——一旦該規定於2028年生效,將面臨檢測排期延長、物流需要改道及成本上升的局面。香港作為電子產品轉銷西方市場的轉口港這一長期角色,令本地貿易公司及貨運代理商在供應鏈一旦放緩時,尤其容易受到衝擊。

中國商務部促請華盛頓方面「立即撤銷」有關措施,並指中方早前已建議就雙邊互認協議進行磋商,惟美方回應始終反應冷淡。中方未有進一步交代反制措施的細節,僅表示若美方一意孤行,中方將採取反制措施。香港貿易分析人士警告,這場爭議正值中美兩國元首計劃於深圳APEC峰會會晤前數週爆發,恐為延續雙方脆弱的關稅休戰談判蒙上陰影,對嚴重依賴貨物自由進出的香港而言,亦將帶來連鎖影響。
