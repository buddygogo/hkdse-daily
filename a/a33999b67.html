Hong Kong has rolled outto launch or introduce something推出 a new AI Privacy Sandbox for Schools, a government-backed scheme that allows publicly funded primary and secondary schools to trial artificial intelligence tools in a controlled environmenta regulated setting designed to limit risk受控環境 before adopting them more widely. Applications for the first phase are open to schools until 30 October 2026, with a briefing session having already been held to explain how the scheme will operate. Only fifteen schools will be selected to take part in the initial six-month pilot.

It is widely argued that the initiative reflects a broader attempt to strike a balanceto achieve an even compromise between two demands取得平衡 between technological advancement and the protection of students' personal data. Schools taking part will be able to experiment withto test out in practice嘗試 AI-powered marking, personalised learning platforms and administrative tools, while regulators monitor how student information is collected, stored and used. Officials say the sandbox model will help the Education Bureau formulateto devise or work out a plan or policy制定 clearer guidelines before AI is rolled out across all schools territory-wide.

Critics contend that six months is too short a period to assess the long-term implicationsto evaluate the lasting effects or consequences評估長遠影響 of AI adoption in classrooms, particularly given how quickly the technology evolves. Nevertheless, supporters maintain that a cautious, phased approacha plan carried out in stages rather than all at once分階段方式 is preferable to rushing untested systems into daily use. By the same token, parents' groups have called for greater transparency, urging the government to discloseto make information known publicly披露 exactly what data each participating school collects. As Hong Kong positions itself as a regional AI hub, this small-scale trial may prove to be an important litmus testa decisive indicator of future success or failure試金石 for how the city balances innovation with accountability in education.

中文翻譯

香港推出全新「學校人工智能私隱沙盒」,這項由政府支持的計劃,讓接受公帑資助的中小學在受控環境下試用人工智能工具,然後才更廣泛地採用。首階段的申請將開放至2026年10月30日,當局亦已舉辦簡介會,講解計劃的運作方式。首輪為期六個月的試驗計劃只會挑選十五所學校參與。

普遍認為,這項計劃反映當局嘗試在科技發展與保障學生個人資料之間取得平衡。參與學校將可嘗試人工智能評卷、個人化學習平台及行政工具,而監管機構則會監察學生資料如何被收集、儲存及使用。官員表示,沙盒模式將有助教育局在人工智能全港學校推行前,制定更清晰的指引。

批評者認為,六個月時間過短,難以就人工智能應用於課堂一事評估長遠影響,尤其是科技發展日新月異。然而,支持者堅持審慎、分階段的方式,總比倉促將未經測試的系統投入日常使用更為可取。同樣地,家長團體要求當局提高透明度,促請政府披露每所參與學校所收集的具體資料。隨著香港致力定位為區域人工智能樞紐,這項小規模試驗或將成為衡量本港如何在教育上平衡創新與問責的重要試金石。
