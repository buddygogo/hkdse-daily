English

It is widely reported that Chief Executive John Lee Ka-chiu has defended the economic measures set out in his 2026 Policy Address, insisting that private enterprisesbusinesses owned and operated by individuals or companies rather than the government私營企業 must still strive to remain competitive even as the government extends fresh support. Speaking on Saturday, Mr Lee stressed that the livelihood section of this year's address was the broadest in scope to date, forming part of the city's inaugural five-year development plana government blueprint setting economic and social goals over a five-year period五年發展規劃.

Critics contend that repeated rounds of government assistance risk fostering a culture of dependency on state interventiona reliance on government action rather than independent effort依賴政府干預, particularly among small and medium enterprises still recovering from years of subdued consumer spendingweaker than usual spending by households on goods and services疲弱的消費支出. Nevertheless, officials argue that targeted relief remains necessary to prevent further business closures while the broader economy adjusts to structural change.

By the same token, Mr Lee emphasised that businesses must first help the economy grow before the government can expand support for livelihoods, a formulation suggesting that welfare spending will remain contingent on continued economic growthan increase in the production and consumption of goods and services within an economy經濟增長 rather than expanding indefinitely. It is worth noting that this framing places considerable pressure on firms to demonstrate market-driven resiliencethe ability of businesses to adapt and recover through their own commercial strategies rather than external aid市場主導的韌性 rather than treating government support as a permanent safety net.

One cannot deny that the tension between offering meaningful assistance and avoiding an over-reliance on public funds will remain a defining feature of Hong Kong's economic policy in the years ahead. For students, the debate offers a useful illustration of how governments must constantly rebalance short-term relief against long-term fiscal sustainabilitythe capacity of a government to maintain its spending levels without risking future financial instability財政可持續性.

中文翻譯

據廣泛報導，行政長官李家超為其2026年施政報告中的經濟措施辯護，堅稱即使政府提供新一輪支援，私營企業仍須努力保持競爭力。他於星期六發言時強調，今年施政報告的民生部分涵蓋範圍是歷來最廣的，是本港首份五年發展規劃的一部分。

批評者認為，連續多輪的政府援助有可能助長依賴政府干預的風氣，對於仍在從多年疲弱的消費支出中復元的中小企尤其如此。然而，官員認為，在整體經濟適應結構性轉變之際，針對性紓困措施仍有必要，以防止更多商戶結業。

同樣地，李家超強調，企業必須先推動經濟增長，政府才能擴大民生支援，這種說法意味福利開支將取決於持續的經濟增長，而非無限擴張。值得注意的是，這種框架對企業造成相當大的壓力，要求它們展現市場主導的韌性，而非將政府支援視為永久的安全網。

不可否認，在提供有意義的援助與避免過度依賴公共資金之間取得平衡的張力，將繼續是香港未來多年經濟政策的一大特徵。對學生而言，這場爭論正好說明政府必須不斷在短期紓困與長期財政可持續性之間重新取得平衡。
