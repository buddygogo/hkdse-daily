English

Chief Executive John Lee told delegates at the Belt and Road Green Innovation Conference on Monday that Hong Kong is well placed to act as a green gatewaya city or hub that connects trading partners through environmentally friendly projects連接各方進行環保貿易及項目的城市或樞紐 linking Belt and Road economies. The gathering, held in the city for the first time alongside Eco Expo Asia 2026 at AsiaWorld-Expo, drew more than 400 government officials and industry experts from 16 countries and regions, reflecting growing interest in pooling resources for greener growth across the trading network.

Speaking to the sustainable financefinancial services and investment that support environmentally and socially responsible projects支持環保及社會責任項目的金融服務與投資 sector, Lee pitched Hong Kong's expertise in financing, project management and risk managementthe process of identifying and controlling potential financial or project dangers識別及控制潛在財務或項目風險的過程 services for green infrastructure works across the Belt and Road. He argued the city could help economies along the route combine their strengths to advance green technologies and environmentally responsible business. The Chief Executive also floated the idea of cross-boundary carbon tradingbuying and selling carbon emission credits between different jurisdictions不同司法管轄區之間買賣碳排放配額, saying Hong Kong would explore the mechanism to help the country build a carbon market carrying greater international weight.

Central to the pitch is a Green Technology Bridgehead Programmea scheme that gives green enterprises an initial foothold to expand into new markets為環保企業提供初步據點以拓展新市場的計劃, due to launch next year, through which mainland and overseas green enterprises can gain a footholdan initial secure position from which further progress can be made可作進一步發展的初步據點 in Belt and Road markets. Officials said at least five firms have already signed up to receive training under the scheme. The initiative builds on a Belt and Road Sustainable Green Development Training Centre set up in the city in May, which offers courses to personnel from countries along the route, and signals Hong Kong's ambition to be seen not merely as a financial centre but as a genuine hub for environmentally sustainable trade and innovation.

中文翻譯

行政長官李家超星期一在「一帶一路綠色創新大會」上向與會者表示，香港有條件擔任連接「一帶一路」沿線經濟體的綠色門戶。今次大會首次在本港舉行，與「2026環保節亞洲展」同步於亞洲國際博覽館舉行，吸引逾400名來自16個國家及地區的政府官員及業界專家出席，反映沿線貿易網絡對匯聚資源推動綠色發展的興趣日增。

李家超向可持續金融業界推銷香港在綠色基建融資、項目管理及風險管理服務方面的優勢。他認為香港可協助沿線經濟體匯聚力量，推動綠色科技及具環保責任的商業發展。行政長官亦提出探討跨境碳交易的可能性，稱香港將研究有關機制，協助國家建立具更大國際影響力的碳市場。

這次推銷的核心是明年將推出的綠色科技橋頭堡計劃，讓內地及海外綠色企業可在「一帶一路」市場取得初步據點。官員表示，已有至少五家企業報名參與計劃培訓。該計劃建基於今年五月在本港成立的「一帶一路可持續綠色發展培訓中心」，為沿線國家人員提供課程，顯示香港不僅欲擔任金融中心，更希望成為真正推動環保可持續貿易及創新的樞紐。
