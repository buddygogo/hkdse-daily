English

More than 180,000 people packed the streets of Tai Kok Tsui on Sunday for the twentieth edition of the district's long-running temple faira traditional festival held regularly over many years歷史悠久的廟會, organised by the Mong Kok Kaifong Association with backing from property developer Sino Group. Held under the theme "Temple Fair Brings People Together, Culture Converges in Hong Kong," the one-day event centred on the Fuk Tsun Street shrine dedicated to Hung Shing, a sea goda deity associated with the sea, worshipped by fishing communities海神 traditionally honoured by fishing communities, and combined a morning procession with an evening of performance and spectacle.

The day's programme ran from a grand paradea large, impressive public procession盛大巡遊 through the neighbourhood's narrow streets at dawn to an eighteen-strong southern lion dance performed on raised poleselevated poles used as a performance platform高樁 at dusk, a feat demanding considerable balance and nerve from performers. Crowds were further drawn by complimentary poon choi, a layered communal stewa shared dish cooked in stacked layers of ingredients多層共享盆菜 traditionally eaten during festive gatherings, distributed free of charge throughout the afternoon, while Sino Group laid on shuttle buses from nearby Olympian City to ease the crush of visitorsreduce overcrowding caused by many visitors緩解人潮擁擠 converging on the narrow district.

As dusk fell, attention turned to Fuk Tsun Street itself, where a 500-foot illuminated dragon wound its way above a sea of floating fish lanterns, bringing the day-long celebration to a close. Organisers said the fair's growing popularity reflected a renewed appetitea fresh or rekindled desire for something重新燃起的興趣 among Hongkongers, young and old, for communal ritualsshared traditional practices performed together as a community社區共同習俗 rooted in the city's fishing-village past, even as the surrounding neighbourhood has been transformed by decades of redevelopment into dense residential towers.

中文翻譯

逾十八萬人星期日湧到大角咀街頭，參與由旺角街坊會主辦、地產發展商信和集團支持的第二十屆歷史悠久的廟會。活動以「廟會凝聚人心　文化匯聚香江」為主題，圍繞福全街供奉海神洪聖的廟宇舉行，洪聖傳統上為漁民社群所敬奉，整日活動由早上巡遊展開，直至晚間的表演及盛大場面。

當日節目由黎明時份穿梭社區狹窄街道的盛大巡遊開始，至黃昏時份十八頭南獅在高樁上表演作結，考驗表演者相當的平衡力及膽量。免費派發的盆菜——一種節慶場合常見的多層共享盆菜——亦吸引大批人潮，整個下午免費供應；信和集團另安排來往鄰近奧海城的接駁巴士，以緩解人潮擁擠湧向這個狹窄社區的情況。

入夜後，目光轉向福全街本身，一條長五百尺的巨龍燈飾在一片魚形花燈之上蜿蜒遊動，為一整日的慶典畫上句號。主辦方表示，廟會日益高漲的人氣，反映港人不論老幼，對植根於本港漁村歷史的傳統重新燃起興趣，渴望參與這類社區共同習俗，即使周邊社區歷經數十年重建，早已變成密集的住宅大廈林立之地。
