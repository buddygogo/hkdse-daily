English

A coalition calling itself Yeshua Families Built in Love has submitted an online petitiona formal written request, usually gathered and signed through a website, calling on authorities to take or reverse an action網上聯署 to Hong Kong's legislature and government departments, urging authorities to scrap the Hong Kong Tourism Board's first-ever month-long “Halloween Month” campaign before weekend street parties begin this Saturday. The petition's Google Form had gathered around 70 signatures by Wednesday, most of them from other Christian organisations rather than individual residents, and it frames the secular celebrationa celebration with no religious character, intended for the general public rather than any particular faith世俗慶典 running throughout October as something closer to a spiritual threat than a harmless seasonal promotion.

In their submission, signatories describe the festival as being “filled with the worship… of death, evil spirits, curses, and the dark world,” arguing that an occasion with such origins should not receive official backing, let alone be funded with public fundsmoney collected and controlled by the government, intended to serve the whole community rather than private interests公共資金 intended to serve the whole community. Their target is less the private celebration of Halloween, which has taken place in bars and clubs for years, than the Tourism Board's active promotion of it through a coordinated tourism strategya deliberate plan devised by an official body to attract visitors and boost tourist spending旅遊推廣策略: citywide cosplay street parties, electronic music events and free tram rides offered to costumed visitors who clear a set spending thresholda minimum amount of money a customer must spend before becoming eligible for an offer or reward消費門檻 at partner venues.

The campaign itself traces back to February's budget speech, in which officials signalled an intent to promote festivals that carry less weight on the mainland as a way of diversifying Hong Kong's calendar of tourist draws. Whether the government responds to this religious objectionopposition to something based specifically on religious beliefs or teachings宗教上的反對 with any changes before the first parties begin on October 17 remains unclear, but the episode illustrates a recurring tension in a cultural importa custom or practice adopted from another country or culture, as opposed to one that originated locally外來文化 once confined to nightlife districts now being turned into official, publicly promoted entertainment.

中文翻譯

一個名為「以馬內利愛家」(Yeshua Families Built in Love) 的團體,向香港立法會及多個政府部門遞交網上聯署,要求當局在本週六街頭派對開始前,撤回香港旅遊發展局首次舉辦的全月「香港萬聖節月」活動。截至週三,聯署所用的Google表單已收集約七十個簽名,當中大部分來自其他基督教團體而非個人市民,並將這個貫穿整個十月的世俗慶典形容為帶有靈性威脅,而非單純無害的季節性推廣活動。

聯署人在意見書中形容該節日「充斥着對死亡、邪靈、咒詛及黑暗世界的崇拜」,認為一個有此淵源的節日不應獲官方背書,更不應動用本應服務全體市民的公共資金予以資助。他們針對的,與其說是多年來一直在酒吧及夜店舉行的私人萬聖節慶祝活動,不如說是旅發局透過一套協調一致的旅遊推廣策略積極推動此節日:全城性的扮裝街頭派對、電子音樂活動,以及向達到指定消費門檻的扮裝旅客提供免費電車乘搭優惠。

該活動的構思可追溯至二月的財政預算案演辭,當時官員表明有意推廣在內地較少受重視的節日,藉以豐富香港的旅遊節慶日曆。政府會否因應這次宗教上的反對,在十月十七日首場派對開始前作出任何調整,目前仍不清楚;但事件反映出,一種一度只局限於夜生活場所的外來文化,如今正被轉化為官方公開推廣的娛樂活動,其中所存在的持續張力。
