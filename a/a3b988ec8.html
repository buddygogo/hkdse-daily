English

Senior mainland officials have reportedly been shuttling through Hong Kong this month on what insiders describe as a wide-ranging fact-finding missionan organised visit intended to gather first-hand information before an important decision is made實地考察, according to a report by The Standard. The visiting delegation is said to have held a series of closed-door meetingsprivate discussions held away from public view or media attention閉門會議 with figures from the business and political sectors, sounding outasking people informally for their views in order to gauge likely reaction試探意見 potential candidates’ willingness to serve in the city’s next government while also gauging the health of the local economy.

Political analyst Lau Siu-kai told the paper that such visits are standard practicean accepted, routine way of doing something that is expected rather than unusual慣常做法, noting that the central government regularly consults through various channelsa range of different routes or means by which information or opinion is gathered不同途徑 before settling on the make-up of the next Hong Kong cabinet. The timing is significant: a small-circle election is being organised to renew the 1,500-member Election Committee, the Beijing-loyalist body that in turn selects the chief executive, fuelling speculation over whether incumbent John Lee will be given a second term.

Beijing has also signalled, through the Hong Kong and Macau Affairs Office, what it expects from the incoming team, calling for officials chosen on the basis of integrity and competencehonesty and moral soundness combined with the skill needed to do a job well廉潔與才能 and urging the city to overhaul how it trains, selects and manages governance talentcapable individuals suited to holding public office and running a government治理人才. Commentators say the consultations illustrate how firmly the choice of the next chief executive and cabinet remains a Beijing-led processa process whose outcome is ultimately decided and directed by the central government由北京主導的程序, even as local business and political figures are given a chance to voice their views.

中文翻譯

據《英文虎報》（The Standard）報道，多名內地高層官員本月據報一直在香港穿梭，進行一次消息人士形容為範圍廣泛的實地考察。消息指，到訪的代表團與商界及政界人士舉行了一連串閉門會議，一方面試探意見，了解有意人士是否願意加入下屆政府，另一方面亦藉機了解本港經濟狀況。

政治評論員劉兆佳向該報表示，這類到訪屬慣常做法，並指中央政府在敲定下屆香港班子人選前，一直會透過不同途徑進行諮詢。而今次的時機別具意義：香港正籌組選舉，以改選由1500名委員組成的選舉委員會——這個親北京陣營主導的機構將負責選出下任行政長官，令外界猜測現任特首李家超能否獲得連任。

北京亦已透過國務院港澳事務辦公室表明對新一屆管治班子的期望，要求挑選具備廉潔與才能的官員，並促請本港全面檢討培訓、選拔及管理治理人才的方式。評論人士指出，今次連串諮詢顯示，下任行政長官及班子人選始終是一項由北京主導的程序，即使本地商界及政界人士獲邀表達意見，亦難以改變這一格局。
