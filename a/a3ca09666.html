A video showing a young girl in Tuen Mun's Fu Tai Estate carrying beer cansholding several tins of beer攜帶啤酒罐 and a worn-out schoolbaga bag damaged from heavy use破舊書包 has gone viral online, triggering a child-welfare investigation and a wider debate about the city's welfare safety netthe system of support meant to protect the needy福利安全網. Her parents were arrested on suspicion of child abusebelieved to have mistreated a child涉嫌虐兒 and have since been released on bail, while the girl and her two younger siblings have been placed in temporary institutional careshort-term housing in a care facility rather than at home臨時院舍照顧 pending a full assessment.

The case took a further turn after social media users accused the family of abusing welfare paymentsmisusing financial assistance meant for the needy濫用綜援, pointing to reports that the household — which receives roughly eighteen thousand dollars a month under the Comprehensive Social Security Assistance scheme — had travelled overseas and visited a theme park. Secretary for Labour and Welfare Chris Sun confirmed that the Social Welfare Department is reviewing the family's eligibilitychecking whether they still qualify for support檢視家庭領取資格, and said that if serious irregularities are uncovered, the case could be referred to the policepassed on for criminal investigation轉介警方 for criminal follow-up.

Officials say a multi-disciplinary case conference involving social workers, doctors and police will decide whether to seek a court protection order, while commentators warn that the public's rush to pass judgement onlinecriticise someone publicly before all facts are known網上妄下判斷 risks overshadowing the more complicated question of whether the city's welfare system has the resources to properly safeguard vulnerable childrenprotect children who are at risk of harm保障弱勢兒童. Child-welfare groups have urged the public to consider kinship care options before any long-term removal from the family.

中文翻譯

一段在屯門富泰邨拍攝的片段在網上瘋傳，片中一名女童攜帶啤酒罐及一個破舊書包，事件觸發一宗兒童福利調查，並引發社會對本港福利安全網的廣泛討論。女童父母因涉嫌虐兒被捕，其後獲准保釋候查，而女童與她的兩名年幼弟妹則已被安排臨時院舍照顧，以待社署作全面評估。

事件其後再起波瀾，有網民指責該家庭濫用綜援，並指該家庭每月領取約一萬八千元綜援金，卻被揭發曾多次外遊及到主題樂園遊玩。勞工及福利局局長孫玉菡證實，社會福利署正檢視家庭領取資格，並表示若發現嚴重違規情況，個案不排除會轉介警方循刑事方向跟進。

官員表示，一個由社工、醫生及警方組成的多專業個案會議，將會決定是否需要向法庭申請保護令，而評論則警告，公眾急於網上妄下判斷，恐怕會掩蓋一個更複雜的問題——本港福利制度是否具備足夠資源，真正保障弱勢兒童。兒童福利機構呼籲公眾，在考慮長期安置之前，應優先考慮由親屬照顧的可能。
