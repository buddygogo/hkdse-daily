English

Hong Kong's public universities are raising tuition fees for non-local undergraduatesuniversity students who are not permanent residents of the city where they are studying非本地本科生 by up to 26 per cent for the 2027-28 academic year, even as the government keeps expanding the non-local student quotathe maximum proportion of places at a public university that may be filled by students from outside the city非本地生限額. Seven of the eight public universities have published new rates, with an average rise of 17 per cent; the University of Hong Kong's medicine and dentistry programmes face the steepest jump, with annual fees rising 137 per cent to HK$590,000.

City University and Baptist University both raised fees by 26 per cent to HK$240,000 a year, while HKUST and PolyU announced increases of 21 and 20 per cent respectively. Taken together with previous rounds, the 2027-28 rates represent a cumulative risethe total increase built up over several separate steps, rather than from a single change累積升幅 of 41 per cent since 2023-24. Universities cite rising teaching costs and international benchmarkingcomparing fees or standards against similar institutions overseas to judge whether they are competitive與國際院校水平比較 to justify the increases, while stressing that Hong Kong fees remain below those of top overseas rivals.

The fee rise comes as the non-local quota has climbed from 20 to 50 per cent of local undergraduate places since 2023, with intake reaching 27.1 per cent of local places last year, over 70 per cent of them mainland students. Critics argue the twin trends risk turning internationalisationthe process of attracting more students, staff or partnerships from beyond one's own city or country國際化 into a revenue strategy that prices out less wealthy applicants, even as some universities expand scholarships to offset the burdento reduce or balance out a cost or difficulty by providing something in return抵銷負擔 on students who cannot afford the steeper rates.

中文翻譯

香港各所公立大學將於二零二七至二八學年，向非本地本科生大幅加價，加幅最高達百分之二十六，而政府同時持續擴大非本地生限額。八所公立大學中，七所已公布新學費，平均加幅為百分之十七；香港大學醫科及牙科課程的加幅最為顯著，年度學費將上升百分之一百三十七，至五十九萬港元。

香港城市大學及香港浸會大學均加費百分之二十六，至每年二十四萬港元，香港科技大學及香港理工大學則分別上調百分之二十一及二十。綜合歷年加幅，二零二七至二八學年的學費較二零二三至二四學年已累積上升百分之四十一，屬累積升幅。各大學以教學成本上升及與國際院校水平比較為加價理據，同時強調本港學費仍低於海外頂尖院校。

加費之際，非本地生限額自二零二三年起已由本地學額的百分之二十提升至百分之五十，去年非本地生人數已佔本地學額百分之二十七點一，當中逾七成來自中國內地。批評者認為，學費與限額雙雙上升，或令國際化淪為一種收入策略，將較不富裕的申請人拒於門外，儘管部分大學已擴大獎學金，以抵銷負擔，幫助未能負擔加幅的學生。
