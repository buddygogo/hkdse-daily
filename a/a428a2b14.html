English

Hong Kong Customs officers arrested a 28-year-old Malaysian woman at the airport on Sunday after finding about 15 kilograms of suspected ketamine, worth roughly HK$5.4 million, hidden inside packages of coffee powder in her checked suitcase. She had flown in from Paris via Taipei and was intercepted as a transit passengera traveller passing through an airport on the way to a final destination, rather than ending their journey there過境旅客, prompting officers to pull her aside for a closer inspection.

Coffee powder is a long-favoured concealment methoda technique for hiding something, here used to disguise illegal drugs from detection藏毒手法 among traffickers because its strong smell is thought to throw off sniffer dogs and mask the chemical odour of the drug beneath it. Customs said her travel pattern fit a profile long associated with couriers working for international syndicates, and that officers had relied on risk profilingthe practice of assessing which travellers are more likely to be involved in illegal activity, based on patterns seen in past cases風險分析 alongside routine screening to flag her.

The force described her as a suspected drug mulea low-level courier paid to carry illegal drugs across borders on behalf of a larger criminal network運毒者 rather than an organiser, reflecting how trafficking networkan organised group that coordinates the movement and sale of illegal drugs across multiple locations販毒網絡s increasingly rely on travellers with no criminal record to move product through hubs like Hong Kong's airport. Investigators are tracing her point of origin and intended buyers, and Customs reiterated that drug trafficking carries a maximum penaltythe harshest punishment the law allows for a given offence最高刑罰 of a HK$5 million fine and life imprisonment, however minor a courier's role might seem.

中文翻譯

香港海關人員星期日在香港國際機場拘捕一名28歲馬來西亞女子,並在她的寄艙行李中搜獲以咖啡粉包裝藏起的懷疑氯胺酮(K仔),約重15公斤,估計市值約540萬港元。該名女子由巴黎經台北轉機抵港,以過境旅客身份入境時被截查,其後遭關員抽查行李。

咖啡粉向來是販毒者慣用的藏毒手法,因其氣味濃烈,被認為可混淆緝毒犬的嗅覺,並掩蓋藏於粉末下方毒品的化學氣味。海關表示,該名女子的行程模式及所乘搭的航線,與過往多宗涉及國際販毒集團「運毒」案件的特徵相似,關員結合風險分析與日常行李檢查,才將她截查。

警方形容她為涉嫌運毒者,而非幕後主腦,反映販毒網絡日益傾向利用沒有犯罪紀錄的旅客,經香港機場等主要交通樞紐運送毒品。調查人員正追查其毒品來源及目標買家,海關及稅務署重申,販毒罪最高刑罰為罰款500萬港元及終身監禁,不論毒品以何種方式藏起,或「運毒者」角色看似多麼次要。
