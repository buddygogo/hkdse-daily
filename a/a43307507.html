English

Canadian singer The Weeknd closed his Hong Kong residencya run of shows by the same act at one venue駐場演出 on 30 and 31 October, wrapping up a four-night, sold-outwith every ticket purchased門票售罄 run at Kai Tak Stadium as part of his “After Hours Til Dawn” world tour. Returning to the city for the first time in eight years, the star drew fans from across the region, with Ming Pao reporting a first-night crowd of more than 40,000. The series’ standout moment, however, was not a medley of old hits but a brand-new track titled “Hong Kong”, unveiled on opening night.

The song builds its hook around a sample of “季候風” (Seasonal Wind), a 1993 recording by Cantopop icon Faye Wong, woven into a production that blends R&B with the original’s dreamy synths. According to the music video’s director, the footage — shot in roughly a week — was conceived as a homagea show of respect or admiration致敬 to Hong Kong’s cultural golden eraa period regarded as the best of its kind黃金時代, splicing neon-lit street scenes with imagery evoking the city’s 1990s pop landscape. The gesture struck an emotional chord: longtime Cantopop listeners welcomed the tribute as validation of a musical cultural heritagetraditions and achievements passed down within a society文化遺産 often overshadowed by newer regional trends, while younger fans discovered Faye Wong’s back catalogue for the first time, sparking a wave of nostalgiaa sentimental longing for the past懷舊之情 online.

The concerts also left a visible mark on the city’s infrastructure and economy. Authorities rolled out more than a dozen dedicated post-show routes, including overnight cross-boundarycrossing from one jurisdiction into another跨境 coaches to Shenzhen’s Huanggang checkpoint, to clear tens of thousands of departing fans without overwhelming the MTR. Nearby malls extended trading hours and offered ticket-holder discounts. For cultural commentators, the episode illustrates how a single cross-generationalappealing to people of different age groups跨代 gesture from an international star can generate real soft powerinfluence gained through culture rather than force軟実力 for the city, reminding global audiences that Hong Kong’s musical legacy still resonates far beyond its borders, even amid intense competition from K-pop and Mandopop acts touring the region.

中文翻譯

加拿大歌手Weeknd於十月三十及三十一日結束其香港駐場演出，在啟德體育園一連四晚的門票售罄騷正式閉幕，壓軸場次是其「After Hours Til Dawn」世界巡演的一部分。歌手八年來首次重返香江，吸引區內大批樂迷捧場，《明報》報道首場演出入場人數超過四萬人。系列演出的焦點並非串燒金曲，而是一首於首演夜曝光的全新單曲《Hong Kong》。

這首歌以香港樂壇天后王菲一九九三年經典《季候風》作取樣，混合節奏布魯斯曲風與原曲夢幻合成器音色。據音樂影片導演透露，片段以約一星期時間拍攝完成，旨在向香港文化黃金時代致以致敬，將霓虹街景與九十年代流行文化意象交織呈現。此舉觸動不少樂迷情緒：資深廣東歌聽眾視之為對常被地區新興潮流掩蓋的音樂文化遺產一種肯定，而年輕樂迷則藉此首次接觸王菲的舊作，在網上掀起一片懷舊之情。

演唱會同時在城市基建與經濟層面留下明顯痕跡。當局開設逾十條專線散場巴士，包括通宵跨境巴士直達深圳皇崗口岸，以疏導數萬名散場樂迷，避免港鐵系統不勝負荷。鄰近商場亦延長營業時間，並向持票人士提供購物優惠。文化評論員認為，事件正好說明一位國際巨星單一個跨代舉動，足以為城市帶來實際軟實力，向全球觀眾提醒：即使在韓流及國語流行音樂於區內激烈競爭下，香港的音樂傳承依然餘韻悠長，影響力遠超本土。
