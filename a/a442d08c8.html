English

Actor Nick Cheung Ka-fai is set to headline a three-day variety showa stage programme that combines different kinds of entertainment such as comedy, singing and dancing綜藝表演 at the Hong Kong Coliseum this coming Lunar New Year, marking his first time leading such a production at the 12,500-seat venue. The veteran performer announced The ChaChaFai Show on Thursday, revealing that the run will take place from February 6 to 8, coinciding with the first three days of the Lunar New Yearthe lunar calendar festival marking the start of the Chinese new year農曆新年 holiday.

Rather than mount a conventional concert, Cheung said he wanted the show to feel like a full stage productionan organised theatrical performance built around sets, scripts and staging舞台製作, weaving comedy sketches, songs and dance numbers around a loose narrative thread. In a promotional video released alongside the announcement, Cheung revisited five of his best-known signature rolesthe parts an actor is most famous for playing代表角色, from the boxing drama Unbeatable to the gangster comedy The Conman 1999, before appearing in a white tuxedo to confirm the show in person.

Industry observers see the production as both a box-office drawsomething expected to attract large paying audiences票房保證 and a calculated appeal to nostalgiaa sentimental longing for the past懷舊情懷, as fans who grew up watching Cheung's films are invited to relive familiar characters in a fresh format. The Coliseum, long associated with pop concerts by Cantopop stars, has in recent years begun hosting a wider range of formats, and organisers say the elaborate live stagingthe physical setting and arrangement of a performance on stage現場舞台布置 for Cheung's show is still being finalised ahead of ticket sales.

中文翻譯

影星張家輝將於即將來臨的農曆新年期間，領銜主演一場為期三日的綜藝表演，在可容納12,500人的香港體育館舉行，是他首次在此場館擔綱此類製作。這位資深演員星期四宣布舉行《ChaChaFai Show》，透露演出將於2月6日至8日舉行，正值農曆新年假期首三日。

張家輝表示，他不希望以傳統演唱會形式呈現，而是希望整個演出猶如一場完整的舞台製作，將喜劇環節、歌曲及舞蹈編排在一條鬆散的故事線之中。在隨公布發布的宣傳片中，張家輝重現五個他最為人熟悉的代表角色，包括拳擊電影《激戰》及黑幫喜劇《賭俠1999》中的角色，最後以一身白色禮服現身，親自確認演出。

業界人士認為，這次製作既是票房保證，亦是對懷舊情懷的精心迎合，讓一班自小觀看張家輝電影長大的影迷，可以在全新形式下重遇熟悉的角色。香港體育館一向與粵語流行音樂巨星的演唱會關係密切，但近年開始接納更多元化的演出形式，主辦單位表示，張家輝演出的現場舞台布置仍在最後定案階段，尚未公開發售門票。
