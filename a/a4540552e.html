English

Hong Kong needs to broadento make something wider in range or scope擴闊；擴大範圍 mental health support well beyond students already flagged as high-risk, a key government adviser has urged, warning that the city's current approach may be missing many young people in genuine distress. Dr Lam Ching-choi, a member of the Executive Council and chairman of the Advisory Committee on Mental Health, pointed out that some students who took their own lives had shown no obvious warning signsindications that something bad might happen警號；預警跡象 at home or in school.

It is widely argued that this pattern complicates prevention efforts considerably. According to Dr Lam, several of the affected teenagers came from stable, middle-class families, performed well academically, and were not known to have experienced bullying — meaning that schemes targetingaiming at or directed towards a particular group針對（某群體） only "high-risk" individuals may fail to reach them in time. He attributed part of the problem to social media, where young people can be exposed to harmful contentmaterial that causes damage, especially psychologically有害內容 and online bullying largely invisible to parents and teachers.

Nevertheless, there are signs of a wider policy shift. Dr Lam called for support measures to be extended to the general student populationall students as a whole, not just a specific subgroup全體學生 rather than narrowly targeted groups, and separately, a prominent local tycoon has pledged funding to empower more young philanthropists to tackle the issue. By the same token, mental health experts caution that any expanded scheme must be adequately resourced, or risk becoming merely symbolicrepresenting an idea rather than having real, practical effect象徵性的；流於表面的 rather than genuinely protective. Critics contend that without sustained investment in school counselling and community outreach, Hong Kong's youth mental health crisis will continue to worsen.

中文翻譯

一名重要的政府顧問促請香港將精神健康支援擴闊至遠超現時被標籤為高風險的學生範圍，並警告現行做法可能令許多正承受真正困擾的年輕人被忽略。行政會議成員兼精神健康諮詢委員會主席林正財指出，部分選擇結束自己生命的學生，在家中或學校均未曾顯露明顯的警號。

普遍認為，這種模式令預防工作大大複雜化。據林正財所述，多名受影響的青少年來自穩定的中產家庭，學業成績良好，亦沒有曾遭欺凌的紀錄——意味著只針對「高風險」人士的計劃或未能及時觸及他們。他將部分問題歸因於社交媒體，年輕人在當中可能接觸到有害內容及家長與教師難以察覺的網絡欺凌。

然而，政策方向出現更廣泛轉變的跡象。林正財呼籲將支援措施擴展至全體學生，而非僅限於狹窄的目標群體；另外，一名著名本地大亨亦承諾提供資金，培育更多年輕慈善家參與應對這問題。同樣道理，精神健康專家警告，任何擴展計劃必須獲得充足資源支持，否則只會流於象徵性而非真正具保護作用。評論者認為，若缺乏對學校輔導及社區外展服務的持續投資，香港青少年精神健康危機將會持續惡化。
