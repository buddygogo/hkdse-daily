The Education Bureau has unveiledto reveal or make public公布 the Values Education Curriculum Framework (2026), a roadmap that all primary and secondary schools in Hong Kong are required to implementto put a plan into effect實施 from the 2026/27 school year onwards. It is widely argued that the framework, compiled by the Standing Committee on Values Education, marks one of the most significant shifts in moral and civic education in recent years, as it formally sets out twelve core values — including perseverance, integrity, empathy and national identity — that schools are expected to weave into everyday teaching.

Critics contend that mandating such a broad and prescriptive set of values risks reducing a nuanced subject to a tick-box exercisea task completed superficially just to satisfy a requirement形式主義的任務, leaving teachers with little room to encourage independent moral reasoning among students. Supporters, however, maintain that a coherent frameworka logically connected and consistent set of principles連貫一致的框架 is long overdue, arguing that fragmented, ad hoc lessons have previously failed to cultivate a strong sense ofto develop and nurture over time培養強烈的……感 responsibility and belonging among young people. The framework also places notable emphasis on Chinese culture, national education and media and information literacythe ability to critically evaluate and responsibly use information and digital media媒體與資訊素養, reflecting growing official concern over how students navigate an increasingly complex online environment.

Nevertheless, questions remain over how schools with limited resources will be able to translate policy into practiceto turn an abstract plan into concrete, workable action將政策轉化為實踐 without additional funding or teacher training. By the same token, some educators worry that an overly centralised approach could stifleto prevent something from developing or being expressed freely扼殺 the flexibility that individual schools currently enjoy in tailoring moral education to their own student populations. As the 2026/27 school year approaches, it is expected that schools across the city will begin drawing up detailed implementation plans, with the Bureau pledging further guidance and support in the coming months.

中文翻譯

教育局公布《價值觀教育課程框架》（2026年），為全港中小學訂立發展路向，所有學校須由2026/27學年起實施。有人廣泛認為，這份由價值觀教育常務委員會編製的框架，標誌着近年德育及公民教育最重大的轉變之一，因為當中正式訂明十二項核心價值——包括堅毅、誠信、同理心及國民身份認同——學校須將其融入日常教學之中。

批評者指出，強制推行如此廣泛而具規範性的一套價值觀，或有可能將一個細緻微妙的議題，淪為形式主義的任務，令教師缺乏空間培養學生獨立的道德思考能力。然而，支持者則認為，一個連貫一致的框架可說是早該推行，並指出過往零散、各自為政的課堂安排，一直未能有效培養強烈的……感，使學生對社會產生責任感及歸屬感。此框架亦特別強調中華文化、國民教育及媒體與資訊素養，反映當局日益關注學生在日趨複雜的網絡環境中應如何自處。

然而，資源有限的學校能否在缺乏額外撥款或教師培訓的情況下，將政策轉化為實踐，仍是一大疑問。同樣地，有教育工作者擔心，過於中央主導的做法，可能會扼殺個別學校目前因應本身學生特質，靈活調整德育教學的空間。隨着2026/27學年漸近，預料全港學校將開始制訂詳細的實施計劃，而教育局亦承諾在未來數月提供進一步指引及支援。
