English

Hong Kong is set to overhaul its free trade arrangementmake major structural changes to an existing trade agreement全面革新自由貿易安排 with the mainland for a third time, in a move authorities say will make the city more attractive to international investors. Commerce Secretary Algernon Yau announced that under the third revision to the Closer Economic Partnership Arrangement, or CEPA, foreign companies manufacturing goods in Hong Kong will be granted preferential mainland market accessspecial, more favourable conditions for entering and trading in the mainland Chinese market優惠的內地市場准入 on equal terms with local Hong Kong firms.

Under the proposed amendment, foreign-invested manufacturers will be eligible for zero-tariff entrythe ability to import goods without paying any customs duty零關稅准入 into the mainland, provided their products meet the criteria for "Made in Hong Kong" origin. It is widely argued that this represents a significant departure from previous rounds of CEPA, which had largely reserved such benefits for companies with substantial local Hong Kong ownership. Critics of the status quothe existing state of affairs before a change is introduced現狀 had long complained that international firms based in the city faced an uneven playing field compared with mainland-linked competitors.

By the same token, the revision forms part of a broader commitment outlined in Hong Kong's first Five-Year Plana government document setting out development goals and policy direction over a five-year period五年規劃, which pledged to pursue a comprehensive upgrade of CEPA and deepen cooperation with mainland service sectors. Analysts suggest that by lowering barriers for foreign direct investmentmoney invested by an overseas company or individual into productive assets within another economy外商直接投資, Hong Kong hopes to reinforce its position as a gateway connecting international capital with the vast mainland consumer market, at a time when the city faces intensifying competition from rival regional trading hubsother cities or economies competing to serve as centres of international trade區域貿易樞紐 such as Singapore and Shenzhen.

中文翻譯

香港將第三次全面革新與內地的自由貿易安排，當局表示此舉將令本港對國際投資者更具吸引力。商務及經濟發展局局長丘應樺宣布，根據《內地與香港關於建立更緊密經貿關係的安排》（CEPA）第三次修訂，在香港設廠生產的外資企業將可與本地港資企業一樣，獲得優惠的內地市場准入待遇。

根據建議修訂，只要產品符合「香港製造」原產地標準，外資製造商將有資格獲得零關稅准入內地市場。普遍認為，這與過往多輪CEPA修訂有顯著不同，過往此類優惠大致只保留給具有相當本地港資股權的企業。現狀的批評者早已投訴，相比與內地有聯繫的競爭對手，駐港國際企業一直處於不公平的競爭環境。

同樣道理，此次修訂是香港首份五年規劃所列更廣泛承諾的一部分，該規劃承諾全面升級CEPA，並深化與內地服務業的合作。分析人士認為，透過降低外商直接投資的門檻，香港希望強化其作為連接國際資金與龐大內地消費市場的門戶地位，尤其是在本港面臨新加坡及深圳等區域貿易樞紐競爭日益激烈之際。
