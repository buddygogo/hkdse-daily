English

Hong Kong's home-soil hopes dimmed on 7 and 8 November when the World Triathlon Cup returned to the Central Harbourfront. Local favourite Bailee Brown endured a lacklustre showinga performance that fails to meet expectations令人失望的表現 in front of a home crowd, finishing well down the field as Russia's Diana Isakova claimed her third World Cup gold of the season. For Brown, the result was simply the latest chapter in a season she has described as unforgiving.

The 24-year-old's 2026 campaign has been plagued by setbackstroubled continuously by problems or misfortune屢遭挫折 almost from the start. She first fell ill with a combination of E. coli and norovirus after a Hong Kong race, then suffered a gruelling ordealan extremely difficult and exhausting experience艱辛的考驗 on a trail run that left a finger badly damaged, requiring four separate operations to repair. A subsequent bone stress injury forced her out of training for weeks. Her coach, Andrew Wright, admitted candidly that the stress injury had been entirely avoidableable to have been prevented with proper care完全可以避免 in hindsight, and that he bore some responsibility for pushing her training too hard.

Despite the accumulated toll, Brown insists she has not lost her unwavering resolvea firm determination that does not weaken堅定不移的決心 to keep competing. She describes triathlon as “brutal, unforgiving” yet says she still loves the sport that has tested her so severely. Brown will now travel to Jordan to draw the curtain on the seasonto bring something to its conclusion為賽季畫上句號 at the Asia Sprint Championships, hoping a better-prepared body can finally deliver a performance that matches her ambition after a year defined more by sheer perseverancecontinued effort despite difficulty純粹的堅持不懈 than by results.

中文翻譯

香港的主場捧盃希望在11月7日至8日世界三項鐵人賽重返中環海濱時黯然失色。本地名將Bailee Brown在主場觀眾面前錄得令人失望的表現，排名遠落後於奪得本季第三個世界盃金牌的俄羅斯選手Diana Isakova。對Brown而言，這個成績只是她形容為「無情」的一季中，最新的一章。

這位24歲選手的2026年賽季幾乎從一開始就屢遭挫折。她先是在香港一場賽事後染上大腸桿菌及諾羅病毒的雙重感染，接著在越野跑時經歷了一場艱辛的考驗，導致手指嚴重受損，需要接受四次手術修補。隨後的骨應力損傷更令她被迫停訓數週。她的教練Andrew Wright坦然承認，回想起來，這次骨應力損傷完全可以避免，並表示自己對訓練安排過於嚴苛負有部分責任。

儘管歷盡傷患折騰，Brown堅稱自己仍未失去堅定不移的決心去繼續征戰。她形容三項鐵人是「殘酷無情」的運動，但表示自己仍然熱愛這項嚴苛考驗她的運動。Brown現將前往約旦，在亞洲短距離錦標賽為賽季畫上句號，希望在身體狀態更好的情況下，終能交出一個配得上她野心的成績——在這一年裡，純粹的堅持不懈比成績本身更能定義她。
