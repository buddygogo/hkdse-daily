English

Acting Housing Secretary Michael Wong has defended the government's plan to set up a new Public Housing Building Control Sectiona new government office created to oversee building safety on public housing projects公共房屋建築監管科 this month, pushing back against lawmakers who warn the reform merely adds another layer of bureaucracy rather than fixing the real problem. The new section will take over from the Independent Checking Unitthe body responsible for inspecting building and maintenance work, now being replaced獨立查核組, which an official inquiry into the deadly Tai Po fire named as the arm of government bearing the greatest responsibility for the disaster.

Wong told lawmakers the Housing Department and the Buildings Department already enforce identical statutory standardsexactly the same legally required rules and benchmarks完全相同的法定標準 under the Buildings Ordinance, so the new section would not create a genuinely separate set of rules; instead, the Buildings Department will second a senior officer into the section to keep technical oversight consistent. He argued this arrangement closes the specific supervisory gapa weak point in the system where no one is properly checking that work is done safely監管缺口 the inquiry identified, rather than simply duplicating existing checks.

Several lawmakers remained sceptical, noting that residents' legal representatives had earlier proposed folding the discredited unit directly into the Buildings Department instead of creating a new parallel structurea separate system that exists alongside another one doing a similar job平行架構, which they feared could blur lines of accountabilitythe obligation to accept responsibility and explain one’s actions問責性 if something again went wrong. Wong said the government would monitor the new section's performance closely and remained open to further structural adjustmentschanges to the way an organisation or system is built and organised架構調整 if gaps persisted, but insisted immediate action was needed given the scale of the Tai Po tragedy.

中文翻譯

署理房屋局局長黃偉綸為政府計劃於本月成立新的公共房屋建築監管科辯護，反駁有議員質疑今次改革只是新增一層官僚架構，未能真正解決問題。新設立的監管科將取代獨立查核組，而一項就大埔致命火災展開的官方調查報告指出，該查核組是政府部門之中須承擔最大責任的一環。

黃偉綸向議員表示，房屋署與屋宇署一直依據《建築物條例》執行完全相同的法定標準，因此新設立的監管科並不會另訂一套截然不同的規則；相反，屋宇署會調派一名高級官員到該科，以確保技術監管保持一致。他指出，此安排正是為了堵塞調查報告所指出的特定監管缺口，而非單純重複現有的檢查工作。

多名議員仍然表示懷疑，指出受災居民的法律代表早前已建議將該信譽受損的部門直接併入屋宇署，而非另設平行架構，擔心一旦日後再出事故，將難以釐清問責性。黃偉綸表示，政府會密切監察新監管科的表現，若監管缺口持續存在，仍會考慮進一步架構調整，但強調鑑於大埔火災的災情規模，政府必須立即採取行動。
