English

It is widely recognised that artificial intelligence is reshapingto change the form or nature of something重塑 classrooms around the world, and Hong Kong schools are now being asked to catch up in earnest. Under the Education Bureau’s newly released Blueprint for Digital Education Development, all publicly funded schools must integrateto combine one thing with another so they work as a whole融入 digital education into their school development plans for the 2026-27 academic year, alongside a fresh AI literacy learning framework built around three guiding principles: learning AI well, using it effectively, and using it responsibly.

To support this transition, the bureau has disbursed a one-off grant of $500,000 to every publicly funded school under a three-year funding programme aimed at empowering learning and teaching through AI. Teachers, meanwhile, will be required to complete no fewer than 30 hours of digital education training within each three-year professional development cycle, with the bureau pledging to offer some 50,000 training places annually. Nevertheless, some educators have voiced reservationsdoubts or concerns about a plan or idea保留意見 about whether such training can be delivered at scale without overburdening already stretched teaching staff.

By the same token, proponents argue that equipping the next generation with genuine AI literacy is no longer optional but essential, given how pervasiveexisting or spreading widely throughout a place or group無處不在的 the technology has already become in daily life. Whether this ambitious blueprint succeeds will depend, in no small part, on how effectively schools translate policy directives into meaningful classroom practice.

中文翻譯

普遍認為，人工智能正在重塑全球各地的課堂，香港學校現正被要求認真急起直追。根據教育局最新公布的《數碼教育發展藍圖》，所有公帑資助學校必須在2026-27學年將數碼教育融入學校發展計劃，同時配合一套嶄新的人工智能素養學習框架，圍繞三大指導原則建構：學好人工智能、善用人工智能及負責任地使用人工智能。

為支持這項轉變，教育局已向每所公帑資助學校發放50萬元一次性資助，作為一項三年資助計劃的一部分，旨在透過人工智能增強教與學。同時，教師須在每個三年專業發展周期內完成不少於30小時的數碼教育培訓，教育局承諾每年提供約50,000個培訓名額。然而，有部分教育工作者對於能否在不令已經人手緊絀的教學人員百上加斤的情況下大規模推行培訓表達保留意見。

同樣道理，支持者認為，裝備下一代真正的人工智能素養已不再是可有可無，而是必不可少，鑑於這項技術在日常生活中已變得如此無處不在。這套具野心的藍圖能否成功，在很大程度上將取決於學校能否有效地將政策指示轉化為有意義的課堂實踐。
