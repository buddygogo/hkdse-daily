Under the Education Bureau’s newly released Blueprint for Digital Education Development, all publicly funded schools must integrateto combine one thing with another so they work as a whole融入 digital education into their school development plans for the 2026-27 academic year, alongside a fresh AI literacy learning framework built around three guiding principles: learning AI well, using it effectively, and using it responsibly.

To support this transition, the bureau has disbursed a one-off grant of $500,000 to every publicly funded school under a three-year funding programme aimed at empowering learning and teaching through AI. Teachers, meanwhile, will be required to complete no fewer than 30 hours of digital education training within each three-year professional development cycle, with the bureau pledging to offer some 50,000 training places annually.
