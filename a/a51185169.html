English

Hong Kong's High Court has sentenced a 19-year-old to eight years in prison for carrying out a corrosive-substance attackan assault in which a chemical such as acid is thrown or poured onto a victim腐蝕性物質襲擊 on a 36-year-old Singaporean tourist in June last year. Mr Justice Andrew Chan Hing-wai told the defendant, who had already entered a guilty pleaa formal admission of criminal responsibility made in court before trial認罪, that the attack had left the victim with burns that would cause a "lifelong impact" on her health and confidence, and that the case was serious enough to merit a lengthy custodial term.

The judge fixed the starting pointthe base term a judge selects for an offence before any adjustment量刑起點 at twelve years, reflecting the premeditated nature of the assault, before granting a one-third sentencing discounta reduction in punishment granted in exchange for an early admission of guilt刑期扣減 for the early admission. Defence counsel had argued that a lengthy term risked undermining the teenager's rehabilitation prospectsthe likelihood that an offender can be reformed and reintegrated into society更生前景, while prosecutors stressed the lasting harm caused by the attack.

The case has reignited debate over how the courts should balance deterrent sentencingpunishment designed mainly to discourage others from committing similar crimes具懲戒作用的判刑 against the reform of young offenders, particularly where visitors are targeted. Victim-support groups have called for tougher sentencing guidelinesofficial standards judges follow when deciding the length of a punishment判刑指引 for attacks involving corrosive substances, warning that lenient outcomes could erode traveller trustthe degree of trust that overseas visitors place in the safety of a city they travel to旅客信任 in the city as a safe place to visit.

中文翻譯

香港高等法院判處一名十九歲青年入獄八年，罪名是去年六月向一名三十六歲新加坡女遊客潑灑腐蝕性液體，犯下腐蝕性物質襲擊。被告早前已認罪，法官陳慶偉告知被告，受害人身上的燒傷將對她的健康及自信造成「終身影響」，案情亦嚴重得足以判處長期監禁。

法官訂立量刑起點為十二年，以反映案件的預謀性質，再因被告提早認罪而給予三分之一的刑期扣減。辯方律師曾指出，漫長刑期可能損害這名少年的更生前景，而控方則強調襲擊造成的傷害影響深遠。

本案再次引發社會討論：法庭應如何在具懲戒作用的判刑與年輕犯人的改造之間取得平衡，特別是當受害者為訪港旅客時。受害人支援團體要求當局訂立更嚴厲的判刑指引，以處理涉及腐蝕性物質的襲擊案件，並警告輕判可能削弱外界對本港作為安全旅遊目的地的旅客信任。
