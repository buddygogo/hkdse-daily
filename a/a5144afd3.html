English

Hong Kong will next week host the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation Finance Ministers' Meeting for the first time, bringing roughly five hundred officials from the grouping's twenty-one member economiesthe twenty-one Pacific Rim economies that together make up the APEC forum亞太經合組織二十一個成員體 to the Convention and Exhibition Centre in Wan Chai. Financial Secretary Paul Chan has framed the gathering as a chance to showcase the city's convening powera place or institution's ability to bring together influential people or organisations to discuss shared issues號召各方匯聚的能力, even as regional economies navigate persistent trade tensions.

Ahead of the two-day meeting on October 20 and 21, Chan outlined four priorities he hopes the forum will address: fiscal policies that safeguard ordinary livelihoodsgovernment tax and spending decisions designed to protect the everyday financial wellbeing of citizens保障民生的財政政策, financing for digital infrastructure, inclusive financefinancial services made accessible and affordable to groups who are usually excluded, such as the poor or small businesses普惠金融, and the cultivation of young financial talent. He argued Hong Kong is well placed to contribute on each front, citing the city's experience in green bond issuancethe sale of bonds whose proceeds are reserved for environmentally friendly projects綠色債券發行 and its ambition to deepen regional cooperation on artificial intelligence.

Officials describe the meeting as more than a diplomatic formality: it is also meant to reinforce Hong Kong's claim to remain a credible financial intermediarya trusted institution or city that channels money and deals between different parties可信的金融中介 even as supply chains and capital flows realign around shifting tariffs. Whether the city can translate a successful hosting into lasting policy influencethe power to shape the decisions and rules that governments or institutions eventually adopt政策影響力, rather than simply a diplomatic photo opportunity, is likely to be judged over the months that follow.

中文翻譯

香港下週將首次主辦亞太經合組織財長會議,屆時將有約五百名官員,來自組織屬下二十一個成員體,雲集灣仔香港會議展覽中心。財政司司長陳茂波將是次聚會形容為一個機會,藉以展現本港的號召各方匯聚的能力,即使區內經濟體仍須應對持續的貿易緊張局勢。

在十月二十及二十一日為期兩天的會議舉行前,陳茂波提出四項他寄望會議能夠處理的優先事項:保障民生的財政政策、數碼基建融資、普惠金融,以及培育年青金融人才。他認為香港在每一方面均具備優勢,並舉出本港在綠色債券發行方面的經驗,以及深化區域人工智能合作的雄心。

官員形容是次會議不僅僅是一場外交禮節:其目的亦在於鞏固香港作為可信的金融中介的地位,即使供應鏈與資金流向正隨關稅變化而重新調整。至於香港能否將是次成功主辦,轉化為持久的政策影響力,而非僅屬一次外交層面的擺拍機會,相信須待日後數月方能見真章。
