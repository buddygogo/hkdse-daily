English

Samsung Electronics is reportedly winding downgradually bringing an operation to an end逐步結束 its home appliance business in Hong Kong, months after the South Korean giant quietly pulled large appliances from shelves across mainland China. Local outlets found refrigerators, washing machines and air conditioners listed as “sold out” on Samsung’s Hong Kong website, with no option to add them to a shopping cart, while phones, tablets, televisions and monitors remained unaffectednot changed or influenced by something未受影響. The report traces back to Korea Economic TV, which cited industry sources saying the local unit began preparing the move one to two months ago.

Samsung’s Hong Kong office has stopped short of confirming a full exita withdrawal from a market or activity澈出, saying only that it is “continually reviewing” its product line-up to match shifting consumer preferenceschanges in what buyers want to purchase消費者偏好變化. Some customer-service staff have told reporters the gaps are a temporary stock shortagean insufficient supply of goods存貨不足 rather than a market withdrawal, and a handful of retailers still list Samsung fridges and washers for sale, leaving shoppers and distributors with conflicting signals about the brand’s future in the city.

If confirmed, the pullback would reshape Hong Kong’s roughly HK$15.8 billion home appliance market, where imported, premium-priced models have historically commanded strong demand. Rival brands and local distributors stand to pick up market share, while households that already own Samsung appliances now face uncertainty over future after-sales supportrepair and maintenance service provided once a product is bought售後支援. The episode also illustrates a wider corporate strategy shifta change in a company's overall business approach企業策略轉向 among global electronics makers, several of which are narrowing their focus toward mobile devices and display technology rather than competing in a crowded, low-margin appliance market where local and mainland brands increasingly undercut established names on price.

中文翻譯

有報道指三星電子正逐步結束其香港家電業務，此前這間韓國巨企已靜悄悄地將大型家電撤出中國內地市場。本地傳媒發現，雪揃、洗衣機及冷氣機在三星香港網站上均顯示「售罄」，且無法加入購物車，而手機、平板、電視及顯示器業務則未受影響。報道源頭為韓國經濟電視台，引述業界消息指本地分公司早於一兩個月前已開始部署此次行動。

三星香港辦事處未有正式確認會全面撤出市場，僅表示正「持續檢討」產品組合，以配合消費者偏好變化。部分客戶服務人員向記者表示，貨架空缺純粹是暫時性存貨不足，並非退出市場，而部分零售商仍有出售三星雪揃及洗衣機，令消費者與分銷商對品牌在本港的前景感到混亂。

若消息屬實，此次收縮將重塑本港估值約一百五十八億港元的家電市場，當中進口、高價型號向來需求強勁。競爭品牌及本地分銷商有望藉機搶佔市場份額，而已購入三星家電的家庭則要面對日後售後支援的不明朗因素。事件亦反映全球電子生產商一個更廣泛的企業策略轉向：不少品牌正將重心收窄至手機及顯示技術，而非繼續在競爭激烈、利潤微薄的家電市場中，與愈來愈多以低價搶客的本地及內地品牌硬碰。
