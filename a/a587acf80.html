English

Kai Tak Hospital, now Hong Kong's largest public hospitalthe biggest government-run hospital in the city全港最大公立醫院, is set to take its next step in a phased openinga gradual launch carried out in stages分階段啟用 when specialist outpatient clinics begin receiving patients on October 26, according to hospital chief executive Dr Eric Cheung. The facility opened its doors on October 5 with a Family Medicine Integrated Centre handling around 350 consultations a day, and officials say the coming phase will mark a significant scaling-up of clinical capacitya large increase in how many patients can be treated擴大臨床服務量.

Under the new arrangement, follow-up patientspatients returning for a repeat consultation覆診病人 from surgery, neurosurgery and clinical oncology will be the first to be transferred from Queen Elizabeth Hospitalmoved from the older hospital in Yau Ma Tei由伊利沙伯醫院轉介, with around 500 specialist attendances expected each day once the phase begins. A further expansion covering medicine clinics is due by mid-November, and the Hospital Authority projects that daily follow-up attendances across all specialties could reach roughly 1,800 by the end of the year as the caseload migratesthe volume of patients gradually shifts elsewhere服務量轉移 from the ageing Queen Elizabeth site.

Health officials say the staggered rollout is designed to avoid overwhelming new staff and systems while the hospital beds down its operations, though some clinicians have questioned whether the timeline leaves enough buffer capacityspare capacity kept in reserve for unexpected demand緩衝量 for unexpected surges in demand. For patients used to attending Queen Elizabeth Hospital for years, the transfer also means adjusting to an unfamiliar campus, prompting calls for clearer travel information to ease the transition periodthe time during which a change is being carried out過渡期 as services gradually relocate to Kai Tak.

中文翻譯

啟德醫院現為全港最大公立醫院，院方正準備踏入分階段啟用的下一步，專科門診將於十月二十六日開始接收病人，院長張偉麟醫生表示。醫院已於十月五日率先啟用普通科╱家庭醫學綜合中心，每日提供約三百五十個診症名額，院方指即將展開的新階段，將意味著擴大臨床服務量的重要一步。

在新安排下，外科、神經外科及臨床腫瘤科的覆診病人將會是首批由伊利沙伯醫院轉介的病人，預計新階段展開後，每日將有約五百個專科門診診症名額。另一波涵蓋內科門診的擴展，預計於十一月中展開，醫院管理局預測，隨著服務量轉移離開老化的伊利沙伯醫院舊址，到年底各專科的每日覆診人次或可達約一千八百人次。

衛生官員表示，分階段推行是為免新員工及系統在短時間內不勝負荷，讓醫院逐步穩定運作，不過部分臨床醫生質疑，這個時間表是否留有足夠緩衝量，以應付需求突然激增的情況。對於多年來習慣到伊利沙伯醫院求診的病人而言，轉介亦意味著需要適應一個陌生的院址，有意見促請當局提供更清晰的交通資訊，以紓緩服務逐步遷往啟德期間的過渡期。
