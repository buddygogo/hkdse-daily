English

Hang Lung Group, one of Hong Kong's most prominent property developers, has confirmed a significant leadership transitiona change from one state or stage to another過渡；轉變 at the top of the company. Weber Lo officially stepped down as Chief Executive Officer and executive director on 1 October, handing the role to Leo Tsoi Tak-lun, a veteran of more than three decades in retail and business management across Asia, who had joined the firm the previous month as CEO-designate to ensure a smooth handoverthe act of transferring control or responsibility交接；交班.

It is widely argued that such carefully staged successions reflect a broader trend among Hong Kong's family-controlled conglomerates, which have faced mounting pressure to future-proofto make something able to withstand future developments or needs為未來做好準備；具前瞻性 their leadership structures amid a challenging property market. Lo, who had led the group through a lengthy period of retail headwindsdifficult conditions that hinder progress in the retail sector零售業的不利因素 and shifting consumer habits, will remain with the company for a further year as Advisor to the Chair, lending continuity even as day-to-day decisions pass to his successor.

Nevertheless, analysts caution that leadership change alone will not resolve the structural challenges facing Hong Kong's real estate sector, including elevated interest rates and subdued mainland tourist spending. By the same token, some investors view Tsoi's extensive retail background as a potentially favourableadvantageous or beneficial有利的 sign, given the group's heavy reliance on shopping mall revenue. Critics contend that genuine recovery will depend less on who occupies the corner office than on whether the broader economy can regain momentumto start moving forward successfully again重新獲得動力；復甦.

中文翻譯

恒隆集團作為香港最具代表性的地產發展商之一，確認公司最高層出現重大的領導層過渡。羅義德於十月一日正式卸任行政總裁及執行董事一職，將大任交予蔡德銓——一位在亞洲零售及商業管理領域擁有逾三十年經驗的資深人士，他於上月已以候任行政總裁身份加入公司，確保交接工作順利進行。

普遍認為，這種精心安排的接班程序反映香港家族企業的廣泛趨勢——它們在充滿挑戰的地產市場下，正面臨日益增加的壓力，須為其領導架構未來做好準備。羅義德曾帶領集團走過一段長時間的零售業不利因素及消費習慣轉變的時期，他將再留任公司一年，擔任主席顧問，即使日常決策已交由其接任者負責，亦能提供延續性。

然而，分析師警告，單靠領導層更替無法解決香港地產業面對的結構性挑戰，包括偏高的利率及疲弱的內地旅客消費。同樣道理，部分投資者認為蔡德銓豐富的零售背景可能是一個有利的訊號，因為集團高度依賴商場租金收入。評論者則認為，真正的復甦與誰坐在高層辦公室的關係不大，反而取決於整體經濟能否重新獲得動力。
