Legendary K-pop group BigBang are set for a triumphant comeback toura concert tour marking an artist's return after a period of absence復出巡演 to Hong Kong, with four nights scheduled at the newly built Kai Tak Stadium as part of their 2026–2027 world tour, "XX: Cosmos." It is widely argued that BigBang's continued drawing power reflects the broader resilience of first-generation K-pop groups, whose appeal now spans multiple generations. Long-time fandoman organised community of devoted fans surrounding a particular artist or franchise粉絲文化 members, many now in their thirties and forties, are joined by younger listeners discovering the group retrospectively through social media clips. By the same token, this phenomenon has fuelled a wider nostalgia economythe commercial demand generated by consumers' fondness for the past懷舊經濟, in which reunion tours by veteran acts consistently sell out arenas worldwide.

Nevertheless, the concerts also serve as a testament to Hong Kong's continued status as a regional touring hub, with the new stadium's venue infrastructurethe physical facilities and systems that support large-scale events場地基建 helping to attract major international acts. The announcement has, however, sparked familiar debate over high ticket prices and the risk of ticket scalpingthe illegal or unethical resale of tickets at inflated prices黃牛炒票. Even so, for many fans, the chance to relive a defining era of K-pop in person is expected to outweigh such concerns entirely.

中文翻譯

傳奇韓流組合BigBang即將以一場令人振奮的復出巡演重返香港,作為2026至2027年「XX: Cosmos」世界巡演的一部分,將連續四晚進駐新建成的啓德體育園主場館。會員優先售票將於本週一開始,由於組合出道二十年後人氣依然不減,一般門票需求預料將會相當熾熱。

輿論普遍認為,BigBang持續的號召力,正好反映第一代韓流組合展現出的頑強生命力,其吸引力更橫跨多個世代。長期支持的粉絲文化成員,如今不少已屆三、四十歲,加上透過社交媒體短片認識組合的年輕聽眾加入,令支持者陣容更見壯大。同樣地,這種現象亦助長了更廣泛的懷舊經濟——不同地區的資深組合重組巡演,總能一再令場館迅速售罄。

然而,這次演唱會亦見證了香港繼續作為地區巡演樞紐的地位,新落成體育園的場地基建有助吸引更多國際級演出進駐。消息公布後亦引來慣常的討論,包括門票價格高昂,以及黃牛炒票的風險。即使如此,對許多樂迷而言,能夠親身重溫這段韓流經典年代的機會,預料將遠遠抵銷上述種種憂慮。
