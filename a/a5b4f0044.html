English

ReThink HK 2026, now in its seventh edition, opened its two-day run at the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre on Thursday, conveningto bring people together for a formal meeting or event召集 more than 10,000 attendees, 500 speakers and 350 sustainability solution providers from around the world. Miss Diane Wong, Under Secretary for Environment and Ecology, opened the event by outlining Hong Kong's latest climate priorities, while overseas pavilions from Austria, France, Spain, Japan and Sweden underscored the event's international scopethe range or extent of something across different countries國際規模.

This year's programme was restructured around five thematic zones — Cities, Energy, Impact, Innovation and Resources — reflecting an attempt to translateto convert something from one form into another, more practical form轉化 broad climate commitments into concrete, operational results. It is widely argued that Hong Kong is well placed to serve as a regional hub for such discussions, given its position as a financial centre with close links to both mainland China and international markets. Sessions at the Innovation Zone showcased AI-driven sustainability tools and university spin-outs, while the Resources Zone focused on circular economy solutions across Asia's manufacturing and supply chains.

Nevertheless, sceptics contend that large sustainability conferences can risk becoming exercises in rhetoricactivities focused on fine words rather than concrete action流於空談 unless commitments made on stage are matched by measurable follow-through. By the same token, organisers argue that the event's restructured format—placing a greater emphasis onspecial importance or attention given to something著重於 implementation rather than ambition alone—is a direct response to that criticism. As regulatory pressure on companies to disclose climate impact intensifies globally, many argue that platforms like ReThink HK will only grow in importance as venues where businesses, governments and NGOs can coordinateto organise different elements so that they work together effectively協調 their next steps.

中文翻譯

「ReThink HK 2026」已踏入第七屆,本週四於香港會議展覽中心展開兩日活動,召集來自世界各地逾一萬名參加者、五百位講者及三百五十家可持續發展方案供應商。環境及生態局副局長黃淑嫻在開幕致辭中,概述香港最新的氣候政策重點,而來自奧地利、法國、西班牙、日本及瑞典的海外展館,則凸顯了活動的國際規模。

今年的活動重新規劃為五個主題展區——城市、能源、影響力、創新及資源——反映主辦方嘗試將廣泛的氣候承諾轉化為具體、可操作的成果。普遍認為,憑藉作為金融中心、與內地及國際市場關係緊密的地位,香港具備優勢成為此類討論的區域樞紐。創新展區的多個環節展示人工智能驅動的可持續方案及大學初創項目,資源展區則聚焦亞洲製造業及供應鏈的循環經濟方案。

然而,懷疑論者認為,大型可持續發展會議若未能將台上承諾轉化為可衡量的落實成果,便有淪為流於空談的風險。同樣地,主辦方認為,今年活動重新編排,更著重於落實執行而非單純談論願景,正是對上述批評的直接回應。隨著全球企業披露氣候影響的監管壓力日益加劇,不少人認為,像ReThink HK這樣的平台將變得愈發重要,成為企業、政府及非政府組織協調下一步行動的場所。
