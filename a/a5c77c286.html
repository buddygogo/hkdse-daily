English

Hong Kong's Primary Three pupils recorded their worst-ever performance in basic mathematics this year, according to results from the territory-wide System Assessment released on Thursday, even as their English results climbed to a fresh record. Only 84.7 per cent of pupils met the required basic competencythe minimum level of skill considered acceptable基本能力水平 threshold in maths, a drop of 0.7 percentage points from last year and the lowest reading since the assessment was introduced in 2004. English, by contrast, posted its strongest showing yet, with 86.3 per cent of pupils reaching the benchmark, a jump of 3.1 points that comfortably eclipsedsurpassed or exceeded by a large amount大幅超越 the previous high set only last year.

This year's assessment, which tested only Primary Three and Secondary Three students, is designed to flagidentify or draw attention to a problem識別 pupils falling behind in core subjects rather than to rank schools, and officials were quick to stress that the figures "remained stable" overall and broadly matched patterns seen in previous years. Education experts were less reassured about the mathematics figures specifically, suggesting the slidea gradual decline in performance下滑 may be a lingering consequence of pandemic-era disruption to classroom routines, with some pupils' study habits and intrinsic motivationan inner drive to do something for its own sake內在動機 for numerical subjects never fully recovering even years after schools reopened.

The divergencea difference or split between two things分歧 between the two subjects has puzzled some educators, who note that English teaching has benefited from targeted remedial supportextra help given to struggling learners補救支援 and increased exposure through digital media, while mathematics instruction has seen comparatively little structural reform. Parent groups have called on the Education Bureau to commission a fuller review of numeracythe ability to understand and work with numbers數字能力 teaching methods, warning that early gaps in mathematical foundations tend to compoundbecome worse over time by accumulating累積惡化 as pupils progress through the curriculum, making early intervention far more cost-effective than remedial teaching later on.

中文翻譯

根據星期四公布的全港性系統評估結果，香港小三學生今年的基本數學能力錄得歷來最差成績，與此同時，英文成績卻攀上新高。僅84.7%學生達到數學科的基本能力水平要求，較去年下跌0.7個百分點，為該評估自二零零四年推行以來的最低紀錄。相比之下，英文科表現則錄得歷來最佳成績，86.3%學生達標，較去年上升3.1個百分點，輕易大幅超越去年才剛創下的紀錄。

今年的評估只涵蓋小三及中三學生，目的是識別學習進度落後於核心科目的學生，而非為學校排名；官方強調，整體數字「維持穩定」，與過往年度的模式大致相符。不過，教育專家對數學科的數字則未敢過於樂觀，認為這次下滑可能是疫情期間課堂常規受擾所留下的後遺症，部分學生對數字科目的學習習慣及內在動機，即使學校復課多年後仍未完全恢復。

兩科成績之間的分歧令部分教育界人士感到困惑，他們指出英文科教學近年得益於針對性的補救支援，以及數碼媒體帶來的更多接觸機會，但數學科教學卻鮮有結構性改革。家長團體要求教育局就數字能力教學方法展開全面檢討，並警告數學基礎上的早期落差，隨著學生升級會不斷累積惡化，故此早期介入遠比日後補課更具成本效益。
