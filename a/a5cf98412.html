English

As K-pop cements its status as a global, multibillion-dollar cultural exporta form of entertainment or art that is successfully sold and popularised in other countries文化輸出, commentators have increasingly asked why neither Cantopop nor Mandopop has produced an act with the same global ubiquitythe quality of being present or recognised almost everywhere全球普及度 as groups such as Blackpink or Stray Kids. It is widely argued that South Korea's success rests on a highly systematised idol training systema structured, long-term programme used to develop performers before their public debut偶像訓練制度, paired with aggressive, coordinated marketing across international markets from its earliest stages.

Nevertheless, Hong Kong's own Cantopop scene has been undergoing a quiet but notable revival closer to home. Large-scale concerts injected an estimated HK$2.2 billion into the local economy between 2023 and 2024, while grassroots cultural eventscommunity-driven events organised informally rather than by major commercial institutions草根文化活動 such as DJ nights have drawn crowds of thousands, with one collective progressing from small venues to supporting international acts at sold-out shows. More than a thousand new Cantopop tracks were released in 2025 alone, and homegrown artists have come to dominate local streaming charts, a marked change from a decade ago when Cantopop was widely dismissed by younger listeners as unfashionable.

By the same token, critics caution against overstating the parallel between the two phenomena. Whereas K-pop's success is underpinned by deliberate global positioninga strategy consciously designed to appeal to international audiences from the outset刻意的全球定位, Hong Kong's revival remains, for now, a largely domestically driven phenomenona trend whose momentum comes mainly from the local market rather than international demand以本地市場為主導的現象. Some industry observers argue that this organic, community-rooted growth may prove more sustainable in the long run, even if it never achieves the sheer international scale of its Korean counterpart; others insist that without deliberate investment in overseas distribution channelsthe networks and platforms used to sell or stream content internationally海外發行渠道, Cantopop's revival risks remaining a largely local success story.

中文翻譯

隨著K-pop鞏固其作為全球、價值數十億美元文化輸出的地位，愈來愈多評論員質疑，為何粵語流行曲及國語流行曲未能孕育出像Blackpink或Stray Kids般具備同等全球普及度的組合。普遍認為，南韓的成功建基於高度系統化的偶像訓練制度，並由出道初期已配合進取且有協調的國際市場推廣。

然而，香港本地的粵語流行曲樂壇近年正悄然卻明顯地復甦。大型演唱會在2023至2024年間估計為本地經濟注入約22億港元，而草根文化活動如DJ之夜等亦吸引數以千計的觀眾，其中一個組合更由小型場地逐步發展至在售罄的國際演出中擔任嘉賓。單在2025年，已有逾千首新粵語流行曲面世，本土歌手亦主宰了本地串流排行榜，與十年前粵語流行曲被年輕聽眾普遍視為過時的情況相比，可謂顯著轉變。

同樣道理，評論者警告不應過分誇大兩者之間的相似之處。K-pop的成功以刻意的全球定位為基礎，而香港的復甦現時仍主要是以本地市場為主導的現象。部分業內人士認為，這種有機、植根於社區的增長從長遠而言可能更為持續，即使其永遠無法達到南韓同業的國際規模；另一些人則堅持，若不對海外發行渠道作出刻意投資，粵語流行曲的復甦恐終究只是一個局限於本地的成功故事。
