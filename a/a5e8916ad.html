English

Hong Kong's financial regulator is exploring new rules that would require banks to disclose more information about how they fund climate-related projectsinitiatives connected to mitigating or adapting to climate change氣候相關項目, according to a report published on 21 September. It is widely argued that greater transparency is essential if the city's sustainable finance ambitions are to be taken seriously by international investors. The Hong Kong Monetary Authority plans to conduct a territory-wide survey next year examining how lenders are applying the existing sustainable finance taxonomyan official classification system distinguishing genuinely green investments from others可持續金融分類法.

The move comes amid growing scrutiny of so-called greenwashingthe practice of overstating or falsely claiming the environmental benefits of an activity漂綠, whereby financial institutions overstate the environmental benefits of the projects they fund. Nevertheless, banking industry representatives have cautioned that overly stringent disclosure requirements could increase compliance coststhe expenses incurred in meeting regulatory requirements合規成本 and discourage smaller lenders from participating in the green finance market altogether. By the same token, environmental groups maintain that without mandatory and standardised reportingcompulsory reporting that follows a uniform, consistent format強制性及標準化匯報, it remains difficult to hold institutions accountable for their claims, undermining public confidenceweakening the trust the public places in an institution or system削弱公眾信心 in the sector as a whole.

The initiative forms part of a broader effort to position Hong Kong as a hub for climate finance in the Asia-Pacific region, complementing measures such as the Monetary Authority's ongoing consultation on decarbonisation strategiesplans aimed at reducing carbon emissions across an economy or sector去碳化策略 for the banking sector. Given that climate change poses mounting risks to the financial system, officials argue that closer scrutiny of how capital is allocated is no longer a peripheral concern but a central pillar of long-term economic resiliencethe capacity of an economy to withstand and recover from shocks經濟韌性.

中文翻譯

香港金融監管機構正研究新規定，要求銀行披露更多有關其如何資助氣候相關項目的資訊，一份於9月21日發表的報告指出。普遍認為，若要國際投資者認真看待本港發展可持續金融的雄心，提高透明度至關重要。香港金融管理局計劃於明年進行一項全港性調查，審視銀行如何應用現有的可持續金融分類法。

此舉正值所謂漂綠行為日益受到關注之際，即金融機構誇大其資助項目的環保效益。然而，銀行業界代表警告，過於嚴格的披露要求可能增加合規成本，甚至令小型銀行完全放棄參與綠色金融市場。同樣道理，環保團體則堅稱，若缺乏強制性及標準化的匯報，便難以追究機構對其聲稱的責任，從而削弱公眾對整個行業的信心。

此項措施是更廣泛工作的一部分，旨在將香港定位為亞太區氣候金融中心，並與金管局就銀行業去碳化策略進行的持續諮詢相輔相成。鑑於氣候變化對金融體系構成日益嚴重的風險，官員認為，密切監察資金分配方式已不再是次要問題，而是長遠經濟韌性的核心支柱。
