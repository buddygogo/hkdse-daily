English

The parents of a girl who died after a medical procedure at a public hospital have filed a civil suita legal case brought by one private party against another, or a public body, seeking compensation rather than criminal punishment民事訴訟 against the Hospital Authority, alleging negligence that led to their daughter's death nearly a year after the original incident. According to a writ of summonsthe formal document that officially starts a civil lawsuit in court傳訊令狀 filed at the District Courta Hong Kong court that handles civil claims above a certain value and some criminal cases, ranking below the High Court區域法院 last Thursday, Lai Sum-yuet, then aged four, was taken to Yan Chai Hospital in Tsuen Wan in May 2024 to have a head wound from a fall treated, a routine suturing procedurea medical procedure in which stitches are used to close a wound縫合程序 expected to take only a few minutes.

The writ states that once the brief procedure was completed, a nurse found that the child had lost consciousness and had no pulse, and that medical staff spent 18 minutes performing resuscitation before restoring her heartbeat following a cardiac arresta sudden stop in effective heart function, cutting off blood flow to the body心臟停頓. Lai never fully recovered and died in February this year, at the age of five. Her family has previously said they witnessed hospital staff pressing the girl's head firmly against a pillow during the procedure, a claim at the centre of separate criminal proceedings in which two medical staff members have been charged with the ill-treatment or neglect of a childa criminal offence covering wilful cruelty or culpable failure to care for a child in one's charge對兒童虐待或忽略罪.

The Hospital Authority has apologised for the family's loss but has maintained that staff followed established protocolsthe official, standard procedures that staff are trained and expected to follow既定程序 throughout the procedure, a position the lawsuit now directly challenges. The case has also been referred to the Coroner's Courta court that formally investigates the cause and circumstances of an unexplained or unnatural death死因裁判法庭 for a separate inquest into the circumstances of the child's death, running alongside both the criminal case and the newly filed civil claim. The writ does not specify the amount of damages being sought, but it marks the family's latest step in a long pursuit of accountability for what they describe as an entirely preventable tragedy.

中文翻譯

一名女童在公立醫院接受醫療程序後死亡,其父母已就院方疏忽,向醫院管理局提出民事訴訟,事件發生近一年後終告上法庭。根據上周四提交的傳訊令狀,案件已於區域法院展開法律程序。令狀指,黎心悅(譯名)在2024年5月,當時年僅四歲,因跌傷頭部被送往荃灣仁濟醫院,接受一項原本預計僅需數分鐘的常規縫合程序。

令狀指出,該簡短程序完成後,護士發現女童已失去知覺並無脈搏,醫護人員經過18分鐘的搶救,方令她在一次心臟停頓後恢復心跳。黎心悅自此未能完全康復,並於今年2月,年僅五歲時離世。其家人早前表示,曾目擊院方職員在進行程序期間,用力將女童的頭按向枕頭,此說法正是另一宗刑事案件的爭議核心——兩名醫護人員已被控對兒童虐待或忽略罪。

醫院管理局已就該家庭的喪女之痛致歉,但一直堅稱職員在整個程序中均遵從既定程序,而今次民事訴訟,正正直接挑戰這一說法。案件亦已轉交死因裁判法庭另行展開死因調查,與刑事案件及今次新提出的民事索償同步進行。令狀並未指明索償金額,但事件標誌着這家庭在追究一場他們形容為完全可以避免的悲劇責任路上,邁出的最新一步。
