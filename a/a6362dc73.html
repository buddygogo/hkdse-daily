English

Chief Executive John Lee Ka-chiu embarked onstarted out on a journey or task開始(旅程或任務) a four-day visit to Beijing and Shanghai on Wednesday, a trip the government says will deepen economic tiesstrengthen economic relationships加深經濟關係 between Hong Kong and the mainland at a moment of considerable geopolitical uncertaintyinstability in international politics affecting a region地緣政治不穩定. Speaking at the Beijing–Hong Kong Economic Cooperation Symposium, Mr Lee set out three priorities for the visit: innovation and technology, further financial liberalisationthe process of making rules or systems less restrictive自由化, and helping mainland enterprises venture abroadtake a risk by operating in a foreign market冒險拓展海外業務 through Hong Kong's international networks.

Central to this last goal is the government's GoGlobal Task Force, which Mr Lee said has already assisted 340 mainland companies in expanding into overseas markets, using Hong Kong as a springboard for their international ambitions. He is due to travel on to Shanghai on Thursday to attend the China International Import Expo, where he is expected to promote Hong Kong's role as a two-way conduita channel allowing flow in both directions雙向渠道 for trade and investment between the mainland and the rest of the world.

The trip follows remarks Mr Lee made earlier in the week at the Global Financial Leaders' Investment Summit in Hong Kong, where he said the city must rethink its playbookreconsider one's established strategy重新思考策略 and offer stabilitythe state of being steady and not easily changed穩定 amid shifting global trade patterns. He pointed to a new gold clearing and settlement system, developed jointly with the Shanghai Gold Exchange and due to begin operating in the first quarter of next year, as evidence of Hong Kong's ambition to become a global commodities hub. Analysts say the visit underscores Hong Kong's attempt to position itself as an indispensable superconnectoran intermediary that links different systems or regions together超級聯繫人 between East and West, even as some economists caution that leaning too heavily on mainland-driven growth could leave the city more exposed should cross-border ties ever cool.

中文翻譯

行政長官李家超星期三展開為期四天的北京及上海訪問行程,政府表示此行將加深香港與內地的經濟聯繫,正值國際局勢面臨相當大的地緣政治不穩定之際。李家超在京港經濟合作研討會上致辭,訂下此行三大重點:創新科技、進一步金融自由化,以及協助內地企業透過香港的國際網絡拓展海外業務。

這項目標的核心是政府的「出海支援任務小組」,李家超表示,該小組已協助340間內地企業拓展海外市場,以香港作為實現國際發展野心的跳板。他星期四將轉往上海,出席中國國際進口博覽會,預期將在會上推廣香港作為內地與世界各地貿易投資雙向渠道的角色。

此行緊接李家超早前在香港舉行的「全球金融領袖投資峰會」上的講話,他當時表示,面對全球貿易格局轉變,香港必須重新思考策略,並提供穩定的環境。他提到與上海黃金交易所合作開發的全新黃金中央結算系統,預計明年首季投入運作,證明香港有意成為全球商品交易樞紐。分析人士指出,此行凸顯香港力圖定位為東西方之間不可或缺的超級聯繫人,但部分經濟學者亦警告,過度依賴內地帶動的增長,一旦兩地關係轉冷,香港將更容易受到衝擊。
