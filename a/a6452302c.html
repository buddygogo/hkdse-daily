English

Hong Kong’s Centre for Health Protection (CHP) has confirmed that a 14-month-old girl with no prior history of significant illness is in critical conditionin a dangerous state requiring intensive medical care危殆 after contracting influenza A, having developed the rare and severe complication of acute necrotizing encephalopathya sudden, severe form of brain inflammation and tissue damage急性壞死性腦病 alongside multiorgan failure. Laboratory tests on a respiratory specimen confirmed infection with influenza A (H1), and investigators found no record of the toddler travelling outside Hong Kong during the incubation period, while she had not received this season’s influenza vaccine. Her case is the latest in a string of severe paediatric infections to strainput heavy pressure on a system’s resources造成壓力 the city’s public hospitals this autumn.

According to CHP controller Edwin Tsui, Hong Kong has now recorded 27 severe paediatric influenza cases since the season began in late June, with 20 patients already recovered and discharged, two in stable condition, three still critical and two confirmed dead. Although overall flu activity for the week of 18 to 24 October showed early signs of easinginitial indications that a situation is becoming less severe緩和的初步跡象, officials cautioned that transmission remains well above the seasonal baseline, meaning further severe cases cannot be ruled outexcluded as a possibility排除 in the weeks ahead.

Paediatricians have renewed their appeal for parents of infants and toddlers to arrange vaccination as early as possible, noting that very young children are especially susceptibleeasily affected or harmed by something容易受影響 to rare but devastating neurological complications such as encephalopathy once infected. Health officials stressed that prompt medical attentionfast action to see a doctor without delay及時求醫 at the first sign of high fever, seizures or unusual drowsiness can be decisive in preventing a mild infection from escalatingincreasing rapidly in seriousness惡化升級 into a life-threatening emergency. For HKDSE students, the case illustrates how even healthy infants without underlying conditions can face grave outcomes from a common seasonal virus, underscoringemphasising the importance of something突顯 the importance of early-childhood immunisation programmes.

中文翻譯

香港衛生防護中心證實，一名過往並無嚴重病史的14個月大女嬰，在感染甲型流感後情況危殆，她出現罕見而嚴重的併發症——急性壞死性腦病，並伴隨多重器官衰竭。女嬰的呼吸道樣本化驗證實感染甲型流感（H1亞型），調查人員並無發現她在潛伏期內離開香港的紀錄，而她亦未有接種本季流感疫苗。她的個案是本季一連串嚴重兒童感染個案之一，持續對本港公立醫院造成壓力。

衛生防護中心總監徐德義表示，自本季流感於六月底開始以來，香港已錄得27宗嚴重兒童感染個案，其中20名病人已康復出院，兩名情況穩定，三名仍然危殆，兩名已證實死亡。儘管十月十八日至二十四日期間的整體流感活動顯示緩和的初步跡象，官員警告病毒傳播程度仍遠高於季節性基線，意味著未來數週內仍未能排除出現更多嚴重個案的可能。

兒科醫生再次呼籲嬰幼兒的家長盡早安排子女接種疫苗，並指出年幼兒童一旦受感染，特別容易受影響，出現如腦病等罕見但嚴重的神經系統併發症。衛生官員強調，一旦出現高燒、抽搐或異常昏睡的初步徵狀，及時求醫可以是防止輕微感染惡化升級為致命危機的關鍵。對香港中學文憑考試學生而言，此案例說明即使健康、沒有潛在疾病的嬰兒，亦可能因常見的季節性病毒而面臨嚴重後果，這突顯了幼兒早期免疫接種計劃的重要性。
