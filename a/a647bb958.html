Secretary for Education Christine Choi announced on 18 September that a new scheme encouraging publicly funded primary and secondary schools to merge would be launched as early as the 2027/28 academic year. It is widely acknowledged that Hong Kong's declining birth ratethe ongoing fall in the number of babies born each year出生率下降 has placed considerable strain on smaller schools, with more than 120 aided and government primary schools having already trimmed the number ofreduced the quantity of削減……的數目 Primary One classes they offer. The scheme is intended to consolidate resourcescombine and streamline resources for greater efficiency整合資源 rather than force outright closures, allowing schools facing dwindling enrolment to pool staff, facilities and funding.

Nevertheless, the proposal has proven contentiousturned out to be a source of dispute or disagreement引起爭議 among educators and parents alike. Critics contend that mergers risk eroding the distinct charactergradually wearing away the unique identity侵蝕獨特特色 and community ties that many long-established schools have cultivated over decades. Some school sponsoring bodies have voiced concern that consolidation could be imposed too hastily, without adequate consultationsufficient discussion with relevant stakeholders beforehand充分諮詢 with staff and parents who fear job losses or disruption to students' learning environments. By the same token, supporters argue that maintaining chronically under-enrolled schools is unsustainableunable to be maintained at the current level over the long term不可持續的 and ultimately does students a disservicecauses harm despite good intentions對學生造成損害, as limited resources make it difficult to offer a broad curriculum.

For students themselves, the implications are considerable. A merger could mean a change of campus, new classmates, and an adjustment perioda span of time needed to become accustomed to new circumstances適應期 that some worry may affect academic performance and emotional wellbeing. Education Bureau officials have stressed that further details, including eligibility criteria and transitional supportassistance provided during a period of change過渡支援, will be announced in due course, and that affected schools will be given sufficient lead time to prepare.

中文翻譯

教育局局長蔡若蓮於9月18日宣布，一項鼓勵公帑資助小學及中學合併的新計劃最快將於2027/28學年推行。普遍認為，香港的出生率下降已令小型學校承受相當大的壓力，逾120所資助及官立小學已經削減小一班級的數目。該計劃旨在整合資源，而非強制學校徹底停辦，讓收生持續下降的學校能夠集中人手、設施及資金。

然而，建議在教育界及家長之間引起爭議。評論者認為，合併或會侵蝕許多歷史悠久學校數十年來建立的獨特特色及社區聯繫。部分辦學團體表示擔心，合併可能在未經與教職員及家長充分諮詢的情況下倉促推行，而他們擔心會失去工作或影響學生的學習環境。同樣道理，支持者則認為，長期維持收生嚴重不足的學校並不可持續，最終亦損害學生，因為有限的資源難以提供廣泛的課程。

對學生而言，影響不容忽視。合併或意味著轉換校園、認識新同學，並經歷一段適應期，有人擔心這可能影響學業成績及情緒健康。教育局官員強調，包括資格準則及過渡支援等進一步詳情將適時公布，而受影響學校將獲得充足的準備時間。
