English

The Hong Kong government announced on Friday that the minimum allowable wagethe lowest salary an employer is legally permitted to pay法定最低工資 for foreign domestic helpers will rise by 2.35 per cent, from HK$5,100 to HK$5,220 a month, with the new rate applying to all contracts signed on or after October 3. The food allowance that employers must provide will remain unchanged at no less than HK$1,236 a month. The adjustment, the latest in an annual reviewa yearly process of checking and updating something一年一度的檢討 that has become something of a ritual for the city's roughly 380,000 households employing live-in helpers, arrives as the cost of domestic help continues to climb alongside broader living expenses.

Officials said the revised figure took into account Hong Kong's overall economic performance, prevailing labour market conditions, employers' ability to pay, and the basic livelihood needsthe essential requirements for a person to live decently基本生活需要 of helpers themselves. The government stressed that contracts signed before the cut-off at the old rate would still be processed, provided applications reach the Immigration Department within a grace periodextra time allowed before a rule takes full effect寬限期. Unlike previous years, when adjustments were sometimes frozen amid economic headwinds, this year's rise, though modest, signals a continuation of incremental increasessmall, gradual rises over time逐步的小幅加幅 rather than a pause.

The announcement has nonetheless reignited a familiar tug-of-wara struggle between two sides pulling in opposite directions拉鋸戰 between stakeholders. Helper unions, which had lobbied for a far steeper rise to as much as HK$6,670 citing persistent inflation, dismissed the increase as inadequate, while employer associations warned that even incremental rises strain household budgetsthe amount of money a family has to spend家庭開支預算 already squeezed by a sluggish economy. For the tens of thousands of dual-income familieshouseholds where both partners earn a salary雙職家庭 and elderly households that depend on helpers for childcare and caregiving, the episode is a reminder of how deeply this workforce is woven into the fabric of domestic lifedeeply and inseparably part of everyday home life深深融入家庭生活之中 in the city.

中文翻譯

香港政府星期五宣布，外籍家庭傭工的法定最低工資將由每月五千一百元上調百分之二點三五至五千二百二十元，新工資水平適用於十月三日或之後簽訂的所有合約。僱主須提供的膳食津貼則維持不變，每月不少於一千二百三十六元。這次調整是一年一度檢討機制下的最新安排，對本港約三十八萬個聘用住家外傭的家庭而言已幾近成為慣例，而外傭成本亦隨著整體生活開支上升而持續攀升。

官員表示，新工資水平已考慮香港整體經濟表現、現行勞工市場狀況、僱主的負擔能力，以及外傭本身的基本生活需要。政府強調，於截止日期前以舊工資水平簽訂的合約仍會獲處理，惟申請須在寬限期內送達入境處。與過往部分年度因經濟逆風而工資凍結不同，今年的升幅雖然輕微，卻顯示工資持續小幅上調，而非再次停滯不前。

不過，今次公布亦重新燃起各方之間一場熟悉的角力。外傭工會一直以持續通脹為理由，爭取大幅加薪至每月六千六百七十元，故斥今次升幅並不足夠；僱主組織則警告，即使是輕微的加幅，亦會令本已因經濟疲弱而捉襟見肘的家庭開支百上加斤。對於數以萬計依靠外傭照顧子女及長者的雙職家庭和長者住戶而言，這次爭議提醒了大眾，這批勞動力早已深深融入本港家庭生活的日常肌理之中。
