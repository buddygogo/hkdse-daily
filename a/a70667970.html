English

Cantopop veteran Joey Yung Tso-yi choked up during a live-streamed announcement on Tuesday night, telling fans she had added a third date, January 18, 2027, to her forthcoming "AURA" concert series at Kai Tak Stadium. The additional show joins two dates already pencilled in for January 22 and 23, and came only hours before tickets for the original runthe dates for a show that were first announced or scheduled, before any extra ones were added原定場次 went on general sale.

For Yung, the moment carries weight beyond a simple scheduling update. The singer had spoken publicly as far back as last year about her hope of one day headlining a solo stadium showbeing the main performer of an entire concert held in a large stadium venue, rather than sharing the bill with others獨挑大樑主演體育館演唱會 at Kai Tak, having previously appeared there only as part of a joint billa concert programme shared between several different performers rather than led by just one聯合演出場次 with other Emperor Entertainment artists. Seeing an additional date added within hours of the original announcement appeared to confirm a level of fan demand she had clearly hoped for but could not guarantee.

Tickets for the three shows are priced between HK$680 and HK$1,680, and Yung suggested further dates were possible should momentum holdif the current strong level of public interest or demand continues without slowing down熱度持續不減. The episode also reflects a wider pattern in Hong Kong's concert economy, where stadium-scale bookingsconcerts large enough to fill an entire sports stadium rather than a smaller indoor arena體育館規模的演出檔期 at the 50,000-seat venue have become a yardstick local singers use to measure their own drawing powera performer's ability to attract a large paying audience號召力 against international acts.

中文翻譯

樂壇常青樹容祖兒週二晚在直播公布加場消息時一度哽咽,向歌迷透露將於「AURA」演唱會系列加開第三場,日期定於二零二七年一月十八日,地點同樣設於啟德體育園。新增場次是在原定一月二十二及二十三日兩場之上再加開,而公布時間僅在原定場次門票正式公開發售前數小時。

對容祖兒而言,這一刻的意義遠超一次單純的檔期更新。她早於去年已曾公開表示,希望終有一天能在啟德獨挑大樑主演體育館演唱會,因為她過往在該處演出,僅限於與英皇娛樂旗下其他藝人同台的聯合演出場次。在原定公布後僅數小時便需要加場,似乎印證了她一直盼望、卻未敢完全確信的歌迷熱度。

三場演唱會的門票定價介乎680至1680港元,容祖兒亦表示,若熱度持續不減,不排除會再加開場次。這次加場亦反映香港演唱會市場的一個更廣泛現象:在這個可容納五萬人的場館舉行體育館規模的演出檔期,已成為本地歌手用以衡量自身號召力、與國際級藝人一較高下的指標。
