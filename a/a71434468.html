English

Almost 2,000 customers of Hong Kong Airlines and two electrical-appliance retailers have received suspected scam calls after a string of data breachesincidents where protected information is accessed without permission資料外泄事件 exposed their personal details. Hong Kong Airlines notified the Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data on 2 October that 815 passengers who travelled between 29 September and 22 October had their names, phone numbers and flight information exposed. Retailers Yoho Hong Kong and Broadway Photo Supply separately reported similar leaks affecting hundreds more shoppers.

In each case, fraudsters used the leaked details to sound convincing, impersonatingpretending to be someone else in order to deceive傆装成 airline staff and claiming flights had been cancelled, or posing as couriers offering refunds for damaged goods. One Broadway customer received a scam call within an hour of buying an air fryer; a Yoho shopper was contacted the very next day about a supposedly faulty hair dryer. The Hong Kong Computer Emergency Response Team Coordination Centre said police confirmed one retireea person who has stopped working, typically due to age退休人士 alone lost HK$1.2 million after being talked into a bank transfer during one such call.

The privacy watchdog has received 23 complaints and 20 inquiries and has opened investigationsformal inquiries into the facts of an incident調查 into all three incidents. One information-technology expert suggested the almost identical scripts used across the three cases point to a possible shared supply-chain breacha security failure in a shared vendor or system used by several companies供應鏈漏洞 rather than three unconnected hacks, though this remains his personal assessment rather than a confirmed finding. Authorities are urging anyone contacted about a recent purchase or flight to verify the caller through official channels, refuse any pressured money transfer, and report suspicious calls to the anti-scam helplinea dedicated phone line for reporting suspected fraud防骗溜通專線 on 18222 before any money changes hands.

中文翻譯

香港航空及兩間電器零售商的近二千名顧客，在一連串資料外洩事件後接獲懷疑詐騙電話。香港航空於十月二日通知個人資料私隱專員公署，指有八百一十五名於九月二十九日至十月二十二日期間乘搭航班的乘客，姓名、電話號碼及航班資料均告外洩。零售商友和及百老匯則分別呈報類似事件，涉及數百名顧客。

在每宗個案中，騙徒均利用外洩資料增加可信度，假扮成航空公司職員，聲稱航班已取消，或假扮速遞員聲稱可為受損貨品提供退款。一名百老匯顧客在購買氣炸鍋後一小時內即接獲詐騙電話；一名友和顧客亦於翌日接獲聲稱吹風機有缺陷的來電。香港電腦保安事故協調中心表示，警方證實一名退休人士單獨損失一百二十萬港元，皆因被說服經電話進行銀行轉賬。

私隱專員公署已接獲二十三宗投訴及二十宗查詢，並已就三宗事件展開調查。一名資訊科技專家指出，三宗個案中騙徒所用對白幾乎一致，認為事件可能源於共同的供應鏈漏洞，而非三次互不相關的入侵事件，不過此說法仍屬其個人判斷，未獲正式證實。當局呼籲近期有購物或訂機票的市民，如接獲相關來電，應透過官方渠道核實身份，拒絕任何受壓下的轉賬要求，並在付款前致電防騙熱線18222舉報懷疑騙案。
