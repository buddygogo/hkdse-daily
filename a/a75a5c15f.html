English

Hong Kong's fresh graduates are facing an increasingly uncertain labour marketthe overall system of supply and demand for workers勞動市場, as artificial intelligence steadily absorbs the routine tasks that once served as a stepping stonean action or event that helps achieve a bigger goal踏腳石；過渡階段 into full-time careers. According to labour market experts, the number of full-time vacancies suitable for university graduates has plummetedto fall suddenly and sharply急劍下跌 from roughly 80,000 in 2022 to just 31,000 last year, a decline widely attributed to the rapid adoption of AI tools in offices across the city.

It is widely argued that administrative support, clerical duties and other entry-levelrelating to a job requiring little or no prior experience初級；入門級的 roles — historically the easiest point of entry for young job-seekers — are being systematically replaced by automation. One expert warned starkly that "it is no longer the case that a young person willing to learn and work hard will definitely find a job," underscoring how traditional assumptions about meritocracythe belief that success is earned through ability and effort精英制度；論功行賞的制度 in the workplace are being tested. A member of the Labour Advisory Board further cautioned that employers' growing emphasis on immediate productivity has made many reluctant to invest in on-the-job trainingskills training given to employees while they work在職培訓 for inexperienced hires.

Nevertheless, not every voice is pessimistic. By the same token that automation threatens certain roles, it is also reshaping demand towards new skill sets: a global survey found that employers increasingly prize critical thinking, data analysis and ethical decision-makingmaking choices guided by moral principles合乎道德的決策 over routine competence. Critics contend, however, that without targeted retraining schemes and government support, a persistent shortagea continuing lack of something over a long period持續的短缺 of experienced local talent could emerge, even as entry-level opportunities continue to disappear.

中文翻譯

香港的新畢業生正面對日益不明朗的勞動市場，因為人工智能正逐步取代昔日曾作為踏入全職事業踏腳石的常規工作。據勞動市場專家指出，適合大學畢業生的全職職位空缺數目，已由二零二二年約八萬個，急劇下跌至去年僅三萬一千個，這一跌幅普遍被歸因於全城各辦公室迅速採用人工智能工具。

普遍認為，行政支援、文書工作及其他入門級職位——向來是年輕求職者最容易踏入職場的途徑——正被自動化系統性取代。一名專家直言警告：「年輕人願意學習及努力工作，已不再必然能夠找到工作」，凸顯職場上有關精英制度的傳統假設正受到考驗。勞工顧問委員會一名成員進一步警告，僱主日益重視即時生產力，令許多機構不願意為缺乏經驗的新人投資在職培訓。

然而，並非所有聲音都偏向悲觀。正如自動化威脅著某些職位，它同時亦正在將需求轉向新的技能組合：一項全球調查發現，僱主日益重視批判思考、數據分析及合乎道德的決策，多於單純的常規能力。評論者則認為，若缺乏針對性的再培訓計劃及政府支援，即使入門機會持續消失，亦可能出現經驗豐富的本地人才持續短缺的情況。
