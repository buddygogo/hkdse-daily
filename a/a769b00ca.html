English

The Hong Kong Palace Museum has signed a memorandum of understandinga formal agreement between two parties setting out the terms of future cooperation合作備忘錄 with Amsterdam's Rijksmuseum, paving the way for roughly 100 works by Dutch masters to travel to the city next year. The deal was signed in Amsterdam on October 21 by museum director Louis Ng Chi-wa and his Rijksmuseum counterpart, Taco Dibbits, and will bring together paintings, ship models, ceramics, metalwork, textiles, prints and drawings in what organisers are calling a marquee exhibitiona major, high-profile exhibition intended to be the main attraction of a season重點展覽 — the first time such a broad survey of the Rijksmuseum's holdings has travelled to Hong Kong or the wider region.

Among the highlights will be The Love Letter, a prized piece that has never before been shown in this part of the world, alongside multimedia installations intended to place the works in their historical contextthe circumstances and background surrounding an event or object that help explain its meaning歷史背景. For the Hong Kong Palace Museum, which opened in 2022 as part of the West Kowloon Cultural District, the partnership marks its latest attempt to position itself as a bridge between Chinese and Western connoisseurshipexpert knowledge and appreciation of art, antiques or fine craftsmanship鑑賞, following earlier tie-ups built around its core collection on loan from Beijing's Palace Museum.

Beyond the exhibition itself, the two institutions have agreed to pursue joint researchresearch carried out together by two or more organisations sharing resources and expertise聯合研究 and educational programmes, including staff exchanges and shared scholarship on conservation techniques. Supporters argue that such partnerships strengthen Hong Kong's claim to be a genuine cultural huba city that serves as a central meeting point for artistic and cultural activity in a region文化樞紐 for Asia, capable of hosting world-class collections rather than simply receiving touring shows designed elsewhere. Sceptics, however, note that the museum's long-term success will depend less on any single headline deala prominent agreement or announcement that attracts significant public and media attention頭條合作 than on whether it can keep drawing local audiences back once the initial novelty of each new exhibition wears off.

中文翻譯

香港故宮文化博物館與阿姆斯特丹國立博物館簽署合作備忘錄，為明年引入約一百件荷蘭大師作品鋪路。協議於十月二十一日在阿姆斯特丹簽訂，由故宮文化博物館館長吳志華與國立博物館館長戴克斯代表雙方簽字，展覽將匯聚油畫、船隻模型、陶瓷、金屬工藝、織物及版畫等展品，被主辦方稱為一次重點展覽——是國立博物館館藏首次如此大規模地遠赴香港及區內其他地方展出。

展覽焦點之一是從未在這個地區展出過的珍品《情書》，加上多媒體裝置，協助觀眾了解作品的歷史背景。對於二零二二年隨西九文化區啟用的香港故宮文化博物館而言，這次合作是其最新一次嘗試，定位為連繫中西鑑賞傳統的橋樑，延續過往以北京故宮博物院借出館藏為核心所建立的其他合作關係。

除展覽本身，兩個機構亦同意推展聯合研究及教育計劃，包括人員交流及共享文物保育技術的學術成果。支持者認為，這類合作有助鞏固香港作為亞洲真正文化樞紐的地位，有能力接待世界級館藏，而非單純接收他人設計好的巡迴展覽。然而，亦有質疑者指出，博物館的長遠成功，並非取決於任何一次頭條合作，而是取決於每次新展覽的新鮮感過後，能否持續吸引本地觀眾再次入場。
