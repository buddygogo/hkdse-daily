English

Hong Kong's universities posted their strongest-ever showing in Stanford University's closely watched World's Top 2% Scientists list, a citation-based rankinga ranking system that measures a researcher's influence by counting how often other scholars cite their published work以引用次數為基礎的排名 updated annually to track career-long and single-year scholarly impact across every discipline. Lingnan University, the Chinese University of Hong Kong and the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology led the city's showing, though the result drawing the most attention came from Lingnan, a university historically known for the humanities rather than the sciences.

Lingnan's tally of listed scholars rose to 54 this year from 43 previously, with more than twenty researchers placing within the top 100 globally in their fields. President S. Joe Qin retained his position as the top-ranked scholar from China and Hong Kong in industrial engineering and automation. The jump reflects a deliberate strategy among smaller institutions to build niche specialisationa strategy of concentrating resources on a narrow research area rather than spreading effort across many fields專科化策略s rather than compete head-on with larger, better-funded rivals across every discipline at once.

Supporters argue that global visibilitythe degree to which a person or institution is recognised and noticed internationally全球知名度 on such rankings helps universities attract funding and sharpens their appeal in faculty recruitmentthe process of hiring academic staff, especially senior researchers教職招聘, particularly when competing against Singapore, mainland China and the West for the same talent. Critics counter that an overly metric-driven culturean environment in which numerical measurements are treated as the main measure of success, sometimes at the expense of other considerations唯指標主導的文化 risks pushing scholars towards research outputthe total volume of papers, data and other scholarly work a researcher produces研究產出 that is easy to cite rather than original, a tension local universities must manage as rankings shape recruitment and funding.

中文翻譯

香港各大學在史丹福大學備受關注的「全球首2%科學家」榜單中,取得歷來最佳成績。這項以引用次數為基礎的排名每年更新一次,追蹤學者在各學術領域的長期及單年度影響力。今年榜單中,嶺南大學、香港中文大學及香港科技大學在本港院校中表現領先,但最受關注的結果來自嶺南大學——一所傳統上以人文學科見稱,而非以理科著稱的院校。

嶺南大學上榜學者人數由上一屆的43人增至今年的54人,當中逾二十人在其學科領域躋身全球首100位。校長冉世雄(S. Joe Qin)繼續保持中國及香港在工業工程及自動化學科的首位排名。這次躍升反映部分規模較小的院校採取有意識的策略,集中發展專科化策略,而非在每個學科上與資源更豐厚的大型研究型大學全面競爭。

支持榜單的人士認為,在此類排名中取得全球知名度有助大學爭取研究資助,並在教職招聘上更具優勢,尤其是在與新加坡、中國內地及西方院校爭奪同一批人才之際。批評者則反駁,過度唯指標主導的文化可能令學者傾向產出容易被引用、而非真正具原創性的研究產出,而這正是本港各大學日後若進一步倚賴排名作為招聘及資助工具時,必須處理的張力。
