事件背景

A property agent told the Abby Choi murder trial that her ex-father-in-law personally received a HK$6.97 million cash rebate on a flat she funded.

一名地產代理向蔡天鳳謀殺案審訊供稱，其前姻親親自收取一筆由她出資購買物業所得的六百九十七萬港元現金回贈。

The testimony adds further detail to a web of money flowing between Choi and her in-laws, which prosecutors say ultimately fuelled resentment.

證供進一步揭示蔡天鳳與姻親之間錯綜複雜的金錢往來，檢方指這最終滋生了怨恨。

English

An estate agenta licensed professional who arranges and negotiates property sales on behalf of buyers, sellers or developers地產代理 told the High Court on Wednesday that Abby Choi's former father-in-law, Kwong Kau, personally asked for and received a cash rebatea sum of money paid back to a buyer after a purchase, often deducted from a commission or the sale price現金回贈 of roughly HK$6.97 million after the purchase of a Kadoorie Hill flat funded by the slain socialite. Marco Wong Tak-sing, giving evidence on the 20th day of the murder trial, said his agency earned about HK$8.18 million in sales commissiona fee, usually a percentage of the sale price, paid to an agent for arranging a transaction銷售佣金 from the flat's developer, and that most of that sum was then passed back to Kwong, the flat's registered ownerthe person or entity whose name legally appears on a property's title documents登記業主, at his own request.

Wong told the court that a deposit chequea cheque used to pay the initial, partial payment required to secure a property purchase訂金支票 worth HK$3.74 million had been drawn directly on Choi's own bank account, and that Kwong explained at the time that his daughter-in-law had provided the funds. Under cross-examinationthe questioning of a witness by the opposing side in a trial, used to test or challenge their evidence盤問, Wong was pressed on why the buyer's correspondence address listed on the tender documentthe formal paperwork submitted when bidding to purchase a property through a sealed-bid process投標文件 was his own agency's address rather than Kwong's, and replied that the arrangement was meant to stop the purchaser from contacting the developer directly — a point Kwong is said to have agreed to at the time.

The testimony adds financial detail to the prosecution's broader case, which alleges that Kwong Kau held the flat only in a beneficial ownershipthe right to enjoy the benefits of an asset even though it is legally registered in someone else's name實益擁有權 arrangement on Choi's behalf, and that his resentment over her eventual decision to sell it helped drive the killing. Kwong, 68, is on trial alongside his sons Alex and Anthony Kwong, all of whom deny murdering the 28-year-old influencer in 2023; the two sons have separately pleaded guilty to preventing her lawful burial, a charge their father continues to contest.

中文翻譯

一名地產代理周三在高等法院作證指,名模Abby Choi的前家翁鄺球,在以Choi出資購入嘉道理山一個單位後,曾親自要求並取得約697萬元的現金回贈。黃德誠(譯名)在謀殺審訊第20日作供時表示,其代理行就該單位的買賣,從發展商處收取約818萬元銷售佣金,其中大部分款項隨後按鄺球本人的要求,回贈予身為單位登記業主的他。

黃又向法庭表示,一張金額374萬元的訂金支票,直接從Choi本人的銀行帳戶支取,鄺球當時解釋,款項是由其媳婦提供。在盤問環節中,黃被追問為何投標文件上所列的買家聯絡地址,是其代理行的地址,而非鄺球本人的地址,他回應指,此安排是為免買家直接聯絡發展商——而鄺球當時據稱亦同意此做法。

今次證供,為控方更廣泛的案情增添財務細節——控方指,鄺球僅以實益擁有權的安排,代表Choi持有該單位,而他對Choi最終決定出售單位心存不滿,正是促使這宗謀殺案發生的原因之一。68歲的鄺球,與其兩名兒子Alex Kwong及Anthony Kwong一同受審,三人均否認於2023年謀殺這名28歲網絡紅人;兩名兒子已就妨礙合法埋葬罪另行認罪,惟其父則繼續否認該項控罪。
