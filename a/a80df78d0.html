English

Hong Kong's Direct Subsidy Scheme (DSS) schools have taken in roughly 20 additional non-local students this academic year under a government initiative designed to bolster the city's standingto strengthen or support someone's or something's position, reputation or status提升本地地位 as a regional education hub, according to the Education Bureau. Deputy Secretary for Education Ida Lee Bik-sai said the total number of visa-holding pupilsstudents who require an official travel or residence permit to study in a particular place, as opposed to local residents持學生簽證學生 enrolled in DSS schools on a self-financing basis has now reached 170, a 13 percent increase on the previous year. The newly admitted students hail from a notably diverse range of countries, including Thailand, South Korea, Singapore, the United States and several European nations.

The scheme, first unveiled in the 2025 Policy Address, allows DSS schools to apply either to expand class sizes or to add entirely new classes in order to accommodate additional fee-paying overseas pupils. Crucially, these extra places are treated as supplementaryadded in order to complete or enhance something, here meaning additional to the existing total rather than replacing it額外增補的 to existing enrolment and therefore do not count towards the official cap on the proportion of students permitted to follow non-local curricula, meaning local children's chances of securing places are not meant to be affected. Schools with boarding facilities are additionally required to reserve at least half of their boarding places for non-local, visa-holding students, a condition intended to make the relocation processthe process of moving from one country or place to live or study in another遷居過程 smoother for families moving from abroad.

While officials have described the response from schools as enthusiastic, with more than 40 DSS institutions submitting formal applications, at least one principal has urged the bureau to step up promotional effortsto increase or intensify activities aimed at advertising or raising awareness of something加強宣傳工作 among prospective overseas families, arguing that awareness of the scheme remains limited outside Hong Kong. The policy notably excludes applicants from the mainland, Taiwan and a handful of other jurisdictions, since current immigration arrangements do not permit student visas for those groups. Education observers say the scheme's modest but steady growth reflects incremental progressgradual, step-by-step advancement rather than sudden or dramatic change漸進式進展 towards the government's broader ambition of positioning Hong Kong as Asia's leading international study destination.

中文翻譯

據教育局表示,香港直接資助計劃(直資)學校在本學年透過一項政府措施,額外錄取約20名非本地學生,旨在提升本地地位,使香港成為區內教育樞紐。教育局副秘書長李碧茜表示,目前以自費形式就讀直資學校的持學生簽證學生總數已增至170人,較去年上升13%。新錄取的學生來自多個不同國家,包括泰國、南韓、新加坡、美國及多個歐洲國家。

該計劃於2025年施政報告中首次公布,容許直資學校申請擴大班級人數,或增設全新班級,以容納更多繳付學費的海外學生。重要的是,這些額外學額被視為現有學額以外額外增補的名額,因此不會計入現行限制非本地課程學生比例的上限之內,意味著本地學童的入學機會理論上不受影響。設有寄宿設施的學校,亦須額外預留至少一半宿位予持簽證的非本地學生,以期讓從海外遷居的家庭,能有更順暢的遷居過程。

當局形容學校方面反應踴躍,逾40間直資學校已提交正式申請,惟至少一名校長呼籲教育局向海外的潛在家庭加強宣傳工作,認為計劃在香港以外的知名度仍然有限。該政策明確排除來自內地、台灣及其他若干地區的申請人,原因是現行入境安排並不容許該些人士申請學生簽證。教育界人士指出,計劃雖增長溫和但穩定,反映香港朝著成為亞洲首要國際升學目的地這一宏大目標,邁出漸進式進展的步伐。
