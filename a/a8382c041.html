An independent inquiry into the deadly fire at Wang Fuk Court, which claimed 168 lives, has placed the heaviest blame on the main contractor, its consultant and a registered building inspector, concluding that their actions allowed a routine renovation project to turn into one of Hong Kong’s worst disasters in decades. The committee’s final report found that the main contractor repeatedly forgedcreated a false document or signature to deceive others偽造 certificates covering the flammable scaffolding netting wrapped around the towers, concealed failed combustion tests, and even deleted inspection footage that would have exposed the deceptionthe act of deliberately misleading someone欺騙.

The report identified the likely cause as a strayhaving strayed from its intended place; random, errant隨意 lit cigarette that ignited paper materials stored on a rooftop outside one block, with the resulting blaze spreading rapidly across netting and bamboo scaffolding that should never have passed a safety inspection. Investigators concluded bluntly that “the deception succeeded because the regulators’ own practices enabled it,” pointing to laxnot strict or careful enough; insufficiently thorough鬆懈 oversight that let falsified paperwork go unchallengednot confronted, questioned or opposed未受質疑 for months. The property manager was separately criticised for allowing unqualified staff to switch off the fire alarm’s power supply.

Housing chief Winnie Ho has since resigned, telling reporters she took responsibility for the regulatory failures identified in the report even as she insisted that the government’s broader housing programme “can still deliver” for residents. Her departure makes her the most senior official to step down over the tragedy, and comes as the government weighs new legislation that would allow it to compulsorily acquiretake ownership of property by legal force, with or without the owner’s consent強制收購 remaining flats from owners who refuse to sell into its buyout scheme, with roughly 80 per cent of households across the complex having already signed sale agreements. 中文翻譯

一項針對奪去168人命的宏福苑大火的獨立調查委員會,將最主要責任歸咎於主要承建商、顧問公司及一名註冊驗樓師,裁定正是他們的所作所為,令一項日常裝修工程演變成香港數十年來最嚴重的災難之一。委員會最終報告發現,主要承建商Prestige Construction & Engineering多次偽造覆蓋大廈外牆易燃防護網的證書,隱瞞未能通過的燃燒測試結果,甚至刪除可揭露這場欺騙行為的巡查錄影片段。

報告指出,火警最可能的起因,是一支隨意遺留的未熄香煙,引燃存放於其中一座大廈天台外的紙質物料,火勢隨後迅速蔓延至本應無法通過安全檢驗的防護網及竹製棚架。調查人員直言不諱:「監管機構自身的做法,正正讓這場欺騙得以成功。」並指出鬆懈的監管,令偽造文件在數月內未受質疑而一直流通。物業管理公司亦被另行批評,指其容許無相關資格的員工關閉火警鐘電源。

房屋署署長Winnie Ho其後辭職,向記者表示她為報告中指出的監管失誤承擔責任,但同時堅稱政府整套房屋計劃「仍能夠兌現承諾」,服務居民。她的辭職,使她成為因這場悲劇而下台的最高級官員,而政府亦正考慮立法,容許當局強制收購拒絕出售物業、加入回購計劃的業主所持單位,目前整個屋苑約八成住戶已簽署買賣協議。保安局局長鄧炳強則另行承諾推行更廣泛的消防安全改革,惟批評者指,單憑一名官員辭職,並不足以恢復公眾對一個容讓偽造文件長期不被發現的監管制度的信任。
