English

Prosecutors opened their case on Thursday in the long-awaited murder trial over the killing of Hong Kong socialite Abby Choi Tin-fung, alleging that her former husband and his relatives had meticulously plannedprepared something with extreme care and attention to detail精心策劃 the crime well before her remains were discovered. Alex Kwong Kong-chi, his father Kwong Kau and his brother Anthony Kwong Kong-kit stand jointly charged with murder and with preventing the lawful burial of a body, more than three and a half years after Choi's dismembered remainsa body that has been cut into separate pieces被肢解的遺體 were found at a rented village house in Tai Po.

According to the prosecution, the trio rented the ground-floor property in the weeks before Choi's death and embarked on a series of purchases that betrayed their intentrevealed a hidden plan or motive暴露了他們的意圖: among the items acquired was an industrial meat grinder, and the court heard that Kwong Kau had also enquired about a bone grinder while making the purchase. Taken together with other equipment later recovered at the scene, prosecutors argued the shopping spree pointed unmistakably to a premeditated plana plan made and thought through in advance預謀計劃 to dismember and dispose of a body, rather than a crime committed in the heat of the momenta sudden burst of emotion without forethought一時衝動.

The trial, expected to run for 45 days, had already been delayed once after a juror was discharged on health grounds, forcing a replacement to be empanelledformally selected to serve on a jury獲遴選加入陪審團. Legal commentators note that the case has drawn intense public scrutinyclose and critical attention from the public公眾的高度關注 partly because it exposes how a bitter family dispute over property and finances could escalate into calculated brutalitygrow step by step into deliberate, extreme violence演變成有計算的殘暴行為, raising uncomfortable questions about domestic conflict, inheritance disputes and the adequacy of safeguards for individuals caught in deteriorating family relationships long before matters turn violent.

中文翻譯

檢方星期四在萬眾期待的香港名媛蔡天鳳遇害案審訊中開案陳詞，指其前夫及其家人早於遺體被發現之前，已經精心策劃這場謀殺案。鄺港智、其父鄺球及其兄鄺港傑三人被控謀殺罪及阻止遺體合法埋葬罪，距離蔡天鳳被肢解的遺體在大埔一間租用的村屋內被發現，已逾三年半。

根據控方陳述，三人在蔡天鳳死前數星期租用該地下村屋，並連日購物，而這些購物行為正正暴露了他們的意圖：當中購買的物品包括一台工業用碎肉機，而法庭亦聽到鄺球在購買碎肉機時，亦曾問及碎骨機。結合現場後來起出的其他器具，控方指這連日的購物行為明確顯示，這是一場有預謀的肢解及棄屍計劃，而非一時衝動所犯下的罪行。

審訊預計將持續四十五天，早前已因一名陪審員以健康理由獲准退任，需要重新遴選替補陪審員而一度延後。法律評論員指出，此案備受公眾高度關注，部分原因在於案件揭示一場因財產及金錢而起的激烈家庭糾紛，竟可演變成有計算的殘暴行為，亦引起社會對家庭衝突、遺產糾紛，以及在關係惡化至演變成暴力之前，社會能否及時為相關人士提供足夠保障等問題的不安思考。
