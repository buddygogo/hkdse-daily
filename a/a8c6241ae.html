English

Hong Kong's Secretary for Labour and Welfare has confirmed that a long-awaited scheme to bring in foreign care workersdomestic helpers recruited from overseas specifically trained to look after elderly or disabled people外地護理工 for the elderly will be introduced gradually, with the earliest arrivals unlikely before the second half of 2027. Chris Sun said the government would run a small-scale carer intakea limited initial batch of workers introduced before any wider programme is rolled out小規模護理工引入計劃 admitting only a few hundred carers in its first year, with detailed arrangements to be settled after further consultation.

Under the proposed terms, carers would need to complete at least 150 hours of training before travelling to Hong Kong, and would earn 10 to 20 per cent more than ordinary domestic helpersforeign workers employed to carry out household duties such as cooking and cleaning外籍家庭傭工, reflecting the specialised nature of elder care. The scheme is intended to relieve the burden on an estimated 180,000 informal caregiversunpaid family members who provide day-to-day care without formal training or employment非正式照顧者 across the city, many of whom experience chronic carer exhaustiona state of physical and emotional exhaustion caused by prolonged, unsupported caregiving照顧者長期身心俱疲, with few accessing formal temporary care reliefshort-term professional care that gives a family caregiver a temporary break臨時暫託服務 in the meantime.

Employers' groups have cautiously welcomed the plan but warn that a sudden influx of better-paid carers could trigger wage spillover effectsupward pressure on pay in a related job market caused by changes elsewhere薪酬外溢效應 across the existing helper market, driving up costs for households that cannot afford specialised care. Advocates for the elderly counter that without such workers, Hong Kong's demographic ageinga demographic pattern in which the proportion of older people in society is steadily rising人口老化趨勢 will continue to strain families already stretched thin by long working hours and shrinking household sizes.

中文翻譯

香港勞工及福利局局長孫玉菡證實，政府將分階段推出籌劃已久的輸入外地護理工計劃，供長者使用，最快亦要到2027年下半年才有首批人手抵港。孫玉菡表示，政府首年只會推行小規模的小規模護理工引入計劃，僅容許數百名護理工來港，具體安排仍有待進一步諮詢後落實。

根據建議方案，護理工須在來港前完成最少150小時培訓，薪酬將較一般外籍家庭傭工高出一至兩成，以反映長者照顧工作的專業性質。計劃旨在減輕本港估計十八萬名非正式照顧者的負擔，當中許多人出現照顧者長期身心俱疲的情況，而暫時甚少人能獲得正式的臨時暫託服務。

僱主團體對計劃審慎表示歡迎，但警告一旦大量薪酬較高的護理工湧入，可能在現有家傭市場引發薪酬外溢效應，令未能負擔專業護理服務的家庭百上加斤。長者權益倡導者則反駁，若無這批工人，本港人口老化趨勢只會持續加重家庭負擔，尤其是在長工時與家庭規模縮小的雙重壓力下。
