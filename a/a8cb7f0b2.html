English

Hong Kong extended its economic reach into the mainland market this week as the city's pavilion at the ninth China International Import Expo in Shanghai drew industry insidersexperienced professionals working within a particular trade or sector業內人士 eager to discuss closer collaboration in artificial intelligence, aviation and professional services. Representatives from Hong Kong's trade body said the five-day fair, one of the mainland's largest showcases for international business, gave local firms a rare platform to pitch their expertise directly to mainland companies hunting for overseas partners.

Several delegates described Hong Kong as forging closer tiesbuilding stronger, more frequent connections and cooperation建立更緊密的聯繫 with mainland artificial intelligence firms that want to expand beyond China's borders, arguing that the city's accountants, lawyers and bankers are well placed to guide first-time exporters through unfamiliar foreign rules. Aviation executives at the fair pointed to Hong Kong's international flight network as a natural extension for mainland carriers, while officials highlighted the Northern Metropolis megaprojecta huge planned development zone near the mainland border meant to attract technology firms北部都會區大型發展計劃 as fresh ground for technology investment once its industrial parks open.

The push comes as global supply chain realignmentthe large-scale shifting of manufacturing and trade routes between countries供應鏈重組 accelerates and mainland firms look for a trusted offshore financial huba city outside a country’s borders that provides international banking and investment services境外金融中心 to manage overseas deals. Supporters say Hong Kong's common law frameworka legal system based on judicial precedent, widely trusted by international investors普通法制度 gives it an edge no mainland city can match, but economists at the expo cautioned that merely connecting others to the world will not be enough; Hong Kong, they said, must also build its own innovation capacitythe ability to independently research, create and commercialise new technology創新能力 rather than relying solely on its role as a middleman.

中文翻譯

香港本週透過在上海舉行的第九屆中國國際進口博覽會的展館，進一步拓展其與內地市場的經濟聯繫，吸引不少業內人士前來洽談在人工智能、航空及專業服務方面加強合作的機會。香港貿易機構代表表示，這場為期五天的展覽會是內地其中一個最大型的國際商貿展示平台，為本地企業提供了難得的機會，可以直接向正在尋找海外夥伴的內地公司推銷自身的專業優勢。

多名與會代表形容，香港正與希望拓展海外業務的內地人工智能企業建立更緊密的聯繫，並指出本港的會計師、律師及銀行家十分適合協助首次「出海」的企業應對陌生的外國法規。多名航空業高層在展會上指出，香港的國際航空網絡是內地航空公司拓展業務的天然延伸，而官員則強調北部都會區大型發展計劃一旦其工業園區落成啟用，將為科技投資提供新的土地資源。

這項推動正值全球供應鏈重組加速進行，內地企業亦正尋找一個可信賴的境外金融中心來處理海外交易。支持者認為，香港的普通法制度令其具備內地任何城市都無法比擬的優勢；但有經濟學者在展會上警告，單靠充當連接各方的橋樑並不足夠，香港還必須建立自身的創新能力，而不能只依賴中介角色。
