English

Police are investigating the suspected theftthe act of taking property without permission盜窃 of sculptures from the Exhibition Hall at Hong Kong City Hall in Central, after organisers preparing the Microwave International New Media Arts Festival discovered several pieces missing on Friday. The missing works are reported to be five styrofoam sculptures by local new-media artist Keith Lam Yan-kit, part of an eight-piece installation that was due to go on public display the very next day.

According to the timeline given by organisers, City Hall staff sealed the venue’s entrances once assembly work ended at 8pm on Monday, leaving the hall untouched until crew returned days later to finish setting up the display. It was only then, while arranging the remaining exhibits, that staff realised the pieces were unaccounted fornot explained or located下落不明. Officers were called at 2.31pm and the case has provisionally been classifiedformally categorised according to type分類 as theft, with the Central district crime unit now reviewing CCTV footage covering the hall’s entrances and exits — the only areas under camera coverage.

The Leisure and Cultural Services Department, which manages City Hall, said the festival’s organiser had formally reported a set of exhibits missing and that the venue would assist the inquiry. No arrest or recovery has been reported so far. For the artist, whose work often explores themes of impermanence and technology, the loss carries a bitter ironya situation with an outcome contrary to what was expected變訊式的諾刺: pieces meant to spark reflection on how fragile physical objects can be have themselves vanished before the public ever saw them. The incident has also renewed debate over whether venue securitymeasures taken to protect a building and its contents場地保安 at public cultural spaces, where surveillance coveragethe extent to which an area is monitored by cameras監控範圍 is often limited to entrances, is adequate for the valuable, one-of-a-kind artworks increasingly staged there.

中文翻譯

警方正調查涉嫌從中環香港大會堂展覽廳盜取雕塑一案，事緣微波國際新媒體藝術節的籌辦人在上星期五佈置場地時，發現數件展品不見了。報道指失蹤作品為本地新媒體藝術家林欣傑八件組合裝置中的五件保麗龍雕塑，原定於翌日起向公眾開放展出。

據主辦單位提供的時間表，大會堂職員在星期一組裝工作於晚上八時結束後，已封閉場館出入口，其後數日場館一直維持原狀，直至工作人員數天後返回完成餘下佈置。正正就在整理剩餘展品期間，職員才發現該批作品下落不明。警方接報時間為下午二時三十一分，案件暫被列作盜竊案處理，中區重案調查組現正翻查場館出入口閉路電視畫面——該處亦是唯一有鏡頭覆蓋的範圍。

負責管理大會堂的康樂及文化事務署表示，藝術節主辦單位已正式報稱一批展品失蹤，場館將協助調查。截至目前未有拘捕或尋回展品的消息。對於作品常探討無常與科技議題的藝術家而言，此次損失帶有一種苦澀的諷刺意味：本意啟發觀眾反思實物何其脆弱的作品，竟在公眾尚未一睹真貌前已先行消失。事件亦重新引發外界質疑，公共文化場館的場地保安——其監控範圍往往僅限於出入口——對於日益增多於此展出的珍貴獨一藝術品而言，是否足夠。
