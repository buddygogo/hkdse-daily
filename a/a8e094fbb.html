Hong Kong has just recorded its hottest October since records began 142 years ago, the Observatory confirmed this week, with the average daily maximum reaching 30.5 degrees Celsius and the monthly mean climbing to 27.6 degrees — both unprecedentednever having happened or existed before前所未有的 for the month. The spell of unusually warm weather culminatedreached its highest point or climax達到頂點 in the latest-ever issuance of a Very Hot Weather Warning, triggered when temperatures in Yuen Long hit 36 degrees in mid-October, long after such extremes would typically have faded for the year.

Meteorologists attributed the prolonged heat to a confluencea coming together of several things at once匯聚 of factors: the broader warming trend driven by climate change, an active El Niño pattern, a weaker-than-usual northeast monsoon, and a stubborn high-pressure system anchoredfixed firmly in one place盤踞 over southern China that suppressed rain and kept skies clear. Taken together, these conditions compoundedmade a problem or effect worse by adding to it使情況加劇 one another, allowing summer-like heat to persist deep into what is normally one of the city’s most comfortable months.

Relief now appears to be on the way. The Observatory expects a strengthening northeast monsoon to usher into cause something new to begin帶來 markedly drier, cooler air from next Monday, when the urban minimum is forecast to dip to 23 degrees, falling further to 22 degrees the following day and to as low as 19 degrees in parts of the New Territories. Forecasters cautioned, however, that the abruptnessthe quality of happening suddenly and unexpectedly突然性 of the transition — from near-record heat to autumn chill within days — illustrates just how volatileliable to change suddenly and unpredictably反覆無常的 Hong Kong’s weather patterns have become amid a changing climate.

中文翻譯

天文台本週確認,香港剛錄得142年來最熾熱的10月,月內平均每日最高氣溫達攝氏30.5度,月平均氣溫亦升至27.6度,兩者均屬該月前所未有的紀錄。這股異常酷熱的天氣,達到頂點之際,更觸發歷來最遲發出的酷熱天氣警告,原因是元朗氣溫於10月中一度飆升至36度,遠超該類極端天氣通常在年內持續的時間。

氣象學家指出,持續高溫由多項因素匯聚造成:氣候變化帶動的整體升溫趨勢、活躍的厄爾尼諾現象、較正常偏弱的東北季候風,以及一個盤踞華南上空、抑制降雨並令天空持續晴朗的頑固高壓系統。這些因素互相使情況加劇,令本應是全年最舒適月份之一的10月,持續出現如夏季般的酷熱天氣。

紓緩天氣終於有望來臨。天文台預計,一股增強的東北季候風將於下週一帶來明顯更乾燥及涼爽的空氣,當日市區最低氣溫預料跌至23度,翌日再跌至22度,部分新界地區更可能低見19度。然而,預報員提醒,這次由接近破紀錄高溫急轉直下至初秋涼意的突然性轉變,正好說明在氣候變化下,香港的天氣模式已變得何等反覆無常。
