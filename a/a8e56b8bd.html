English

At the opening night of his “After Hours Til Dawn” tour at Kai Tak Stadium on Saturday, Canadian singer The Weeknd surprised fans with an unannounced track titled “Hong Kong,” built around a samplein music, a short section of an existing recording reused in a new song音樂上指重複使用現有錄音中一段作為新歌的素材 of “Seasonal Wind” (季候風), a melancholic 1993 ballad by Cantopop icona hugely influential and widely recognised star within a particular field在某個領域極具影響力及備受認同的標誌性人物 Faye Wong. The gesture, widely read as a tributesomething done to show respect or admiration for someone or something為表達對人或事物的敬意而做的舉動 to the city, arrived without warning midway through the set, its title appearing suddenly on the stadium's giant screens before a Hong Kong-only music video began to play.

The accompanying visuals leaned heavily on the moody, neon-lit aesthetica particular visual style or set of ideas about what looks appealing一種特定的視覺風格或審美取向 associated with director Wong Kar-wai's films, cutting between scenes of the city's streets and harbour. Fans quickly linked the gesture to the singer's earlier social-media posts from his trip, including a photo with actor Tony Leung set to Faye Wong's “Dream Person” and a recommendation of her 1996 album Fuzao, suggesting a deliberate, carefully researched homagea show of respect or honour for someone's work, often through imitation透過模仿或致敬的方式對他人作品表達尊重 rather than a passing gimmick.

Footage of the moment, uploaded in the early hours of Sunday, had drawn roughly 9.3 million views, 600,000 likes and thousands of comments within a day, many from fans who said the tribute showed real respect for Hong Kong's musical heritagethe body of music and musical traditions passed down within a culture一個文化中世代傳承下來的音樂及音樂傳統. Others debated whether an international pop star repackaging a local classic for a global audience amounted to genuine appreciation or a form of cultural borrowingtaking elements from another culture, which can be respectful or exploitative depending on context借用另一文化的元素，視乎情況可以是尊重亦可以是剝削 that benefits the visiting artist more than the city itself. With three more shows still to come, the song has already become the week's biggest talking point among local fans and cultural commentators alike.

中文翻譯

上周六（十月廿四日），加拿大歌手The Weeknd在啟德體育園舉行「After Hours Til Dawn」巡唱開騷夜，突然為樂迷帶來一首從未公布的新曲《Hong Kong》，歌曲取樣自粵語流行音樂樂壇巨星王菲1993年的憂鬱民謠《季候風》。這份向本港表達敬意的舉動毫無預警地在表演進行到一半時出現，歌名突然顯示在體育園的巨型屏幕上，隨即播放一段只在香港放送的音樂錄像。

配合歌曲播放的畫面大量運用導演王家衛電影中那種陰鬱、霓虹燈光的美學風格，畫面在城市街景與海港之間交替呈現。樂迷迅速將這舉動與歌手旅程中早前在社交媒體發布的帖文聯繫起來，包括一張與演員梁朝偉合照、配上王菲《夢中人》的照片，以及他推薦王菲1996年專輯《浮躁》，顯示這是一次經深思熟慮、而非隨便為之的致敬之作。

演出片段在星期日凌晨上載後，一天內已錄得約930萬次觀看、60萬個讚好及數千則留言，不少樂迷稱讚這份致意充分展現對香港音樂傳統的尊重。亦有人質疑，一位國際流行巨星將本地經典重新包裝，呈現給全球觀眾，究竟是真心欣賞，還是一種令來訪藝人得益多於本地文化的文化挪用。隨著餘下三場演出陸續舉行，這首歌已成為本周樂迷及文化評論界熱議的話題。
