English

Hong Kong's three leading auction houses — Christie's, Sotheby's and Phillips — concluded their autumn sales from 28 to 30 September with a combined totalthe sum of several amounts added together總額 exceeding HK$2 billion, a notable rebound from the HK$1.3 billion recorded last spring. Christie's led the field with HK$956.5 million, up 17 per cent year on year, a result the house attributed to a 30 per cent influx of first-time buyersa sudden arrival of a large number of new participants新買家湧入 drawn to blue-chip names. The sale's top lot, Jean-Michel Basquiat's Ancient Scientist (1984), fetched HK$77 million, underscoring sustained demand for museum-grade worksartworks of a quality considered worthy of display in major museums具博物館級水準的作品.

Sotheby's matched its rival almost lot for lot, posting HK$956 million — a 35 per cent jump on its March series — with a 91 per cent sell-through ratethe proportion of lots offered that actually find a buyer成交比率 and five new artist records. Yoshitomo Nara's Present (1994) topped the house's combined sales at HK$71.1 million. Phillips, the smallest of the three, rounded out the season with HK$94.5 million and an even higher 96 per cent sell-through rate by value, suggesting buyers were highly selectivechoosing very carefully rather than buying indiscriminately非常挑剔 rather than universally bullish.

Not every lot fared so well. Yayoi Kusama's A-Pumpkin (YHP) (2011), estimated at HK$48 million to HK$68 million, met a muted responsea reaction that is noticeably weaker or quieter than expected反應冷淡 in what was the first major test of the artist's posthumous market since her death last month. Dealers said the result hints at buyer fatiguea loss of enthusiasm among buyers after repeated exposure to similar works買家興趣減退 towards an artist whose pumpkin motifs have flooded the market for years. Analysts nonetheless described the season as a sign that Hong Kong's art market, having weathered two subdued years, is regaining its footingbecoming stable and successful again after a period of difficulty重新站穩腳步 as a regional hub, even as collectors grow more discerning about what commands a premium.

中文翻譯

香港三大拍賣行——佳士得、蘇富比和富藝斯——於9月28日至30日完成秋季拍賣，總額超過20億港元，較去年春季拍賣錄得的13億港元明顯回升。佳士得以9.565億港元奪得頭籌，按年上升17%，拍行將此歸因於新買家湧入三成，吸引他們的是藍籌級名家作品。本季焦點拍品——尚·米榭·巴斯基亞（Jean-Michel Basquiat）1984年作品《Ancient Scientist》——以7700萬港元成交，印證市場對具博物館級水準的作品的持續需求。

蘇富比幾乎與對手勢均力敵，總成交額達9.56億港元，較今年三月拍賣上升35%，成交比率達91%，並刷新五位藝術家的拍賣紀錄。奈良美智（Yoshitomo Nara）1994年作品《Present》以7110萬港元成為該行組合拍賣的最高價拍品。三家拍行中規模最小的富藝斯則以9450萬港元為本季作結，按金額計的成交比率更高達96%，反映買家在落槌前非常挑剔，而非一味追捧。

並非所有拍品都表現亮眼。草間彌生（Yayoi Kusama）2011年作品《A-Pumpkin (YHP)》估價4800萬至6800萬港元，卻遇上反應冷淡的場面——這是她上月逝世後，其身後市場首次接受的重大考驗。有交易商指，結果反映買家對這位南瓜圖案已充斥市場多年的藝術家出現買家興趣減退的跡象。分析人士認為，經歷兩年低迷後，本季拍賣顯示香港藝術市場正重新站穩腳步，重拾區內樞紐地位，但同時藏家對何者值得溢價變得更為審慎。
