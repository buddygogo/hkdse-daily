English

It is widely reported that mainland Chinese investors have been reallocating capitalmoving money from one investment to another重新分配資金 away from traditional financial stocks and towards artificial intelligence firms listed in Hong Kong. Southbound funds channelled a record HK$10.6 billion into MiniMax, an AI start-up, during August alone, an inflow that eclipsedsurpassed; overshadowed超越；蓋過 even the sums directed towards established giants such as Alibaba and Tencent. Since becoming eligible for trading through Stock Connect on 6 August, MiniMax has seen mainland investors accumulate a 9.7 per cent stake, with shares surging as much as 22.8 per cent on their first day of southbound trading.

Critics contend that such a concentrated bet on a single, relatively young sector is not without risk. Nevertheless, proponents argue that the enthusiasm reflects a broader paradigm shifta fundamental change in approach範式轉移 in how mainland capital views Hong Kong's stock market: no longer merely a venue for banks and property developers, but increasingly a gatewaya means of access or entry門戶；途徑 to cutting-edge technology. MiniMax and its peer Zhipu AI, both listed in January 2026, rallied by more than 300 per cent by June and were subsequently admitted to the Hang Seng Tech Index, a milestone that cementedmade firm or secure鞏固 their status among the city's most closely watched equities.

By the same token, analysts caution that this AI-driven rally could prove volatileliable to change rapidly and unpredictably反覆無常的；動蕩的 once initial lock-up periods expire and early investors seek to realise profits. It is nonetheless undeniable that Hong Kong's role as a hub for AI listings has been significantly strengthened, offering a timely case study for students examining how technological innovation and capital markets are inextricably linkedconnected in a way that cannot be separated密不可分地聯繫 in the modern economy.

中文翻譯

據報導,內地投資者正逐步將資金重新分配,由傳統金融股轉投在香港上市的人工智能企業。南向資金單在八月份已向人工智能初創企業MiniMax注入破紀錄的106億港元,流入金額甚至超越了流向阿里巴巴及騰訊等老牌巨企的資金。自八月六日起獲納入港股通交易以來,內地投資者已累積持有MiniMax百分之九點七的股權,股份更在南向交易首日一度飆升達百分之二十二點八。

有意見認為,如此集中押注於一個相對新興的行業,並非沒有風險。然而,支持者則認為,這股熱潮反映內地資金看待香港股市的方式出現更廣泛的範式轉移——香港股市不再僅僅是銀行與地產發展商的集資場地,而是逐漸成為通往尖端科技的門戶。MiniMax及其同業智譜AI同於2026年一月上市,股價截至六月累計上升逾百分之三百,其後更獲納入恒生科技指數,此一里程碑鞏固了兩者作為全城焦點股份的地位。

與此同時,分析師警告,一旦首批禁售期屆滿,早期投資者爭相套現,這股由人工智能帶動的升浪或會變得反覆無常。然而不可否認的是,香港作為人工智能企業上市樞紐的角色已顯著增強,為學生提供了一個切合時宜的案例,探討科技創新與資本市場在現代經濟中如何密不可分。
