English

Hong Kong's labour market is undergoing what China Daily describes as a fundamental shift, as artificial intelligence adoption accelerates across industries far faster than most employers anticipated. Roughly a quarter of local organisations now report widely deployingto put a resource, system or technology into active use部署 AI in their operations, three times the proportion recorded just a year earlier, while understanding and applying the technology has become, according to surveyed executives, the most crucial skill demanded of employees at every level of seniority.

It is widely argued that this shift carries a double edgehaving both a beneficial and a harmful aspect at the same time雙面刃. Merchandise exports of AI-related electronic products have grown sharply, and hundreds of companies have set up or expanded local operations this year alone, suggesting the technology is fuelling genuine economic momentum. Nevertheless, critics contend that the transition is unevenly distributednot spread equally or fairly across a group分布不均: nearly forty percent of firms remain confined to small-scale pilot projects, while full-time vacancies suitable for university graduates have fallen by roughly sixty percent since 2022, with entry-level administrative roles hit hardest of allaffected most severely compared to others受影響最深.

By the same token, employers appear notably more cautiouscareful to avoid risk or danger謹慎 about future hiring than in previous years, with a larger share now expecting headcount reductions than increases — the weakest outlook in recent memory. For secondary school students weighing their eventual career paths, the implication seems clear: technical literacy in AI is fast becoming a baseline expectationa minimum standard that is assumed or required基本要求 rather than a specialised advantage, and traditional entry routes into the workforce can no longer be taken for granted.

中文翻譯

香港勞動市場正經歷《中國日報》形容為根本性的轉變,人工智能在各行業的應用速度遠超大多數僱主預期。目前約四分之一的本地機構表示正廣泛部署人工智能於日常運作,比率是一年前的三倍;據受訪行政人員指出,理解並應用這項技術,已成為各層級僱員最關鍵的必備技能。

普遍認為,這種轉變帶有雙面刃的性質。與人工智能相關的電子產品出口貨值大幅增長,單是今年已有數百家企業在本港設立或擴展業務,反映這項技術正帶動實質經濟動力。然而,批評者認為,這場轉型分布不均:近四成企業仍停留於小規模試點項目階段,而適合大學畢業生的全職職位空缺,自2022年以來已下跌約六成,當中初級行政職位受影響最深。

同樣地,僱主對未來招聘明顯較以往謹慎,預期裁減人手的比例現已超過預期增聘的比例——是近年最疲弱的前景。對於正在衡量未來職業路向的中學生而言,啟示似乎十分清楚:人工智能的技術素養正迅速成為基本要求,而非專門優勢,而傳統的入職途徑亦再也不能被視為必然。
