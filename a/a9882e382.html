English

Hong Kong's government has signalled it will not automatically rule out channelling public moneydirecting funds towards a particular purpose撥款資助 towards artificial intelligence-generated films, as it carries out a sweeping review of the Film Development Fund. Secretary for Culture, Sports and Tourism Rosanna Law told lawmakers on Monday that the review, first flagged in this year's Policy Address, will examine how far the fund's long-standing rules should adapt to emerging production technologiesnew technical methods that are only beginning to be widely used新興的製作技術.

The question was raised by lawmaker Lawrence Tang Fei, who pointed to rapid advances in AI film-making tools and the mainland's increasingly aggressive embracean eager and determined acceptance of something積極擁抱 of the technology. Hong Kong's fund already has some exposure to the field, having previously backed an AI Hub at this year's FILMART, suggesting officials are not starting from a position of complete resistance. Even so, Law stressed that key details remain unsettled, including whether a production would need to be wholly AI-generatedcreated entirely by artificial intelligence with no human-made elements完全由人工智能製作 to qualify, or whether hybrid works combining human and machine input could also apply.

The debate exposes a deeper tension at the heart ofa central conflict within a situation核心矛盾 the fund's mission. It has historically prioritised sustaining employment and livelihoods across the local film industry, from technicians to supporting actors, many of whose jobs could be rendered redundantmade unnecessary or unneeded, especially regarding a job變得多餘 by AI tools that can generate scenes without large crews. Whether Hong Kong can reward technological innovation while still safeguarding the livelihoodsprotecting the means by which people earn a living保障生計 of its film workers will likely shape the fund's final rules.

中文翻譯

香港政府表示，在全面檢討電影發展基金的過程中，不會一開始就排除撥款資助人工智能生成的電影。文化體育及旅遊局局長羅淑佩星期一向議員表示，這項今年《施政報告》中率先提出的檢討，將研究基金一向的規則應在多大程度上適應新興的製作技術。

這個問題是由議員鄧飛提出的，他指出人工智能電影製作工具發展迅速，加上內地日益積極擁抱這項技術。香港的基金其實早已與這個領域有所接觸，曾經資助今年香港國際影視展的人工智能展區，顯示當局並非完全抗拒這項科技。即使如此，羅淑佩強調多項關鍵細節仍未確定，包括作品是否需要完全由人工智能製作才符合資格，又或結合人手與機器製作的混合作品是否同樣適用。

這場爭論暴露出基金宗旨的核心矛盾。基金一向以維持本地電影業的就業及生計為首要目標，涵蓋技術人員以至配角演員，而當中不少職位可能因為人工智能工具能夠在無需大量工作人員下製作畫面而變得多餘。香港能否在獎勵科技創新的同時，仍然為電影業從業員保障生計，將可能左右基金最終的規則。
