English

A foreign domestic helper has been arrested on suspicion of ill-treating a childdeliberately harming or mistreating a child in one’s care虐待兒童 after the mother of a four-year-old girl accused her of spitting into the toddler's food at their home in Kennedy Town. Police said the mother lodged a complaint at a local station on Friday morning after reportedly witnessing the incident at The Belcher's, a private housing estate on Hong Kong Island, and that officers arrested the 37-year-old Filipino helper the same day.

Investigators said preliminary findingsthe first, not-yet-final results of an investigation初步調查結果 supported the mother's account, though the case remains under review and no charges have been laid. The episode has reignited public anxiety over the power imbalancean unequal relationship in which one side has far more control than the other權力不對等 between live-in helpers and the families who employ them, with commentators noting that young children, who cannot easily report mistreatment themselves, are especially vulnerable when left largely in a helper's care for long stretches of the day.

Migrant worker advocates urged the public not to rush to judgement, pointing out that Hong Kong's roughly 370,000 foreign domestic helpers often endure gruelling schedulesextremely demanding and exhausting daily routines繁重的工作時間表 with little rest, which can erode patience even among otherwise caring workers. At the same time, child protection groups called for clearer reporting channelsofficial routes through which concerns or complaints can be raised舉報渠道 so that signs of distress in very young children are caught earlier, and for employers to install basic safeguards such as occasional unannounced check-ins rather than relying on covert surveillancesecretly watching someone without their knowledge秘密監視 alone.

中文翻譯

一名外籍家庭傭工因涉嫌虐待兒童被捕，事緣一名四歲女童的母親指控她在堅尼地城寓所內向女童的食物吐口水。警方表示，事主母親於上週五早上在一間警署報案，稱目擊事件發生於港島堅尼地城的私人屋苑寶翠園，警方同日將該37歲菲律賓籍傭工拘捕。

調查人員表示，初步調查結果支持事主母親的說法，但案件仍在覆核中，暫未有人被落案起訴。事件再次引發公眾對住家傭工與僱主家庭之間權力不對等的憂慮，有評論指出，年幼子女難以自行舉報受虐情況，若長時間主要由傭工照顧，處境尤其脆弱。

外勞權益團體呼籲市民切勿倉卒判斷，指出本港約37萬名外籍家庭傭工經常要應付繁重的工作時間表，休息時間有限，即使本身有愛心的傭工也可能因此失去耐性。與此同時，保護兒童團體呼籲設立更清晰的舉報渠道，以便及早發現年幼子女的異常表現，亦促請僱主採取基本保障措施，例如偶爾進行突擊探訪，而非單靠秘密監視。
