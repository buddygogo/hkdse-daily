English

Shares of Shanghai Biren Technology, a Hong Kong-listed designer of artificial intelligence chips, tumbled more than twelve per centfell sharply and suddenly in value急跌超過百分之十二 on Thursday after the firm disclosed a fresh share placementthe sale of new company shares directly to selected investors, outside a public offering配售新股 to raise roughly HK$4 billion. Placing agents were engaged on a best-effort basisan arrangement in which an agent tries to sell as many shares as possible but gives no guarantee of success不保證包銷的盡力基礎 to sell 130 million new shares at a price about ten per cent below the stock's last close, a discount steep enough to send holders rushing to sell before their stakes were diluted.

This is the firm's second placement since its January listing, after a July round that raised HK$7 billion; the two together have pulled in over HK$11 billion in three months. Biren said most proceeds would secure manufacturing capacitythe maximum amount a factory or supplier can produce within a given period生產產能 ahead of the commercial launchthe point at which a product is officially made available for sale to customers商業推出 of its next chips. Despite the losses, revenue surgedincome from sales increased sharply and rapidly收入急升 roughly twenty-one-fold in the first half of 2026, even as the firm remained loss-makingoperating at a financial loss rather than a profit處於虧損狀態, with its deficit narrowing from 1.6 billion yuan to 377 million yuan.

The repeated fundraising reflects how capital-intensiverequiring very large amounts of money to operate or expand資本密集 chipmaking remains, even for firms riding China's push for chip self-sufficiencythe ability to design and produce semiconductors domestically without relying on foreign suppliers晶片自給自足 amid tightening access to foreign technology. Rivals Moore Threads and MetaX are separately preparing Hong Kong listings, underscoring how the city's exchange has become a key financing stage for an industry racing to narrow the gap with overseas leaders.

中文翻譯

香港上市的人工智能芯片設計商上海璧仞科技（Biren Technology）股價周四急跌超過百分之十二，原因是該公司披露一項新的配售新股計劃，擬籌集約四十億港元。配售代理以不保證包銷的盡力基礎方式，以較股份上一個收市價折讓約一成的價格，出售一億三千萬股新股，折讓幅度之大，促使現有股東趕於股權進一步被攤薄前拋售持股。

這是該公司自今年一月上市以來的第二次配售，此前七月的一輪配售已籌得七十億港元；兩輪合計在短短三個月內已籌得逾一百一十億港元。璧仞表示，大部分新資金將用於確保生產產能及零部件供應，為新一代芯片的商業推出作準備，餘額則分別用於研發及營運資金。儘管仍然虧損，公司今年上半年收入急升約二十一倍，即使仍然處於虧損狀態，同期虧損額亦由十六億元人民幣收窄至三億七千七百萬元人民幣。

一再集資的情況反映，即使是乘著中國推動晶片自給自足、以應對外國技術限制收緊的東風，芯片製造行業仍然極為資本密集。對手芯片商摩爾線程及沐曦亦分別籌備在香港上市，顯示本港交易所已成為這場追趕海外龍頭企業競賽中的重要融資平台。
