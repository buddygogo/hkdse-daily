English

Hong Kong this week hosted the APEC Finance Ministers' Meeting for the first time, bringing finance chiefs from across the Asia-Pacific to the city as part of the broader APEC China 2026 calendar ahead of next month's APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting in Shenzhen. Addressing the gathering, Chief Executive John Lee Ka-chiu described Hong Kong as a super connectora place that links different economies or systems together, facilitating flows of trade, capital and people超級聯繫人 and “super value-adder” between the Chinese mainland and the rest of the world, pointing to the city's common-law systema legal system based on judicial precedent and case law rather than solely on statutes普通法制度, low taxes and zero tariffs as evidence of its continued relevance as an international financial huba city that serves as a major centre for banking, investment and other financial services金融中心.

The two-day ministerial-level meetinga formal gathering attended by government ministers rather than lower-ranking officials部長級會議 concluded on Wednesday with the release of the 2026 APEC Finance Ministers' joint statementan official declaration issued and agreed upon by multiple parties聯合聲明, in which members pledged closer cooperation on economic resilience amid wider global uncertainty, though it offered few new specifics. After the formal session, Lee and senior officials met the mainland's finance minister, Lan Fo'an, at Government House to exchange views on the economic and financial development of the mainland and Hong Kong.

Speaking separately, Financial Secretary Paul Chan struck a more cautious tone, warning that the global economy's resilience remained uneven and that the outlook carried significant downside riskthe possibility that an economic outcome will turn out worse than expected下行風險, driven by persistent geopolitical tensionsstrained relations between countries or regions arising from political, strategic or territorial disputes地緣政治緊張局勢 and continuing trade uncertaintya lack of predictability about the future rules, tariffs or flows of international trade貿易前景不確定性. His remarks underscored the balancing act facing Hong Kong: showcasing itself as an open, rules-based financial centre at a summit built around a freshly signed joint statement, while navigating a global order increasingly shaped by great-power rivalry rather than multilateral consensus.

中文翻譯

香港本週首次主辦亞太經合組織（APEC）財長會議，吸引區內多國財金首長來港出席，是「APEC中國2026」系列活動之一，為下月在深圳舉行的APEC經濟領袖會議作準備。行政長官李家超在會上致辭，形容香港是中國內地與世界之間的超級聯繫人，亦是「超級增值人」，並指出本港的普通法制度、低稅率及零關稅，正正體現香港作為國際金融中心的持續價值。

這場為期兩天的部長級會議於星期三閉幕，並發表二零二六年APEC財長聯合聲明，各成員承諾在環球局勢不穩下加強經濟韌性方面的合作，但聲明內容未見太多新猶。會議結束後，李家超與一眾高級官員在禮賓府會見國家財政部部長藍佛安，就內地與香港的經濟金融發展交換意見。

財政司司長陳茂波則語氣較為謹慎，他表示環球經濟的韌力參差不齊，前景的下行風險較大，原因是地緣政治緊張局勢持續，加上貿易前景不確定性未見緩和。他的言論反映香港所面對的兩難：一方面要在簽署聯合聲明的峰會上，展現自己作為開放、具規則基礎的金融中心的形象；另一方面則要在一個日益由大國競逐、而非多邊共識主導的國際秩序中尋找定位。
