It is widely argued that university orientation camps, while intended to foster camaraderiebuild friendship and mutual trust培養情誼 among new students, have in recent years become associated with troubling practices. This controversy resurfaced this week at the City University of Hong Kong, where fresh photographs surfacedcame to public attention浮現；曝光 showing male and female students with visible bruising on their thighs and red marks across their backs. The images circulated widely on social media only days after an earlier video, depicting a shirtless male student carrying a woman dressed in red underwear, had already gone viral and drawn public criticism.

Critics contend that such initiation rituals amount to a form of hazinghumiliating or dangerous initiation activities迎新迫害；欺凌式迎新 that normalises physical harm under the guise of tradition. In response, the university has established a disciplinary investigation committee, which is currently interviewing both the camp's organisers and its participants in order to establish accountability. The student union committee responsible for staging the event, known by the name “Messianis”, has since announced its own dissolution, a move widely seen as an attempt to contain the falloutlimit the negative consequences of an incident控制事件的後果 from the scandal.

Nevertheless, commentators point out that dissolving a single committee does little to address the underlying culture that permits such practices to persist year after year. By the same token, it is argued that universities must move beyond reactive damage control and instead implement robust, preventive safeguards — including clear codes of conduct and independent monitoring — if genuine reform is to be achieved. The episode has reignited a broader debate about student welfare, peer pressure, and the balance between tradition and safety on Hong Kong's university campuses.

中文翻譯

大學迎新營雖然原意是培養新生之間的情誼,但近年卻屢屢被指與令人憂慮的做法扯上關係。這場爭議本週在香港城市大學再度浮現,新曝光的照片顯示多名男女學生大腿上有明顯瘀傷,背部亦有紅印。這些照片在社交媒體上廣泛流傳,距離早前一段片段曝光、引發公眾批評僅僅數日——片中一名赤裸上身的男學生揹著一名穿著紅色內衣的女子。

有評論指出,這類迎新儀式實質上屬於一種迎新迫害,以傳統之名將身體傷害正常化。校方已回應,成立紀律調查委員會,現正約見活動籌辦人員及參加者,以釐清責任誰屬。負責籌辦是次活動、名為「彌賽尼斯」的學生會屬會其後宣布自行解散,此舉普遍被視為試圖控制事件所造成的後果。

不過,評論者指出,單單解散一個委員會,無助解決容讓此類做法年復一年持續存在的深層文化。同樣道理,有意見認為,大學必須超越被動的危機處理,轉而推行具體而有力的預防措施——包括訂立清晰的行為守則及引入獨立監察機制,方能實現真正的改革。事件亦重新引發社會對學生福祉、同儕壓力,以及香港各大學校園內傳統與安全之間如何取得平衡的廣泛討論。
