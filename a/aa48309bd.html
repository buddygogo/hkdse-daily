English

The Hong Kong, China delegation has wrapped up its campaign at the fifth Asian Para Games in Aichi-Nagoya with 41 medals, comprising 3 gold, 14 silver and 24 bronze, across five different sports. In recognition of this haula quantity of something gained or obtained收穫；戰績, the Hong Kong Jockey Club is distributing over HK$5.56 million directly to medallists through its Athlete Incentive Awards Scheme, a three-year sponsorship arrangement run jointly with the Hong Kong Sports Institute. Individual gold medallists receive roughly HK$250,000, with silver and bronze winners earning HK$125,000 and HK$62,500 respectively, while team events command even higher payouts.

Boccia emerged as the standout discipline, delivering the squad's best-ever return of two golds, three silvers and three bronzes. The final gold of the Games came on the last day of competition, when pairs specialists Leung Yuk-wing and Cheung Yuen clinchedto win something after a close or hard-fought contest奪得；贏得 victory in the mixed pairs BC4 category. Eighty Hong Kong athletes had competed across ten sports throughout the week-long Games, a scale of participationthe act of taking part in an activity參與 that organisers say reflects deepening investment in para-sport pathways.

Chief Executive John Lee personally congratulated the delegation on its achievements, which officials hope will galvaniseto inspire or encourage sudden action or enthusiasm激勵；推動 greater public interest and funding for para-sport in Hong Kong. The Games closed with a ceremony marking the formal handover to Doha, which will host the next edition. For the city's young athletes, the result offers fresh evidence that sustained financial backing, such as the Jockey Club's incentive scheme, can translate directly into podium success on the regional stage.

中文翻譯

中國香港代表團在日本愛知名古屋舉行的第五屆亞洲殘疾人運動會中,以五個項目共奪得四十一面獎牌,包括三金十四銀二十四銀圓滿結束賽事(三金、十四銀、二十四銅)。為表揚這份戰績,香港賽馬會透過與香港體育學院合辦為期三年的「運動員奬勵計劃」,向獎牌得主直接頒發逾五百五十六萬港元獎金。個人金牌得主可獲約二十五萬港元,銀牌及銅牌得主則分別獲十二萬五千元及六萬二千五百元,而團隊項目的獎金更高。

硬地滾球成為表現最突出的項目,為代表隊帶來歷來最佳戰績——兩金三銀三銅。賽事最後一面金牌於賽事最後一天誕生,由混合雙人BC4級選手梁玉瑩及張源奪得勝利。整個為期一週的賽事期間,八十名香港運動員於十個項目中參與角逐,主辦方表示,這個規模反映本港對殘疾人體育發展路徑的投資日益加深。

行政長官李家超親自向代表團致賀,官方期望這些成績能激勵公眾對殘疾人體育投入更大關注與資源。賽事以交接儀式閉幕,象徵下一屆主辦權正式移交多哈。對本港年輕運動員而言,這次成果再次證明持續的財政支持,例如賽馬會的奬勵計劃,確實能轉化為區域賽場上實實在在的獎牌佳績。
