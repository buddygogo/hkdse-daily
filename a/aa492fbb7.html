English

Hong Kong secured the first gold medal of its campaign at the 2026 Asian Para Games in Nagoya on Wednesday, after boccia player Vivian Lau Wai-yan defeated her own compatriota fellow citizen from the same country or territory, here used of a teammate from the same delegation同胞（此處指同一代表隊的隊友） Cheung Yuen in an all-Hong Kong final. Bocciaa Paralympic precision ball sport in which players release soft balls toward a target, designed for athletes with severe impairments affecting motor control硬地滾球（殘奧會的一項講求準繩度的球類運動） is a precision sport designed for athletes with severe impairments affecting motor control, in which competitors roll or release soft leather balls toward a target ball from a fixed position on court. The sport operates under a classification systema framework that groups para-athletes into categories based on the type and severity of their impairment, so that competitors face others with comparable abilities殘疾分級制度 that groups players by the degree of physical impairment, ensuring that athletes with comparable support needs compete against one another rather than against the field as a whole.

The gold formed part of a strong day for the Hong Kong delegation, which added four silver and four bronze medals across several disciplines, lifting the city's overall medal tallythe running total of medals won by a team or country during a multi-sport event獎牌總數 at the Games to 21 — one gold, eight silver and twelve bronze — and placing Hong Kong 17th in the medal standingsthe ranking of countries or teams according to the number and type of medals they have won獎牌榜排名. The Games, running from 18 to 24 October across venues in Aichi and Nagoya, mark the fifth edition of the regional competition, held three years after Hong Kong's elite training squada specially selected group of top-performing athletes who train together at the highest competitive level精英訓練隊 returned from the 2023 Hangzhou Games with a then-record 47 medals.

Local sports officials have pointed to Wednesday's results as evidence that sustained parasports fundingfinancial support, usually from government or sports bodies, dedicated to training and competition for athletes with disabilities殘疾人士體育資助 and improved training facilities are beginning to pay off, with Hong Kong's para-athletes increasingly rivalling their able-bodied counterparts for public attention. Advocates for disability sport argue that each podium appearancean occasion on which an athlete finishes among the top three and is honoured on the winners' platform登上頒獎台 helps normalise the presence of athletes with impairments in mainstream sporting narratives, rather than confining their achievements to a separate, lower-profile category. With three days of competition remaining, officials have not ruled out further medals from a squad many regard as deeper and more competitive than in previous editions.

中文翻譯

香港代表團周三在名古屋舉行的2026年亞洲殘疾人運動會中,贏得今屆賽事首金——硬地滾球選手劉慧茵在全港內部對決的決賽中,擊敗同為代表隊的同胞隊友Cheung Yuen。硬地滾球是專為肢體活動能力嚴重受限的運動員而設的講求準繩度的運動項目,參賽者須從場上固定位置,將軟身皮球擲向或滾向目標球。該項運動設有殘疾分級制度,按運動員肢體損傷的嚴重程度分組,確保支援需要相若的選手彼此對壘,而非與所有選手一概比較。

這面金牌是香港代表團表現出色的一天的縮影——當日港隊在多個項目中再添四銀四銅,令代表團在今屆運動會的獎牌總數增至21面(一金、八銀、十二銅),在獎牌榜排名中位列第十七。本屆賽事為期自10月18日至24日,在愛知及名古屋多個場館舉行,是這項地區性賽事的第五屆,距離港隊精英訓練隊在2023年杭州亞殘運創下當時破紀錄的47面獎牌,僅三年之隔。

本地體育官員指,周三的成績證明持續的殘疾人士體育資助及訓練設施的改善,正逐漸見到成效,香港殘疾運動員在公眾關注度上,正日益追上健全運動員。殘疾體育的支持者認為,每一次登上頒獎台的機會,都有助令傷殘運動員的身影,更自然地融入主流體育敘事之中,而非被局限於另一個較少受關注的類別。隨着賽事尚餘三日,官員未排除港隊這支普遍被視為陣容更深厚、競爭力更強的隊伍,能再添獎牌。
