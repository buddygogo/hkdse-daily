It is widely reported that the Centre for Health Protection has recorded three additional severe paediatric influenza cases, all involving previously healthy children who had shown no history of travel during their incubation periodthe time between infection and the appearance of symptoms潛伏期. Health officials have accordingly renewed their appealrepeated a request or urgent call again再次呼籲 for members of the public, particularly children and the elderly, to seek vaccination ahead of the Seasonal Influenza Vaccination Programme, which begins on 17 September.

Since the end of June, a cumulative total of sixteen severe paediatric cases have been documentedofficially recorded in writing記錄在案, of which eight patients have since recovered and been discharged, two remain in a stable condition, three are classified as serious, two are critical, and one has tragically died. In one instance, two classmates of an affected six-year-old girl subsequently developed mild symptoms, though neither required hospitalisation; the school concerned has since been advised to step upincrease or intensify an effort加強 infection-control measures such as more frequent disinfection and closer monitoring of student attendance.

Nevertheless, with Hong Kong having entered its influenza season as early as late June and activity levels remaining stubbornly highpersistently high despite efforts to lower it居高不下, the CHP has cautioned that the coming weeks — coinciding with the start of the new school term — could see a further uptick in transmission. By the same token, parents and school staff alike are being urged to remain vigilantwatchful and alert to possible danger保持警惕 for early symptoms, to observe good personal hygiene, and to seek timely medical attention, lest a single overlooked case snowballgrow rapidly in size or seriousness迅速惡化；滾雪球式擴大 into a wider school outbreak.
