English

Kai Tak Sports Park has welcomed more than 2.8 million spectators to its Main Stadium in the 18 months since opening, the venue operator said this week, as the former airport site cements its role as Hong Kong’s new mega-event huba large, purpose-built venue capable of hosting very large-scale events大型活動樞紐. Since opening in March 2025, the park has staged more than 190 international and local eventsevents that include both overseas productions and ones organised within Hong Kong國際及本地活動, ranging from football friendlies and track meets to large-scale concerts.

The venue’s concert calendar is only getting busier. This week alone, K-pop veterans BIGBANG are playing a sold-out three-night run at the Main Stadium to mark the group’s 20th anniversary, and organisers have confirmed further headlining performancesconcerts in which a single famous act is the main, most important attraction主打表演 by BTS, Charlie Puth and Bruno Mars in the months ahead. The steady stream of A-list bookingsengagements by the most famous and sought-after performers in the entertainment industry一線藝人演出 has helped the 50,000-seat stadium establish itself among Asia’s leading concert destinations within a remarkably short time.

Tourism officials have credited the park with driving footfallcausing a noticeable increase in the number of people visiting a place帶動人流 to the wider Kai Tak district, with nearby hotels and the adjoining shopping mall reporting a knock-on boost in business on concert weekends. Transport officials are meanwhile moving to ease congestionovercrowding that slows or blocks the normal flow of people or traffic擁堵 around the Tuen Ma line’s Kai Tak station, with extra train frequencies planned to help the venue cope with crowds of tens of thousands arriving for a single show. Analysts say the park’s early success illustrates how a single well-designed mega-event venuea site built specifically to attract and host very large events大型活動場館 can reshape a neighbourhood’s economy, though some residents living nearby have also raised concerns about noise disturbancean unwanted and disruptive level of sound affecting those living or working nearby噪音困擾 on concert nights.

中文翻譯

啟德體育園主場館自開幕以來18個月內，已接待超過280萬名觀眾入場，場館營運方本周表示，這座前機場舊址正逐步確立其作為香港新大型活動樞紐的地位。體育園自2025年3月開幕以來，已舉辦超過190項國際及本地活動，項目涵蓋足球友賽、田徑賽事以至大型演唱會。

場館的演唱會檔期只會愈趨繁忙。單是本周，韓國組合BIGBANG已於主場館一連三晚舉行門票售罄的二十周年紀念演出，而主辦方亦已確認未來數月將迎來防彈少年團（BTS）、Charlie Puth及Bruno Mars等主打表演。接連不斷的一線藝人演出，令這座可容納五萬人的主場館在短時間內，已躍身成為亞洲區內數一數二的演唱會勝地。

旅遊官員表示，體育園有助帶動人流進入啟德一帶，附近酒店及毗鄰商場亦報稱於演唱會周末生意有所提升。與此同時，運輸部門正著手緩解屯馬線啟德站周邊的擁堵情況，計劃加密班次，以應付單場演出動輒數萬人湧至的人流壓力。分析人士指出，體育園的初步成功，正好說明一座設計得宜的大型活動場館如何能夠重塑一個社區的經濟面貌，不過亦有附近居民對演唱會舉行期間的噪音困擾表達關注。
