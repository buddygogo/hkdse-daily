English

Hong Kong police revealed this week that residents lost more than $60 million to online investment scamsfraudulent schemes that trick victims into believing they are making a profitable investment投資騙案 in just seven days, with more than 30 cases reported across the city. The single largest loss involved a woman who was lured into a bogus platforma fake service or website set up to deceive people虛假平台 claiming to offer lucrative returns on environmental projects, ultimately parting with $45 million.

According to police, the victim was first approached online and gradually persuaded to open an account on the platform, where a dashboard showed what appeared to be steadily rising returns. She then made more than 60 separate transfers into various personal bank accountsbank accounts held in an individual's own name, often used here to move stolen funds個人銀行帳戶, acting on instructions relayed by fake customer-service representativesstaff who claim to handle customer inquiries and requests客戶服務代表 who assured her that larger deposits would unlock bigger profits. By the time she grew suspicious, her life savingsall the money a person has accumulated over many years畢生積蓄 had already been transferred to accounts controlled by so-called money mulespeople who move illegally obtained money on behalf of others, usually through their own bank accounts洗錢工具人.

Officers urged the public to treat unsolicited investment offers, especially those promising guaranteed or unusually high returns, as a red flaga warning sign that something may not be legitimate危險訊號. They also advised residents to verify any platform's licence with the Securities and Futures Commission before transferring money, and to be especially wary of pressure to act quickly or to use personal rather than corporate bank accounts for payment. Police said investigations into this latest wave of scams were ongoing, with officers tracing the flow of funds through multiple accounts in an effort to identify the organisers.

中文翻譯

香港警方本週透露，過去七日內有市民因網上投資騙案損失超過6,000萬元，全港共錄得逾30宗案件。當中損失最大的一宗涉及一名女子，她被誘騙加入一個聲稱提供環保項目豐厚回報的虛假平台，最終損失4,500萬元。

警方指出，受害人最初在網上被搭訕，並逐步被說服在該平台開戶，平台的儀表板顯示回報持續上升。她隨後分開逾60次，將款項轉入多個個人銀行帳戶，過程中一直按照冒充客戶服務代表的指示行事，對方聲稱存入更多款項便可解鎖更高利潤。到她開始生疑時，她的畢生積蓄早已轉入由所謂洗錢工具人控制的帳戶。

警方促請市民將未經請求的投資邀約，尤其是承諾保證或異常高回報的項目，視為危險訊號。他們亦建議市民在轉帳前，先向證券及期貨事務監察委員會核實平台的牌照，並特別留意任何要求盡快行動，或要求使用個人（而非公司）銀行帳戶付款的要求。警方表示正就最新一輪騙案展開調查，追查款項流向多個帳戶的軌跡，以圖辨識幕後主腦。
