English

A new profile has traced how Tony Leung Chiu-wai became, by wide consensus, the most garlandedwidely honoured with awards and prizes獲獎無數 actor in Hong Kong cinema history, even though he would be the last person to say so himself. Across four decades, Leung has built a career on interior performancesacting that conveys emotion through subtle inner feeling rather than outward expression內斂的演繹 — minute shifts of the eyes, a half-finished sentence, a held breath — that let him command the screendominate attention on screen through presence alone, without needing loud or dramatic action單憑氣場主宰銀幕 without ever raising his voice. From television soap operas in the 1980s to arthouse classics and, more recently, a Marvel blockbuster, he has rarely repeated himself, yet audiences and critics alike have continued to call him unmistakably, quietly, himself.

His decades-long partnership with director Wong Kar-wai produced some of Hong Kong's most internationally admired films, including the Cannes-winning In the Mood for Love, while later roles in Ang Lee's Lust, Caution and the crime thriller Infernal Affairs showed a willingness to inhabit morally ambiguous characters that most stars would avoid for fear of tarnishing their imagedamaging one's public reputation or image損害個人形象. In 2023, Venice awarded him a lifetime achievement honouran award recognising an entire career's contribution rather than a single work終身成就獎 usually reserved for directors and veterans twice his age, a recognition that cemented his status not merely as a Hong Kong star but as one of world cinema's most respected actors.

In an industry increasingly built on self-promotiondeliberately drawing public attention to oneself in order to build fame自我宣傳 — curated social media feeds, carefully managed public appearances — Leung remains a conspicuous exception, granting few interviews and offering even fewer opinions on his own legacy. For a city whose entertainment industry has long doubled as a source of cultural soft powera country or city's influence gained through culture and reputation rather than force軟實力, his example raises a pointed question for Hong Kong's next generation of performers: whether lasting recognition is better earned through visibility, or through the patient, unglamorouslacking excitement or outward appeal; plain and unremarkable毫不亮麗 accumulation of craft.

中文翻譯

一份最新人物專訪，追溯梁朝偉如何在廣泛共識下，成為香港電影史上獲獎最多的演員——儘管他自己絕不會這樣形容自己。在長達四十年的演藝生涯中，梁朝偉建立起一套以內斂的演繹為本的表演方式——眼神的細微轉動、一句未說完的話、一次刻意按捺的呼吸——讓他無需提高嗓子，便能單憑氣場主宰銀幕。從八十年代的電視劇集，到藝術電影經典，以至近年的一部漫威電影，他甚少重複過往的演出，但無論觀眾抑或評論家，依然異口同聲地稱他——不動聲色，卻獨一無二。

他與導演王家衛長達數十年的合作，造就了多部香港最獲國際讚譽的電影，包括在康城封帝的《花樣年華》；而他後來在李安《色，戒》及警匪片《無間道》中的角色，亦顯示他甘願飾演道德模糊的人物——這類角色，大多數明星都會因擔心損害個人形象而迴避。二零二三年，威尼斯電影節向他頒發終身成就獎——這項榮譽通常只授予年紀比他長一倍的導演或影壇老將，此舉確立了他的地位：他不僅是香港明星，更是全球影壇最受尊崇的演員之一。

在一個愈趨依賴自我宣傳的行業裡——經過精心策劃的社交媒體形象、刻意安排的公開場合——梁朝偉始終是一個明顯的例外，鮮少接受訪問，更甚少評論自己的影壇地位。對於一個娛樂產業長期兼具文化軟實力角色的城市而言，他的例子向香港新一代演員提出一個尖銳問題：持久的聲望，究竟應該靠曝光率贏得，還是靠耐心而毫不亮麗地累積演技而來？
