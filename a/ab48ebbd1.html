English

Hong Kong health authorities have issued a fresh appeal for parents to vaccinate their children as the city faces a marked increase in flu-related hospitalisations among young people. According to recent public health data, a growing number of paediatric patientspatients who are children兒科病人 have presented with high fevers exceeding 40 degrees Celsius that persistcontinue without stopping持續 for several days, alongside other severe influenza symptoms. Doctors have noted that this summer's flu season has proven unusually persistent, straining paediatric wards and prompting renewed calls for widespread immunisationthe process of being made immune to a disease, typically by vaccination免疫接種 before the situation worsens further.

It is widely argued that early vaccination remains the most effective safeguard against severe illness, particularly among younger children whose immune systems are less equipped to fight off aggressive strains of the virus. Public health experts contend that a robust vaccination programme not only protects individual children but also plays a pivotal role inis extremely important in擔當關鍵角色 curbing transmissionpreventing a disease from spreading further遏止傳播 within schools and households, thereby easing pressure on an already stretched healthcare system. By the same token, health officials argue that timely intervention could prevent a repeat of previous flu seasons, during which emergency departments were pushed to capacity.

Nevertheless, critics contend that vaccine uptake among Hong Kong families remains inconsistent, hampered by lingering vaccine hesitancyreluctance to be vaccinated despite the vaccine being available疫苗猶豫 and a lack of awareness regarding the severity of this year's outbreak. Some parents remain reluctant, citing mild side effects or scepticism about the necessity of annual immunisation for otherwise healthy children. Proponents counter that the current spike in severe cases should serve as a wake-up callan event that makes people realise a problem needs urgent attention警號, urging households to weigh the proven benefits of vaccination against comparatively minor risks. Whether public health messaging can shift entrenched attitudesfirmly held beliefs that are difficult to change根深蒂固的態度 in time to curb this outbreak remains to be seen, but the accumulating clinical evidenceevidence obtained through medical observation or treatment臨床證據 lends considerable weight to the case for prompt action.

中文翻譯

香港衞生當局再次呼籲家長為子女接種疫苗,因為全港兒童因流感入院的個案顯著上升。根據近期公共衞生數據顯示,愈來愈多兒科病人出現超過攝氏40度的高燒,並持續多日,同時伴隨其他嚴重流感症狀。醫生指出,今年夏季流感季節異常持久,令兒科病房不勝負荷,促使當局再次呼籲市民廣泛接受疫苗接種,以免情況進一步惡化。

有人認為,及早接種疫苗仍是預防重症最有效的方法,對免疫系統較弱、難以抵抗病毒強勁變異株的幼童而言尤其重要。公共衞生專家指出,完善的疫苗接種計劃不僅保護個別兒童,亦擔當關鍵角色,協助遏止病毒傳播於學校及家庭之間,從而紓緩已經不勝負荷的醫療系統壓力。與此同時,衞生官員認為,及時介入可避免過往流感季節的情況重演,當時急症室一度逼近飽和。

然而,批評者認為,香港家庭的疫苗接種率仍然參差不齊,原因包括揮之不去的疫苗猶豫情緒,以及對今年疫情嚴重程度認知不足。部分家長仍然猶豫,理由包括擔心輕微副作用,或質疑讓原本健康的兒童每年接種疫苗是否必要。支持者則反駁,指現時重症病例急升的情況應被視為警號,促請各家庭權衡接種疫苗已證實的效益,相對其相對輕微的風險。公共衞生宣傳能否及時扭轉根深蒂固的觀念以遏止疫情,仍是未知之數,但不斷累積的臨床證據,大大加強了即時採取行動的理據。
