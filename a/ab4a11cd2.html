English

Hong Kong's unemployment ratethe percentage of the labour force that is without work but actively seeking employment失業率 climbed to 3.9 percent in the three months to September, according to figures released by the Census and Statistics Department on Thursday, as a wave of fresh graduatespeople who have recently completed their studies and are entering the workforce for the first time新畢業生 and school leavers joined an already competitive job market. The seasonally adjusted rate edged up from 3.8 percent in the preceding period, with the number of unemployed residents reaching approximately 155,000, a rise of roughly 1,100 from the previous three-month window. The labour forcethe total number of people who are either employed or actively looking for work勞動人口 itself expanded modestly, growing by about 4,100 to reach 3.81 million, even as total employment inched up by only around 3,000.

Government data further revealed that underemploymenta situation in which workers have jobs but are not working as many hours as they would like, or are overqualified for their roles就業不足 rose from 1.9 to 2.0 percent, with particular strain evident in the food and beverage and retail sectors. Meanwhile, job losses were most pronounced in social work and in transport, warehousing and related support services, suggesting that the slowdown is not evenly distributed across industries but instead concentrated in specific, consumption-sensitive corners of the economy. Non-seasonally adjusted figures, which strip out predictable yearly patterns, told a similarly subdued story, with the rate rising to 4.1 percent from 4.0 percent, while youth unemployment among those aged 20 to 29 held steady at a comparatively high 9.1 percent.

Despite the uptick, Secretary for Labour and Welfare Chris Sun struck a tone of cautious optimisma careful, moderate sense of hope that acknowledges remaining risks or uncertainties審慎樂觀, attributing his outlook to sustained economic expansion, improving business sentiment and continued growth in visitor arrivalsthe number of tourists entering a place, often used as an indicator of the health of the tourism sector訪客人數, which he expects will support employment in consumption and tourism-related trades. He nonetheless acknowledged that the labour market would remain exposed to external economic headwindsnegative economic forces or conditions that slow down growth or progress經濟逆風, including uncertain global trade conditions, and pledged close monitoring of how such pressures filter through to local entry-level positionsjobs intended for people with little or no prior work experience, often the first step into a particular career初級職位 in the months ahead.

中文翻譯

根據政府統計處周四公布的數據,香港在截至九月的三個月內,失業率攀升至百分之3.9,其中大批新畢業生及離校學生湧入原已競爭激烈的就業市場。經季節性調整後的失業率,由上一期的百分之3.8輕微上升,失業人數增至約15.5萬人,較上一個三個月期間增加約1,100人。勞動人口本身亦溫和擴張,增加約4,100人至381萬人,而總就業人數僅微升約3,000人。

政府數據亦顯示,就業不足率由百分之1.9上升至百分之2.0,飲食及零售行業的壓力尤為明顯。同時,職位流失最嚴重的行業為社會工作服務,以及運輸、倉庫及相關支援服務,顯示經濟放緩並非平均分布於各行業,而是集中在消費相關的特定環節。剔除慣常季節性因素後的數字同樣反映疲弱狀況,失業率由百分之4.0上升至百分之4.1,而20至29歲青年的失業率則維持在相對高企的百分之9.1。

儘管數字有所上升,勞工及福利局局長孫玉菡仍抱持審慎樂觀的態度,他將此歸因於經濟持續擴張、商業信心改善,以及訪客人數持續增長,預期這將有助支撐消費及旅遊相關行業的就業情況。不過,他亦承認勞工市場仍將面對外來經濟逆風,包括環球貿易前景不明朗,並承諾密切監察這些壓力在未來數月如何影響本地初級職位。
