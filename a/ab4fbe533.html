English

CK Asset Holdings has confirmed that three residential towers at its Starter Homes development on Anderson Road, Kwun Tong, will be demolished and rebuilt from scratchtorn down completely and constructed again徹底拆卸並重新興建, after investigators found that steel reinforcement bars across all six blocks had deviated significantlydeparted by a large margin from a standard嚴重偏離標準 from government-approved plans. The Buildings Department has given the green lightgranted official approval批准放行 to remediation proposals for the remaining three towers, allowing structural repair work to proceed without full demolition, though it has yet to receive a formal application to begin tearing down the other blocks.

The scandal first surfaced last year, prompting the government to launch legal proceedingsformal court action法律程序 against the original contractor, with 205 prosecutions filed to date. Ten people connected to the project have also been arrested on suspicion of briberybelieved to have offered or accepted illegal payments涉嫌行賄 and related offences. CK Asset has appointed a new main contractor to oversee the rebuilding, arguing that starting from scratch would prove the most cost-effectivegiving the best value for money spent符合成本效益 solution, even though it means a longer wait for buyers who had already put down deposits on homes sold under a scheme meant to offer first-time buyers flats below market price.

The episode has reignited concerns about quality control in subsidised housing, with commentators warning that repeated scandals risk further eroding public trustgradually wearing away people's confidence侵蝕公眾信任 at a time when the government is counting on Starter Homes to widen the property laddercreate more routes into home ownership擴闊置業階梯 for middle-income families priced out of the private market. Analysts say the case also highlights the broader challenge of policing construction quality, given the scale of building activity the city needs to meet its long-term housing targets. For hundreds of households still waiting to move in, the rebuilding timeline remains the most pressing question, with officials yet to confirm when the demolished towers will be ready for a fresh round of structural approval.

中文翻譯

長江實業集團（CK Asset）證實，位於九龍觀塘安達臣道的「首置」項目中，有三座住宅大廈將會推倒重建，原因是調查人員發現全部六座大廈的鋼筋均嚴重偏離政府批核的圖則標準。屋宇署已就其餘三座大廈的修復方案開綠燈，容許結構修復工程繼續進行，毋須全面拆卸，不過署方表示至今仍未收到正式申請，以展開拆卸其餘大廈的工程。

這宗醜聞早於去年曝光，促使政府對原承建商展開法律程序，至今已提出二百零五宗檢控。另有十人因涉及該項目而被捕，涉嫌行賄及其他相關罪行。長實已委任新的主要承建商負責重建工程，並指從頭興建才是最符合成本效益的做法，儘管這意味著已經支付訂金的買家須等候更長時間——這批單位原本是在資助計劃下，以低於市價出售予首次置業人士。

事件再次引發公眾對資助房屋質量監管的關注，評論指類似醜聞一再發生，恐怕會進一步侵蝕公眾信任，尤其政府正寄望透過「首置」計劃為被私人市場拒諸門外的中產家庭擴闊置業階梯。分析人士指出，事件亦突顯當局在監管建築質量上面對的更大挑戰，因為要達到長遠房屋目標，本港仍需維持龐大的建築工程量。對仍在輪候入伙的數百個家庭而言，重建時間表才是最逼切的問題，官員至今仍未確認拆卸後的大廈何時能完成新一輪結構審批。
