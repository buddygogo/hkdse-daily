English

Financial Secretary Paul Chan has stated that artificial intelligence is set to reshape the job marketto fundamentally change the nature of employment opportunities重塑就業市場 in Hong Kong, generating fresh employment opportunities while simultaneously fuelling economic growthdriving or accelerating economic expansion推動經濟增長. Speaking at the weekend, Mr Chan pointed to a striking year-on-year increase of 53 percent in exports of AI-related electronic products in August alone, a statistic he described as clear evidence that the sector is fast becoming a cornerstonea fundamental and essential part of something基石 of the local economy. This growth, he suggested, reflects surging global demand for hardware manufactured in the city and across the wider region.

It is widely argued that Hong Kong's long-term competitiveness hinges on its capacity to cultivate a workforce equipped with genuine AI literacythe knowledge and skills needed to understand and use AI effectively人工智能素養. Mr Chan stressed that the city urgently requires professionals who not only grasp the intricaciesto fully understand the complex details of something掌握箇中複雜細節 of AI systems but who are also willing to venture into uncharted territoryto attempt something new and unexplored踏足未知領域 by exploring novel applications across industries such as finance, logistics and healthcare. Nevertheless, he was careful to note that certain human capabilities remain irreplaceableimpossible to replace with something or someone else不可替代的, even by the most sophisticated algorithms, implying that critical judgement and interpersonal skills will continue to command a premium in the labour market.

By the same token, the government has pledged to nearly double its expenditure on innovation and technology, aiming to raise research spending to three percent of GDP after 2030. Critics contend that such ambitious targets will only be met if schools and universities respond swiftly, embedding digital and AI skills into mainstream curricula rather than treating them as an afterthought. For secondary students weighing up their future careers, the message is unmistakable: those who proactively upskillto learn new or advanced skills提升技能 themselves now are far more likely to secure a competitive edgean advantage over rivals競爭優勢 in an economy being reshaped by automation at an unprecedented pace.

中文翻譯

財政司司長陳茂波表示,人工智能勢將重塑香港的就業市場,在創造新職位的同時,亦同步推動經濟增長。陳茂波指出,今年八月人工智能相關電子產品出口按年大增百分之五十三,形容這是清楚證據,顯示該行業正迅速成為本地經濟的基石。他認為,這種增長反映全球對本港以至區內製造硬件的需求正在急升。

輿論普遍認為,香港長遠的競爭力,取決於能否培養出具備真正人工智能素養的勞動人口。陳茂波強調,香港急需的,是不單能掌握人工智能系統箇中複雜細節的專才,更要願意踏足未知領域,在金融、物流及醫療等行業探索全新應用。然而,他亦小心指出,即使最先進的演算法,也無法取代某些不可替代的人類能力,意味著批判思維及人際技巧,在勞動市場仍將繼續佔有溢價。

同樣地,政府承諾將創科開支增加近一倍,目標是在2030年後將研發開支提升至本地生產總值的百分之三。批評者認為,這些宏大目標,只有在學校及大學迅速回應下才能達成——將數碼及人工智能技能真正納入主流課程,而非視之為附帶項目。對於正在考慮未來事業的中學生而言,信息相當明確:那些主動提升自身技能的人,將更有可能在被自動化以前所未見速度重塑的經濟中,取得競爭優勢。
