English

The Hong Kong government has unveiled a major upskilling drivean organised effort to teach people new, higher-level skills提升技能行動 aimed at preparing the city's workforce for the growing role of artificial intelligence. Financial Secretary Paul Chan announced that professional AI courses would roll out from November, forming part of a broader HK$50 million initiative targeting 50,000 workers through some 200 training activities over two years. The scheme is designed to reach employees across a wide range of sectors, not merely those already working in technology, reflecting official recognition that AI is set to permeatespread through and become a noticeable feature of something滲透 almost every corner of the economy.

It is widely argued that such a programme is both timely and necessary. Hong Kong has been ranked among the world's leading economies in AI readiness, trailing only New York and London on one recent global index, and the city's computing power is projected to expand dramatically by 2032. Proponents maintain that without a workforce capable of applying these tools effectively, such infrastructure investment risks going to waste. Critics contend that the funding, while welcome, remains modest in scalesmall when compared with what is actually needed規模有限 relative to the scale of disruption AI is expected to bring, and that many workers in vulnerable sectorsindustries where jobs are more likely to be lost or changed易受影響的行業 may still be left behind.

By the same token, education experts point out that upskilling alone cannot substitute for deeper curriculum reform, particularly for young people entering the job market. There is growing consensus that AI literacy should be treated as a foundational competencya basic skill that underlies many other abilities基礎能力 comparable to digital literacy a decade ago, rather than an optional add-on. Whether this latest initiative can be scaled up quickly enough to keep pace with rapid technological change remains an open question, but it nevertheless marks a significant step in the government's effort to ensure the workforce is not caught off guardunprepared for something that happens措手不及 by the AI transition.

中文翻譯

香港政府公布一項大型技能提升計劃,旨在為日益重要的人工智能發展,做好本港勞動人口的準備。財政司司長陳茂波宣布,專業人工智能課程將由十一月起推出,屬於一項五千萬港元計劃的一部分,該計劃透過約二百項培訓活動,於兩年內惠及五萬名在職人士。該計劃的對象涵蓋各行各業的僱員,而非僅限於科技業從業員,反映官方意識到人工智能將滲透幾乎每個經濟環節。

有人認為,這項計劃既切合時機,亦有其必要性。香港在全球人工智能準備程度排名中位居前列,在其中一項全球指數中僅次於紐約和倫敦,而本港的運算能力預計將在2032年前大幅擴展。支持者堅持,若欠缺有能力善用這些工具的勞動人口,這類基建投資恐怕會付諸浪費。批評者則認為,這筆資金雖值得歡迎,但相對於人工智能預料帶來的衝擊規模而言,仍然規模有限,而不少身處易受衝擊行業的工人,仍有可能被遺留在後。

同樣地,教育專家指出,單靠技能提升無法取代更深層的課程改革,對準備投身職場的年輕人尤其如此。社會上日漸形成一種共識:人工智能素養應被視為一項基礎能力,猶如十年前的數碼素養一樣,而非可有可無的附加課程。這項最新計劃能否迅速擴展,追上科技急速變化的步伐,仍是未知之數,但它無疑標誌著政府致力確保勞動人口不致措手不及面對人工智能轉型的一個重要舉措。
