Hong Kong Rolls Out 'Forced Curve' Performance Ranking for 41,000 Civil Servants

DSE英文時事議題'Forced Curve' Ranking for Civil Servants
HKDSE DAILY 每日英語
BACKGROUND
事件背景

Hong Kong introduced a forced-distribution performance ranking for 41,000 civil servants, with about 5% placed in the bottom bands.

香港為四萬一千名公務員推行強制分佈評核制度，每個職系約百分之五的人會被評為最低等級。

事件分析

The reform signals a shift from seniority-based pay rises toward a more competitive, results-driven civil service culture.

這項改革標誌著公務員文化由論資排輩加薪，轉向更講求競爭與成果的方向。

BOTH SIDES
支持及反對原因
SUPPORT 支持原因

Officials argue a fixed ranking distribution is the fairest way to ensure genuine differences in performance are recognised.

官員認為，固定比例的評核分佈，是確保真正表現差異獲得承認最公平的方法。

OPPOSITION 反對原因

Unions worry that forcing a fixed proportion into low bands unfairly penalises strong teams with no genuinely weak performers.

工會則擔心，強制將固定比例評為低等級，會不公平地懲罰那些根本沒有表現欠佳成員的優秀團隊。

English

Hong Kong's Secretary for the Civil Service, Ingrid Yeung Ho Poi-yan, has unveiled the first phase of a performance assessment mechanisma system for rating how well staff are doing their jobs評核表現的制度, rating some 41,000 officers across roughly 100 job grades against a forced distributiona system requiring a fixed proportion of staff to be ranked in each category強制分佈制度 for the first time. The bureau's own four general grades will act as a demonstration gradea group chosen to go first and set an example for others示範職系, setting a precedent before other departments follow.

Roughly five per cent of staff in each grade are expected to land in the bottom bands, delaying their salary incrementa regular rise in pay added to one’s existing salary薪酬增幅. Yeung stressed department heads face no rigid departmental quotaa fixed number or proportion that a department must meet部門配額: a small or unusually strong grade can be adjusted through careful assessment calibrationfine-tuning a rating system so it reflects reality fairly評核校準 given a reasonable explanation. She also rejected fears that 60,000 staff already at the top of their pay scale would be unfairly used to "make up the numbers", insisting no sensible manager would mark them down merely for lacking further promotion prospectsthe likelihood of being promoted to a higher position晉升機會.

Staff who disagree with a rating may object at the appraisal interviewa meeting in which a supervisor discusses an employee’s performance rating with them評核面談. After two full cycles, the bureau will review the scheme before a staggered rollout reaches every remaining grade within two to three years, part of a wider push to build a stronger accountability culture across the civil service.

中文翻譯

公務員事務局局長楊何蓓茵宣布政府籌劃已久的評核表現的制度將推行第一期，約100個職系、合共4.1萬名公務員將首次按強制分佈制度接受評級。公務員事務局轄下的四個一般職系獲選作為示範職系，當局希望此舉能為其他部門樹立榜樣，然後再將政策推展至更廣泛範圍。

在新機制下，每個參與職系約有百分之五的員工預料會被評為最低三級，結果可能延遲他們的薪酬增幅。楊何蓓茵強調，部門首長並非受制於嚴格的部門配額：如某職系人數太少，或整體表現特別出色，主管可提出合理解釋，再經仔細評核校準調整評級。她亦回應外界對約六萬名已處於薪酬頂點的公務員可能被用作「交人頭」的憂慮，強調精明的部門首長不會單憑該批員工已無晉升機會便將他們評為最低等級。

當局承諾，任何不認同評級的員工均可在評核面談中提出異議。經過兩個完整評核周期後，當局計劃檢討方案，再分階段將機制擴展至其餘職系，預計兩至三年內完成，作為加強香港公務員問責文化的一部分。
