Hong Kong’s much-promoted "Study in Hong Kong" campaign should look past institutional league tablesranked lists comparing the performance of institutions排名榜 if it genuinely wants to keep hold of overseas talent, leaders from several leading British universities argued recently, as the city’s publicly funded institutions prepare more than 160 recruitment events overseas and across the mainland this academic year.

Duncan Ivison, president and vice-chancellor of the University of Manchester, said Hong Kong could draw inattract and bring into a place吸引 international scholars not merely through its rankings but through research initiatives such as InnoHK, which pair overseas academics with local laboratories. He and other speakers argued that rankings alone cannot compensate formake up for a lack or disadvantage彌補 the absence of clear, government-backed career incentives once students graduate.

Hong Kong already offers the Immigration Arrangements for Non-local Graduates, which lets students who complete a full-time local degree stay for up to 24 months to search for work, alongside a dedicated entry routea pathway or channel for entering a place or system入境途徑 under the Top Talent Pass Scheme for recent graduates of top overseas universities. Speakers at the forum argued that these schemes need to be publicisedmade widely known to the public宣傳；公開 far more aggressively alongside academic rankings if Hong Kong hopes to compete with destinations such as Singapore and Australia, where post-study work rights are already a selling pointan attractive feature used to persuade someone to choose something賣點 in their own recruitment pitches, rather than an afterthought buried in immigration paperwork.

中文翻譯

香港大力推廣的「留學香港」計劃,若要真正留住海外人才,就應該不再只倚重院校排名榜,多位英國頂尖大學校長近日在一場論壇上提出這個觀點,同時香港多所資助大學正籌備在本學年舉辦逾160場海外及內地招生活動。

曼徹斯特大學校長伊維森(Duncan Ivison)表示,香港除了憑藉排名外,亦可透過InnoHK等研究計劃,將海外學者與本地實驗室配對,藉此吸引國際學者。他與其他發言者認為,單靠排名並不能彌補畢業後缺乏明確、由政府支持的職業激勵措施這個缺口。

香港現時已設有「非本地畢業生留港/回港就業安排」,讓完成本地全日制學位課程的學生可留港最多24個月求職,另設有「高端人才通行證計劃」下專為頂尖海外大學畢業生而設的入境途徑。論壇發言者認為,這些計劃需要與學術排名一併更積極地宣傳,香港才有望與新加坡、澳洲等地競爭——這些地方早已將畢業後工作權利列為招生宣傳中的賣點,而非埋藏在移民文件中的附帶條款。
