English

The Education Bureau told the Legislative Council this week that a disproportionate sharea portion of something that is unexpectedly large compared with the rest不成比例的比重 of suspected student suicide cases logged over the past three school years involved children aged 15 or younger, overturning the common assumption that such tragedies chiefly affect older teenagers facing examination pressure. Officials disclosed that roughly six in ten cases fell within this younger age band, a pattern they described as deeply concerning given that early interventiontaking action as soon as possible after a problem is identified, before it becomes more serious及早介入 is widely regarded as most effective before adolescence.

Under the three-tier support mechanisma support system organised into three levels of increasing seriousness or urgency三層支援機制 introduced after a spike in cases, principals may refer pupils showing signs of underlying distressdeep emotional pain that is not immediately obvious on the surface潛在情緒困擾 directly to Hospital Authority psychiatrists, bypassing slower school-based channels; bureau figures show schools have made such psychiatric referralscases passed on to a mental health specialist for assessment or treatment精神科轉介 at an average pace of roughly twenty a month. Lawmakers welcomed the mechanism but questioned whether it amounts to little more than a stopgap measurea temporary solution used until a more permanent one can be found權宜之計, noting that referrals have continued to climb even as the scheme has matured, suggesting that compounding pressuresmultiple sources of stress that build on and intensify one another疊加壓力 on young people are outpacing the system built to catch them.

Mental health advocates argue the figures point to systemic gapsweaknesses built into the structure of a system, rather than isolated errors系統性缺口 rather than isolated failures, calling for counsellors to be embedded in primary as well as secondary schools and for teacher training to address mental wellbeinga person's overall psychological and emotional health心理健康 warning signs specific to younger children. The bureau says it is reviewing the mechanism's scope, though it has not committed to a timeline. For now, the data leaves parents and educators grappling with an uncomfortable question: whether a system designed around teenage stress is adequately equipped to protect children barely out of primary school.

中文翻譯

教育局本周向立法會表示，在過去三個學年錄得的懷疑學生自殺個案中，有不成比例的比重涉及十五歲或以下的兒童，推翻了一般人認為此類悲劇主要發生在面對考試壓力的年長學生身上的看法。官員透露，約六成個案屬於這個較年輕的年齡組別，並形容此情況令人深感憂慮，因為及早介入普遍被視為在踏入青春期前最為有效。

在因個案急增而推行的三層支援機制下，校長可將出現潛在情緒困擾徵狀的學生直接轉介予醫院管理局的精神科醫生，繞過速度較慢的校內渠道；教育局數字顯示，學校平均每月作出約二十宗精神科轉介。議員歡迎該機制，但質疑它是否只是一項權宜之計，指出即使計劃已推行一段時間，轉介需求仍持續攀升，反映年輕人面對的疊加壓力正超出這個系統所能應付的範圍。

精神健康倡議人士認為，這些數字反映的是系統性缺口，而非個別事件的失誤，呼籲在小學（而非僅在中學）設立駐校輔導員，並加強教師培訓，以識別針對較年幼兒童的心理健康警號。教育局表示正在檢討機制的涵蓋範圍，但未承諾具體時間表。目前，這些數字令家長和教育工作者面對一個令人不安的問題：一套圍繞青少年壓力而設計的系統，是否足以保護那些年紀尚小、剛離開小學的兒童。
