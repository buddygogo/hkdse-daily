English

Hong Kong's commerce chief has brushed asidedismissed something as unimportant without much consideration不以為意 concerns that geopolitical tension is driving foreign capital away from the city, insisting that American and British firms are still setting up shop in growing numbers. Secretary for Commerce and Economic Development Algernon Yau Ying-wah acknowledged that 2026 had been a testing yeara difficult period that puts someone's resolve or ability under pressure充滿考驗的一年 on the geopolitical front, but said the figures told a different story.

According to InvestHK, the government's investment promotion arma government body dedicated to attracting investment into a place投資推廣機構, 516 overseas and mainland Chinese companies set up or expanded operations in Hong Kong during the first eight months of the year. Mainland China accounted for the largest share at 307, followed by Singapore with 29, the US with 27 and Britain with 21. "Money is money. Businesspeople are very practical," Yau said, adding that firms are reassured once they see for themselves that the city remains stable and unencumberedfree from burdens or restrictions that would otherwise hold something back穩定且不受束縛.

Yau pointed to a decade-long trade surplus between Hong Kong and the United States as evidence that American companies have tangible commercial incentivesreal, concrete reasons related to profit or business advantage, as opposed to abstract ones實際的商業動機 to stay invested regardless of diplomatic friction. Critics, however, note that headline figures can mask underlying cautionhide a more careful or hesitant attitude that isn't obvious from the numbers alone掩蓋潛藏的審慎態度, since a rising company count does not necessarily reflect the scale or permanence of the investment involved. Still, with Hong Kong due to host the Apec Finance Ministers' Meeting later this month, officials are hoping to parlay the occasionuse an opportunity or event to achieve a further advantage藉此機會爭取更大優勢 into fresh pledges of investment from Western economies.

中文翻譯

香港商務及經濟發展局局長對有關地緣政治緊張局勢正令外資流走的憂慮不以為意，強調美資和英資企業仍陸續在港設業。局長丘應樺承認，就地緣政治而言，2026年是充滿考驗的一年，但他指數字說明了另一個故事。

據政府投資推廣機構「投資推廣署」統計，今年首八個月共有516間海外及內地企業在港開設或擴展業務，當中內地以307間佔最大份額，其次是新加坡29間、美國27間及英國21間。丘應樺說：「錢就是錢，商人是很實際的。」他續稱，企業一旦親身察覺本港仍然穩定且不受束縛，自然會安心前來投資。

丘應樺指出，港美之間長達十年的貿易順差，正是美資企業不論外交摩擦仍有實際的商業動機留港投資的證據。然而，有評論指相關數字或會掩蓋潛藏的審慎態度，因為企業數目上升未必反映投資的規模或持久性。即使如此，隨著香港將於本月稍後主辦亞太經合組織（APEC）財長會議，官員仍希望藉此機會爭取更大優勢，為西方經濟體爭取新一輪投資承諾。
