English

Concerns over building safety have once again surfaced in Hong Kong's public housing sector, after the Housing Authority disclosed that the structural integritythe ability of a building or structure to withstand its intended load without failing結構承受預期負荷而不損壞的能力 of concrete used in three blocks at the Tung Chung Area 42 estate requires further investigation. The issue came to light during routine concrete cube strength tests conducted between late August and early September, when engineers found that certain structural components in Blocks 3, 4 and 5 did not meet the expected quality benchmarks, prompting authorities to order additional, more rigorous testing before construction can proceed.

The main contractor overseeing the project, Able Engineering, is no stranger to scrutinyclose and careful examination仔細和認真的審查; the firm is separately embroiled in a scandal involving defective lift installation work at an expansion project at Queen Mary Hospital. This is by no means the first time that quality control issues have affected Hong Kong's public housing pipeline, and it is widely argued that such recurring lapses raise serious questions about oversightthe act of supervising or monitoring a process監督或監察過程 within the construction industry, particularly given the government's ambitious targets for accelerating the supply of subsidised flats to address the city's chronic housing shortage.

Nevertheless, officials have sought to reassure the public that safety will not be compromised for the sake of speed, stressing that no units will be handed over until the concrete's quality is fully verified. By the same token, critics contend that greater transparency and stricter accountability measuressystems that ensure people or companies answer for their actions確保人或公司對其行為負責的制度 for contractors are urgently needed, arguing that public trust in the housing supply chain cannot be rebuilt through reassurances alone, but only through consistently rigorous enforcement of building standards.

中文翻譯

香港公共房屋界的建築安全問題再次引起關注,房屋署披露東涌42區一個公共房屋地盤有三座大廈所使用的混凝土,其結構完整性須進一步調查。事件源於八月底至九月初進行的混凝土立方體強度測試,工程人員發現第3、4及5座部分結構構件未達到預期的質量標準,當局因此下令在工程繼續進行前展開更嚴格的額外檢測。

負責該項目的主要承建商「安利工程」(Able Engineering)並非首次受到審視——該公司另涉及瑪麗醫院擴建工程升降機安裝工程出現缺陷的醜聞。這並非香港公共房屋供應鏈首次出現質量管控問題,有人認為,這類反覆出現的失誤令建造業的監管備受質疑,尤其是在政府正致力加快資助房屋供應,以應對本港長期房屋短缺問題之際。

然而,官員試圖安撫公眾,強調不會為求趕工而犧牲安全,並重申在混凝土質量完全獲得確認之前,不會向居民交樓。與此同時,批評者認為,當局必須加強透明度,並對承建商實施更嚴格的問責機制,強調單靠安撫言辭無法重建公眾對房屋供應鏈的信心,唯有持續嚴格執行建築標準才能做到。
