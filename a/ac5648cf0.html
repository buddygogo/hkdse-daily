English

Hong Kong’s job market for fresh graduates has grown noticeably bleakergloomier, offering little hope更黯淡、欠缺希望 this year, as new data lay bare how quickly artificial intelligence is reshaping entry-level hiring. According to figures released by recruitment platform Jobsdb by SEEK, job advertisements aimed specifically at university leavers plungedfell sharply and suddenly急挫 by 22 per cent in the first nine months of 2026 compared with the same period last year. Bill Lee Chern-hsing, the platform’s Hong Kong managing director, cautioned graduates against clinging toholding onto something tightly, often stubbornly緊抱不放 outdated expectations, warning that they “don’t have the illusion” that a degree alone still guarantees a smooth transition into stable employment.

The slump builds on a broader trend. Separate government figures earlier this year found that vacancies traditionally reserved for degree-holders had fallen by roughly 60 per cent since 2022, with clerical and administrative roles proving especially vulnerableexposed to harm or being replaced容易受衝擊 to automation. Labour officials estimate that tens of thousands of clerical workers in their late forties and fifties, many without post-secondary qualifications, now face a genuine risk of being phased outgradually removed or discontinued逐步淘汰 as firms adopt AI tools for routine paperwork and data entry.

Employers, for their part, describe themselves as cautiously hopeful about next year’s recovery, even as they grow more discerningcareful and selective in judgement審慎挑剔 about whom they hire. Many now treat familiarity with AI tools as a decisive factor when sifting throughexamining items carefully one by one to select the best仔細篩選 applications, leaving graduates who cannot demonstrate such fluency at an immediate disadvantage. Mr Lee urged students to pursue internships while still studying and to cultivate soft skills — communication, adaptability and problem-solving — that cannot easily be replicatedcopied or reproduced exactly複製 by a machine. Critics counter that the burden of adaptation is falling unfairly on the youngest jobseekers, who had no part in designing the very technology now disrupting their prospects, and are calling on the government and employers to share more of the responsibility for retraining and mentorship as the transition continues to unfold.

中文翻譯

隨著人工智能迅速重塑初級職位的招聘模式,香港大學畢業生的就業市場今年明顯變得更黯淡。求職平台Jobsdb by SEEK公佈的數據顯示,專門招聘大學畢業生的職位廣告,在2026年首九個月按年急挫22%。該平台香港區總經理李鎮軒提醒畢業生切勿緊抱不切實際的幻想,強調單憑一張學位證書,已不再保證能順利過渡至穩定的工作崗位。

這次下滑屬於更廣泛趨勢的一部分。政府今年較早時公布的另一組數字顯示,傳統上專屬學位持有人的職位空缺,自2022年以來大跌約六成,當中文書及行政類職位在自動化浪潮下顯得尤其容易受衝擊。勞工官員估計,數以萬計年近五十歲、且大多未持專上學歷的文書工作者,隨著企業逐步採用人工智能處理日常文件及數據輸入工作,正面臨被逐步淘汰的真實風險。

僱主方面則形容自己對明年經濟復蘇抱持審慎樂觀態度,但在招聘人手時亦變得更加審慎挑剔。不少僱主在仔細篩選求職申請時,已將求職者對人工智能工具的熟悉程度視為關鍵考慮因素,令未能展示相關能力的畢業生即時處於劣勢。李鎮軒促請學生在仍然就讀期間爭取實習機會,並培養難以輕易被機器複製的軟技能——包括溝通能力、應變能力及解難能力。批評者則反駁指,適應轉型的重擔不公平地落在最年輕的求職者身上,他們根本無從參與設計這項正顛覆其前景的科技,並呼籲政府與僱主在再培訓及師友輔導方面,承擔更多責任,以應對這場仍在持續演變的轉變。
