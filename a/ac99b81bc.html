English

Hong Kong University of Science and Technology has revealed another senior appointment for its planned medical school — the city's third — naming Anskar Leung Yu-hung, a former vice-dean at the University of Hong Kong's medical faculty, as senior associate deana senior leadership role in a university faculty, ranking just below the dean高級副院長. Leung, a haematologythe branch of medicine concerned with diseases of the blood血液學 professor who studies acute myeloid leukaemia, presented a combination therapya treatment approach that uses two or more drugs or methods together聯合療法 in May that reduces the risk of relapse in leukaemia patients. His move follows the recruitment of a professor formerly of Australia's Murdoch University, and comes as HKUST president Nancy Ip confirmed the school has already hired seven clinical professorssenior doctors who also hold teaching and research positions at a university臨床教授.

The hires have inevitably raised questions about whether HKUST is drawing talent away from Hong Kong's two existing medical schools at a time when the city already faces a doctor shortagea situation in which there are not enough qualified doctors to meet demand醫生短缺. Ip has repeatedly stressed that the university will not poach staff from HKU or the Chinese University, framing the relationship instead as cooperation aimed at building Hong Kong into an international health innovation hub rather than pitting the three institutions against one another in a zero-sum contesta competition in which one side's gain is necessarily the other side's loss零和競爭 for scarce clinical expertise.

Founding deanthe first person to lead a newly established faculty or school創校院長 King Li took office in June, and the school is targeting a first cohort by 2028, with an interim teaching complex under construction at HKUST's Clear Water Bay campus and a permanent site planned for Ngau Tam Mei in the Northern Metropolis. The four-year, graduate-entry programmea professional degree course open only to students who already hold a first degree研究生入學課程 is expected to open applications in mid-2027. Supporters say a third medical school is essential to easing Hong Kong's doctor shortage, while sceptics, including rival deans, continue to question whether the city has enough senior clinicians to staff three schools at once without diluting standards at any of them.

中文翻譯

香港科技大學為其籌備中、全港第三所醫學院再添一名高層人選，宣布前香港大學醫學院副院長梁如鴻擔任高級副院長。梁教授為血液學專家，專注研究急性骨髓性白血病，今年五月曾公布一套能降低病人復發風險的聯合療法。繼聘得一名曾於澳洲梅鐸大學任教的教授後，科大校長葉玉如亦確認，醫學院已聘得七位臨床教授。

連番招聘不免令人質疑，科大是否正在本港已面臨醫生短缺之際，從現有兩所醫學院手中搶奪人才。葉玉如多次強調，大學不會從港大或中大「挖角」，並將這種關係定性為合作，旨在將香港建設成國際醫療創新樞紐，而非令三所院校陷入爭奪稀缺臨床專才的零和競爭。

創校院長King Li六月已上任，醫學院目標二零二八年迎接首批學生，現正於科大清水灣校園興建臨時教學大樓，永久校址則計劃設於北部都會區的牛潭尾。為期四年的研究生入學課程，預計二零二七年年中開始接受申請。支持者認為，第三所醫學院對緩解香港醫生短缺至關重要；質疑者，包括對手院校的院長，則仍關注本港是否有足夠資深臨床醫生，可同時支援三所醫學院運作而不影響質素。
