English

Hong Kong's Consumer Council has cast doubt onraised doubts about something質疑 the genuine value of "buy-one-get-one-free" and bulk-buy promotionsdiscounts offered for purchasing items in large quantities大量購買優惠 offered by the city's three major supermarket chains — AEON, ParknShop and Wellcome. A year-long survey of 222 everyday products, ranging from instant noodles to tissue paper, found that the single-item price during some promotional periods was in fact higher than the price charged outside the promotion, meaning shoppers saved considerably less than the advertised discount implied, if anything at all. The watchdog further noted that no single "bargain day" applies consistently across the three chains, complicating efforts by consumers to plan their grocery shopping around genuine savings.

It is widely argued that such findings expose a troubling gap between the perceived and actual value of retail promotions, particularly at a time when households are already grappling with a rising cost of living. Consumer advocates contend that headline discounts of around fifty percent create a powerful psychological incentivea mental motivation that encourages a particular behaviour心理誘因 to purchase, even when the underlying saving is minimal or non-existent. By the same token, the Council's call for shoppers to compare unit prices rather than relying on promotional labels reflects a broader push towards greater price transparencyopenness and clarity about how prices are set價格透明度, a principle increasingly regarded as essential to consumer protectionmeasures to safeguard the rights and interests of buyers消費者保障 in a competitive market.

Nevertheless, industry representatives might counter that pricing strategies of this nature are commonplace across global retail markets and do not necessarily amount to deliberate deception, given that prices naturally fluctuate according to supply costs and inventory cycles. Critics, however, maintain that supermarkets bear a responsibility to ensure promotional labellinglabels used to advertise discounts or special offers促銷標籤 accurately reflects genuine savings, especially given the disproportionate impactan effect that is not fairly or reasonably proportioned不成比例的影響 of inflated grocery bills on lower-income families. Whether this report will prompt tighter regulation of promotional pricing remains uncertain, but it has undeniably reignited public debatecaused a discussion or disagreement to start again重燃公眾爭論 over transparency and accountabilitythe state of being responsible for one's actions問責性 in Hong Kong's retail sector.

中文翻譯

香港消費者委員會質疑本港三大連鎖超市——AEON、百佳及惠康——所推出的「買一送一」及大量購買優惠的真實價值。一項歷時一年、涵蓋222種日常用品(由即食麵到廁紙)的調查發現,部分促銷期間的單件價格,實際上高於促銷期以外的售價,意味著消費者所節省的金額,遠較宣傳折扣所暗示的少,甚至可能完全沒有節省。消委會並指出,三大連鎖超市並無劃一的「割價日」,令消費者難以按真正的優惠來規劃日常採購。

有人認為,這項調查結果揭示零售促銷的實際價值與消費者感覺之間存在令人憂慮的差距,尤其是在家庭本已面對生活成本上升的時刻。消費者權益倡議者指出,高達五成的標榜折扣,製造出強烈的心理誘因促使消費,即使實際節省微乎其微甚至根本不存在。與此同時,消委會呼籲消費者比較單位價格,而非單憑促銷標籤判斷,此舉反映社會日益推動更高的價格透明度——此原則在競爭激烈的市場中,被視為消費者保障不可或缺的一環。

然而,業界代表或會反駁,指這類定價策略在全球零售市場相當普遍,並不必然構成蓄意欺詐,因為價格本就會隨供應成本及存貨周期自然波動。不過,批評者仍然堅持,超市有責任確保促銷標籤準確反映真正的優惠,尤其考慮到雜貨帳單虛高對低收入家庭造成的不成比例的影響。這份報告能否促使當局加強規管促銷定價仍屬未知,但無可否認,它已重新引發公眾爭論,觸及香港零售業的透明度與問責性。
