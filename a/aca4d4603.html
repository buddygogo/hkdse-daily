English

Baidu's self-driving unit, Apollo Go, has opened what the company calls Hong Kong's first large-scale public triala real-world test open to ordinary members of the public, not just engineers公眾試驗 of autonomous vehiclesvehicles that can drive themselves without a human controlling the steering自動駕駛車輛, offering more than 1,000 Tung Chung residents a free ride between 21 October and 3 November. Riders travel a 5.1-kilometre designated routea specific path officially approved for a particular use指定路線 that starts near Yat Tung Estate, passes a hotel, and loops back to the start, with each trip lasting roughly 15 minutes. Tickets, distributed through the Islands District Council Secretariat, are capped at 600, enough for up to 1,200 passenger journeys since each car carries at most two riders.

Unlike a fully commercial service, every vehicle still carries a safety operatora trained person who sits in a self-driving vehicle ready to intervene if something goes wrong安全員 in the driver's seat, a reminder that the technology remains firmly in testing. Baidu says each operator has roughly a decade of driving experience and at least 100 hours of dedicated autonomous-driving training. Passengers are asked to fill in feedback questionnaires after each journey, data the company will use to fine-tune its ride-hailinga service that matches passengers with vehicles for hire, usually booked through an app網約車 algorithms before seeking approval for a wider rollout. Officials note the vehicles currently lack barrier-free facilities, underlining how far the project still sits from a full commercial launch.

The trial fits into a broader, cautious strategy by Hong Kong's Transport Department, which has gradually expanded test routes for autonomous vehicles across Lantau since 2024 rather than approving a sudden commercial rolloutthe full-scale public launch of a product or service for ordinary paying customers商業化推出. Supporters argue the staged approach lets engineers iron out safety issues before robotaxis share the road with pedestrians and cyclists at scale. Sceptics, including some in the taxi and minibus trades, worry that even a modest pilot scheme signals the eventual displacement of professional driversthe replacing of human workers in a job by machines or technology專業司機被取代, a tension likely to sharpen as the technology matures.

中文翻譯

百度旗下自動駕駛部門「Apollo Go」在香港展開該公司稱為全港首個大型公眾試驗的自動駕駛車輛計劃，由10月21日至11月3日，向逾千名東涌居民提供免費試乘。乘客可沿一條全長5.1公里的指定路線行駛，路線由映灣園一帶出發，經過一間酒店後折返起點，全程約需15分鐘。車票經離島區議會秘書處派發，數量限於600張，由於每輛車最多可載兩位乘客，故合共可供約1,200人次試乘。

與全面商業服務不同，每輛車仍設有安全員坐在司機座位，提醒公眾這項技術仍處於測試階段。百度表示，每位安全員均具備約十年駕駛經驗，並接受最少100小時的自動駕駛專項訓練。乘客需於每程完結後填寫意見問卷，公司將利用相關數據優化其網約車演算法，然後才會申請擴大推行範圍。有關方面指出，現時車輛尚未設有無障礙設施，顯示計劃距離全面商業啟動仍有一段路。

今次試驗屬於運輸署一套較審慎的整體策略的一部分，該署自2024年起逐步擴大大嶼山一帶的自動駕駛測試路線，而非一下子批准商業化推出。支持者認為，循序漸進的做法讓工程師可在無人駕駛的士大規模與行人及單車使用者共用道路之前，先解決安全問題。然而，包括部分的士及小巴業界人士的質疑者擔心，即使是規模有限的試驗計劃，也預示着專業司機被取代的終局，隨着技術日趨成熟，這種張力相信會愈趨明顯。
