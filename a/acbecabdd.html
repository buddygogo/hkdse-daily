English

Hong Kong police arrested Tang Ho-wing, founder of the independent outlet Boom News, on 30 September on suspicion of acting with "seditiousintended to incite rebellion against authority漜動性的 intention", making him the first journalist publicly known to be detainedheld in custody by authorities被拘留 under this charge since the city’s homegrown national security law took effect in 2024. The arrest was linked to Boom News’ coverage of a small gathering on 31 August, where participants held yellow umbrellas to commemoratehonour the memory of an event紀念 the seventh anniversary of a 2019 police operation at Prince Edward MTR station.

Tang was released on bail on 2 October after roughly 48 hours in custody, though he must report back to police at a later date while inquiriesofficial investigations調查 continue. The Hong Kong Journalists Association, a press union, warned that if reporters can be arrested merely for being present at newsworthy events, "all frontline reporters will inevitably bear the same risks" when covering public gatherings in future, raising concerns about the chilling effectdiscouragement of lawful activity through fear寒蝉效應 on independent reporting.

Press freedom advocates argue the case illustrates a widening gap between official assurances that journalism remains protected and the practical experience of reporters covering sensitive anniversariesdates marking a significant past event each year周年紀念日. Authorities maintain that the arrest concerned conduct rather than reporting itself, insisting that genuine news-gathering remains lawful. For students of current affairs, the episode offers a timely example of how legal definitions of seditionspeech or action inciting rebellion漜動罪 can be contested, and how such ambiguity may encourage self-censorshiprestraining oneself from expression without being told to自我審查 among journalists wary of crossing an unclear line.

中文翻譯

香港警方於9月30日拘捕獨立媒體《Boom News》創辦人鄒皖文，指他涱嫌懷有「漜動意圖」而作出相關行為，使他成為本港2024年《維護國家安全条例》生效以来，首位公開證实因此罪名被拘留的新聞工作者。此次拘捕與《Boom News》於8月31日報道一場小規模集會有關，當日有參與者手持黃雨僕，紀念2019年太子站警方行動七周年。

鄒皖文於10月2日，即被拘留約48小時後，獲允保釋候查，但須在稍後日子向警方到報，而相關調查仍在進行。香港記者協會（一個新聞工作者團體）警告，若記者僅因在場採訪具新聞價值的事件便可被捕，「所有前線記者日後報道公眠活動時，難免同樣面对風險」，令人關注事件會对獨立報道造成寒蝉效應。

新聞自由倡議者認為，事件反映官方一再保證新聞工作受到保障，與記者在報道敏感周年紀念場合時的實际處境之間，存在日益擴大的落差。當局則堅稱，被捕原因在於相關行為而非採訪本身，強調真正的新聞採集活動仍然合法。对於修讀時事科目的學生而言，此案正好說明法律上对「漜動罪」的定義可以存在爭議，而這種含模之處，或會令擔心黜越模糊界線的記者傾向自我審查。
