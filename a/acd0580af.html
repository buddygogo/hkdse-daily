English

Hong Kong will throw open its doors to the region's innovators next week as the Hong Kong Trade Development Council stages the 16th Business of IP Asia Forum alongside the 18th Entrepreneur Day at the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre on 19 and 20 November. The twin events will bring together roughly 100 speakers and more than 370 start-ups, inventors and support organisations from 14 countries and regions, offering a rare one-stop platforma single venue providing everything needed for a purpose一站式平台 for anyone hoping to build a career at the intersection of technology and business.

This year's headline addition is a dedicated “Tech Chapter”, designed to help inventors move beyond the laboratory and commercialise their ideasto turn an invention into a product that can be sold for profit將創意商品化 through technology transfer and structured entrepreneurship support. Organisers hope the new strand will act as a bridge between research and marketa connection linking scientific work to real commercial use連接研究與市場的橋樑, giving university teams and early-stage founders direct access to investors, licensing partners and potential clients they would otherwise struggle to reach.

For Hong Kong's young graduates, who have watched entry-level vacancies shrink as employers automate routine tasksto use machines or software to perform repetitive work previously done by people將常規工作自動化 with artificial intelligence, the forum offers a different route into the job market: building something of their own rather than waiting to be hired. Yet turning a patent or prototype into a sustainable business remains a formidable undertakinga task that is difficult and requires great effort艱巨的任務, requiring funding, legal know-how and market timing that few student inventors possess on their own. Whether such forums can meaningfully narrow the gap between invention and incomereduce the difference between creating something and earning money from it縮窄發明與收入之間的差距 for Hong Kong's next generation of entrepreneurs remains an open question.

中文翻譯

香港下星期將向全亞洲的創新者敞開大門，香港貿易發展局將於11月19日至20日假香港會議展覽中心舉辦第16屆「亞洲知識產權營商論壇」，並同場舉行第18屆「創業日」。兩項活動將匯聚約100位演講嘉賓，以及超過370個來自14個國家及地區的初創企業、發明家及支援機構，為任何希望在科技與商業交界發展事業的人，提供一個難得的一站式平台。

今年活動的焦點新增項目是一個名為「科技篇章」的專題環節，旨在協助發明家走出實驗室，透過技術轉移及有系統的創業支援，將創意商品化。主辦方希望這個新環節能成為連接研究與市場的橋樑，讓大學團隊及初創創辦人直接接觸投資者、授權夥伴及潛在客戶——這些資源他們單靠自己往往難以觸及。

對於眼見人工智能令僱主將常規工作自動化、入門職位日漸萎縮的香港年青畢業生而言，這個論壇提供了另一條進入職場的途徑：自行創業，而非坐等被聘用。然而，將一項專利或原型發展成可持續經營的企業，仍是一項艱巨的任務，需要資金、法律知識及準確的市場時機，而這些往往不是學生發明家單憑一己之力就能擁有的。這類論壇能否真正為香港下一代企業家縮窄發明與收入之間的差距，仍是一個未有答案的問題。
