English

Hong Kong will begin pulping all locally recovered waste paper at a newly upgraded facility in EcoPark, Tuen Mun, from October 1, marking a long-awaited shift away from the city's decades-long reliance on exporting recyclables overseas. It is widely argued that this move addresses a chronic vulnerabilitya long-standing weakness that persists over time長期存在的弱點 in Hong Kong's recycling system, which has repeatedly been disrupted whenever importing countries tightened restrictions on foreign waste.

Under the new arrangement, the range of paper accepted for recycling will be substantially broadenedincreased or expanded to a great extent大幅擴闊 beyond the traditional categories of cardboard, newspaper and office paper to include mixed items such as paper bags and packaging boxes, with annual processing capacity expected to rise from roughly 500,000 to 720,000 tonnes. Supporters contend that this represents a genuine step toward a closed-loop, waste-to-resources modela system in which waste materials are fully converted back into usable resources domestically廢物轉化為資源的閉環模式, rather than merely shifting the problem elsewhere. Nevertheless, environmentalists caution that local processing capacity alone cannot resolve Hong Kong's underlying over-reliance onexcessive dependence on過度依賴 single-use packagingpackaging materials designed to be thrown away after one use一次性包裝.

By the same token, some observers point out that the success of this initiative will ultimately hinge on public participation in sorting waste correctly at source. It is often observed that even the most sophisticated recycling infrastructureadvanced and technically well-developed facilities for recycling先進的回收基礎設施 cannot function effectively without consistent cooperation from households and businesses. As Hong Kong positions itself as a regional leader in sustainability, this facility stands as a test case forserves as an example that will show whether something works作為……的試金石 whether ambitious environmental policy can translate into everyday behavioural change among residents.

中文翻譯

香港將由10月1日起,在屯門環保園全新升級的設施,將所有本地回收的廢紙全部就地製成紙漿,標誌著香港擺脫數十年來依賴出口可回收物海外處理的做法。輿論普遍認為,此舉正正針對香港回收系統一個長期存在的弱點——每當進口國收緊外國廢物入口限制,回收系統便屢次受到衝擊。

在新安排下,可接受回收的紙張種類將大幅擴闊,除傳統的紙皮、報紙及文書紙外,更涵蓋紙袋及包裝紙盒等混合紙類,每年處理量預計由約五十萬噸提升至七十二萬噸。支持者認為,這代表香港邁向一個真正的廢物轉化為資源的閉環模式,而非只是將問題轉移到別處。然而,環保人士提醒,單靠本地處理能力,並不能解決香港過度依賴一次性包裝的根本問題。

同樣地,有人指出,計劃能否成功,最終將取決於公眾能否在源頭做好垃圾分類。有人觀察到,即使擁有最先進的回收基礎設施,若沒有住戶及商戶持續配合,也無法有效運作。隨著香港力求成為區內可持續發展的典範,這座設施也成為一塊試金石,考驗著這項雄心勃勃的環保政策,能否轉化為居民日常的行為改變。
