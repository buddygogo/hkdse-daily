English

Security Secretary Chris Tang has pledged a package of legal reforms to tighten fire safety regulation, after an independent committee handed Chief Executive John Lee its report into the deadly Wang Fuk Court fire on 22 October. The report singles out the Independent Checking Unit, the Buildings Department, the Fire Services Department and the Labour Department as bearing responsibility for the disaster, with the Independent Checking Unit shouldering the lion's sharethe largest part of something最大部分 of the blame. Notably, it stops short of naming individual officials, leaving that politically sensitive task to the government itself.

The findings will not be made public immediately, since the Department of Justice must first confirm that releasing them would not prejudiceto cause harm or disadvantage to a legal process損害；妨礙 ongoing criminal proceedings. Significantly, the committee chose not to recommend that its inquiry be upgraded into a statutory commission, a decision that has left some survivors worried that key figures could ultimately escape accountabilityto avoid being held responsible for one's actions逃避問責. Tang insisted, however, that prosecutors would pursue anyone against whom there is evidence of wrongdoing, regardless of whether that person testifies before the committee.

Building on administrative measures already rolled out over the past year, Tang said the government would now pursue statutory changes, including raising penalties and widening the scope of legal responsibility for fire safety in residential buildings. Proposed measures reportedly include new criminal penalties aimed at curbingrestraining or keeping something under control遏止；抑制 bid-rigging during major renovation contracts, together with tighter oversight of the professionals who supervise such projects. The Fire Services Department, meanwhile, will take on a backstopa safeguard that steps in when other systems fail後備保障；最後把關者 role, retaining oversight of fire-related complaints until another agency formally assumes responsibility for them.

中文翻譯

保安局局長鄧炳強承諾推行一系列法律改革,以加強消防安全規管,此前獨立委員會已於十月二十二日向行政長官李家超提交宏福苑大火調查報告。報告指出獨立檢查單位、屋宇署、消防處及勞工處須對這場奪命大火負上責任,其中獨立檢查單位承擔最大部分的責任。值得注意的是,報告並未點名個別官員,將這項政治上敏感的任務留給政府自行處理。

調查結果不會即時公開,因為律政司須先確認公開內容不會損害正在進行的刑事訴訟程序。值得留意的是,委員會選擇不建議將調查升格為法定調查委員會,這項決定令部分生還者擔心,關鍵人物最終可能逃避問責。不過,鄧炳強強調,只要有證據顯示某人涉及不當行為,檢控機關將予以追究,不論該人是否曾向委員會作證。

在過去一年已推行的行政措施基礎上,鄧炳強表示政府現將推動法例修訂,包括提高罰則及擴大住宅大廈消防安全的法律責任範圍。據報,建議措施包括訂立新的刑事罰則,以遏止大型翻新工程中的圍標行為,並加強對相關專業人士的監管。至於消防處,則將擔當後備保障角色,在其他部門正式接手前,繼續監察與消防有關的投訴。
