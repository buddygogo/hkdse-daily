English

Hong Kong's banking regulator has pressed HSBC for an explanation after the lender chose Singapore, rather than its largest market, as the base for a new global AI centre of excellencea dedicated unit bringing together the best staff and resources in a particular technology人工智能卓越中心, sources say. The HKMA approached HSBC in recent months over the decision, which will see the bank hire over 100 AI specialists and 100 wealth managers in the city-state by year end, as both hubs compete for the region's thin pool of AI talent.

HSBC's Singapore team will focus on agentic AIartificial intelligence systems that can act and make decisions with little human guidance具自主行動能力的人工智能 tools that automate banking tasks, on personalising wealth managementprofessional services that manage a client’s investments and assets財富管理 advice, and on expanding digital payment capabilitiesthe technical ability to process payments electronically數碼支付能力 across Asia. The announcement came weeks after HSBC agreed to sell its Singaporean insurance arm to Allianz, leading some to suggest the move was partly meant to reassureto restore someone’s confidence or remove their doubts重新讓……安心 Singapore rather than a purely strategic allocationa deliberate decision about where to place resources for long-term advantage策略性資源分配 of resources.

The episode has reignited concerns that Hong Kong risks losing its first-mover advantagethe edge gained by being the first to act in a market or field先行者優勢 as the region's dominant financial huba city that serves as a centre for banking and financial services金融中心 if banks look elsewhere for technology investment. The HKMA is also reported to have urged HSBC and Standard Chartered to shift senior roles back to the city, sharpening a fierce talent competitionrivalry between places or companies to attract skilled workers人才競爭 with Singapore. HSBC declined to comment.

中文翻譯

據知情人士透露，香港的銀行業監管機構已要求滙豐銀行解釋，為何選擇新加坡而非其最大市場，作為新設全球人工智能卓越中心的基地。據悉，香港金融管理局近月已就此決定向滙豐查詢，該決定將令滙豐於年底前在這個城邦國家增聘逾一百名人工智能專才及一百名財富管理人員，令兩個金融中心同時爭奪區內有限的人工智能人才。

滙豐在新加坡的團隊預料將專注於具自主行動能力的人工智能工具，用以自動處理複雜銀行事務，並為客戶提供更個人化的財富管理建議，以及擴展該行在亞洲的數碼支付能力。據知悉討論內情的人士透露，滙豐宣布此決定僅在同意將新加坡的人壽及醫療保險業務出售予德國安聯之後數週，因此有人認為，這項人工智能投資部分是為了重新讓新加坡政府安心，顯示銀行對該市場的持續承諾，而非純粹的策略性資源分配。

事件再次引發外界憂慮，香港作為區內主導金融中心的先行者優勢可能逐漸流失，因為環球銀行在尖端科技投資上愈來愈傾向另覓他處。另外，據報金管局亦促請滙豐及渣打將更多高級管理職位調回香港，令香港與新加坡之間本已激烈的人才競爭進一步升溫。滙豐拒絕評論與監管機構的討論內容，金管局則只表示一直與認可機構就廣泛事宜保持溝通，外界則須自行判斷這次決定對香港的影響應有多大。
