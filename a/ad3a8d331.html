English

Hong Kong's Legislative Council passed a bill on Wednesday requiring cryptocurrency exchanges, brokers and other digital-asset firms to collect and hand over customer transaction records to the taxman, in a move officials say will close loopholes used for cross-border tax evasionavoiding tax by hiding income or assets in another country跨境逃稅. The Inland Revenue (Amendment) (Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework and Amended Common Reporting Standard) Bill 2026 cleared its third readingthe final vote stage by which a bill officially becomes law三讀 after lawmakers debated concerns from the digital-asset industry over implementation costs.

Under the new regulatory frameworkthe system of rules used to oversee an activity監管框架, crypto-asset service providers with a reporting nexusa legal connection to a place that obliges a business to report there申報聯繫 to Hong Kong, including exchanges, brokers and operators of cryptocurrency ATMs, must register with the Inland Revenue Department and carry out due diligencethe careful checks a business must perform before acting盡職審查 on their customers. Data collection begins on January 1, 2027, with the automatic exchange of informationthe routine sharing of financial data between tax authorities without a specific request資料自動交換 with partner jurisdictions to follow from 2028, aligning the city with international standards set by the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development.

Industry groups broadly welcomed the move as a step that would bolster Hong Kong's credibility as a regulated financial hub, though some warned that the new compliance burdenthe cost and effort involved in following rules and regulations合規負擔 could weigh heavily on smaller start-ups. The Hong Kong Securities and Futures Professionals Association said it backed the framework in principle but urged regulators to allow more flexible record-keeping arrangements for firms with limited manpower. Officials maintain that the rules target systemic tax avoidance rather than ordinary retail investors, and stressed that failing to register or report accurately could still expose firms to penalties under the ordinance.

中文翻譯

香港立法會星期三通過一項法案，要求加密貨幣交易所、經紀及其他數碼資產公司收集客戶交易紀錄並交予稅務機關，官員表示此舉旨在堵塞被用於跨境逃稅的漏洞。《2026年稅務（修訂）（加密資產報告框架及經修訂共同匯報標準）條例草案》在議員就業界對實施成本的疑慮進行辯論後，完成三讀程序。

根據新的監管框架，與香港具有申報聯繫的加密資產服務提供者，包括交易所、經紀及加密貨幣自動櫃員機營運商，均須向稅務局登記，並對客戶進行盡職審查。資料收集工作將於2027年1月1日展開，與其他司法管轄區之間的資料自動交換則由2028年起實施，使香港與經濟合作與發展組織所訂立的國際標準看齊。

業界普遍歡迎此舉，認為有助鞏固香港作為受規管金融中心的信譽，但亦有意見擔心新增的合規負擔可能令規模較小的初創企業承受較大壓力。香港證券及期貨從業員協會表示原則上支持新框架，但促請監管機構為人手有限的公司提供更具彈性的紀錄保存安排。官員強調，新規定針對的是系統性逃稅行為，而非一般散戶投資者，並指出未能登記或準確申報的公司仍可能須根據條例承擔罰則。
