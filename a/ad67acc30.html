English

The Independent Commission Against Corruption has charged six people, including a former Cyberport section head, over an alleged scheme to defraud the government of roughly HK$20 million in technology subsidies. Alice So Nga-lai, formerly head of entrepreneurship at Cyberport, appeared in court on one count of a public servant accepting an advantagea public official taking a bribe, gift or other benefit in connection with their official duties公職人員接受利益, alongside Viola Lam Yuen-lee, the sole proprietor of an IT consultancy accused of offering her the advantage, and four other defendants linked to the firm.

Prosecutors allege the consultancy ran a conspiracy to defrauda secret agreement between two or more people to deceive someone, usually for financial gain串謀詐騙 under the Digital Transformation Support Pilot Programme, in which it falsely claimed small businesses had engaged its services and paid half the agreed fee upfront, a fabricated paper traila set of fake or misleading documents created to make a false claim look genuine偽造的書面紀錄 meant to persuade Cyberport to approve the subsidy and release the remaining balance. Investigators say more than 400 applications were affected, and ICAC officers allege the scheme relied on the former official using her position to bypass internal vettingto avoid or skip the checks an organisation normally requires before approving something繞過內部審查 in exchange for undisclosed benefits.

No pleas were entered at the brief hearing, and the case was adjourned to January while the defendants were released on bail of between HK$20,000 and HK$100,000. Cyberport, which administers the subsidy on the government’s behalf, said it takes the allegations extremely seriously and is strengthening its own due diligencethe careful checking of facts and documents before approving or agreeing to something盡職審查 procedures so that a single insider cannot again approve suspect claims without wider oversight. The case follows a separate, larger ICAC operation earlier this year and has renewed questions in the Legislative Council over whether public funds meant to nurture small and medium enterprisesto support and help smaller businesses grow, typically through funding or training扶助中小企業 are adequately protected from insider abuse.

中文翻譯

廉政公署已起訴六人,包括一名前數碼港創業培育部門主管,指他們涉嫌串謀詐騙政府約2,000萬港元的科技資助。前數碼港創業培育主管蘇雅麗,現正面對一項公職人員接受利益罪名,與她同被起訴的還有一間資訊科技顧問公司獨資經營人林婉莉(涉嫌向她提供利益),以及與該公司有關連的另外四名被告。

控方指控該顧問公司在「數碼轉型支援先導計劃」下進行串謀詐騙,虛報中小企已聘用其服務並預繳一半費用,藉此製造偽造的書面紀錄,令數碼港批出資助並發放餘款。調查人員指逾400宗申請涉及其中,廉署亦指控該名前任職員利用職權,在收取未經申報的利益後繞過內部審查。

各被告在簡短聆訊中未有答辯,案件押後至明年一月,各人獲准以2萬至10萬港元不等的保釋金外出。負責代政府管理該資助的數碼港表示,對有關指控極度重視,並正加強盡職審查程序,以防單一內部人員日後能在缺乏更廣泛監察下批出可疑申請。此案緊接今年較早前另一宗規模更大的廉署行動,亦令立法會再度關注,旨在扶助中小企業的公共資金是否有足夠措施防範內部人員濫用。
