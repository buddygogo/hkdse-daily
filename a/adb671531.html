English

Hong Kong’s economy expanded at its fastest nine-month pace in five years, according to advance estimatespreliminary figures released before the final, confirmed data初步估算數字 published by the Census and Statistics Department on Monday. Real gross domestic product grew 5.2 per cent year on yearcompared with the same period twelve months earlier與去年同期比較 in the third quarter, comfortably eclipsing forecaststurning out higher than experts had predicted超出預期 of 3.7 per cent and quickening from the 4.2 per cent pace logged in the second quarter. On a quarter-on-quarter, seasonally adjustedcompared with the immediately preceding three months, after removing the effect of regular seasonal patterns按季度並經季節性調整 basis, the economy grew 1.9 per cent.

The latest reading extends Hong Kong’s run of growth to a fifteenth consecutive quarter of expansionthe fifteenth three-month period in a row in which the economy has grown rather than shrunk連續第十五個季度擴張, with officials attributing the surge largely to exports. Shipments of goods surged 30.4 per cent, buoyed by continued global demand for artificial-intelligence-related hardware and electronic components, while private consumptionspending by households on goods and services私人消費 rose 2.8 per cent as retail sales and local dining steadily recovered. For the first nine months of the year as a whole, the economy expanded by 5.1 per cent, the strongest such run in five years.

Financial Secretary Paul Chan said the figures reflected resilient economic momentumthe strength and pace at which growth is being sustained經濟動力 despite lingering uncertainty over global trade tensions and the pace of interest-rate cuts abroad, adding that the government may revise its full-year growth forecast upward when revised figures are released on November 13. Economists cautioned, however, that the export-led rebound could prove uneven, since much of the growth is concentrated in a narrow range of high-value technology goods, leaving sectors such as retail and tourism to continue their slower, more gradual recovery.

中文翻譯

根據政府統計處星期一公布的初步估算數字，香港經濟創下五年來最快的首三季增長步伐。本地生產總值在第三季按年增長5.2%，遠遠超出預期的3.7%幅度，並較第二季的4.2%進一步加快。若以按季度並經季節性調整計算，經濟增長1.9%。

最新數據令香港經濟增長勢頭延續至連續第十五個季度擴張，官員指出，增長主要由出口帶動。貨物出口受惠於全球對人工智能相關硬件及電子零件的持續需求，大幅上升30.4%；同時，私人消費因零售及本地飲食業持續復甦而上升2.8%。今年首三季整體計算，經濟增長達5.1%，是五年來同期最強勁的表現。

財政司司長陳茂波表示，數據反映香港經濟動力仍然強韌，儘管全球貿易緊張局勢及海外減息步伐仍存在不明朗因素；他並補充，政府在11月13日公布經修訂數字時，或會上調全年經濟增長預測。不過，經濟學者提醒，由出口帶動的反彈可能並不均衡，因為大部分增長集中於小部分高增值科技產品，而零售及旅遊等行業的復甦步伐則依然較為緩慢。
