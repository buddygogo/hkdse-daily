English

Hong Kong recorded its hottest “Frost” solar termone of 24 traditional divisions of the Chinese calendar year marking seasonal changes節氣 since records began in 1884, with the mercury reaching 32.3 degrees Celsius at the Observatory's Tsim Sha Tsui headquarters on Friday afternoon, comfortably eclipsing the previous benchmark of 31 degrees set in 2006. The reading came just two days after the Observatory issued its latest-ever Very Hot Weather Warningan official Observatory alert issued when temperatures are expected to reach a high threshold酷熱天氣警告, raised at 6.45am on Wednesday and beating a record previously set in October 2024, as the city sweltered through 33.8 degrees that day.

Explaining the unusual persistence of heat so late in the year, Chinese University atmospheric scientist Amos Tai pointed to three overlapping factors: subsiding airair that is sinking and warming as it descends, often suppressing cloud formation下沉氣流 left behind by Super Typhoon Champi, a super El Niñoan unusually strong occurrence of the El Niño climate pattern, which involves warmer-than-usual Pacific waters超強厄爾尼諾現象 that has weakened the northeast monsoonthe seasonal wind pattern that normally brings cooler, drier air to southern China in autumn and winter東北季候風 normally responsible for cooling the city in autumn, and the city's own long-term warming trenda gradual rise in temperatures observed over many years, often linked to climate change長期變暖趨勢. With less cool air flowing down from the north, Hong Kong has effectively been left trapped under a lingering summer-like air mass well into late October.

The Observatory has separately warned of a very dry weekend ahead, with a wide day-night temperature gapthe difference between daytime highs and nighttime lows日夜溫差 expected as clear skies allow heat to escape rapidly after dark. Tai cautioned that if the current El Niño episode persists into spring, the city could face one of its warmest winters on record, extending a string of weather-related recordsinstances in which a measured meteorological value surpasses all previous recorded values天氣紀錄 that has characterised 2026 and adding to concerns that Hong Kong's climate is shifting further away from its traditional four-season rhythm.

中文翻譯

香港錄得自一八八四年有紀錄以來最熾熱的「霜降」節氣，天文台尖沙咀總部星期五下午錄得攝氏三十二點三度高溫，較二零零六年訂下的三十一度紀錄明顯高出。這個紀錄出現僅兩天前，天文台已於星期三早上六時四十五分發出歷來最遲的酷熱天氣警告，打破二零二四年十月訂下的紀錄，當日本港氣溫更高見三十三點八度。

中文大學大氣科學學者戴沛權解釋，今年入秋後暑熱持續不退，與三個因素互相重疊有關：其一是超強颱風「薔琵」過後留下的下沉氣流；其二是超強厄爾尼諾現象，令本應為本港帶來降溫的東北季候風轉弱；其三則是本港自身的長期變暖趨勢。由於北方冷空氣未能有效南下，香港十月下旬仍彷如停留在近似盛夏的氣團之下。

天文台另外預告週末天氣將非常乾燥，預料天色晴朗導致熱量在日落後迅速流失，造成顯著的日夜溫差。戴沛權警告，若今次厄爾尼諾現象持續至明年春季，本港有可能迎來歷來最暖的其中一個冬季，延續二零二六年以來一連串的天氣紀錄，亦令人憂慮香港的氣候正進一步偏離傳統四季分明的規律。
