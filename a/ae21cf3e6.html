English

Hong Kong police have begun mounting round-the-clock patrolscontinuous patrols kept up 24 hours a day without a break24小時不間斷巡邏 around the Wan Chai harbourfront ahead of the city's first Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Finance Ministers' Meeting, due to run from October 19 to 21 at the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre. Sources familiar with the operation say hundreds of officers from the force's tactical unit will watch over both the venue itself and a cluster of nearby hotels, including the Grand Hyatt and the Renaissance Harbour View, where visiting delegationsofficial groups of representatives sent by a government or organisation to attend a meeting or event代表團 are expected to stay.

Security Secretary Chris Tang has said the arrangements must cover hotel security, venue security and transport for a high-level gatheringa meeting attended by senior officials or leaders rather than lower-ranking staff高層會議 of finance ministers and senior officials from the bloc's 21 member economies, with roughly 500 guests expected in total. He added that the force has drawn up contingency plansplans prepared in advance to deal with an emergency or an unexpected situation應變計劃 for possible terrorist or cyberattack threats, and that specialist teams will run cyber patrols alongside the physical presence on the ground, reflecting how summit security now spans both streets and servers.

Officials have stressed that the heightened securitysecurity measures that have been increased beyond their usual, everyday level加強保安 is not intended to cut Wan Chai off from ordinary life, insisting that diplomatic protocolthe formal rules and customs that govern how officials and dignitaries are received and treated外交禮節 can be observed without unduly inconveniencing residents or commuters passing through the district. For a city keen to present itself as a reliable host citythe place chosen to stage and organise an international event or meeting主辦城市 for international finance, how smoothly the meeting runs may matter almost as much as what is decided inside the venue.

中文翻譯

香港警方已開始在灣仔海濱一帶部署24小時不間斷巡邏,為本港首次主辦的亞太經合組織(APEC)財長會議做好準備,會議預計於十月十九日至二十一日假香港會議展覽中心舉行。據了解行動安排的消息人士透露,警隊將派出數百名機動部隊人員,同時駐守會場本身及鄰近多間酒店,包括君悅酒店及港麗酒店,預料到訪的代表團將入住該處。

保安局局長鄧炳強表示,有關安排須涵蓋酒店保安、會場保安及交通安排,以應付這場雲集來自二十一個成員體財長及高級官員的高層會議,預計總共約有五百名賓客出席。他補充指,警方已制定應變計劃,以應付可能出現的恐怖襲擊或網絡攻擊威脅,而專責小組亦會同步進行網絡巡查,反映現今的峰會保安工作已由街頭延伸至網絡系統。

官員強調,加強保安並非要將灣仔與日常生活隔絕,並堅稱在不過度影響居民或途經該區通勤人士的情況下,仍可妥善遵守外交禮節。對於一直希望展現自己是可靠的國際金融主辦城市的香港而言,會議能否順利進行,其重要性或許不亞於會上所作出的決定。
