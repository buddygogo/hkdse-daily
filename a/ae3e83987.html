English

Quality concerns have prompted the Housing Department to order further testing of concrete used in three blocks of a public housing development under construction in Tung Chung, on Lantau Island. Between late August and early September, routine cube testsstandard tests used to check the compressive strength of concrete立方體強度測試 flagged irregularities in the concrete used for Blocks 3, 4 and 5 of the Tung Chung Area 42 project, prompting the site contractor, Able Engineering Company Limited, to switch concrete suppliers as a precautionary measurean action taken in advance to prevent a possible problem預防措施. Officials have since instructed the contractor to submit a detailed report along with proposals for any necessary corrective and repair work, while engineers have ordered additional core samples to be taken to verify whether the concrete's compressive strengththe ability of a material to withstand pushing forces without breaking抗壓強度 meets required design specifications.

It is widely argued that rigorous quality control in public housing construction is non-negotiablesomething that must be accepted and cannot be changed or argued about不容妥協, given that any structural compromise could have far-reaching consequences for residents' safety and long-term confidence in publicly funded housing. Housing advocates contend that the prompt identification of the issue through routine testing demonstrates that existing quality assurance mechanismssystems designed to ensure a consistent standard of quality品質保證機制 are functioning as intended, catching potential problems before flats are handed over to tenants. By the same token, officials argue that transparency in reporting such concerns, rather than concealing them, is essential to maintaining public trustpreserving people's confidence in an institution or process維持公眾信任 in a construction sector that has faced scrutiny over similar incidents in the past.

Nevertheless, critics contend that recurring quality issues across public housing projects raise uncomfortable questions about oversight of contractors amid an intensifying drive to accelerate housing supply. Given Hong Kong's chronic housing shortagea persistent, long-term lack of available housing長期房屋短缺 and lengthy waiting lists for public rental flats, any delay caused by remedial workwork carried out to correct or repair a defect補救工程 risks compounding an already pressing problem, leaving would-be tenants waiting even longer. Supporters of the current approach counter that swift, thorough testing now is preferable to rushing flawed structures into occupation, even if it means a short-term setback to delivery targets. Whether this incident will lead to tighter contractor oversight across future projects remains to be seen, but it has undeniably placed renewed scrutinyincreased or repeated close examination重新受到審視 on construction quality standards in Hong Kong's public housing pipeline.

中文翻譯

品質問題促使房屋署下令對東涌一個在建公共房屋項目中三座大樓所使用的混凝土，進行進一步測試。在八月底至九月初期間，常規立方體強度測試發現東涌第42區項目第3、4及5座所用混凝土存在異常，促使承建商巨業工程有限公司作為預防措施更換混凝土供應商。當局已要求承建商提交詳細報告，並就任何必要的修補及維修工程提出方案，而工程師亦已下令抽取額外岩芯樣本，以核實混凝土的抗壓強度是否符合設計規格要求。

有人廣泛認為，公共房屋建築的嚴格品質監控不容妥協，因為任何結構上的隱患都可能對居民安全及公眾對公營房屋的長遠信心造成深遠影響。房屋事務倡議者認為，透過常規測試及時發現問題，正正證明現有品質保證機制行之有效，能在單位交付住戶前揪出潛在問題。同樣地，官員認為坦誠公開有關問題，而非加以隱瞞，對於一個曾因類似事件備受關注的建造業而言，是維持公眾信任的關鍵所在。

然而，有評論指出，公共房屋項目接連出現的品質問題，令人質疑在加快供應房屋的壓力下，當局對承建商的監管是否足夠。鑑於香港面臨長期房屋短缺，公屋輪候時間冗長，任何因補救工程而造成的延誤，都可能令本已嚴峻的問題雪上加霜，令有意上樓的住戶等候更久。現行做法的支持者則反駁，指現時進行徹底測試，總比倉促讓有缺陷的建築物投入使用更為可取，即使短期內會延遲落成目標。這次事件會否促使日後加強對承建商的監管，仍是未知數，但無疑已令香港公共房屋工程的建築品質標準，再次面臨重新審視。
