English

The government has gazettedformally published an official notice in the government's legal record, giving it legal effect刊登憲報 Wan Chai's Hung Shing Temple and Stanley Mosque as declared monumentsbuildings or sites formally designated under law as having the highest level of heritage protection法定古蹟 under the Antiquities and Monuments Ordinance, granting both sites the city's highest statutory heritage protection. The notice, published on 2 October, follows a recommendation from the Antiquities Advisory Board and marks a rare occasion on which a Chinese temple and an Islamic place of worship were elevated together.

Hung Shing Temple, believed to date from between 1847 and 1852, remains an active venue for ritual practicestraditional ceremonies and customs carried out according to established religious or cultural procedures儀式活動 and is currently managed by the Tung Wah Group of Hospitals, which the heritage office says will receive support for repairing its rooftop's ceramic figurinessmall decorative statues made of fired clay, often found on the ridges of traditional Chinese temple roofs陶塑瓷像, some of which have become deformed over time. Stanley Mosque, built in 1936-37, is the city's second-oldest mosque and the only one standing inside a correctional facilitya building, such as a prison, used to detain and rehabilitate convicted offenders懲教設施; officials say it has kept its original form through only minor, reversible alterations.

Because the mosque sits within an active prison, the Antiquities and Monuments Office says it is liaisingcommunicating and coordinating with another party to reach an agreement or arrange something聯絡協調 with the Correctional Services Department to arrange visits that do not compromise security. Heritage advocates have welcomed the dual declaration as a rare acknowledgement that Hong Kong's built heritagehistoric buildings and structures that reflect a place's architectural and cultural history建築文物 spans more than one faith and community, even as some note that genuine public access, not protection on paper alone, will decide whether the upgrade means much to residents.

中文翻譯

政府已將灣仔洪聖廟及赤柱清真寺刊登憲報，根據《古物及古蹟條例》列為法定古蹟，令兩處地點獲得本港最高級別的法定文物保護。公告於十月二日刊出，是古物諮詢委員會提出建議後的結果，亦標誌著一座中式廟宇與一座伊斯蘭教場所罕有地同時獲提升保護級別。

洪聖廟估計建於一八四七年至一八五二年間，至今仍是舉行儀式活動的活躍場所，現由東華三院管理；文物保育辦事處表示，將支援修復廟頂部分已出現變形的陶塑瓷像。至於建於一九三六至三七年的赤柱清真寺，則是本港第二古老的清真寺，亦是唯一一座座落於懲教設施之內的清真寺；官方指它僅經歷輕微且可還原的改動，大致保留原有面貌。

由於清真寺位於仍在運作的監獄範圍內，古物古蹟辦事處表示正與懲教署聯絡協調，安排公眾參觀，惟不能影響保安及日常運作。文物保育人士歡迎此次同時列為古蹟的安排，認為這罕有地肯定了香港的建築文物跨越不止一個信仰與社群，惟亦有人指出，真正的公眾可達性，而非僅屬紙面上的保護，才是這次升級對普通市民是否具意義的關鍵。
