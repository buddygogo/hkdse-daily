English

A seemingly routine radio phone-in call to the Chief Executive has snowballed into a public rowgrown quickly from something small into a major dispute演變成一場公眾爭議 over Hong Kong's nursing job market, after a caller identified only as Mr Kwok questioned whether an influx of non-locally trained nurses was crowding out fresh graduatestaking up opportunities that would otherwise go to new graduates擠走新鮮人畢業生 from the profession. The remark prompted Executive Council member Tommy Cheung Yu-yan to suggest the caller had been coached by a unionsecretly guided or instructed by a labour organisation受工會操控 pushing its own agenda, an accusation Kwok firmly rejected, insisting he held no grudge against mainland medical staff and describing himself as a committed supporter of the establishment.

Stepping into the fray, Secretary for Health Lo Chung-mau urged all sides to anchor the debate in verified figuresbase an argument firmly on confirmed, factual data以核實數據作討論基礎 rather than hearsay, warning that unsubstantiated claimsstatements made without solid supporting evidence未經證實的言論 about the job market only deter prospective recruits and ultimately leave Hong Kong to bear the consequences of a worsening shortfall. Officials have repeatedly pointed to rising numbers of full-time nurses and a falling attrition ratethe rate at which staff leave a job or profession流失率 as evidence that recruitment reforms, including pathways for non-locally trained staff, are easing pressure rather than displacing local talent.

The episode lays bare a delicate balancing acta situation requiring careful management of competing needs微妙的平衡任務 facing the city: plugging an acute shortage of healthcare workers through imported talent while reassuring a new generation of local nursing graduates that their career prospects have not been sacrificed in the process. With training places having expanded sharply in recent years, how authorities reconcile these competing pressures is likely to shape not only the morale of the nursing workforce but also public confidence in the government's broader manpower planningthe strategic organisation of a workforce to meet future needs人力規劃.

中文翻譯

一個看似平常的電台烽煙節目來電，演變成一場有關本港護士就業市場的公眾爭議。一名僅以「郭先生」身分受訪的來電者質疑，大量非本地培訓護士湧入會否擠走本地應屆畢業生的就業機會。行政會議成員張宇人聞言後暗示，該名來電者是受某工會操控以推動其自身議程，郭先生則堅決否認此說法，強調自己對內地醫護人員並無怨懟，並形容自己是堅定支持建制的愛國人士。

醫務衛生局局長盧寵茂亦加入爭論，呼籲各方應以核實數據作討論基礎，而非道聽途說，並警告有關就業市場的不實言論只會嚇怕有意入行的新血，最終令香港要自行承受人手短缺惡化的後果。官員多次強調，全職護士人數上升、流失率回落，正正證明包括容許非本地培訓人員執業等招聘改革，是在緩解人手壓力，而非擠走本地人才。

這場爭議揭示本港正面對一項微妙的平衡任務：既要透過輸入人才填補醫療人手的嚴重短缺，同時亦要讓新一代本地護士畢業生安心，確保他們的事業前景沒有因此犧牲。隨著近年培訓學額大幅增加，當局如何在這些互相競逐的壓力之間取得平衡，不但將影響護理行業的士氣，亦勢將左右公眾對政府整體人力規劃的信心。
