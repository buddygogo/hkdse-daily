English

Hong Kong's red-hot IPO market, on track for its strongest year in over a decade, is showing cracks as cornerstone investorslarge investors who agree in advance to buy a fixed stake in a new share listing基礎投資者 grow hesitant about committing to new listings. More than 60 per cent of companies that went public in the third quarter are now trading below their offer priceat a lower price than the one investors first paid when the shares were issued跌穿發售價, prompting institutional backers who had verbally agreed to anchorto provide a stable, reassuring commitment that supports something坐鎮；穩住 upcoming deals to quietly withdraw, forcing at least one planned October listing to be delayed until next month at the earliest.

The retreat strikes at the heart of a financing model Hong Kong's exchange has leaned on for decades. Cornerstone investors typically commit to buying a fixed stake before a listing even opens to the public, a move meant to reassure skittish retail buyers that deep-pocketedhaving a large amount of money available to spend or invest財力雄厚 names stand behind the deal. Without that early vote of confidence, underwritersfinancial institutions that help arrange and guarantee the sale of new shares包銷商 must work harder to drum up demand, and issuers risk pricing their shares lower just to get a deal done at all, a trade-off that can dent the very enthusiasm cornerstone investors were meant to generate in the first place.

Regulators, wary of a different kind of problem, have separately cracked down on what they call IPO-rigging syndicatescoordinated groups accused of illegally manipulating demand for a new share listing新股造假集團, suspending trading in one stock and restricting dealing in roughly HK$4.7 billion of assets held across 31 brokerages. Officials insist the two trends are not directly connected, but together they complicate an already delicate balancing act: keeping Hong Kong's listing pipelinethe series of companies preparing to go public in the near future上市項目儲備 attractive to global capital while weeding out the kind of inflated demanddemand that appears artificially larger than it actually is虛增需求 that can leave ordinary investors holding an overpriced stake.

中文翻譯

香港熾熱的新股市場原本有望創下逾十年以來最佳全年表現，但隨着基礎投資者對認購新上市公司漸趨猶豫，市場已開始出現裂痕。第三季掛牌的公司中，逾六成股價已跌穿發售價，促使早前口頭答應坐鎮上市交易的機構投資者悄悄退出，導致至少一宗原定十月上市的計劃需延至最快下月進行。

這波退潮正衝擊香港交易所數十年來一直倚賴的融資模式核心。基礎投資者一般會在上市公開認購前，承諾購入固定股份，目的是向容易動搖的散戶投資者保證，有財力雄厚的機構在背後撐腰。失去這份初始信心保證，包銷商便須更費力地招攬需求，而發行人亦可能要壓低定價，才能完成交易——但這種取捨，恰恰可能削弱基礎投資者原本應該帶來的那份信心。

監管機構則另有顧慮，近日打擊所謂的新股造假集團，暫停一隻股份的交易，並限制涉及31間經紀行、約47億港元資產的交易活動。官方強調兩者並無直接關連，但兩股趨勢同時出現，令本已微妙的平衡更形複雜：既要維持香港上市項目儲備對全球資金的吸引力，同時亦要剔除可能令普通投資者高價接貨的虛增需求。
