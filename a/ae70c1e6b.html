English

A new Greenpeace study has found that nearly 40 per cent of Hong Kong's elderly population live in districts facing medium to extreme heat risk, following a summer in which the mercury reached a record 39.8 degrees Celsius in Sheung Shui. Researchers assessedto evaluate or judge the nature of something評估 each district according to day and night temperatures, the concentration of senior citizens, and household income, before identifying Kwun Tong, Kwai Tsing, Tuen Mun and Wong Tai Sin as the four highest-risk areas.

It is widely argued that these districts are especially vulnerableeasily harmed or badly affected by something脆弱；易受傷害 because they combine older buildings with a high number of low-income elderly residents, some of whom live alone with limited access to air conditioning. The report notes that each of these districts has only a single heat shelter, and that such facilities are often located far from where vulnerable residents actually live, while also lackingto not have something that is needed缺乏 sufficient outdoor green space to help cool the surrounding area. By contrast, neighbourhoods with more greenery and country parks, such as Shek O and Pok Fu Lam, were found to pose comparatively low risk.

Greenpeace has called on the government to expandto make something larger in size, number or importance擴展 the network of community cooling centres, increase greenery coverage, and provide a hot weather allowance for low-income residents. Critics contend that current measures remain reactive rather than preventiveresponding after a problem occurs instead of acting to stop it beforehand被動應對而非防患未然, and that without structural change, vulnerable elderly residents will continue to bear the brunt of rising temperatures each summer. Nevertheless, officials point to existing heat shelter schemes as evidence that support is already in place. By the same token, environmental groups argue that isolated shelters cannot substitute for long-term urban planning that mitigatesto make a problem or its effects less severe緩解 the underlying causes of urban heat.

中文翻譯

綠色和平一項最新研究發現,香港近四成長者居住在面臨中度至極端酷熱風險的地區,此前的夏季,上水氣溫更創下39.8度的歷史新高。研究人員根據日夜溫度、長者人口密度及住戶收入評估各區情況,結果顯示觀塘、葵青、屯門及黃大仙為風險最高的四個地區。

普遍認為,這些地區特別脆弱,因為樓齡偏高的舊式建築與大量低收入長者居民並存,部分長者獨居,使用冷氣的機會亦有限。報告指出,這些地區各自只有一個納涼中心,而這些設施往往遠離脆弱居民的實際居所,同時亦缺乏足夠的戶外綠化空間以助降溫。相比之下,綠化較多及鄰近郊野公園的地區,例如石澳及薄扶林,風險相對較低。

綠色和平呼籲政府擴展社區納涼中心網絡,增加綠化覆蓋率,並為低收入居民提供酷熱津貼。批評者認為,現行措施仍然被動應對而非防患未然,若無結構性改變,脆弱長者每年夏天仍將首當其衝承受氣溫上升的影響。然而,官員指現有的納涼中心計劃已證明支援措施早已到位。同樣地,環保團體認為,孤立的納涼中心不能取代長遠的城市規劃,後者才能緩解都市酷熱的根本成因。
