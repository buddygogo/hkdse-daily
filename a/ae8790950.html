English

Police are investigating a suspected suicide pactan agreement between two people to end their lives together自殺協議 after an elderly couple were found unresponsive in their public housing flat in Shan King Estate, Tuen Mun, on Sunday afternoon. Their daughter, alerted by a concerned friend after repeated unanswered phone calls, discovered her parents and raised the alarm. Both were rushed to hospital but were pronounced dead shortly after arrival. Investigators found a tray of burnt charcoal and a note inside the flat, and initial inquiries suggest the pair had been unable to bearcould not endure or tolerate無法承受 the strain of the woman’s recent Parkinson’s disease diagnosis.

The case has reignited public concern over Hong Kong’s growing population of isolated elderly residentsolder people cut off from regular social contact or support孤獨無依的長者, more than 152,000 of whom aged 65 or above live alone across the city. Mental health experts note that senior citizens aged 60 and above recorded the highest suicide rate of any age group in 2024, a statistic that lays barereveals clearly and starkly揭示 the mounting pressure faced by ageing couples managing chronic illness with little outside support. Advocates argue that caregiving responsibilities, often shouldered entirely by a spouse or a single adult child, can take an immense tollcause great damage or suffering over time造成巨大的損耗 on both physical and emotional wellbeing, particularly once a degenerative conditionan illness that steadily worsens over time退化性疾病 such as Parkinson’s disease begins to progress.

In response to a string of similar tragedies, the government has pledged HK$500 million in annual recurrent funding to support carers, alongside a pilot scheme to bring in trained carers from overseas to ease pressure on families. Yet critics maintain that such measures remain reactive rather than preventiveresponding only after harm occurs, instead of acting beforehand to stop it屬被動而非預防性, and that far more needs to be done to identify at-risk households before crisis point is reached. For HKDSE students, the tragedy offers a poignant case in pointa clear example that illustrates a general point切實例子 for discussing how an ageing society, chronic illness and insufficient community support can converge with devastating consequences.

中文翻譯

警方正調查一宗懷疑自殺協議案件，一對老年夫婦星期日下午被發現在屯門山景邨的公屋單位內昏迷。他們的女兒在一名朋友因多次致電不獲接聽而報警後，發現了父母並報案求助。兩人送院後證實不治。調查人員在單位內發現一個燒過的炭盆及一張字條，初步調查顯示，兩人可能是無法承受太太最近確診柏金遜症所帶來的煎熬。

此案再次引起公眾對本港日益增多的孤獨無依的長者的關注，全港現時有超過152,000名65歲或以上的長者獨居。精神健康專家指出，60歲或以上的長者在2024年的自殺率為各年齡組別中最高，這項數字揭示年老夫婦在缺乏外界支援下獨自應對長期病患所承受的沉重壓力。倡議人士認為，照顧責任往往全落在配偶或單一成年子女身上，這種責任可能對身心造成巨大的損耗，尤其是當柏金遜症等退化性疾病逐漸惡化時。

面對一連串類似的悲劇，政府已承諾每年投放5億港元經常性資助支援照顧者，並推出引入海外受訓照顧者的試驗計劃，以減輕家庭的壓力。然而，批評者認為這些措施仍然屬被動而非預防性，當局需要在危機發生前做更多工夫，及早識別高風險家庭。對香港中學文憑考試學生而言，這宗慘劇正好是討論人口老化、長期病患與社區支援不足三者如何交織，造成災難性後果的切實例子。
