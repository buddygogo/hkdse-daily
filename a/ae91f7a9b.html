English

Hong Kong's various talent admission schemes have now drawn roughly 690,000 applications as of September, with about 320,000 successful applicants having already relocatedto move to live or work in a different place遷居；移居 to the city, Chief Secretary for Administration Eric Chan Kwok-ki revealed. He shared the figures at the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area High-Quality Talent Development Conference held in Dongguan, framing the numbers as evidence that the city's schemes are steadily bearing fruitto produce successful or beneficial results取得成果.

The figures mark a significant climb from earlier benchmarks, when cumulative applications and arrivals were far lower, suggesting the schemes have gained tractionincreasing acceptance, popularity or progress普及；受關注 as the city's economic recovery has strengthened. Chan said Hong Kong Talent Engage, the dedicated unit tasked with courting overseas professionals, would now step up its outreach in key global regions, aiming to attract skilled individuals in sectors ranging from finance to advanced technology. He also stressed the importance of helping newcomers settle smoothly, from housing to schooling for their children.

For students weighing future career paths, the figures underscoreto emphasise the importance of something強調；突顯 how competitive the local job market may become as hundreds of thousands of new arrivals, many highly qualified, enter the workforce. Some commentators have questioned whether local graduates risk being crowded outpushed out of a space or opportunity by competition被排擠；被擠出 of certain roles, while others argue that a larger talent pool ultimately expands the overall size of the economic pie, creating new openings rather than merely dividing existing ones.

中文翻譯

香港各項人才入境計劃截至九月已累積約六十九萬宗申請,當中約三十二萬名成功申請者已遷居本港,政務司司長陳國基透露。他在東莞舉行的粵港澳大灣區高質量人才發展大會上公布這些數字,認為這反映本港的人才計劃正逐步取得成果。

這些數字較以往的基準大幅攀升,當時累積申請數字及抵港人數遠為遜色,顯示隨着本港經濟復蘇力度增強,相關計劃日益受關注。陳國基表示,專責招攬海外專才的「香港人才服務窗口」將加強在主要海外地區的推廣工作,目標是吸納金融以至先進科技等各行各業的人才。他亦強調協助新來港人士妥善安頓的重要性,由住屋至子女教育均須照顧。

對於正在考慮未來事業發展的學生而言,這些數字突顯隨着數以十萬計、不少具高學歷的新來港人才投入職場,本地就業市場可能變得更為激烈。有評論質疑本地畢業生會否因此被擠出某些職位,但另一些人則認為,龐大的人才庫最終能擴大整體經濟規模,創造新機會,而非單純瓜分現有資源。
