English

The Development Bureau has sent the Legislative Council's development panel a revised blueprint for the Hung Hom waterfront, raising the site's residential componentthe part of a development set aside for homes rather than shops, offices or other uses發展項目中劃作住宅而非商舖、寫字樓等其他用途的部分 to 62 per cent of its roughly 206,500 square metres, after officials voiced strong doubts over market demandhow much buyers or tenants currently want to purchase or rent a type of property買家或租戶目前對某類物業的購買或租用意欲 for office space. Under the earlier version, commercial use took up a larger share; now retail, hotels and hostels combined will account for only about a third of the area, while a marina will occupy the remaining five per cent. The government first unveiled plans for the waterfront, next to Hung Hom station and the cross-harbour tunnel, as part of a wider push to revitalise the area for both residents and commuters.

The centrepiece of the new design is a pair of interconnected landmark towersvery tall, visually striking buildings meant to define an area's skyline用以標誌一個地區天際線的顯眼高樓, standing 184 metres and 236 metres tall, intended to give the waterfront a distinctive skyline. To make room for them and for more public open spaceland set aside for anyone to use for leisure or recreation, not built on預留供公眾休憩而不作建築用途的土地, four residential blocks included in the earlier proposal have been scrapped, with those sites converted into space for the public to enjoy views across Victoria Harbour.

Officials stressed that the timelinethe schedule showing when each stage of a project should happen顯示項目各階段應於何時進行的時間表 for making the land available has not changed, with the sites still expected to be ready for development in early 2029. Supporters argue the revised plan responds sensibly to a softening office marketa situation where demand and prices for office space are falling寫字樓需求及價格下跌的情況, but some lawmakers have questioned whether scrapping so much commercial space will leave the district without enough jobs nearby, and whether the promised public realmthe publicly accessible outdoor spaces within a development, such as plazas and waterfront walkways發展項目內供公眾使用的室外空間，如廣場及海濱長廊 will materialise as designed once construction begins. The bureau said it would take lawmakers' feedback into account before finalising technical details, including building heights and traffic arrangements, ahead of a planned tender process.

中文翻譯

發展局向立法會發展事務委員會提交紅磡海濱的修訂方案，將地盤約20.65萬平方米面積中的住宅部分比例提高至62%，原因是官員對寫字樓的市場需求存有強烈疑慮。相比早前版本中商業用途佔較大比例，現時零售、酒店及旅舍合計僅佔整個地盤約三分之一，餘下5%則用作遊艇會。政府最初提出紅磡站及過海隧道旁這片海濱用地的規劃，原意是希望同時造福居民及通勤人士。

新方案的核心是一對互相連接的地標大樓，分別高184米及236米，旨在為海濱締造獨特天際線。為騰出空間興建大樓及增加公共休憩空間，早前方案中四座住宅大樓已被剔除，原址將改劃為供公眾欣賞維多利亞港景致的空間。

官員強調有關土地可供發展的時間表並無改變，預計相關地盤仍會在2029年初可供發展。支持者認為，修訂方案是對疲弱寫字樓市場的合理回應，但部分議員質疑削減大量商業樓面，會否令該區附近職位不足，以及日後落實的公共空間能否如設計所言般實現。發展局表示會參考議員意見，在正式招標前敲定建築高度及交通安排等技術細節。
