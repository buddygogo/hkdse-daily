English

A newly published report has found that Hong Kong is being left off the itinerary of most major international concert tours, intensifying calls for the city to fix long-standing weaknesses in how it books and tickets live shows. Covering 95 Western artist tours between 2023 and 2026, the Hong Kong Future Economy Institute found that only 21.1 percent made a stop inincluded as part of a journey or series of events在行程中加插一站 the city, a figure dwarfed by Singapore's 49.5 percent and Tokyo's remarkable 74.7 percent.

The report points squarely at structural problems rather than a lack of demand. Rising ticketing frauddishonest schemes involving the sale of fake or non-existent event tickets票務詐騙 has eroded fans' trust in buying through official channels, while government-run venues such as the Hong Kong Coliseum operate on a rigid 12-month booking window with no provisional holding systema temporary reservation that can later be confirmed or released暫時留場安排, making it difficult for promoters to lock in dates far enough in advance to attract global superstars. Kai Tak Stadium, built partly to host exactly this kind of event, has so far welcomed only a single Western headliner, Coldplay, underscoring how chronically underusedused far less than its full capacity or potential over a long period長期未被充分利用 the venue remains for international tours.

For a city that has long branded itself an entertainment hub, the findings are a reminder that physical infrastructure alone cannot guarantee a share ofensure receiving part of something保證獲得其中一份 the lucrative “concert economy” without matching flexibility in how venues are run. The institute's recommendations now put pressure on authorities to decide whether loosening bureaucratic rulesmaking official rules and procedures less strict放寬官僚規則 is worth the risk of double-booking or scheduling conflicts at venues that also serve local community needs.

中文翻譯

一份新發表的報告發現，香港被排除在大部分國際巨星巡迴演唱會的行程之外，令社會進一步要求政府正視香港在演唱會場地預訂及票務安排上長期存在的弱點。報告調查了2023年至2026年間95個西方藝人的巡演，發現只有21.1%在行程中加插一站來港演出，遠低於新加坡的49.5%，以及東京驚人的74.7%。

報告直指問題在於結構性缺陷，而非需求不足。日益嚴重的票務詐騙削弱了歌迷對正規購票渠道的信任，而政府管理的場館例如香港體育館，則採用僵化的12個月預訂制度，並無任何暫時留場安排，令主辦單位難以提早足夠時間鎖定場地，以吸引國際級巨星。啟德體育園部分原意正是為了舉辦這類盛事而興建，但至今僅接待過一位西方頭牌藝人Coldplay，反映場館長期未被充分利用於國際巡演。

對於一個長期自詡為娛樂之都的城市而言，這項研究結果提醒大眾：單靠硬件設施，並不能保證獲得其中一份利潤豐厚的「演唱會經濟」，還需要場館管理方式配合相應的彈性。智庫提出的建議，現時令當局面臨壓力，須決定放寬官僚規則是否值得冒上場館重複預訂或與本地社區需要發生時間衝突的風險。
