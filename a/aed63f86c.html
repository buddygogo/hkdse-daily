事件背景

Hong Kong's intangible cultural heritage mart returns for three free days in November, bringing grassroots cultural groups into direct contact with the public.

香港非物質文化遺產市集將於十一月一連三天免費舉行，讓草根文化團體直接接觸公眾。

事件分析

The event highlights how living traditions, unlike physical artefacts, depend on direct public exposure to survive beyond specialist circles.

活動反映活態傳統與實物文物不同，必須透過直接接觸公眾，才能延續到專門圈子以外。

English

Hong Kong's Earthpulse Society has confirmed that a dedicated mart devoted to the city's intangible cultural heritagecultural practices, skills and knowledge — such as crafts, performance and festivals — that are passed down between generations rather than kept as physical objects非物質文化遺產 will return to the Hong Kong Cultural Centre's open piazzas from November 6 to 8, giving residents a free, three-day opportunity to encounter traditions that rarely reach a mainstream stage. Staged under the banner “Crafts in Connection, Tradition in Continuation,” the event forms part of the wider Hong Kong Culture Festival and Muse Fest 2026, and is intended to bring grassroots cultural groupssmall, community-based organisations that promote local traditions, usually without major institutional funding草根文化團體 into direct contact with the public rather than leaving their work confined to specialist circles.

Among the headline attractions is a curated programmea selection of performances or exhibits carefully chosen and arranged by an organiser around a theme精心策劃的節目 combining the Hung Shing Choi Lee Fat Fist fighting style with lion dance and lion drumming, arranged by the Chiu Man On Martial Art Association to show how a single martial arts lineagean unbroken line of teachers and students through which a particular style of martial arts has been passed down over generations武術傳承 can support several performing traditions at once; commentary accompanying the routines will explain the footwork and drumming patterns to spectators unfamiliar with the art form. The Hong Kong Chaoren Culture & Arts Promotion Association will contribute a set of Chiu Chow music, while a five-piece ensemble performs traditional Guangdong instrumental pieces alongside a lion dance adapted for young children.

Organisers say the free admission policy is meant to widen public appreciationa broader recognition and valuing of something among ordinary people, not just specialists大眾的欣賞與重視 of practices that depend on intergenerational transmissionthe passing of skills, knowledge or customs from older generations to younger ones跨代傳承 for their survival, at a time when many of Hong Kong's folk crafts struggle to recruit apprentices. By scheduling veteran practitioners alongside children's performances on the same programme, the mart hopes to show that cultural continuitythe unbroken persistence of a cultural practice across successive generations文化延續性 need not mean standing still, but can instead be sustained by making tradition accessible to a new generation of Hongkongers.

中文翻譯

香港「地球脈信會」(Earthpulse Society) 證實,一個專門展示本港非物質文化遺產的墟市,將於十一月六日至八日一連三日,在香港文化中心露天廣場免費舉行,讓市民有機會接觸鮮少登上主流舞台的傳統文化。活動以「工藝相連,傳統延續」為主題,屬香港文化節及「藝萃2026」的一部分,旨在讓草根文化團體直接與公眾接觸,而非只局限於小圈子之中。

是次活動的焦點節目之一,是由「趙萬年國術會」編排的一個精心策劃的節目,將洪勝蔡李佛拳與醒獅、獅鼓表演結合,展現單一武術傳承如何同時承載多種表演藝術;表演期間亦設有旁述,向不熟悉此武術的觀眾講解步法與鼓點編排。「香港潮人文化藝術推廣會」將帶來一場潮州音樂表演,另有一個五人傳統廣東音樂組合,配合專為小朋友而設的醒獅表演。

主辦方表示,免費入場的安排旨在擴大大眾的欣賞與重視,讓更多市民重視依靠跨代傳承方能延續的技藝,因為現時不少本地民間工藝都面臨招募學徒困難的問題。透過將資深師傅與兒童表演安排在同一節目之中,墟市希望證明文化延續性不代表原地不動,而是可以透過令傳統貼近新一代香港人而得以延續。
