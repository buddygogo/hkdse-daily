English

Having formally put its long-delayed waste-charging scheme on indefinite hold after fierce public backlash, the government says it will instead lean on a carrot-only approacha strategy that relies purely on rewards or convenience to encourage behaviour, rather than penalties單靠獎勵而非懲罰的策略 to raise recycling rates, starting with a wider network of round-the-clock automated collection points across the territory. Secretary for Environment and Ecology Tse Chin-wan said on Saturday that officials remain confident the city can still meet the binding targetsofficial goals that a government has formally committed itself to achieving, with consequences for missing them具約束力的目標 set out in Hong Kong’s first five-year environmental plan, despite dropping the fee that was meant to be the policy’s main lever.

The expanded network will let residents drop off recyclables at self-service machines at any hour rather than only during limited staffed opening times, addressing a long-standing complaint that existing recycling points were too inconveniently scheduledarranged at times that make something difficult or impractical for most people to use開放時間不便 for working households. Tse also pledged to review the permitted profit marginthe maximum level of profit a regulated company is officially allowed to earn准許利潤率 of the city’s two privately run power companies when their current agreements with the government expire in 2033, and said Hong Kong would import more clean electricity from the mainland to help cut emissions in the meantime.

Green groups gave the expansion a cautious welcome but warned that voluntary participationtaking part in something by personal choice rather than because it is legally required自願參與 alone has never been enough to meaningfully cut the roughly four million tonnes of waste Hong Kong still sends to landfill each year. Without a financial disincentivea cost or penalty designed to discourage a particular behaviour經濟上的抑制誘因 for over-producing rubbish, they argued, the burden of hitting the five-year plan’s targets will fall almost entirely on infrastructure the government has yet to fully fund, leaving the city’s landfill capacitythe total amount of waste a landfill site is physically able to hold before it is full堆填區容量 under continued strain as its three remaining landfills edge closer to exhaustion.

中文翻譯

政府在飽受公眾強烈反彈後,已正式將一拖再拖的垃圾收費計劃無限期擱置,表示將轉而依靠單靠獎勵而非懲罰的策略提高回收率,首先是在全港擴展全日24小時運作的自助回收機網絡。環境及生態局局長謝展寰周六表示,即使放棄了原本被視為政策主要手段的收費安排,當局仍有信心能夠達到香港首個五年環保規劃中訂立的具約束力的目標。

擴展後的網絡將讓居民全天候於自助機投放可回收物,而非只限於開放時間有限的有人駐守回收點,藉此回應市民長期批評現有回收點開放時間不便,難以配合在職家庭的需要。謝展寰亦承諾,待兩間私營電力公司與政府的現行協議於2033年屆滿時,將檢討其准許利潤率,並表示香港在此期間將增加從內地輸入清潔電力,以助減少排放。

環保團體對擴展計劃表示有保留的歡迎,但警告單靠自願參與,從來都不足以切實減少香港每年仍送往堆填區的約四百萬噸垃圾。他們認為,若缺乏經濟上的抑制誘因以遏止過度製造垂圾,達成五年規劃目標的重擔幾乎將完全落在政府尚未全面落實資金的基建設施上,令本已持續緊張、三個堆填區逐漸飽和的堆填區容量問題百上加斤。
