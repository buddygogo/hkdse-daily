English

The government and MTR Corporation have signed off on the second phase of the Northern Link railway, with the combined cost of the Main Line and Spur Line now pegged at HK$86.2 billiona sum of money stated at July 2025 prices862億港元 — 28 percent below the original estimate. Secretary for Transport and Logistics Mable Chan and MTR chief executive Jacob Kam signed the comprehensive project agreementa formal, all-encompassing contract covering every stage of a major infrastructure project全面項目協議 after the Chief Executive in Council approved the financial arrangements, clearing the way for construction on the strategic railway projectan infrastructure project seen as crucial to a region’s long-term development plans具戰略意義的鐵路項目 to proceed at pace.

Phase two alone, covering tunnels, stations, a depot and electrical and mechanical systems, is estimated at HK$54.8 billion, with spending spread outdistributed gradually over a period of time instead of paid all at once分攤 over roughly a decade, starting with HK$1.1 billion in 2027. Officials say the savings were achieved partly through design refinements and partly through the rail-plus-property modela funding method that pairs railway construction with nearby property development so sale proceeds help cover costs鐵路加物業模式, under which the government has granted MTR eight sites for residential and commercial development, easing the need for direct government fundingmoney paid straight out of public coffers rather than raised through another mechanism政府直接注資.

The full line remains on track to open by 2034, connecting the western part of the Northern Metropolis to the existing rail network and significantly cutting journey times for residents of new towns planned for the border region. Transport officials say the project is central to unlocking land supplymaking otherwise inaccessible or underused land available for development by providing transport access釋放土地供應 in the Northern Metropolis, Hong Kong’s flagship development zone intended to ease the housing shortage and create space for new industries. Critics, however, have questioned whether cost overrunsspending that ends up higher than originally planned or budgeted超支 seen on other mega-projects might still resurface as construction gets under way, and whether demand for homes in the new towns will materialise as quickly as planners hope.

中文翻譯

政府與港鐵公司已就北環線第二階段工程拍板，主線連同支線的合併造價現定為862億港元（以2025年7月價格計算），較原先估算低28%。運輸及物流局局長陳美寶與港鐵行政總裁甘俊樂，在行政長官會同行政會議批准相關財務安排後，簽署全面項目協議，為這項具戰略意義的鐵路項目的建造工程全速展開鋪平道路。

第二階段工程涵蓋隧道、車站、車廠以及機電系統，估計造價為548億港元，有關開支將分攤約十年支付，首筆款項為2027年的11億港元。官員表示，造價得以下調，部分原因是設計上作出優化，部分則歸功於鐵路加物業模式——政府已批出八個地盤供港鐵發展住宅及商業項目，藉此減少對政府直接注資的需要。

全線預計仍可按計劃於2034年通車，屆時將連接北部都會區西部與現有鐵路網絡，大幅縮短邊境新市鎮居民的出行時間。運輸官員表示，此項目對於釋放土地供應至關重要——北部都會區是香港的旗艦發展計劃，旨在緩解房屋短缺問題，並為新興產業創造空間。不過，有批評者質疑，其他大型基建項目曾出現的超支問題，會否在工程展開後重現，以及新市鎮的住屋需求能否如規劃般迅速湧現。
