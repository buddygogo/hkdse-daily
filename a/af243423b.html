English

Hong Kong's Independent Commission Against Corruption has laid charges againstformally accused someone of a criminal offence正式落案起訴 two former senior executives of Johnson Cleaning Services, accusing them of conspiring to acceptsecretly agreeing together to receive something improperly串謀收受 more than HK$75 million in bribes from a vehicle supplier. William Fung Chi-wang, 54, a former director of the cleaning company and former chief executive of its Hong Kong-listed parent, and Jimmy Lee Sai-leung, 57, the firm's former general manager, both appeared in court this week to face the charges.

According to the prosecution's case, the alleged scheme centred on a single favoured suppliera business given preferential treatment over its rivals獲優待的供應商 of cleaning vehicles. Between November 2024 and January 2026, Johnson Cleaning repeatedly bought entire fleets of vehicles from this one supplier and then routinely sent the same supplier all of its maintenance work, running up a cumulative expenditurea total amount spent, built up over a period of time累計支出 of more than HK$160 million. Prosecutors allege the bribes were a direct reward for repeatedly showing undue favourimproper or unfair preferential treatment不當優待 to the supplier in the company's procurement decisions, with the supplier's own proprietor named among those who allegedly made the payments.

The case adds to a string of recent ICAC actions against the cleaning industry's procurement practicesthe processes and decisions involved in purchasing goods or services採購做法. In January, a separate investigation led to the arrest of fourteen people, including three staff members of the same listed group, over a distinct allegation involving roughly HK$18 million in bribes from three subcontractors. Corporate governance observers say the back-to-back cases lay bareexpose or reveal clearly something previously hidden徹底暴露 how easily a handful of senior staff can quietly steer a large company's spending toward favoured partners, and are pressing listed firms to tighten internal audit and whistle-blowing channels well beyond the cleaning sector.

中文翻譯

香港廉政公署已正式落案起訴兩名莊臣清潔服務的前任高層，指他們串謀收受一名車輛供應商合共超過7500萬港元的賄款。54歲的馮志弘為該清潔公司前董事，亦曾任其香港上市母公司行政總裁；57歲的李世良則為該公司前總經理，兩人本週同時出庭應訊。

根據檢方案情，涉案計劃集中於一間獲優待的供應商，專門供應清潔車輛。在二零二四年十一月至二零二六年一月期間，莊臣清潔多次向同一供應商整批購入車隊，並隨即將所有維修保養工作交予同一供應商，累計支出超過1.6億港元。檢方指控，該筆賄款正是對該公司在採購決策上一再向供應商作出不當優待的直接回報，而供應商本人亦被列為涉嫌付款人之一。

這宗案件接續近期廉政公署多宗針對清潔行業採購做法的行動。今年一月，另一宗獨立調查曾導致十四人被捕，包括同一上市集團的三名員工，涉及另一宗涉款約1800萬港元、來自三個分判商的賄款指控。企業管治觀察人士指出，接連出現的案件徹底暴露少數高層人員如何輕易將大企業的開支暗中導向獲優待的合作夥伴，促使上市公司在清潔行業以外亦須大幅收緊內部審計及舉報渠道。
