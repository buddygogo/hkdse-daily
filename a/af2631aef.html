English

A disturbing video has prompted Hong Kong police to arrest a couple on suspicion of ill-treatmentcruel or abusive treatment of someone in one’s care虐待 after viral footagevideo that spreads rapidly online and is viewed by huge numbers of people在網上瘋傳的片段 showed their 10-year-old daughter struggling along a Tuen Mun footpath under roughly two dozen beer cans in a torn backpack. Passers-by offered to help, but she refused, saying she feared her father would scold her.

The 38-year-old father and 32-year-old mother were both taken into custody, and the Social Welfare Department has convened a multidisciplinary case conferencea meeting bringing together professionals from different fields to assess a family’s needs跨專業個案會議, with recommendations due within two weeks. Sympathy curdled into anger once the parents revealed on television that they rely on Comprehensive Social Security Assistancea government welfare payment for Hong Kong residents in financial need綜合社會保障援助, prompting questions over how a welfare family could afford several overseas holidays a year.

Facing intense scrutinyvery close and critical public attention嚴厲的審視 online, Secretary for Labour and Welfare Chris Sun pledged a strict review of the family's welfare paymentsregular financial support provided by the government to those in need福利援助金 and of how officials verify a claimant's household incomethe total money earned by everyone living in one home家庭總收入. Both parents were bailed pending investigationreleased from custody on the condition that they return while the case is still being looked into獲准保釋候查, the mother briefly hospitalised after feeling unwell. The case has reopened debate on whether earlier social worker interventionsupport or action taken by a trained welfare professional to help a family社工介入 could have caught the trouble sooner, and whether eligibility checks are strict enough.

中文翻譯

一段令人不安的影片促使香港警方拘捕一對夫婦，懷疑他們虐待或疏忽照顧子女，片段顯示他們十歲的女兒在屯門富泰花園一條行人路上，吃力地揹著一個破爛背囊及手提著膠袋，內藏約二十四罐啤酒。據目擊者稱，在網上瘋傳的片段拍下女童步履艱難的一刻，數名途人曾主動提出代她搬運，惟女童拒絕，並解釋若未能完成任務，恐怕會被父親責罵。

38歲父親及32歲母親雙雙被捕，社會福利署已就此召開跨專業個案會議，評估家庭需要，初步建議預計一至兩星期內公布。夫婦另外三名子女已被送往醫院檢查，並暫時留院觀察以策安全。然而，公眾對這家庭的同情迅速轉為憤怒，皆因父母在電視訪問中透露他們正領取綜合社會保障援助，令人質疑一個領取福利援助的家庭何以仍能每年多次出外旅遊。

面對社交媒體上的嚴厲的審視，勞工及福利局局長孫玉菡承諾嚴格檢視這家庭的福利援助金，並更廣泛地檢討當局如何核實申領人的家庭總收入及開支。兩名家長事後均獲准保釋候查，母親則因自稱感到不適而短暫送院。無論結果如何，此案再次引發社會辯論，討論更早的社工介入能否及早發現這家庭的困難，以及現行福利資格審查程序是否足夠嚴謹，在防止制度被濫用的同時，仍能保護真正有需要的兒童。
