English

Hong Kong's Centre for Health Protection confirmed on Wednesday that a 12-year-old girl had died after contracting influenza, becoming the second paediatric fatalitythe second death of a child from a particular illness or cause within a defined period第二宗兒童死亡個案 of the current flu season, which began in late June. The girl, who had underlying health conditionspre-existing medical problems that can make a person more vulnerable to serious illness潛在健康問題 and had not received this year's flu vaccination, first developed a fever, a cough and diarrhoea on 17 October, with vomiting following soon after. Her condition deteriorated rapidly, and she was taken to the accident and emergency department of Eastern Hospital two days later, where she suffered a cardiac arrest before being resuscitated and transferred to the paediatric intensive care unit.

Laboratory tests subsequently confirmed that the girl had contracted the influenza A(H1) virus, and her condition was further complicated by septic shocka life-threatening drop in blood pressure caused by the body's extreme response to infection敗血性休克 and multiple organ failure. She passed away on 20 October, and the case has since been referred to the Coroner's Court, in line with standard procedure for unexpected deaths. CHP controller Edwin Tsui Lok-kin told reporters that 25 severe paediatric influenza infections had been recorded since the season began, and he warned that this year's outbreak could persist for at least five months, considerably longer than in previous years.

Tsui pointed to data showing that unvaccinated children face a risk of severe complications or death roughly five times higher than their vaccinated peers, and he noted with concern that this year's childhood vaccination uptake ratethe proportion of a target population that accepts or receives a particular service, such as a vaccine接種率 is running about five percentage points below last year's. Health authorities have renewed calls for parents to arrange vaccinations for their children without delay, describing the jab as the most effective form of protection against severe illnessmeasures or treatments that reduce the likelihood of a serious or life-threatening outcome重症保障, particularly for children with existing medical vulnerabilities. Separately, a household contact of the girl reportedly developed mild upper respiratory symptoms but did not require hospital treatment, underscoring how readily the virus spreadsdescribes how easily or quickly an infectious disease can pass from one person to another病毒傳播迅速 within households.

中文翻譯

香港衞生防護中心周三證實,一名12歲女童感染流感後死亡,成為本季流感(於六月底開始)以來的第二宗兒童死亡個案。該名女童本身有潛在健康問題,且未曾接種本年度流感疫苗,她於10月17日開始出現發燒、咳嗽及腹瀉,其後很快亦出現嘔吐症狀。其病情迅速惡化,兩日後被送往東區尤德夫人那打素醫院急症室,期間一度心臟停頓,經搶救後轉往兒科深切治療部。

化驗結果證實,女童感染甲型流感(H1)病毒,其後病情進一步複雜化,併發敗血性休克及多重器官衰竭。她於10月20日離世,案件已按慣常程序轉交死因裁判法庭處理。衞生防護中心總監徐樂堅向傳媒表示,本季以來已錄得25宗嚴重兒童流感感染個案,並警告今年疫情可能持續至少五個月,較過往年份明顯延長。

徐樂堅指出,數據顯示未接種疫苗的兒童出現嚴重併發症或死亡的風險,較已接種疫苗的同齡人高出約五倍,他亦關注到今年兒童疫苗接種率較去年同期低約五個百分點。衞生部門再次呼籲家長盡快安排子女接種疫苗,形容疫苗對患有潛在健康問題的兒童而言,是最有效的重症保障措施。另外,該女童的一名家居接觸者據報出現輕微上呼吸道症狀,但無需入院治療,反映病毒在家庭成員之間傳播迅速。
