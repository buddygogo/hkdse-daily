English

Hong Kong police said on Thursday that they had arrested ten people, including a student couple described as suspected core membersthe individuals believed to play the most central or important role in an organised group疑似核心成員, over a fraud and money launderingthe process of disguising the origins of illegally obtained money so it appears legitimate洗黑錢 syndicate that swindled victims out of more than HK$2.1 million. The man and woman, who study at different tertiary institutions, allegedly helped run an operation that posted fake modelling-job advertisements on social media between October 2024 and this May.

According to police, jobseekers lured by the adverts were tricked into registering e-wallet accountsdigital accounts used to store money and make electronic payments電子錢包賬戶 using phone numbers supplied by the syndicate; these were then turned into puppet accountsaccounts controlled by someone other than the registered holder, used to move money while hiding its true source傀儡賬戶 that received funds from separate scams. The operation covered forty-three victims, roughly sixty per cent of whom were students who had fallen for shopping scamsfraudulent schemes that deceive buyers into paying for goods, such as concert tickets, that are never delivered網購騙案, including some offered discounted concert tickets. Police said the proceeds were then spent at tertiary institution canteens, luxury massage parlours and clinics, disguising illicit funds as everyday spending.

All ten suspects face charges of conspiracy to defraudan agreement between two or more people to commit fraud together串謀詐騙 and money laundering. The case has renewed concern among universities and parents that students are increasingly recruited as intermediariespersuaded or hired to act as a go-between, often without fully grasping the criminal nature of the role被招攬作為中介人 in scam networks, sometimes without realising the seriousness of their involvement, prompting police to repeat warnings about job advertisements that promise easy money for minimal work.

中文翻譯

警方周四表示，已拘捕十人，涉及一個詐騙及洗黑錢集團，該集團涉嫌騙取受害人合共逾二百一十萬港元，當中包括一對被形容為疑似核心成員的大學生情侶。這名男子及女子分別就讀於不同的專上院校，涉嫌協助經營一個在二零二四年十月至今年五月期間，於社交媒體刊登虛假模特兒招聘廣告的集團。

警方指出，受騙的求職者被誘使使用集團提供的電話號碼登記電子錢包賬戶，該些帳戶之後被轉為傀儡賬戶，用以接收其他騙案的款項。此次行動涉及四十三名受害人，當中約六成為墮入網購騙案的學生，部分人更曾獲提供優惠演唱會門票。警方表示，犯罪得益隨後被用於專上院校飯堂、豪華按摩院及診所的日常消費，藉此將不法資金偽裝成一般開支。

十名疑犯全部面對串謀詐騙及洗黑錢罪名。此案再次引起大學及家長關注，擔心學生愈來愈容易被招攬作為中介人，捲入詐騙網絡，有時甚至未意識到自己參與行為的嚴重性，警方因而重申，會留意聲稱輕鬆賺快錢的招聘廣告。
