The 20th Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan, closed on Sunday after a fortnight that organisers described as a mix of turmoila state of confusion, disruption or disorder混亂，動盪 and teenage triumph. Hong Kong sent a delegationa group of people chosen to represent a city, country or organisation代表團 of about 580 athletes to compete across 38 sports, and came away with a record medal haulthe largest total number of medals ever won, collected together創紀錄的獎牌總數 of 46 medals — seven gold, 17 silver and 22 bronze.

The sailing squad was a standout, claiming ten medals in total, including four gold, for the team’s best-ever showinga performance or result, especially a good or notable one表現 at an Asian Games. Beyond the medal table, officials pointed to a wave of personal bests and breakthrough performancesresults that mark a sudden and important improvement, often for the first time突破性的表現 by younger athletes, several of whom eclipsedto do much better than a previous achievement, making it seem less impressive超越，蓋過 local records once held by more senior competitors.

Secretary for Culture, Sports and Tourism Rosanna Law, who travelled to Nagoya to support the delegation, praised the athletes’ fighting spirita strong determination to keep trying despite difficulty鬥志 on her return. Athletes have since been arriving home in staggered batches; the karate and football contingents landed first, while Monday’s group included the men’s and women’s rugby sevens teams and the water polo squad. 中文翻譯

第二十屆亞運於星期日在日本愛知・名古屋閉幕，主辦方形容這兩週充滿動盪與年輕選手創下佳績的瞬間。香港派出約五百八十人的代表團，在三十八個項目中出賽，最終取得創紀錄的獎牌總數，共四十六面獎牌，包括七金、十七銀及二十二銅。

帆船隊表現尤為亮眼，共奪十面獎牌，包括四面金牌，是該隊歷來在亞運的最佳表現。除獎牌榜以外，官方亦指出，許多年輕選手創下個人最佳成績，甚至做出突破性的表現，有人更超越資深選手所保持的本地紀錄。

文化體育及旅遊局局長羅淑佩曾親赴名古屋現場支持代表團，回港後讚揚運動員表現出的鬥志。運動員此後分批陸續回港，空手道及足球代表先行抵港，週一則輪到男女欖球隊及水球隊回港。這次成績預期將重新引發論爭，討論創紀錄的獎牌數量究竟應如何影響未來全港基層體育訓練的資源分配。
