English

Hong Kong's Poon Hao-ran, a fifteen-year-old wushu exponenta skilled practitioner of a martial art武術好手, delivered the territory's first-ever gold medal at a Youth Olympic Games on Tuesday, topping the men's taijiquan combined final at the Dakar 2026 Games in Senegal. His score of 18.720 points left him ahead of Vietnam's silver medallist by a razor-thin marginan extremely narrow or small difference極微小的差距 of just 0.028 points, so narrow that judges reportedly double-checked the scoring sheet before confirming the result. Japan's representative settled for bronze. The event marked wushu's medal debutthe first time an event has official medals首次設獎 at the Youth Olympics, lending extra significance to a result Hong Kong athletes had chased in vain since the Games began in 2010.

Poon's route to the podium was far from smooth. He had been battling a nagging colda mild illness that persists annoyingly揮之不去的感冒 in the days before the final, picked up while still adjusting to Dakar's climate, on top of minor training injuries from the lead-up to the competition. Rather than easing off, he redoubled his traininggreatly increased the amount of practice加倍訓練 in the final fortnight, trusting that extra repetitions would make up for lost sharpness. He arrived in Dakar already a known quantity in the sport, having claimed two gold medals and a silver at the world taijiquan championships in May, yet Tuesday's result was his first experience of Youth Olympic competition.

The win carried symbolic as well as sporting weight: Poon had been chosen to bear Hong Kong's flag at the opening ceremony, a duty usually reserved for an athlete the delegation expects to shine. Local sports officials hailed the result as a historic breakthroughan achievement of major, unprecedented importance歷史性突破 that could pave the waycreate conditions that make future success possible鋪路 for greater investment in wushu's youth pipeline, with the city's Olympic committee suggesting it may inspire more young practitioners to take up the sport competitively.

中文翻譯

香港十五歲武術好手潘浩然星期二在塞內加爾達喀爾二零二六年青年奧運會男子太極拳全能決賽中，為香港贏得歷來首面青奧金牌。他以18.720分之成績，只以0.028分之極微小的差距壓倒越南銀牌得主，差距之小令裁判據報在公布結果前兩度覆核計分表以作確認。日本代表屈居銅牌。是次項目為武術首次設獎的青奧比賽項目，令香港運動員自二零一零年青奧創辦以來苦候多年的突破更添意義。

潘浩然的奪金之路並非一帆風順。決賽前數日，他一直受揮之不去的感冒困擾，起因是仍在適應達喀爾的氣候，加上備戰期間留下的輕微訓練傷患。他沒有因此鬆懈，反而在最後兩周加倍訓練，相信額外的練習量能彌補狀態上的落差。他出戰達喀爾前已在武術界薄有名氣，今年五月的世界太極拳錦標賽中奪得兩金一銀，但星期二的賽果卻是他首次參與青年奧運會賽事。

這場勝利除了體育成就，亦具有象徵意義：潘浩然獲選在開幕式擔任香港代表團旗手，此職通常只授予代表團寄予厚望的運動員。本地體育官員形容這次賽果是歷史性突破，有望為武術青訓帶來更多資源投放，鋪路讓更多年輕好手參與這項運動的競技層面，香港奧委會亦認為此舉或能激勵更多年輕人投身武術。
