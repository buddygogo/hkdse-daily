English

Kai Tak Hospital, which is set to become Hong Kong’s largest public hospital, began phased operationsa gradual rollout of services in stages rather than all at once分階段運作 on 5 October, taking over from Queen Elizabeth Hospital as the flagship facilitythe most important institution of its kind, held up as a model or leader旗艦機構 of the Kowloon Central Cluster. The Specialist Out-patient Clinic Block and the Oncology Block were the first to launch, offering follow-up consultations, a family medicine centre and blood-drawing services mainly to ambulatory patientspatients who can walk in for treatment and do not need to be admitted overnight能自行走動的病人. Health authorities estimated that between 2,000 and 3,000 people would visit the hospital daily during this initial stage.

When fully completed, the hospital will have a gross floor areathe total built area of a building, measured for planning purposes總建築樓面面積 of 500,000 square metres and 2,400 beds, making it the city’s single largest public medical institution. In-patient, surgical and accident and emergency services, however, are not expected to come on streamto begin operating or become available for use開始運作 until 2028, meaning the hospital will continue to expand its role gradually over the next two years before it can meaningfully relieve pressure on neighbouring hospitals.

Despite the long-awaited opening, some patients voiced concerns over the hospital’s remote locationa place that is far away and not easy to reach偏遠位置 in Kowloon Bay. An elderly couple travelling from Mong Kok described a circuitousnot direct; following a long, roundabout route迂迴曲折的 45-minute minibus journey that stretched their total travel time to nearly two hours, calling the experience “really inconvenient” and urging the government to introduce direct bus or minibus routes to cut down on transfers. Their complaint points to a broader challenge facing healthcare plannersofficials responsible for designing and organising medical services醫療規劃人員: new hospitals built on reclaimed or peripheral land may struggle to serve the very communities, including the elderly, who rely on them most.

中文翻譯

啟德醫院將成為全港最大的公立醫院，於十月五日起分階段運作，取代伊利沙伯醫院成為九龍中聯網的旗艦機構。專科門診大樓及腫瘤科大樓率先啟用，主要為能自行走動的病人提供覆診服務、家庭醫學中心及抽血服務。衛生當局估計，首階段每日將有二千至三千人到院求診。

醫院全面落成後，總建築樓面面積將達五十萬平方米，設有二千四百張病床，成為全港規模最大的公立醫療機構。然而，住院、外科手術及急症室服務預計要到二零二八年才會開始運作，意味著醫院未來兩年仍須逐步擴展職能，才能真正紓緩鄰近醫院的壓力。

儘管醫院啟用備受期待，部分病人仍對其位於九龍灣的偏遠位置表示憂慮。一對從旺角出發的長者夫婦形容，單程需乘搭四十五分鐘迂迴曲折的小巴，令全程歷時近兩小時，直言「真的很不方便」，並呼籲政府開辦直達巴士或小巴路線，減少轉車次數。他們的怨言揭示了醫療規劃人員面對的更大難題：建於填海或邊陲地段的新醫院，可能難以真正服務最需要它們的社區，包括長者。
