English

After more than two decades lighting up Victoria Harbour, A Symphony of LightsHong Kong’s long-running nightly laser and multimedia show over Victoria Harbour幻彩詠香江 will give its last-ever performance on 18 November, The Standard has learned. First launched in January 2004 and later recognised by Guinness World Records as the world’s largest permanent light and sound show, the display has become one of the harbour’s most recognisable tourist drawsan attraction that is especially effective at bringing visitors to a place吸引遊客的賣點, projecting synchronised lights and music from dozens of buildings on both sides of the harbour every night.

Officials say the decision to retire the show was driven mainly by its mounting running coststhe ever-increasing expense of keeping something operating不斷上升的營運成本 and by a steady decline in appeal among repeat visitors, even after a 2017 revamp added a new musical score performed by the Hong Kong Philharmonic Orchestra. The farewell performance, set for 8pm, will include a special pyrotechnic displayan elaborate fireworks show put on for a special occasion特別煙火表演 as a send-off, and will coincide with the opening night of this year’s WinterFest, described by organisers as the largest-ever editionthe biggest version of a regular event that has ever been held歷來規模最大的一屆 of the festival.

Rather than returning to a single, fixed nightly show, the Tourism Board plans to replace Symphony of Lights with a more flexible rotation of seasonal light programmes spread acrossdistributed over an area rather than concentrated in just one spot分佈於 multiple districts, including Tsim Sha Tsui and Causeway Bay. Secretary for Culture, Sports and Tourism Rosanna Law said the new format need not be confined to Victoria Harbour, framing the change as part of a wider push to encourage visitors to venture beyond the harbourfronttravel to areas further away than the main, well-known waterfront area走出海旁範圍 into neighbourhoods across the city. The move has stirred mixed feelings among residents who grew up with the nightly display as a fixture of harbour life, even as tourism officials insist that reinventing the attractioncompletely changing something to give it a fresh form or purpose為景點注入新意 is necessary to sustain visitor interest in the long run.

中文翻譯

在維多利亞港上空閃耀逾二十年後，《幻彩詠香江》據《英文虎報》報道，將於11月18日舉行最後一場演出。這項燈光音樂匯演於2004年1月首次登場，後來獲健力士世界紀錄認證為全球規模最大的常設燈光音效表演，多年來一直是維港夜景最具代表性的吸引遊客的賣點之一，每晚利用維港兩岸數十座大樓同步投射燈光，並配合音樂演出。

官員表示，決定讓這項表演退役，主要是因為其不斷上升的營運成本，以及重遊旅客的興趣持續下降——即使在2017年完成革新，加入由香港管弦樂團演奏的全新配樂後，情況依然如此。告別演出將於晚上八時舉行，並會加插一場特別煙火表演作為謝幕，而演出當晚亦正值今年冬日祭開幕——主辦方形容今年為該節目歷來規模最大的一屆。

旅遊發展局計劃不再單靠一個固定的每晚表演，而是改以更靈活的季節性燈光節目，分佈於多個地區舉行，包括尖沙咀及銅鑼灣。文化體育及旅遊局局長羅淑佩表示，新形式不必局限於維港一帶，並形容此舉是推動旅客走出海旁範圍，深入全港各區的更廣泛策略之一。這項轉變，令不少自小伴隨這場每晚匯演成長的市民感到五味雜陳，但旅遊官員則堅持，為景點注入新意是維持長遠旅客興趣的必要之舉。
