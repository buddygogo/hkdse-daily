English

Hong Kong’s extended seven-day National Day Golden Week has delivered a welcome boost to the retail and catering trades, with about 1.29 million Mainland visitors expected to cross the border during the holiday. Small and medium-sized hotels reported occupancy ratesthe percentage of available rooms or spaces that are actually being used入住率 above 90 percent, while room rates at budget and chain hotels climbed by 10 to 20 percent as demand outstripped supplywhen the amount people want is greater than the amount available需求超過供應.

More than 3,800 restaurants, ranging from street-side eateries to five-star hotels, rolled out promotional dealsspecial offers designed to attract customers, such as discounts促銷優惠, including 30 percent off the total bill and set meals priced as low as HK$77. The catering industry is projecting a year-on-yearcompared with the same period in the previous year與上年同期相比 sales rise of around 10 percent, pushing dining revenue over the holiday to roughly HK$350 million.

Retailers reported similar gains: shopping malls recorded about a 10 percent increase in footfallthe number of people entering a shop or area, used as a measure of business activity人流量, with vendors seeing revenue climb 10 to 20 percent; jewellers anticipated gains of around 15 percent, apparel sellers roughly 10 percent, and personal-care stores as much as 20 percent. While welcome, such figures should be read with some caution, as holiday-driven spendingspending that happens mainly because of a festival or holiday period, rather than all year round節日性消費 can mask weaker demand during quieter months, leaving the sector’s broader recovery trajectorythe general direction or path along which something develops over time, here referring to economic recovery復原軌跡 still uncertain.

中文翻譯

香港今年延長至七天的國慶黃金週，為零售及餐飲行業帶來一波喜訊，預計約有一百二十九萬名內地遊客在假期期間訪港。中小型酒店的入住率超過九成，連鎖酒店及平價酒店的房價則因需求超過供應而推高百分之十至二十。

超過三千八百家餐廳，上至五星級酒店、下至街邊小店，紛紛推出促銷優惠，包括整單九折及最低只需八十元的套餐。餐飲業預測銷售額將與上年同期相比增長約百分之十，令假期期間的餐飲收入達約三億五千萬元。

零售商亦取得類似成果：商場人流量大約上升百分之十，商戶收入則上升百分之十至二十；珠寶商預期收入可增約百分之十五，服飾零售商約百分之十，個人護理店舖則可能高達百分之二十。然而，這些數字仍須謹慎解讀，因為節日性消費可能掩蓋假期以外月份的消費需求前景，整體零售業的復原軌跡仍未明朗。
