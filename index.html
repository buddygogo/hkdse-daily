Hong Kong Confronts a Deepening Youth Mental Health Crisis After Troubled Start to School Term

Sunday Examiner ·18 September 2026

Hong Kong's return to the classroom this September has been overshadowed by tragedy. Within the first week of the new academic year, at least seven cases of student self-harm or suicide were reported across the city, three of which proved fatal. It is widely argued that such incidents, though distressing in isolation, point to a far more entrenched systemic probleman issue rooted in the structure of a system rather than in isolated individual cases系統性問題 within the city's education culture, one in which academic achievement has become almost synonymous with personal worth.

Critics contend that Hong Kong's intensely examination-oriented culturean educational environment that places overwhelming emphasis on test results以考試為導向的文化 leaves little room for students who struggle to keep pace. Data compiled by the University of Hong Kong's Centre for Suicide Research and Prevention reveal that some 30 per cent of suicide victims under the age of 15 were enrolled in Band One schools, suggesting that academic peer pressurestress arising from competition with high-achieving classmates學業同儕壓力 may weigh most heavily precisely where expectations are highest. Nevertheless, education authorities caution against viewing school banding alone as the root cause, noting that parental expectations and students' own self-imposed expectationsstandards a person sets for themselves, often unrealistically high自我要求 compound the pressure considerably.

By the same token, schools have not been passive in the face of this crisis. A Three-tier School-based Emergency Mechanism, regularisedmade a standard, permanent part of practice rather than a temporary measure常規化 across all secondary schools during the 2025/26 school year, has now been extended on a trial basis to upper primary levels, offering earlier identification of at-risk studentsindividuals identified as being in particular danger of harm高危學生 and swifter referral to counselling. Even so, commentators insist that lasting change demands more than emergency intervention: it requires a fundamental shift towards fostering resiliencehelping someone develop the ability to recover from difficulty培養抗逆力 and genuine emotional well-beinga state of psychological and emotional health情緒健康 throughout a child's schooling, rather than treating mental health merely as a crisis to be managed after the fact.

Food Poisoning Cluster Sickens 39 at Sha Tin Primary School, Prompting Scrutiny of Lunch Suppliers

SCMP ·12 September 2026

An investigation is under way after 39 pupils and staff at a Sha Tin primary school fell ill following a school lunch, in what health officials describe as a suspected food poisoning clustera group of illness cases traced to a common contaminated source食物中毒羣組. The affected group, comprising 37 pupils aged five to eleven and two staff members, had eaten a catered midday meal of soy-braised chicken drumsticks and long pasta at Baptist (Sha Tin Wai) Lui Ming Choi Primary School on 9 September before developing abdominal painpain or discomfort in the stomach area腹痛 and diarrhoea between eight and twenty-two hours later.

According to the Centre for Health Protection, two of the affected pupils sought medical attention but did not require hospital admissionbeing formally admitted to hospital for treatment住院 and have since remained in stable condition, while the remaining cases presented only mild symptoms. It is widely argued that classroom-based lunch portioningthe practice of dividing and serving catered meals inside individual classrooms rather than a central kitchen課室分餐模式, in which catered food is divided among pupils inside the classroom rather than under closer kitchen supervision, may heighten the risk of cross-contaminationthe transfer of harmful bacteria from one food item or surface to another交叉污染 once meals leave the supplier's premises.

Danny Catering Service, the school's lunch supplier, described the outbreak as an isolated incidentan event that occurred on its own, without being part of a wider or recurring pattern單一事件 and has since announced it will discontinue the classroom portioning arrangement at the affected school. Nevertheless, the episode has reignited debate over whether Hong Kong's school lunch suppliers are subject to sufficiently rigorous food safety standardsstrict rules and procedures designed to prevent contamination and illness嚴格的食物安全標準. Parent groups have called on the Education Bureau to conduct unannounced inspections of catering contractors, arguing that public confidence in school meal safety cannot rest on suppliers' assurances alone.

Hong Kong to Grant Foreign Firms Equal Mainland Market Access Under Third CEPA Revision

The Standard ·21 September 2026

Hong Kong is set to overhaul its free trade arrangementmake major structural changes to an existing trade agreement全面革新自由貿易安排 with the mainland for a third time, in a move authorities say will make the city more attractive to international investors. Commerce Secretary Algernon Yau announced that under the third revision to the Closer Economic Partnership Arrangement, or CEPA, foreign companies manufacturing goods in Hong Kong will be granted preferential mainland market accessspecial, more favourable conditions for entering and trading in the mainland Chinese market優惠的內地市場准入 on equal terms with local Hong Kong firms.

Under the proposed amendment, foreign-invested manufacturers will be eligible for zero-tariff entrythe ability to import goods without paying any customs duty零關稅准入 into the mainland, provided their products meet the criteria for "Made in Hong Kong" origin. It is widely argued that this represents a significant departure from previous rounds of CEPA, which had largely reserved such benefits for companies with substantial local Hong Kong ownership. Critics of the status quothe existing state of affairs before a change is introduced現狀 had long complained that international firms based in the city faced an uneven playing field compared with mainland-linked competitors.

By the same token, the revision forms part of a broader commitment outlined in Hong Kong's first Five-Year Plana government document setting out development goals and policy direction over a five-year period五年規劃, which pledged to pursue a comprehensive upgrade of CEPA and deepen cooperation with mainland service sectors. Analysts suggest that by lowering barriers for foreign direct investmentmoney invested by an overseas company or individual into productive assets within another economy外商直接投資, Hong Kong hopes to reinforce its position as a gateway connecting international capital with the vast mainland consumer market, at a time when the city faces intensifying competition from rival regional trading hubsother cities or economies competing to serve as centres of international trade區域貿易樞紐 such as Singapore and Shenzhen.

Why K-pop Conquered Global Pop Culture While Cantopop and C-pop Lagged Behind

Time ·21 September 2026

As K-pop cements its status as a global, multibillion-dollar cultural exporta form of entertainment or art that is successfully sold and popularised in other countries文化輸出, commentators have increasingly asked why neither Cantopop nor Mandopop has produced an act with the same global ubiquitythe quality of being present or recognised almost everywhere全球普及度 as groups such as Blackpink or Stray Kids. It is widely argued that South Korea's success rests on a highly systematised idol training systema structured, long-term programme used to develop performers before their public debut偶像訓練制度, paired with aggressive, coordinated marketing across international markets from its earliest stages.

Nevertheless, Hong Kong's own Cantopop scene has been undergoing a quiet but notable revival closer to home. Large-scale concerts injected an estimated HK$2.2 billion into the local economy between 2023 and 2024, while grassroots cultural eventscommunity-driven events organised informally rather than by major commercial institutions草根文化活動 such as DJ nights have drawn crowds of thousands, with one collective progressing from small venues to supporting international acts at sold-out shows. More than a thousand new Cantopop tracks were released in 2025 alone, and homegrown artists have come to dominate local streaming charts, a marked change from a decade ago when Cantopop was widely dismissed by younger listeners as unfashionable.

By the same token, critics caution against overstating the parallel between the two phenomena. Whereas K-pop's success is underpinned by deliberate global positioninga strategy consciously designed to appeal to international audiences from the outset刻意的全球定位, Hong Kong's revival remains, for now, a largely domestically driven phenomenona trend whose momentum comes mainly from the local market rather than international demand以本地市場為主導的現象. Some industry observers argue that this organic, community-rooted growth may prove more sustainable in the long run, even if it never achieves the sheer international scale of its Korean counterpart; others insist that without deliberate investment in overseas distribution channelsthe networks and platforms used to sell or stream content internationally海外發行渠道, Cantopop's revival risks remaining a largely local success story.

🛗 Hopewell Centre's Glass Lift Becomes Hong Kong's Latest Viral Sensation

Social Trend

A glass observation lift inside Wan Chai's Hopewell Centre, rising from the 17th to the 62nd floor, has unexpectedly become one of Hong Kong's most shareable tourist attractionsa place or experience popular specifically because it is easy and appealing to post on social media熱門打卡景點 on TikTok and Instagram. Clips capturing the sweeping harbour view as the lift ascends have racked up millions of views, with creators queuing specifically to film the ride, often tagged alongside travel itineraries and budget-friendly recommendationssuggestions for activities or experiences that cost little or no money慳錢推介 for visitors. The trend has drawn both overseas tourists and local content creators eager to showcase a free, cinematic slice of the city.

HKDSE Writing Angles
  • Psychological appeal: The gentle thrill of a fast, panoramic ascent combined with the sense of discovering a 'hidden gem' triggers a small dopamine reward, encouraging viewers to seek out the same experience themselves.
  • Social media influence: The lift's smooth vertical motion and dramatic skyline reveal make for highly watchable, easily looped short-form video, which algorithms readily amplify.
  • Consumer culture: As a free or near-free experience offering a premium view, the trend appeals strongly to budget-conscious travellers and reflects a wider appetite for low-cost, high-impact content among Gen Z audiences.
