CityU concludes disciplinary probe after viral orientation camp video sparks controversy

A viral orientation-camp video has plunged the City University of Hong Kong (CityU) into one of its most closely scrutinisedexamined in great detail被仔細審視 controversies of the new academic year. Footage circulating on Threads and Instagram showed a shirtless male student carrying a female peer clad only in underwear at a so-called "disco night", while onlookers in matching T-shirts cheered and waved glowsticks. Further images that later surfaced, showing students with bruising and red marks on their backs and thighs, only served to inflame public outragemake public anger worse激化公憤. It is widely argued that such rituals, dressed up as bonding exercises, in fact normalise a culture ofmake something seem acceptable through repetition使……成為常態 humiliation that few first-year students feel able to refuse.

In the wake ofimmediately following在……之後 the backlash, CityU vowed a policy of "zero tolerance" and convened a Student Discipline Panel to investigate. The inquiry has now concluded: all students involved have admitted wrongdoing, apologised formally, and accepted disciplinary penalties, although the university has withheld specific sanctions to safeguard student privacyprotect students’ personal information保障學生私隱. The Management Sciences Society, the students’ union body that organised the event, acknowledged serious mismanagementadmitted it had handled matters very badly承認嚴重管理失當 and promptly dissolved its executive committee. Critics contend that such swift internal dissolution, while symbolically important, does little to address the deeper question of why so-called "Ocamp" excesses resurface year after yearhappen again and again年復一年地重演 despite repeated pledges of reform.

Nevertheless, the episode has reignited a long-running debate about initiation culture at Hong Kong’s universities. By the same token that fraternities abroad have faced hazing bans after injuries and deaths, local commentators argue it is imperative that institutions move beyond token gesturesstop making only symbolic or superficial changes不再只作表面姿態 and instead overhaul the supervisory mechanismsthoroughly reform the systems of oversight徹底改革監督機制 governing student-run events. There is little doubt that peer pressure, alcohol, and a desire to belong together create fertile ground for poor judgement among freshmen eager to fit in. For this reason, student affairs offices across the sector are now under pressure to strike a balance betweenfind a fair middle ground between two competing needs在……之間取得平衡 preserving cherished traditions of camaraderie and protecting the basic dignity and safety of every participant.

'Covered in bites': pests and extreme heat worsen health risks for Hong Kong's homeless, NGO warns

Hong Kong Free Press ·7 September 2026

As Hong Kong swings between record-breaking heatthe hottest temperatures ever recorded破紀錄的高溫 and sudden heavy downpours, the city’s homeless population is facing a health hazard that has so far received little public attention: pests. A new NGO study by ImpactHK, which monitored four sites — including parks, tunnels and footbridges — over fifteen days, found that cockroach and rat infestation rates reached as high as 79 to 83 percent, while every single respondent at the Tsim Sha Tsui Cultural Centre reported problems with biting midges. It is widely argued that such findings lay bareexpose clearly揭露 a dimension of urban poverty that is too often overlooked.

The consequences extend well beyond mere discomfortsimple inconvenience純粹的不便. Three-quarters of those surveyed reported severely disrupted sleep, with bites frequently leading to skin inflammation or secondary infection. Nearly one in five said the financial burdenthe strain of extra costs經濟負擔 of buying insect repellent had only worsened their already precarious circumstances. Notably, respondents living on the street reported markedly more severe pest problems than those able to stay in hostels, a gap that critics contend exposes the limits ofreveals the shortcomings of揭示……的局限 current outreach services for the unhoused.

Nevertheless, campaigners insist the findings should not be read merely as an environmental health footnote but as evidence of a vicious cycle of deprivationa self-reinforcing pattern of hardship匱乏的惡性循環 in which extreme weather, inadequate shelter and inequality compound one another. By the same token that heatwaves have already been shown to endanger elderly residents in subdivided flats, advocates argue it is imperative that the government extend outreach provisionsexpand support services to reach more people擴展外展服務 and pest-control resources to the city’s most vulnerable groups. For this reason, ImpactHK is calling on policymakers to treat climate-linked health risks among the homeless as a matter of urgent social equity rather than an issue that can be safely ignored.

Hong Kong suspends contractors over 'extremely serious' Queen Mary Hospital lift defects

South China Morning Post ·16 August 2026

Hong Kong’s Development Bureau has taken the unprecedented stepan action never taken before前所未見的舉措 of suspending a main contractor and its lift subcontractor from tendering for public works, after defects left the vast majority of lifts in a newly built clinical block at Queen Mary Hospital unreliable. Of the 28 lifts installed, 24 have been affected, delaying the relocation of clinical servicesthe moving of hospital departments to the new building臨床服務的遷移 to the HK$15.15 billion facility that was originally due to open at the end of last year. Engineers have described the scale of the malfunction as "extremely rare" by Hong Kong standards.

It is widely argued that the episode raises uncomfortable questionsquestions that are difficult but necessary to confront令人尷尬的問題 about quality control on major public infrastructure projects. A lawmaker branded the situation "unacceptable", warning that the defects could further delay a project already years behind schedule. Under the terms of the contract, the firms responsible must bear the full cost of remedial workspay entirely for the repairs themselves承擔全部補救工程費用 and could face further claims for losses stemming from the delay. Health chiefs, for their part, have expressed open disappointment, acknowledging thatadmitting that承認 patients and frontline staff continue to bear the brunt of the setback.

Nevertheless, the Hospital Authority insists that rectification work is proceeding, with a target completion by the fourth quarter of this year. Critics contend that such assurances ring hollowsound insincere or unconvincing顯得空洞 to a public that has already waited years for the redevelopment to bear fruit. By the same token that construction delays elsewhere have eroded public confidence in project oversight, commentators argue it is imperative that the government tighten scrutiny overstrengthen its monitoring of加強對……的監察 contractors on safety-critical builds such as hospitals, where delays are measured not merely in dollars but in the everyday welfare of patients.

Hang Seng Index extends losses as rising oil prices stoke inflation fears

Sunday Guardian Live ·8 September 2026

Hong Kong equities extended their decline this week as surging oil pricesrapidly rising oil prices急升的油價 and heightened geopolitical tension unsettled investors across the region. The Hang Seng Index slipped roughly 0.5 percent to close near 25,283, marking a second consecutive session of losses after selling pressure had already dragged the benchmark down about 0.9 percent, or 226 points, at the start of the week. It is widely argued that the sell-off reflects growing anxiety over how renewed inflationary pressurea fresh rise in inflation重新出現的通脹壓力 might complicate central banks’ plans to ease interest rates further.

Financial, technology and energy-related counters bore the brunt of the sell-off, as traders weighed stronger-than-expected United States employment data against its implications for Federal Reserve policy. Analysts contend that pricier crude has a habit of seeping into broader inflation expectationsgradually affecting how people expect prices to change generally滲入更廣泛的通脹預期, a development that could, by the same token, weigh on Hong Kong households already grappling with elevated living costs. Nevertheless, some strategists point out that softer bond yields have provided regional markets with a degree of cushioning effecta softening or protective impact緩衝作用 against the worst of the volatility.

For this reason, market watchers are now looking ahead to the Federal Reserve’s upcoming policy decision for clearer signals on the direction of borrowing costs. It is imperative, economists argue, that Hong Kong — whose currency is pegged to the US dollar and whose economy remains highly sensitive tostrongly affected by對……高度敏感 global rate movements — closely monitor these external headwindskeep track of unfavourable outside forces密切監察這些外圍逆風 even as domestic growth forecasts for the year have been revised upward. There is little doubt that ordinary residents, from small investors to homeowners with adjustable mortgages, will be watching the coming weeks with considerable interest.

🧋 Hongkongers dip into the viral 'Sprite Tea' trend

Social Trend

A viral drink hack has taken over Hong Kong social media: dunking a tea bag straight into a bottle of Sprite. Creators on TikTok and Instagram have shown the DIY concoctiona homemade mixture people make themselves自製飲品 — typically two tea bags steeped in Sprite, then served over ice with a slice of lemon — racking up a 200 percent jump in related searches within a single week. The trend has proven so powerful that Sprite itself has capitalised on the crazetook commercial advantage of the popular trend把握熱潮, launching a ready-to-drink "Sprite Tea" product and rolling out a mobile pop-up tea stand handing out 80,000 free samples across busy Hong Kong districts.

It is widely argued that the appeal lies less in the taste itself than in the novelty factorthe excitement of trying something new and unusual新奇感 of an unlikely, rule-breaking combination that is cheap, easy to replicate, and instantly shareable. Nevertheless, the speed with which a corporation moved to commercialise a grassroots trend also raises questions about how quickly authentic youth culture can be absorbed into mainstream marketingturned into part of large-scale commercial advertising被納入主流市場推廣.

HKDSE Writing Angles
  • Psychological appeal: The unexpected, rule-breaking combination of tea and soda taps into curiosity and the pleasure of a small, harmless act of rebellion, encouraging viewers to try it themselves.
  • Social media influence: Short-form video platforms reward visually simple, easily replicated content, letting the trend spread rapidly among Hong Kong youth through duets and reaction videos.
  • Consumer culture: A multinational brand's swift move to commercialise the trend shows how corporations increasingly convert organic online creativity into ready-made retail products.
