Post Malone Postpones Hong Kong Stadium Concert, Leaving Fans Frustrated

SCMP ·4 September 2026

American rapper Post Malone has indefinitely postponedput off without a fixed new date無限期推遲 his Hong Kong stadium concert, originally scheduled for 16 September at Kai Tak Stadium as the opening date of his Big Ass Stadium World Tour across Asia. The promoter issued a brief statement confirming that ticket holders who purchased through HK Ticketing, Damai and Trip.com would receive full refundscomplete reimbursement of the purchase price全額退款 within twenty to thirty working days, though no explanation was offered for the sudden change. The announcement, made on 4 September, has sparked widespread disappointmenttriggered a strong negative reaction among many people引起廣泛失望 among fans, many of whom had already booked annual leave, flights and hotel accommodation specifically around the concert date.

It is widely argued that the postponement, which extends to the entire eight-date Asian leg as well as subsequent Australian and New Zealand shows, points to logistical challengespractical difficulties in organising and coordinating an event後勤上的困難 inherent in mounting a stadium tour of unprecedented scalea size or level never seen before前所未有的規模 across multiple time zones and venues. Critics contend that such disruption carries a significant financial cost, not only for the artist and promoter but also for fans who cannot always recover incidental expenses such as travel bookings. Since opening in 2025, Kai Tak Stadium has rapidly become Hong Kong's premier live entertainment huba central location where large concerts and performances are held現場娛樂樞紐, and a high-profile cancellation of this kind inevitably raises questions about the reliability of the city's expanding concert calendar.

Nevertheless, industry observers note that postponements of this nature are not unusual within global touring schedules and do not necessarily reflect on Hong Kong's own capacity to host large-scale events. By the same token, the episode has renewed calls for stronger consumer protectionlegal safeguards for the rights and interests of buyers消費者保障 measures within the ticketing industry, particularly clearer refund timelines and compensation for non-ticket losses. The ripple effecta spreading, indirect consequence of an event連鎖反應 of the cancellation, from disappointed fans to affected hospitality bookings, illustrates how deeply intertwined live entertainment has become with Hong Kong's broader tourism economy, and underscores the importance of transparent communication whenever such changes are unavoidable.

Victoria Park to Host Expanded Mid-Autumn Lantern Displays and Fire Dragon Dance

The Standard ·4 September 2026

Hong Kong will host an expandedmade larger in size, scope or quantity擴大的 Mid-Autumn Festival celebration this September, with the Leisure and Cultural Services Department's long-running Lantern Carnival at Victoria Park now joined by the Hong Kong Tourism Board's first-ever International Lantern Spectacular, running from 17 to 27 September. Centred on the theme of "Reunion", the new display will bring together more than 3,000 lanterns from twenty countries and regions, including intricately crafted Qinhuai lanterns from Nanjing that are inscribed onofficially recorded or listed as被列入 China's National List of Intangible Cultural Heritage.

It is widely argued that pairing a homegrown tradition with international lantern craftsmanship reflects a deliberate strategy to bolsterstrengthen or support增強 Hong Kong's appeal as a cultural tourism destination while still honouring local heritage. Nearby, the centuries-old Tai Hang Fire Dragon Dance, a fixture on the same National Intangible Cultural Heritage list, will mark its 147th edition from 24 to 26 September, with a 67-metre dragon illuminated by 12,000 incense sticks weaving through the neighbourhood's streets. Such events play a pivotal role inhave a crucial and central function in扮演關鍵角色 sustaining intangible cultural practices that might otherwise fade as urban life modernises.

Nevertheless, some heritage advocates caution that commercialisationthe process of managing something mainly for profit商業化 of traditional festivals risks diluting their original spiritual and communal significance. On balance, however, the coordinated programme illustrates how Hong Kong continues to negotiate a balance between preserving authentic tradition and cultivatingdeveloping or promoting deliberately培養 a distinctive, internationally recognised cultural identity.

HKU Partners with Tech Giants to Open AI Labs at Expanded Shanghai Campus

SCMP ·2 September 2026

The University of Hong Kong has partnered with several major technology companies to establish on-site artificial intelligence laboratories at its expanded Shanghai campus, which welcomed more than 300 students at the start of the new academic term earlier this month. The initiative is designed to equip students withprovide someone with the tools or skills needed為學生裝備 hands-on experience in developing and applying AI systems, rather than relying solely on theoretical instruction. It comes amid a broader national push, articulated in the 15th Five-Year Plan, for Hong Kong to serve as a hub for AI research, development and commercialisationthe process of turning research into marketable products商業化.

It is widely argued that partnerships of this kind allow universities to keep pace with an industry that evolves far more rapidly than traditional curricula can accommodate. Critics contend that Hong Kong's tertiary institutions have historically been slow to bridge the gap betweenreduce the difference or distance separating two things彌合……之間的差距 academic theory and workplace-ready skills; the new AI labs are intended to directly address this shortfall. By the same token, Chief Executive John Lee has described AI as a central planka fundamental or defining part of a plan or policy核心支柱 of the city's economic development strategy, and the forthcoming AI Research and Development Institute, due to begin operations later this year, is expected to further catalysecause or accelerate a process促進 commercialisation efforts across sectors.

Nevertheless, some educators caution that access to cutting-edge facilities must be matched by sufficient teaching capacity and clear pathways into employment, or else students may graduate with impressive technical exposure but limited practical opportunity. On the other hand, supporters maintain that positioning Hong Kong students within a cross-borderspanning or operating across national or regional borders跨境的 campus network gives them a distinct competitive advantage as regional demand for AI talent continues to intensify. Ultimately, the success of such initiatives will depend on whether laboratory-based learning translates into tangible innovation and employment outcomes for the students who pass through them.

All Signals Cancelled as Tropical Depression Saudel Weakens Over Guangdong

RTHK ·3 September 2026

The Hong Kong Observatory cancelled all tropical cyclone warning signals earlier this month after Tropical Depression Saudel, which had earlier prompted a Standby Signal No. 1, weakenedbecame less strong or intense減弱 into an area of low pressure over inland Guangdong. The system had made landfall over Zhangzhou in Fujian before tracking across eastern Guangdong and losing strength, with forecasters concluding that a higher signal, such as the Strong Wind Signal No. 3, was unlikely to be warrantednot probable to be justified or necessary不太可能有需要 as the storm dissipated. The episode marked the latest in a string of tropical systems, following closely behind Tropical Storm Krovanh, to brush past the city during an unusually active September.

It is widely argued that the frequency of such systems this year reflects broader climatic shifts affecting the western Pacific, with environmental groups noting that Hong Kong has experienced an uptick inan increase or rise in the frequency of something……的增加 tropical cyclone activity and rainstorm warnings compared with historical averages. Critics contend that while the Observatory's forecasting has grown increasingly accurate, public preparednessthe state of being ready to deal with a situation應變準備 for rapid signal changes remains uneven, particularly among outdoor workers and small business operators who must adjust their schedules at short notice.

Nevertheless, officials point out that swift signal cancellations, such as the one following Saudel's dissipation, allow economic activity to resume quickly and minimise disruptionreduce interference or inconvenience to the smallest possible extent減少干擾 to daily life. By the same token, this pattern of frequent but often short-lived storms underscores the importance of maintaining climate resiliencethe capacity to withstand and recover from climate-related shocks氣候韌性 measures, including improved drainage and early-warning systems, so that the city can adapt as extreme weather events become an increasingly routine feature of the autumn season.
