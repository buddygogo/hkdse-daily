HKTB to Host First-Ever International Lantern Spectacular This Mid-Autumn

It is widely reported that the Hong Kong Tourism Board will stage the city's first-ever International Lantern Spectacular at Victoria Park, running from 17 to 27 September under the theme of “Reunion”.
