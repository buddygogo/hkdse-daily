Eight 'Zero-Class' Primary Schools Approved for Merger as Hong Kong's Birth Rate Hits Record Low

The Standard ·29 August 2026

It is widely reported that the Education Bureau has given the go-ahead for eight so-called “zero-class” primary schools — institutions that failed to secure sufficient enrolmentsucceed in attracting enough students未能取得足夠收生人數 to open a Primary One class this September — to merge with neighbouring institutions, while two others will run self-financing classes and a further four are set to be phased outgradually closed down over time逐步淘汰 altogether. The wave of closures and mergers, among them the case of Fresh Fish Traders School joining forces with a nearby institution, is widely attributed to Hong Kong’s record-low birth ratethe lowest number of births ever recorded歷來最低出生率, with only 31,714 babies registered last year.

Critics contend that such large-scale restructuring, however necessary on paper, risks erodinggradually wearing away or weakening侵蝕；削弱 decades of institutional identity and alumni attachment overnight. Teachers at affected schools have voiced concern over job security, while parents worry about the disruption of moving children mid-way through their primary education to unfamiliar campuses. Nevertheless, the Education Bureau has sought to cushion the impactsoften or reduce the negative effect of something緩衝影響 by granting merged schools a three-year grace period during which class sizes are not strictly enforced, allowing time to iron outto resolve small problems or difficulties解決；理順 practical matters such as curriculum alignment and staff arrangements.

By the same token, education commentators argue that mergers alone are but a stopgap rather than a genuine solution to a much deeper demographic predicamenta difficult and unpleasant situation困境. With Hong Kong’s population continuing to age and its workforce contracting, some have called for a more holisticconsidering the whole picture rather than just individual parts全面的；整體的 population policy that goes beyond the education sector alone. It remains to be seen whether the current wave of consolidation will be sufficient to sustain smaller neighbourhood schools in the years ahead, or whether further closures are simply being deferreddelayed or postponed to a later time推遲 rather than avoided.

Households and Small Businesses Squeezed as Power Fuel Surcharges Soar for Fourth Straight Month

South China Morning Post ·5 September 2026

It is widely reported that HK Electric’s fuel clause charge climbed to 60.7 Hong Kong cents per kilowatt-houra unit for measuring the amount of electricity used千瓦時（度電） this September, marking the fourth consecutivefollowing one after another without interruption連續的 monthly increase since May and pushing the average net tariff up by 1.8 per cent from August. Having surgedincreased suddenly and greatly急升 roughly 1.3 times since March, the mounting surcharge has left many households bracing for heftier bills just as summer’s air-conditioning demand begins to taper off.

Critics contend that the repeated hikes are placing a disproportionate burdena heavy load of responsibility, work, or cost負擔 on small and medium-sized enterprises already grappling with thin profit margins and subdued consumer spending. Business groups have accordingly renewed their lobbyingtrying to persuade officials to support a particular policy游說 for targeted energy subsidies, arguing that without timely relief, some smaller operators could be pushed to the brink of closure. Nevertheless, the power companies maintain that the increases largely reflect volatile global fuel prices and ongoing geopolitical tensions rather than domestic factors, and both operators have rolled out rebate schemes aimed at alleviatingmaking a problem less severe紓緩 the strain on grassroots households.

By the same token, economists caution that unless the upward trend is curbedcontrolled or restricted遏止, the knock-on effect on Hong Kong’s broader cost of living could further squeeze consumers who are already wary of inflation. It is against this backdrop that calls for a more transparent fuel-clause mechanism, and greater governmental oversight of tariff adjustments, have gained tractionincreasing acceptance or support認受性；支持 among legislators and consumer-rights advocates alike.

Three Severe Child Flu Cases Prompt Vaccination Appeal as Hong Kong's Influenza Season Persists

Dim Sum Daily ·5 September 2026

It is widely reported that the Centre for Health Protection has recorded three additional severe paediatric influenza cases, all involving previously healthy children who had shown no history of travel during their incubation periodthe time between infection and the appearance of symptoms潛伏期. Health officials have accordingly renewed their appealrepeated a request or urgent call again再次呼籲 for members of the public, particularly children and the elderly, to seek vaccination ahead of the Seasonal Influenza Vaccination Programme, which begins on 17 September.

Since the end of June, a cumulative total of sixteen severe paediatric cases have been documentedofficially recorded in writing記錄在案, of which eight patients have since recovered and been discharged, two remain in a stable condition, three are classified as serious, two are critical, and one has tragically died. In one instance, two classmates of an affected six-year-old girl subsequently developed mild symptoms, though neither required hospitalisation; the school concerned has since been advised to step upincrease or intensify an effort加強 infection-control measures such as more frequent disinfection and closer monitoring of student attendance.

Nevertheless, with Hong Kong having entered its influenza season as early as late June and activity levels remaining stubbornly highpersistently high despite efforts to lower it居高不下, the CHP has cautioned that the coming weeks — coinciding with the start of the new school term — could see a further uptick in transmission. By the same token, parents and school staff alike are being urged to remain vigilantwatchful and alert to possible danger保持警惕 for early symptoms, to observe good personal hygiene, and to seek timely medical attention, lest a single overlooked case snowballgrow rapidly in size or seriousness迅速惡化；滾雪球式擴大 into a wider school outbreak.

Hong Kong to Roll Out AI Upskilling Drive as Government Concedes Automation Will Displace Some Jobs

South China Morning Post ·4 September 2026

It is widely reported that the government, through Financial Secretary Paul Chan, has unveiled plans for the Employees Retraining Board to join forces withto cooperate or work together with someone與……聯手 major technology firms from November onwards, delivering specialised artificial intelligence courses to the local workforce. The initiative forms part of a broader HK$50 million “AI for All” campaign expected to directly benefitto help someone in a way that has an immediate, clear effect直接惠及 some 50,000 residents through more than two hundred training events over the coming two years.

Critics contend, however, that the scheme’s scale falls conspicuously short of the challenge at hand: current retraining efforts are estimated to reach a mere one per cent of the working population, even as demand for AI-related skills has accelerated markedlyincreased in speed or intensity in a noticeable way顯著加快, with AI job postings rising fifty per cent year-on-year. Compounding matters, full-time vacancies suitable for university graduates have plungedfallen suddenly and sharply急跌 by sixty per cent since 2022, leaving many young jobseekers questioning whether their qualifications remain commensurate withmatching or in proportion to something與……相稱 the demands of an AI-driven labour market.

Nevertheless, officials maintain that government-led upskilling represents only one prongone part of a multi-part plan or strategy（策略的）一環 of a wider strategy, and have called on employers to shoulder greater responsibility for reskilling their own staff. By the same token, career counsellors are increasingly urging secondary and university students alike to treat AI literacythe ability to understand and effectively use artificial intelligence tools人工智能素養 not as an optional extra but as a core, transferableable to be applied in different contexts or jobs可轉移的；通用的 skill essential to safeguarding their long-term employability.

🧵 Threads' 'Pressure-Free Playground' Culture Takes Off in Hong Kong

Social Trend

It is widely observed that Threads has seen explosive growthextremely rapid and sudden increase爆炸性增長 among Hong Kong users this year, driven largely by the city’s longstanding appetite for text-first, forum-style discussion. Local meme pages and lifestyle accounts have taken to describing the platform as a “pressure-free playground”, where users feel free to post spontaneouslywithout planning; on impulse自發地；即興地 without the polish demanded by Instagram. Because Threads’ algorithm is comparatively decentralisednot concentrated in the hands of a few powerful accounts去中心化的, even small accounts can spark a citywide “Threadquake”, giving ordinary users a rare sense that any post might go viral.

HKDSE Writing Angles
  • Psychological appeal: The low-pressure, unpolished format lowers the barrier to posting, giving users a sense of authenticity and relief from curated social media personas.
  • Social media influence: Meme pages and lifestyle influencers actively repost and amplify threads, turning niche posts into citywide talking points almost overnight.
  • Consumer culture: Brands are increasingly adopting a 'dual-sword' strategy, using Threads to spark conversation before funnelling attention to Instagram Reels or e-commerce pages.
